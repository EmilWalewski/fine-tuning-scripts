Dom Development S.A. separate financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2025 identify the Company as the parent of the Dom Development S.A. Group, headquartered at Pl. Piłsudskiego 3, 00-078 Warsaw, registered in the National Court Register under KRS 0000031483 by the District Court for the Capital City of Warsaw, 12th Commercial Division of the National Court Register. Its PKD activity is construction projects related to building construction, PKD 41102; operations are primarily in Warsaw and surrounding areas, with subsidiaries conducting development activity in the Wrocław, Tricity and Kraków markets. The Company is majority-owned by Groupe Belleforét S.a r.l. of Luxembourg, which held 54.81% of shares at 31 December 2025. The ultimate parent of the Dom Development S.A. Group is SCOP 2003 Trust, established under the law of England and Wales; SCOP 2003 Trust is the sole shareholder of SCOP Luxembourg 2007 5.8 r.l., which is the majority shareholder of Groupe Belleforet S.4 r.l. The core business is construction and sale of residential real estate in Poland; duration is indefinite and no business line was discontinued in the twelve months ended 31 December 2025. The separate financial statements were prepared under the historical cost convention, on a going-concern basis, in PLN, with financial data presented in thousands of PLN unless stated otherwise. The Company also prepared consolidated financial statements of the Dom Development S.A. Group for the twelve months ended 31 December 2025, approved by the Management Board on 16 March 2026; top-level consolidated financial statements are prepared by SCOP Luxembourg 2007 S.A r.l., majority shareholder of Groupe Belleforet 8.4 r.l. The Company and subsidiaries operate only in Poland. Management assessed that the Russian invasion of Ukraine on 24 February 2022 and escalation of the Middle East conflict destabilize the regional economic environment and may affect apartment demand, construction costs and subcontractor availability, but at the reporting date all Company projects were progressing according to plan or ahead of schedule; Management continuously monitors project-level, Company-wide and long-term impacts and, at approval date, identified no circumstances threatening going concern. Polish law requires the Company to prepare separate financial statements under IFRS as adopted by the EU; for the year ended 31 December 2025, Management identified no difference between effective IFRS and EU-endorsed IFRS relevant to the Companys accounting policies. The statements are separate financial statements under IAS 27 and comply with all applicable IFRS adopted by the EU, including standards and interpretations accepted by the IASB and IFRIC. Accounting policies are consistent with those used for the year ended 31 December 2024, except amendments effective for annual periods beginning 1 January 2025: amendments to IAS 21, The Effects of Changes in Foreign Exchange Rates, published in August 2023, concerning assessment of currency exchangeability, estimation of spot exchange rates when exchangeability is absent, and related disclosures; Management concluded these amendments have no material effect on the Companys financial position, performance or disclosures. The Company did not early adopt published but not yet effective standards, interpretations or amendments: amendments to IFRS 10 and IAS 28 on sale or contribution of assets between an investor and its associate or joint venture, with EU endorsement deferred; IFRS 18, Presentation and Disclosure in Financial Statements, published in April 2024 to replace IAS 1 from 1 January 2027, mainly affecting the statement of profit or loss, disclosures on performance measures, and aggregation or disaggregation; Annual Improvements to IFRS 1, IFRS 7, IFRS 9, IFRS 10 and IAS 7, effective for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2026; and May 2024 amendments to IFRS 9 and IFRS 7 on classification and measurement of financial instruments, including recognition and derecognition timing for selected financial assets and liabilities, an exemption for selected financial liabilities settled via electronic payment systems, SPPI assessment guidance, disclosures for instruments whose contractual terms may change cash flows, and updated disclosures for equity instruments measured at FVOCI, effective from 1 January 2026. Management is reviewing the impact of these standards but does not expect new standards or amendments to existing standards to have a material impact on the Companys separate financial statements in the first period of application.
