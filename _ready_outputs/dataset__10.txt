The Group carried out a materiality assessment process to identify material impacts, risks and opportunities requiring disclosure, using internal discussion to assess its business model against sustainability aspects including the nature of its sales offering, local markets, supply-chain and labour-market specifics, and sustainability challenges faced by the property development and construction sector. The process covered Group impacts from own operations and business relationships, identifying and assessing factors material to the Group environment, society and corporate governance, including raw-material and material consumption, construction waste management, impacts on local communities, and business-relationship impacts through supplier and subcontractor interactions. The methodology complied with CSRD requirements and European Sustainability Reporting Standards, applying double materiality: impact materiality concerning the Group effect on people and the environment, and financial materiality concerning the effect of sustainability matters on the entity financial position, financial performance, cash flows, access to finance or cost of capital over short-, medium- or long-term horizons. The double materiality analysis was performed for the first time in 2024, using qualitative and quantitative inputs from internal and external stakeholders and involving management, management boards of Group companies and the Supervisory Board. The analysis required activities across stages including Phase 1 business activity analysis, Phase 2 identification of main impacts, Phase 3 prioritisation of the most material impacts, Phase 4 identification and assessment of risks and opportunities, and Phase 5 approval of material topics by the Management Board of Dom Development S.A. and the Supervisory Board. In the second half of 2025, the double materiality analysis was reviewed; the review added surveys of customers and local communities, workshops with NGOs with the option for meeting participants to complete a survey, a repeated employee survey across all Group companies, a repeated competitor analysis based on material-issue disclosures under ESRS, and revision of preliminary analyses. Under Phase 1 business activity analysis, the Group comprehensively assessed its operations by analysing the business plan and strategy to verify alignment of strategic assumptions with current objectives and development directions, reviewing financial statements to assess financial condition and economic stability, reviewing investor communications to assess transparency and compliance with reporting standards, and mapping activity types, offered products and operating locations to capture the scale of operations and market position. In the 2025 review, the Group focused on two main business segments.
