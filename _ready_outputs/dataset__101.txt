XTB Group reports no material reporting-period impact of the low-carbon transition on macroeconomic trends, energy consumption, energy mix or technology development. The Climate Policy directs climate-change mitigation and adaptation, implementation of XTB Group ESG Strategy 2024-2027, and future climate neutrality. Measures cover full carbon-footprint calculation, reduction or compensation options, cyclical review of climate opportunities, risks and time scenarios under TCFD recommendations, climate-risk integration into risk management, and use of climate-related opportunities. Current commitments: annual carbon-footprint calculation and monitoring in Scopes 1, 2 and 3; net-zero Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions under the GHG Protocol; improved data collection for broader footprint and emissions analysis. The policy covers XTB Group and geographies matching offices analysed in climate scenarios; stakeholders include clients, employees, media, capital-market representatives, social environment, and regulatory and market environment. It is publicly available, adopted by Management Board resolution, known to employees, and implemented by the Sustainability Team. With no measurable targets, effectiveness will be assessed through annual review of the document and declared actions; future greenhouse-gas reduction targets will serve as indicators. Reporting-period projects: internal educational programme Naturalna inwestycja, ongoing; review of double materiality research for E, S and G areas, completed in 2025; review of climate opportunities and risks analysis, completed in 2025; GHG Protocol Corporate Accounting and Reporting Standard emissions reporting for Scope 1, 2 and 3, started in 2024 and continued in 2025; ESG Strategy 2024-2027 implementation, ongoing. In 2025 XTB reviewed double materiality results, material climate and environmental topics, and key climate opportunities and risks; no update was needed. The only key physical climate risk remained material: external-events/business-continuity risk from possible sudden extreme weather at locations of some XTB Group companies and branches. These sites have no significant assets or IT infrastructure, but the risk is important due to employees there; it currently does not and should not affect strategy or the business model. Environmental and climate actions include climate-awareness building through Naturalna inwestycja; responsible equipment and waste management, including segregation, repair, reuse of electronic equipment, and resource-consumption control; renewable-energy use and gradual share increase where available; and energy efficiency through newer, less energy-intensive servers and more energy-efficient offices, including XTB headquarters in Warsaw. Actions will be continuous or within four years, including emission-reduction targets. As of publication, XTB is taking or plans annual greenhouse-gas emission calculation, reduction targets based on several periods of emissions data, Scope 1, 2 and 3 footprint-reduction initiatives, analysis of whether a transition plan is needed for the administrative-office profile, annual climate-risk review, and deeper upstream and downstream value-chain dialogue, especially with stakeholders responsible for key Scope 3 emissions, to implement CO2 reductions. Direct fuel use comes from few company cars and power generators; material electricity and heat use comes from administrative-office activity and IT infrastructure. The energy-consumption and energy-mix metric was not externally verified except by the calculation adviser. Missing data were estimated from companies with similar geography and parameters, including whether heat was included in electricity. XTB Group does not operate in or earn revenue from high-climate-impact sectors and does not disclose such sectors. Scope 1 biogenic emissions, outside scopes and from biocomponents in common fuels used by company-owned or controlled cars, fell 59.0% year on year due to lower XTB Group car fuel consumption in 2025. Scope 1 biogenic emissions: XTB S.A. headquarters 0.13 tCO2e/year in 2025 versus 0.28 in 2024, down 53.6%; XTB S.A. branches 0.28 versus 0.72, down 61.1%; other XTB S.A. companies 0.00 versus 0.00; total 0.41 versus 1.00, down 59.0%. Scope 2 biogenic emissions may arise from biomass combustion in energy production, but cannot be calculated because national institutions do not provide fuel-structure data, especially biomass share in total energy production. Scope 3 biogenic emissions may arise in transport categories 4, 6, 7 and 9, but cannot be separated for data in kilometres, passenger-kilometres or tonne-kilometres because exact fuel litres and biocomponent content are unavailable. They may also arise in categories 1 and 2 for purchased products, services and capital goods; included only when supplier data were available, otherwise omitted due to lack of information.
