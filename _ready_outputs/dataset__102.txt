Dom Development S.A. sustainability reporting excerpt describes Management Board and Supervisory Board composition, competence, training and responsibility allocation. The Management Board is diversified by age, education and professional experience, supporting a multidimensional view of sustainability matters. Management Board and Supervisory Board members attend sustainability training, cooperate with external advisers to maintain compliance with rules, and receive knowledge during meetings from business-area owners, including the Non-Financial Reporting Team responsible for the double materiality process and material impacts, risks and opportunities. In 2025 the Management Board had 5 members: 3 men, 60%, and 2 women, 40%; all 5 were executive members, 100%, with no non-executive members. In 01.01.29.10.2024 it had 5 members: 4 men, 80%, and 1 woman, 20%; in 29.1031.12.2024 it comprised five men, 100%. All members had been associated with the Group for many years, were appointed based on knowledge and skills, had economic or real-estate-management education, and had long-term developer-sector experience in Poland and abroad. The representative of persons performing work is the President of the Management Board, responsible for HR. The President directs Management Board work, coordinates other members, supervises operational and financial activity, sets commercial and product policy, supervises marketing and promotion, approves employee-management assumptions including staffing of important managerial positions, exercises ownership supervision over subsidiaries, approves organisational structure and internal regulations, and oversees HR, Administration, Legal, Infrastructure, Interior Arrangement, ESG Coordinator, Risk Management and Compliance Director, Internal Auditor, Personal Data Protection Inspector, and IT & Data Governance. Mikołaj Konopka graduated from the Faculty of Economics at the University of Gdańsk, has lectured since 2018 at the UG Faculty of Management in postgraduate Real Estate and Development Project Management studies, has 19 years of developer-sector experience, and has been associated with the Company and served as Management Board member for 8 years. The Vice-President of the Management Board and Chief Financial Officer sets financial policy for the Company and subsidiaries, manages budgets and financial plans, manages assets, cash, receivables and liabilities, directs the risk-hedging programme by organising the internal control and supervision system, manages corporate matters and investor relations including contacts with GPW, KDPW and KNF, performs financial-condition and economic-situation analyses and reports on Company condition, negotiates and finalises major financial operations such as loans and bond issues, prepares medium-term financial plans and annual budgets, and supervises Finance Department units: Liquidity Management, Financial Planning and Analysis, Accounting, Customer Settlements and Debt Collection. Justyna Wilk has over 20 years of real-estate experience, has been associated with the Company for 15 years, graduated from the Faculty of Geodesy and Cartography at Warsaw University of Technology, and completed postgraduate studies at the SGH Warsaw School of Economics College of Management and Finance. Monika Dobosz has higher economic education, graduated from the Poznań University of Economics and SGH Warsaw School of Economics, and has over 20 years of accounting experience. One Management Board member participates in operational decisions, with particular focus on land-purchase and investment processes. The investment-focused Management Board member sets investment policy, secures the land bank, supervises the Company efficiency-management system, and manages Warsaw operational and investment activity through land acquisition, effective investment execution, and warranty and guarantee assurance; supervised units are Real Estate, Investments, Project Preparation, Investment Support, Investor Supervision and Warranty Service. The customer, sales and marketing Management Board member sets commercial and product policy, ensures customer care and handover-process supervision, supervises marketing and promotion, customer-payment processes across all Dom Development Group companies and Dom Development Kredyty, and oversees Sales, Marketing, Customer Service and Payment Monitoring. Grzegorz Smoliński graduated from the Higher School of Insurance and Banking in Warsaw, now Vistula University of Finance and Business, in Finance and Banking, and completed an MBA at SGH Warsaw School of Economics. Management Board appointment rules and other corporate-body rules are referenced to chapter 4, and roles and duties of administrative, management and supervisory bodies to GOV-2. The Supervisory Board permanently supervises Dom Development S.A. From 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025 it had 8 members, including 3 independent members, 37.5%, and 5 dependent members, 62.5%; all were non-executive. In 2025 the Supervisory Board had 5 men, 62.5%, and 3 women, 37.5%; in 2024 it had 4 men, 57%, and 3 women, 43%. Six Supervisory Board members hold Polish citizenship and two hold British citizenship. The Supervisory Board combines knowledge and experience in management, accounting and finance, law, and the Company sector.
