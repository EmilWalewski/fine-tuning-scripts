Dom Development Group flagship residential estates section describes amenities and 2025 project progress across Wroclaw and Krakow. A preceding estate offered residents a children's playground, two gyms, volleyball and badminton court, picnic tables, benches and fire pit supporting integration; the functional program attracted buyers for 154 units in 2025. Dom Development Wroclaw Sp. z o.o. also held multi-stage investments, led by Wille Biskupin on Wielka Wyspa, a quiet, green, centrally connected part of Wroclaw near the city Zoo, Szczytnicki Park with the Japanese Garden and the UNESCO-listed Centennial Hall; the scheme comprises urban villas immersed in common greenery, with low two-storey architecture referencing modernism and surrounding buildings, and had 162 units under construction in 2025 for handover in 2026. Apartamenty nad Olawka, a premium-standard project directly by the Olawa River, near boulevards, Park na Niskich Lakach, Las Rakowiecki, cycling paths and tram and bus lines, had 174 units under construction at end-2025. Osiedle Miedzyleska, another Group investment in Wroclaw's Tarnogaj district, combined strong transport links to the city centre with proximity to green areas; 313 units were handed over in 2025, the highest among Wroclaw projects, with a further 116 units under construction.
