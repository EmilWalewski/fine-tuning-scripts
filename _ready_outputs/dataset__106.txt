Capital management follows the parent-company Capital Management Policy in XTB S.A., approved by the Supervisory Board and covering capital strategy, long-term objectives, capital structure, contingency plans and planning rules. The policy targets sustainable long-term shareholder value growth and capital sufficient for prudent, effective operations, considering risk profile, prudential rules and risk-based business planning. Capital objectives support a stable capital base, strategy execution and Group risk appetite, reflect strategic plans, expected business development, macroeconomic conditions and business-environment factors, share the business-strategy horizon and require Management Board approval. Capital planning compares current and forecast regulatory and internal requirements with available capital; contingency plans for capital-adequacy crisis are in the Risk Management System in XTB S.A. procedure. In ICARAP, the parent estimates and qualitatively assesses internal capital for all material risks, including stress-test results. The parent must hold own funds above IFR-based requirements under Regulation (EU) 2019/2033 and internal capital under the Polish regulation of 8 December 2021. The IFR requirement is the highest of fixed overhead, permanent minimum initial capital and K-factor requirements; at the financial-statement date K-factor was highest. Own funds are calculated under IFR, CRR and XTB S.A.'s capital-adequacy ratio procedure, not IFRS. The Group has only highest-quality Tier I own funds.
