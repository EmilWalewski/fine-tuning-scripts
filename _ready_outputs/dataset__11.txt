Dom Development S.A. Group consolidated sustainability reporting section S1-10 Adequate Wages states that remuneration in Group companies corresponds to work type and employee qualifications. All employees, regardless of position, receive base salary and bonuses aligned with duties and achieved results, with remuneration matters always agreed with the Management Board and reflecting the quantity and quality of work performed. The Group offers a market-competitive benefits package comprising base salary, bonus schemes and additional benefits. All employees employed under employment contracts receive adequate pay, defined as base remuneration above the minimum wage, the commonly used Polish benchmark also selected by the Group for sustainability reporting purposes. In 2025, Group companies conducted job valuation and assigned positions to subgroups to structure development and career paths and align internal remuneration guidelines and rules with national regulations. Management staff from all Group companies participated in changes to remuneration systems and pay-increase determination. The minimum wage definition references the Act of 10 October 2002 on the minimum wage for work, Journal of Laws 2023, item 1667.
