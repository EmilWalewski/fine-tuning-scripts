Dom Development described itself as a pioneer on the Polish developer market in turnkey apartments, offering interior-design packages aligned with current design trends, saving client time and removing the burden of self-supervised finishing. Clients receive ready package variants, broad material and equipment choice across price ranges, optional cooperation with a designer under individual services, no need to find renovation teams or buy materials independently, execution by verified subcontractors under direct Company supervision, and a two-year statutory warranty. The in-house Interior Design Department provides professional arrangement advice; long-term cooperation with producers and suppliers enables more favorable purchase terms than self-finishing. Buyers for rental usually choose the profit package for quick preparation with minimal time and cost, while owner-occupiers more often choose concept packages with greater personalization and higher-quality materials.
