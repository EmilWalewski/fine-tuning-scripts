Capital management rules were unchanged from note 36 to the 2024 consolidated financial statements. The Group monitors ROE as last-12-month net profit divided by equity; financing structure is monitored through net financial gearing, defined as net debt divided by equity plus net debt, and net debt/EBITDA for the last 12 months, with EBITDA defined as operating profit plus depreciation and amortisation. Net debt includes interest-bearing loans and borrowings and right-of-use liabilities less cash and cash equivalents. Optimal thresholds are net financial gearing not exceeding 50% and net debt/EBITDA not above 2. The capital management objective is to maximise return on equity while maintaining a safe, flexible financing structure, with detailed guidelines considering operating segments, current liquidity and financing for development objectives under the business strategy. To maintain or adjust capital structure, the Group may adjust dividend payments, return capital to shareholders or issue new shares; no changes were introduced to objectives, policies or processes in the presented periods.
