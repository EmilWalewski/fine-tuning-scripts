Mass unavailability of business equipment may disrupt AML/Compliance controls, create regulatory risk from late reporting or missing real-time transaction verification, and weaken brand trust. Data warehouse failure, incomplete/incorrect data or missing required snapshots may prevent regulatory reporting, risk capital adequacy breaches, force extra costs from more conservative hedging, cause data not to be saved or recovered, delay booking of closed positions and MAR alert analysis, and impair other reporting duties; controls include server-load monitoring, critical-process prioritisation, database optimisation, capital-requirement estimation on a server separate from the warehouse, and Risk Control Department completeness/correctness checks. Back-office, record-keeping and financial-accounting system inefficiency may cause booking delays/errors/omissions, staff overload, manual workload, lower motivation, overtime, employee-loss risk, delayed period-result calculation, balance sheet, income statement and tax-reporting errors, late PIT delivery to the tax office, data disintegration, loss of analytics, erroneous SQL/manual reports outside the accounting system, inability or major delay in recording new products, supervisory penalties, fiscal penalties for late/incorrect PITs, labour-inspection penalties and new-hire costs; mitigations include import monitoring, external supplier consultations under the service agreement, import optimisation, trimming or creating a new accounting database if needed, middle-office implementation and database replica use for reporting. DDoS attacks may block access to XTB services, damage credibility, cause client outflow to competitors, trigger mass complaints and compensation for inability to withdraw funds, trade or close positions; mitigations are external certified protection, scenario testing and business-continuity plans. Hacking risk, including ransomware, malware and social engineering, may disclose sensitive client data, cause losses through unauthorised transactions or withdrawals, transfer client funds to fraudsters, or enable market manipulation through unauthorised access to XTB accounts; controls include XTB ICT safeguards, external certified IT security, two-factor authentication, withdrawal-account change authorisation and regular social-engineering tests. Client data leakage or other personal-data law breach may produce mass sensitive-data leakage, loss of client/investor trust, high administrative fines including RODO, class actions, intensified KNF, FCA and CySEC supervision, threat to GPW-listed company financial stability, long-term brand damage, competitive client takeover and costly reactive cybersecurity architecture rebuild; mitigations include XTB internal RODO procedures, internal controls, ICT safeguards and external certified IT security. Operational error in transaction-system handling by the Trading Department may cause losses from transaction corrections or entries, including wrong client leverage, wrong instrument configuration or corporate-action entry error; mitigations include regulated records, automated correction entry, JIRA reporting, access restrictions, four-eyes principle, extra authorisation for larger corrections, and Trading/Risk Control Department checks. Operational error or employee fraud in deposits/withdrawals may cause loss from incorrect withdrawal, or lost investment opportunities and stop-out after unbooked or delayed deposit; mitigations include automated client deposit/withdrawal processing, access restrictions and transfer limits on client bank accounts, daily cash reconciliation between bank accounts and transaction systems by client-account currency, and cyclical verification/control of client cash-account balances through deposit and withdrawal checks in trading and banking systems. Employee manipulation by unauthorised positions or correction may undermine risk controls and limits, create uncontrolled XTB exposure to losses above capital buffers, damage platform integrity, cause investor outflow and image degradation, and threaten sanctions for ineffective supervision; responses are closing unauthorised positions, legal pursuit of employee compensation, transferring surplus free funds from broker accounts to bank accounts, and daily verification of open broker positions against xRisk. Employee or third-party abuse through equipment/data theft, corporate espionage or attempted transfer of company/client funds to an employee private account may impose costs equal to lost assets or reconstruction costs, compromise security, damage company image and client safety perception, trigger civil claims if clients miss market opportunities due to missing account funds, and cause PUODO sanctions after client-data disclosure. Withdrawal controls repeat automated processing, access restrictions, transfer limits, daily bank-to-trading-system cash reconciliation by currency and cyclical balance verification; theft controls include building monitoring, access restrictions, employee training and legal claims; data-theft controls limit access to necessary persons, restrict copying to portable devices and control external content sent outside the organisation. Operational IT-system errors, including unavailable, incomplete or erroneous transaction-system data and human error in transaction, back-office or IT-system handling, may cause decisions based on incorrect data, supervisory penalties and lower BION assessment if capital requirements are breached through underestimated risk, inability to pay dividend, and unnecessary costs from excessive hedging due to wrong exposure estimation; mitigations include reporting-data correctness verification, inter-system reconciliations, and correctness/completeness controls.
