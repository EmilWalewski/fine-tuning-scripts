Analysis of the micro-investing application market indicates that in 2025, 78%% of global internet users used at least one fintech application, supporting outreach to new generations of investors for whom convenient mobile solutions, fast access to information, and transparent communication are critical. In technology, XTB S.A. identifies the key short-term objective for the next 1-2 years as maintaining high stability, security, and availability of trading systems. IT infrastructure must ensure uninterrupted operation of investment platforms, rapid order processing, and resilience to failures and overloads, particularly during periods of increased market volatility. Short-term actions will focus on modernizing existing systems, improving their performance, strengthening monitoring and incident-management mechanisms, developing maintenance processes, and monitoring and implementing well-fitted solutions that increase redundancy and infrastructure resilience to potential disruptions. In the medium term, over 3-5 years, technology development will cover further optimization of system architecture, greater use of cloud or hybrid solutions, and deployment of modern tools for infrastructure management and business continuity, with the objective of maintaining high system stability, service scalability, and capacity to support a growing number of users and transaction volumes. In the long term, beyond 5 years, a key element of organizational development will be the ability to systematically monitor and adapt to changing market and technology trends. Particular importance is assigned to development of new technologies, including artificial intelligence, advanced data analytics, and automation of financial processes, increasingly used for service personalization.
