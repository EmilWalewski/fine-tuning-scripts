Asseco Group's interim condensed consolidated financial statements applied material accounting policies consistent with those described in the 2024 annual consolidated financial statements, except for new or amended standards and interpretations effective for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2025. The only new or amended standard effective from 1 January 2025 identified in the excerpt was Amendments to IAS 21 The Effects of Changes in Foreign Exchange Rates: Lack of Exchangeability, published on 15 August 2023 and applicable to annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2025. Amended standards and interpretations applied for the first time in 2025 had no material impact on the Group's interim condensed consolidated financial statements. Standards and interpretations issued by the International Accounting Standards Board or the IFRS Interpretations Committee but not yet effective included IFRS 18 Presentation and Disclosure in Financial Statements, published on 9 April 2024, not endorsed by the EU by the approval date and applicable to annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027; IFRS 19 Subsidiaries without Public Accountability: Disclosures, published on 9 May 2024, not EU-endorsed and applicable from 1 January 2027; amendments to IFRS 9 and IFRS 7 Contracts Referencing Nature-dependent Electricity, published on 18 December 2024, not EU-endorsed and applicable from 1 January 2026; amendments to IFRS 9 and IFRS 7 Classification and Measurement of Financial Instruments, published on 30 May 2024, not EU-endorsed and applicable from 1 January 2026; and Volume 11 of annual improvements to IAS/IFRS, published on 18 July 2024, not EU-endorsed and applicable from 1 January 2026. Effective dates are those in IASB-issued standards; EU application dates may differ and are announced on EU endorsement. The Group did not elect early application of any published standard, interpretation or amendment not yet effective and is analysing the potential impact of those standards and interpretations on its consolidated financial statements and accounting policies. Under Global Minimum Tax Pillar 2, Council Directive (EU) 2022/2523 adopted in December 2022 introduced EU implementation of OECD BEPS 2.0 measures accepted by over 140 countries, targeting base erosion and profit shifting through a 15% global minimum top-up tax rate. Pillar 2 creates tax and reporting obligations for Polish and international capital groups with revenue of at least EUR 750M, therefore covering Asseco Group, and imposes a minimum tax of 15% of qualified income based on the effective rather than nominal tax rate, calculated by jurisdiction, generally jointly for all group companies in a given country. In Poland, Pillar 2 rules implementing Council Directive (EU) 2022/2523 were published in the Journal of Laws on 19 November 2024, Dz.U. 2024 item 1685, effective from 1 January 2025, with voluntary application from 1 January 2024; the Group did not elect earlier retrospective application from 1 January 2024. Asseco Group continuously monitors legislative implementation of Pillar 2 reform in all jurisdictions where its subsidiaries operate and analyses the potential impact on the Group. Due to Poland's implementation of the IIR income inclusion rule from 2025, Pillar 2 rules for the 2025 reporting year cover Group companies from 57 countries. In the first 3 months of 2025, the Group recognized no current income tax amounts arising from Pillar 2 taxation. The Group applied the exception from recognizing and disclosing deferred tax assets and liabilities related to Pillar 2 income tax under amendments to IAS 12 issued in May 2023. During the reporting period, no changes were made to accounting policies or presentation policies, and no events occurred requiring correction of an error.
