Creotech Instruments Spółka Akcyjna report for the 9 months ended 30 September 2025, condensed interim financial statements, all amounts in PLN thousand unless otherwise stated; additional information for Q3 2025 identifies the company as Creotech Instruments S.A., registered office Piaseczno, Poland, address ul. Jana Pawła II 66, 05-500 Piaseczno, e-mail kontakt@creotech.pl, website www.creotech.pl, core PKD activity 30.30.Z production of aircraft, spacecraft and similar machinery, registry court Sąd Rejonowy dla m.st. Warszawy w Warszawie, XIV Commercial Division of the National Court Register, REGON 141246690, fully paid share capital PLN 285,434.70. On 16 April 2025 the registry court registered statute amendments increasing share capital in connection with issuance of series K shares and authorising the Management Board to increase share capital within authorised capital for the company incentive programme. After registration of the series K-related statute changes, share capital increased from PLN 237,934.70 to PLN 285,434.70 and was divided into 2,854,347 bearer shares with nominal value PLN 0.10 each, comprising series A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, J and K shares. Total voting rights from all issued shares increased by 475,000 to 2,854,347 votes. Series K shares were admitted and introduced to trading on the GPW main market on 30 April 2025, preceded by admission and introduction to trading of rights to those shares on 12 March 2025. As at 30 September 2025 and the report date, the Management Board comprised Grzegorz Brona, President of the Management Board; Paweł Górnicki, Vice-President for Finance; Maciej Misiura, Vice-President; and Jacek Mandas, Vice-President. During the reporting period, due to expiry of the previous Management Board term, the Supervisory Board adopted a resolution on 29 May 2025 dismissing Grzegorz Brona, Paweł Górnicki and Jacek Kosiec from the Management Board effective at the end of 31 May 2025, and by the same resolution appointed a new-term Management Board effective 1 June 2025: Grzegorz Brona as President, Paweł Górnicki as Vice-President for Finance, Maciej Misiura as Vice-President and Jacek Mandas as Vice-President. As at 30 September 2025 and the quarterly report date, the Supervisory Board comprised Jacek Kubrak as Chair and Radosław Koszewski, Waldemar Maj, Adam Piotrowski and Radosław L. Kwaśnicki as members; no Supervisory Board changes occurred during the reporting period. As at 30 September 2025 and the quarterly report date, the Audit Committee comprised Waldemar Maj as Chair and Jacek Kubrak, Radosław Koszewski and Radosław L. Kwaśnicki as members; on 15 January 2025 the Supervisory Board resolved to appoint Waldemar Maj, Jacek Kubrak, Radosław Kwaśnicki and Radosław Koszewski to the Audit Committee. In group-structure changes, following the Management Board information of 16 April 2025 on starting a company division process through separation of the quantum systems segment into a new company, on 6 May 2025 the incorporation deed and statute of Creotech Quantum spółka akcyjna were signed, and on 12 June 2025 the Sąd Rejonowy dla m.st. Warszawy w Warszawie, XIII Commercial Division of the National Court Register entered Creotech Quantum spółka akcyjna in the Register of Entrepreneurs. Following the Management Board information of 29 April 2025 on starting formation of a new company through separation of the drone-related application systems and satellite-data processing segment, on 13 May 2025 the same registry court entered Creotech Geo prosta spółka akcyjna in the Register of Entrepreneurs. Creotech Instruments S.A. is the sole shareholder of both Creotech Quantum spółka akcyjna and Creotech Geo prosta spółka akcyjna and therefore the parent of both subsidiaries.
