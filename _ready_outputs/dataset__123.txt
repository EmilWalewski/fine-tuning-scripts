XTB S.A. Group 2025 consolidated annual report excerpt presents operating KPIs for the retail and institutional segments for the 12 months ended 31 December 2025 versus 31 December 2024. Retail segment new clients increased to 864,278 from 498,435, up 73.4%; total clients rose to 2,164,835 from 1,361,533, up 59.0%; active clients reached 1,189,397 versus 701,065, up 69.7%. Retail transaction count grew to 281,247,909 from 167,669,072, up 67.7%; CFD derivative turnover in lots increased to 8,245,297 from 5,568,398, up 48.1%; net deposits were 14,611.8M PLN versus 8,563.3M PLN, up 70.6%. Average operating revenue per active client declined to 1.8k PLN from 2.6k PLN, down 30.8%; average customer acquisition cost remained 0.7k PLN; profitability per lot decreased to 227 PLN from 296 PLN, down 23.3%. Retail CFD derivative turnover by nominal value rose to USD 4,695,655M from USD 2,542,079M, up 84.7%; profitability per USD 1M of CFD derivative nominal turnover fell to USD 110 from USD 167, down 34.1%; stock and ETF nominal turnover increased to USD 21,813M from USD 9,574M, up 127.8%. Retail definitions specify new clients as clients acquired in each period; active clients as those with at least one transaction, an open position, or free cash on an interest-bearing account; transactions as total opened and closed transactions; lot as a financial-instrument trading unit, with one lot for currency-based CFDs, including cryptocurrencies, equal to 100,000 units of base currency, and other lot sizes defined in instrument specifications; presented CFD lots exclude CFD turnover on shares and ETFs, where one lot equals one share; cryptocurrency CFD comparative data were adjusted after aligning the cryptocurrency CFD lot definition with the currency CFD definition. Net deposits equal client deposits less client withdrawals in the period. Average revenue per client equals segment operating revenue divided by active clients; average acquisition cost equals segment marketing expenditure divided by new clients; profitability per lot equals net result on financial instruments, adjusted for shares and ETFs and CFDs on shares and ETFs in the retail segment, divided by CFD derivative turnover in lots. Profitability per USD 1M of CFD nominal turnover equals net result on financial instruments, adjusted for shares and ETFs in the retail segment, translated into USD at the arithmetic average of NBP average exchange rates from the last day of each reporting-period month, divided by CFD derivative nominal turnover in USD millions. Institutional segment operates under X Open Hub (XOH), providing liquidity and technology to other financial institutions. Institutional new clients increased to 8 from 3, up 166.7%; active clients rose to 25 from 24, up 4.2%; total clients increased to 32 from 31, up 3.2%. Institutional CFD derivative turnover in lots declined to 621,084 from 705,779, down 12.0%; net deposits were 60.5M PLN versus 44.0M PLN, up 37.5%; CFD derivative nominal turnover increased to USD 110,049M from USD 84,498M, up 30.2%; profitability per USD 1M of CFD derivative nominal turnover fell to USD 90 from USD 233, down 61.4%. Institutional definitions mirror retail definitions for new and active clients, lot mechanics, exclusion of share and ETF CFD turnover from presented CFD lots, cryptocurrency CFD comparative adjustment, and net deposits. Institutional profitability per USD 1M of CFD nominal turnover equals net result on financial instruments, adjusted for shares and ETFs in the institutional segment, translated into USD using the arithmetic average of NBP average exchange rates from the last day of each reporting-period month, divided by CFD derivative nominal turnover in USD millions.
