XTB Group maintains an internal Anonymous Irregularity Reporting Procedure covering actual or potential legal breaches, including anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorist-financing rules, internal procedures and ethical standards. The procedure applies to employees, job candidates and other persons performing activities for XTB, including suppliers, implements obligations under the Act of 14 June 2024 on whistleblower protection, and provides protection against retaliation. Reports are submitted through an electronic form at https://www.xtb.com/pl/zawiadomienie and, depending on subject matter, are handled by the Inspector of Supervision, MLRO, Management Board, Supervisory Board or members of those bodies. Employees may also report irregularities to supervisors, employee representatives or the Talents department; the Legal and Compliance department reminds staff of the procedure through internal mailings, and XTB assesses whether explanatory and remedial processes are effective while cyclically communicating available reporting methods. In 2025, following National Labour Inspectorate post-audit recommendations, XTB added an information clause for job candidates on abuse reporting in Polish and English to application forms in all recruitment projects. XTB states that its business influences clients through strategy, business model, services and products, while clients and service recipients inspire product plans and motivate business-development challenges. The Group operates in two segments: retail, offering clients a universal, comprehensive investment application for managing financial resources and using diverse investment products; and institutional, including liquidity-provider services for institutional clients and technologies enabling them to offer financial-instrument trading to their own customers under their own brands. XTB aims to improve comfort and working conditions to reduce employee turnover and departures. In social bargaining and dialogue, XTB Group ensures full freedom of association and openness to employee dialogue. In 2025 Poland was the only country where employment exceeded 50 employees and at least 10% of total employment. No collective bargaining agreements operated at headquarters, giving 0% employee coverage. For EEA social dialogue, 100% of headquarters employees in Poland were represented by employee representatives, equal to 72.6% of total XTB Group employment. In 2025 no agreement was concluded with employees on representation by a European Works Council, Societas Europaea Works Council or Societas Cooperativa Europaea Works Council. As at 31 December 2025, the only foreign unit whose employees were covered by a collective labour agreement was the XTB S.A. branch in France; covered employees represented 1.9% of total employment-contract staff in XTB Group at the last day of the financial year. XTB identifies consumers and end-users as individual clients and institutional clients, i.e. companies and organisations using XTB solutions, and treats them as key stakeholders with mutual impacts. XTB’s impact on consumers and end-users is linked to the use of its investment application and investment platform, allowing investors to invest collected financial resources; advertising and marketing based on responsible marketing practices; and educational activities intended to help investors achieve investment goals. The Group adapts its offer to client needs, continuously develops its financial and investment product portfolio, conducts active marketing including well-known athletes, social-media communication and educational campaigns to reach broad audiences and build investment awareness. As a broker, XTB provides access to many instruments, including high-risk products, while carefully informing clients about risk levels, and runs investor education including the recurring Masterclass conference, which annually attracts thousands of people interested in investing. In 2025 XTB reviewed material impacts, opportunities and risks in consumer cooperation identified in the 2024 double-materiality assessment, including cybersecurity risk described in chapter 1.6, Statements. For 2025 reporting, XTB continued to rely on the 2024 double-materiality assessment, which included a selected group of highly active individual and institutional clients to better understand needs and supplement results with real data; based on this information and the assessment results, XTB updated them in July 2024. All consumers and institutional clients are covered by sustainability-reporting disclosures. XTB Group states it does not negatively affect consumers’ rights to privacy, personal-data protection or freedom of expression, but because it manages and processes data provided by them, a potential negative impact could arise if a data leak occurred. Consumers subject to material impacts depend on accurate and accessible information about XTB products or services and may be vulnerable to marketing and sales strategies, but are not particularly exposed to negative health or privacy effects.
