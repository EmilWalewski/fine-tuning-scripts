XTB states that a responsible modern business must incorporate ESG criteria; as a fast-growing FinTech understanding innovation, it integrates sustainable activities with adopted business objectives. In 2024 the Group adopted an updated ESG Sustainable Development Strategy. A double materiality assessment identified material topics related to climate change and the circular economy, and the Group launched a project to identify key climate opportunities and risks to enable comprehensive climate-change management. In Q3 2025 sustainable-development actions focused mainly on employee initiatives and projects dedicated to education and promotion of financial knowledge. XTB also joined a support program for entrepreneurs implementing innovations and sustainable-development ventures. These activities were handled by the XTB Foundation, which in the reporting period also planned new projects for the following months of 2025 and the beginning of 2026. The XTB Foundation was established in 2020; its mission is to promote financial education, build social awareness, and support sustainable-development actions by equalizing educational opportunities, inspiring and motivating development for a better future, and sharing knowledge necessary for investing and financial management. The Foundation’s registered office is ul. Prosta 67, 00-838 Warsaw; KRS registration date 23.12.2020; Management Board President Sylwia Kozoń; Foundation Council members Omar Arnaout and Paweł Szejko. For the Foundation, Q3 2025 was a period of summarizing and closing ongoing projects and planning further initiatives. Under “Akademia jutra. Finanse z klasą,” preparations were made for the second edition; school classes began at the beginning of October, the current edition involves nearly 5 thousand students, and 20 Q&A sessions with XTB experts were planned. In “Biznes Restart – Pocztówki z powodzi,” a July project for tourism entrepreneurs from Lower Silesia ended; participants submitted applications for financial grants provided, among others, by the XTB Foundation, and a committee composed of campaign partners selected the most interesting initiatives related to innovation and sustainable development. In cooperation with the Polish Economic Society, an international economic knowledge olympiad took place in Greece at the end of August; the Polish team won 4 medals, including 3 silver and 1 bronze, and the XTB Foundation served for the second year as strategic partner of the team. In Q3 2025 XTB continued its strategy focused on building the client base, acquiring a record nearly 222 thousand new clients, up 105.1% year on year; total clients exceeded 1.9 million, up 56.9% year on year, and active clients reached a record 920.0 thousand, up 75.9% year on year from 522.9 thousand. Q3 2025 consolidated net profit was 53.2M PLN versus 203.8M PLN a year earlier; consolidated revenue was 375.8M PLN versus 470.2M PLN in Q3 2024; operating expenses were 322.7M PLN versus 208.5M PLN in Q3 2024. For the nine months of 2025, consolidated revenue was 1.54bn PLN versus 1.41bn PLN a year earlier, up 9.1% year on year; consolidated net profit was 463.3M PLN versus 666.9M PLN in Q1-Q3 2024; consolidated operating profit was 605.3M PLN versus 789.1M PLN; consolidated operating expenses were 931.4M PLN versus 618.9M PLN.
