On 18 July 2025 the Company received confirmation from the European Space Agency that the first part of documentation had been accepted under the Requirements Key Point review of the COUNTRY AWARENESS MISSION IN LAND ANALYSIS CAMILA project. This acceptance entitles the Company to EUR 3,178,284 from ESA; approx. 30% of this remuneration is due directly to the Company, with the balance covering subcontractor remuneration in CAMILA. On 8 August 2025 the Management Board signed an agreement with Italy's Leonardo S.p.A. for assembly of electronic units within LISA COLD GAS MPA MPE MODULES MANUFACTURING / LISA MPE (Laser Interferometer Space Antenna Micro Propulsion Electronics), implemented by Leonardo S.p.A. for ESA. Task value is approx. EUR 3,656,611, subject to slight recalculation during the project, with total project duration of 67 months. The contract was considered material due to value and Creotech's significant role in assembling and integrating electronics for modules of the micro-propulsion control subsystem responsible for precise positioning of satellites in the LISA constellation, and because it supports the Company's contract-manufacturing strategy focused on space-sector orders. On 13 August 2025 the Management Board signed an agreement with Teplice Observatory and Planetarium, Czech Republic, for sale of a specialist sCMOS camera developed by the issuer for searching near-Earth space objects, including space debris. This is the Company's first commercial sale of the astronomical sCMOS camera with additional accessories, opening a new business line intended, after the ongoing division of Creotech Instruments S.A., to be handled by the Quantum Company. Contract value is EUR 196,000 net, with delivery planned within 6 months from signing; terms do not deviate from market practice. On 29 August 2025 the Management Board of the Company and the Management Board of Creotech Quantum S.A. agreed and adopted a division plan under Art. 533 sec. 1 in conjunction with Art. 529 sec. 1 point 4 of the Polish Commercial Companies Code. The division will transfer to the acquiring company an organised part of the divided company's enterprise covering the quantum systems segment: development and sale of control components and control software for quantum computers; development of components, systems and software for quantum key distribution for future sale; development and sale of time distribution and synchronisation systems including White Rabbit-based technology; development of advanced digital cameras for terrestrial applications for future sale; development of ultra-sensitive single-photon detectors based on superconducting technology for future sale; and cooperation with large scientific organisations including CERN. The division will be carried out without reducing the share capital of the divided company. In connection with the division, the acquiring company's share capital will increase from PLN 150,000.00 to PLN 435,434.70, i.e. by PLN 285,434.70, through issuance of 2,854,347 new ordinary bearer series B shares with nominal value PLN 0.10 each. Division shares will be allocated to shareholders of the divided company on the reference date set under KDPW regulations and indicated by the acquiring company's Management Board, at a 1:1 exchange ratio: one acquiring-company share for each one divided-company share. For the division plan, the divided company, including the separated business, was valued at PLN 1,255,090,232 as at 1 July 2025; the separated business at PLN 208,338,788; and the acquiring company at PLN 149,820. Division shares will be covered by the separated business assets worth PLN 208,338,788. Under Art. 530 sec. 2 of the Code, the separated business transfers on registration of the acquiring company's share-capital increase related to the division shares. Shareholders of the divided company will acquire division shares by operation of law, without subscription or payment. The division shares are intended for admission to trading on the regulated market operated by the Warsaw Stock Exchange; the acquiring company will file with the Polish Financial Supervision Authority for approval of a prospectus connected with the public offering and admission to trading. The division plan was published on 29 August 2025 on the websites of the Company and the acquiring company; the expert opinion on correctness and reliability under Art. 537 sec. 1 will be made available after preparation by the expert appointed by the competent registry court.
