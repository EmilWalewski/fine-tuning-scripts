In 2025 and 2024, CFDs remained the product class most frequently selected by XTB investors by number of opened, closed and held positions, but their share decreased year on year by 7.5 p.p.; other categories gained share, with Investment Plans up 3 p.p., shares up 2.6 p.p. and ETFs up 1.9 p.p. In 2025, investors held shares in portfolios for about 205 days versus 180 days in 2024, while more than 80% of CFD positions were held for less than 1 day, similarly to 2024. By age group in 2025, shares represented about 15-18% of opened positions in every group; ETFs and Investment Plans were more popular among clients aged 18-34; CFDs formed a large part of the instrument basket across all age groups and were chosen more often by clients aged 45+. Among new clients, 8.17% selected cryptoasset-based CFDs as their first transaction in 2025 versus 8.85% in 2024. In cryptoasset CFD transactions, clients aged 35-44 predominated in both 2025 and 2024. Among all active clients in 2025, first cryptoasset CFD transactions were most often made by clients aged 35-44 at 31.5% and 45-54 at 25.5%; in 2024 the corresponding shares were 32.4% and 25.1%. The chart data also show 2025 versus 2024 first cryptoasset CFD transaction shares of 13.7% versus 11.4% for ages 18-26, 17.7% versus 18.2% for ages 27-34, 8.8% versus 9.7% for ages 55-64, and 2.8% versus 3.1% for ages >= 65. IKE and IKZE owners are predominantly men: IKZE accounts at 31.12.2025 were 23% women and 77% men; IKE accounts at 31.12.2024 were 14% women and 86% men; IKE accounts at 31.12.2025 were 17% women and 83% men. XTB clients can buy traditional shares of companies listed on the largest global exchanges; a share purchase gives the investor co-ownership rights and the right to seek participation in assets and profits, including dividends. XTB currently offers more than 7 200 shares of companies listed on the largest global stock exchanges. ETFs are presented as part of the broader ETP category, alongside ETC funds, commodity-based products such as gold, and ETNs, debt securities issued by financial institutions. ETFs are baskets covering a specified asset or financial instrument type such as shares or stock indices, are exchange-traded and freely bought and sold during stock-exchange sessions, which increases liquidity; some ETFs periodically distribute dividends from held shares. Some ETFs are passively managed to replicate the return of an underlying asset such as a stock index or basket of shares, while actively managed ETFs seek to outperform the market. ETFs may be the whole or a supplement of an investment portfolio for beginner and experienced investors. XTB currently offers more than 1 900 ETFs. Within the trading platform, the Company provides investment instruments divided into three categories: products for active investing, products enabling passive investing including savings-building, and financial resources management products. XTB pays clients interest on uninvested cash held in their accounts; the interest rate used to determine the interest amount is calculated daily, and interest is paid periodically during the first five business days of the following month. Interest on free investor funds is activated automatically for all clients holding uninvested funds in an account, with no minimum or maximum free-funds amount required. Free-funds interest rates are variable and may be updated once a week; if rates decrease within 90 days from concluding the agreement, lower rates apply, and if rates increase, rates are increased accordingly. In long-term investing and savings products, including retirement-oriented solutions, XTB currently offers Poland-based Individual Retirement Account IKE and Individual Retirement Security Account IKZE, UK Individual Savings Account ISA and Cash Individual Savings Account Cash ISA, and France-based Plan d'Epargne en Actions PEA. IKE was created to encourage and build a habit of saving for future retirement with tax exemption. Because it retains the nature of a bank account, the investor has constant access to accumulated funds and may withdraw them at any time, but withdrawal before acquiring retirement rights, meaning before age 55 or 60, entails payment of 19% tax on gains. An IKE account may be opened by any natural person aged at least 18 who has an investment account with the Company and tax residence in Poland; one person may hold only one IKE account and may not share it with another person.
