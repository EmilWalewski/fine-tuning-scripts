As of 30 September 2025, client financial instruments held in the brokerage accounts totaled PLN 23,802.311 million, up from PLN 13,681.597 million as of 31 December 2024. The total was almost entirely composed of listed shares, ETPs and rights to shares recorded on securities accounts, which amounted to PLN 23,802.104 million at 30 September 2025 compared with PLN 13,681.390 million at year-end 2024, while other securities recorded on clients’ securities accounts remained unchanged at PLN 0.207 million. Unused transaction limits granted to institutional clients amounted to PLN 13.110 million at 30 September 2025, compared with PLN 14.763 million at 31 December 2024. Contributions paid into the compensation system increased from an opening balance of PLN 17.923 million to PLN 22.269 million at 30 September 2025, reflecting increases of PLN 4.346 million during the period; at 31 December 2024 the closing balance had been PLN 17.923 million after increases of PLN 3.937 million from an opening balance of PLN 13.986 million. XTB’s share in benefits from the compensation system amounted to PLN 2.157 million at 30 September 2025, compared with PLN 1.848 million at 31 December 2024.
