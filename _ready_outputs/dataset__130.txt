Group consolidated financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2025 describe IFRS accounting policies for property, inventory, financing, financial instruments, liabilities, provisions and revenue. Buildings and structures are depreciated at 2.5%-4.5%, vehicles at 20%, and equipment and other property, plant and equipment at 10%-30%. Right-of-use office space is recognized under IFRS 16 Leases and depreciated over the lease term. Inventories comprise finished goods, mainly residential units and parking spaces, and work in progress; finished goods are measured at the lower of production cost and net realizable selling price, while work in progress is measured at the lower of purchase price/production cost and net realizable selling price. Net selling price is estimated by the Management Board using market prices, and discrepancies trigger write-downs. Inventory impairment testing for Group development projects is based on analysis of production cost versus net realizable selling price; projects expected to generate a loss cause immediate write-down of work in progress in profit or loss, and write-downs may also cover properties where the development process carries risk of material delay. Each development project has budgets covering historical and future cash flows, updated at least quarterly. For impairment testing, project budgets include all past and forecast net revenues less direct land acquisition, design, construction and other project preparation costs, show-unit costs and on-site sales office costs, plus related historical and forecast external financing costs and expected customer claims where applicable. Budgets apply prudent valuation. A positive project margin means no inventory impairment is required; a negative margin indicates potential impairment and, after detailed cash-flow verification, leads to recognition of an inventory impairment write-down equal to the estimated negative margin. The write-down is recognized in cost of sales as adjustment of inventories to net realizable value; reversal is possible if the expected project margin becomes positive. For multi-stage projects, all future stages are treated as one project for impairment testing, while each stage where sales and construction have started is separated and tested individually. Borrowing costs are expensed in profit or loss when incurred, except capitalized costs directly attributable to qualifying assets, in the Group's case work in progress, as part of production cost. Financial costs are capitalized into work in progress only while a development project is active, meaning design or construction works are performed on acquired land or key administrative decisions required for the project are being obtained. Capitalization ceases when substantially all activities necessary to prepare units for handover to customers are complete, and is suspended when investment activity on a project is halted, including design, construction or permit and administrative-decision processes. Trade and other receivables are measured under IFRS 9 using impairment allowances based on the expected credit loss model. Bank deposits with original maturity above three months are presented as short-term financial assets. Cash and short-term deposits with original maturity up to three months are shown in the statement of financial position at nominal value and include bank cash, cash on hand and short-term deposits; cash and cash equivalents in the cash flow statement equal those balances less outstanding overdrafts. Interest-bearing bank loans, borrowings and debt securities are initially recognized at fair value less transaction costs, then measured at amortized cost using the effective interest method, including loan/borrowing costs and discounts or premiums.
