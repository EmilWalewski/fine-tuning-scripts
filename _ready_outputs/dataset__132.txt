In 2025, one foreign subsidiary of XTB S.A. Group reported 47 tonnes of municipal waste managed by landfilling. Although this represented 13% of the Group's municipal waste mass, it generated 45% of municipal-waste emissions and 44% of total Scope 3 Category 5 emissions. Scope 3 Category 6 business travel emissions were driven mainly by travel using various transport modes, about 87% of category emissions, while about 96% was estimated using the spend-based method. Year-on-year comparability is limited by differences in input-data availability and methodology, as in 2024 a significant part of XTB S.A. emissions was estimated from distance data. Category 6 totals are also sensitive to FX differences in spending by XTB S.A. branches and subsidiaries.

Under E1-7 and E1-8, XTB Group does not engage in greenhouse-gas removal projects or GHG emission-reduction projects financed with carbon credits and does not internally set a greenhouse-gas emission fee. In 2025 the full carbon footprint was calculated for the second time; 2024 is the base period for comprehensive GHG calculation, so emission-reduction targets have not yet been set. Under E1-9, the Group uses the initial-reporting-period exemption for expected financial effects from material physical risks, transition risks and potential climate-related opportunities.

Under Article 8 of Regulation (EU) 2020/852, XTB explains that its OTC-market business model makes asset-portfolio KPIs from own-account transactions materially different from those of other financial institutions and investment firms. Although XTB holds financial assets, the portfolio is not a classic investment portfolio, the Group does not finance business activity or investment projects, and the portfolio generates operating revenue. The Group intends to improve data quality in future reporting periods, especially in material spend categories, and cooperate with suppliers. Taxonomy disclosures cite Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/2178: exposures to central governments, central banks and supranational issuers are excluded from KPI numerator and denominator, while derivatives and exposures to undertakings not subject to non-financial reporting under Articles 19a or 29a of Directive 2013/34/EU are excluded from the numerator.

XTB Group companies did not perform own-account transactions in 2025 other than bond purchases and sales, client-order-related derivative transactions, and auxiliary share and ETF purchases or sales to execute retail client orders. Bonds are excluded under Article 7(1), and own-account transactions were mainly client-order-related derivatives. The disclosed financial-asset portfolio included derivatives at 75.3% of total assets invested in own-account investment-firm activity, government and corporate bonds at 0.6%, and shares and ETF units at about 24.1% of that portfolio and 2.7% of total assets. Shares secure client acquisitions of synthetic shares; the equity portfolio is mainly driven by orders from clients and brokers and adjusted continuously to new client orders, so XTB does not assess or include taxonomy eligibility or alignment when creating it.

For taxonomy reporting, XTB Group classified itself as a financial undertaking. Activities not occurring included reception and transmission of orders for financial instruments, portfolio management, investment advice, underwriting or placing with firm commitment, placing without firm commitment, operating an MTF and operating an OTF. Execution of client orders amounted to about PLN 42.0M at end-2025, including PLN 0 from institutional clients. Own-account dealing consisted of bond purchases and sales of about PLN 12.7M, derivatives of about PLN 1,966M including about PLN 37M from institutional clients, and auxiliary share and ETF purchases to execute client orders of about PLN 36M, including PLN 0 from institutional clients.
