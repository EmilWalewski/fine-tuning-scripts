XTB S.A.'s 2025 annual report excerpt describes Risk Management Committee, Remuneration Committee and Management Board governance. The full Supervisory Board performs the Risk Management Committee function. In 2025 the committee held five meetings, adopted four resolutions and recorded 100% average attendance; attendance was 100% for Aleksander Chlopecki, Bartosz Zablocki, Grzegorz Grabowicz, Katarzyna Dabrowska, Ewa Stefaniak and Jakub Zablocki, while Jan Byrski was marked not applicable because no meetings occurred during his term. As at 1 January 2025, the Risk Management Committee comprised Jan Byrski as Chair and Grzegorz Grabowicz, Katarzyna Dabrowska, Ewa Stefaniak and Bartosz Zablocki as members. As at 31 December 2025, it comprised Aleksander Chlopecki as Chair, Grzegorz Grabowicz and, according to the excerpt, Katarzyna Dabrowska, Ewa Stefaniak, Bartosz Zablocki and Jakub Zablocki as members.

Risk Management Committee tasks include preparing a draft risk appetite document, giving opinions on the Management Board's risk management strategy, supporting Supervisory Board oversight of strategy implementation, reviewing whether remuneration policy aligns remuneration systems with risks, capital, liquidity and income probability and timing, and discussing and approving the Risk Appetite Statement and ICAAP report. The full Supervisory Board also performs the Remuneration Committee function. Its tasks include supporting the Management Board in preparing remuneration solutions compliant with the variable-remuneration policy, issuing opinions on remuneration policy and remuneration components, reviewing the policy, supervising variable remuneration paid to persons materially affecting the investment firm's risk profile, and determining variable remuneration for Management Board members. The Supervisory Board may appoint other committees by resolution.

Management Board changes in 2025 were significant. Andrzej Przybylski informed the Company on 31 March 2025 that he would not seek another term, so his term as Management Board Member for Risk Management expired on 1 July 2025. On 25 June 2025, the Supervisory Board appointed a four-member Management Board for a joint three-year term from 2 July 2025 to 2 July 2028: Omar Arnaout as President, and Pawel Szejko, Filip Kaczmarzyk and Jakub Kubacki as members. On 26 August 2025, the Supervisory Board conditionally appointed Bartosz Osinski as Management Board Member for Risk, subject to Polish Financial Supervision Authority approval; the Company received the approval decision on 1 December 2025. After the balance sheet date, on 3 March 2026, Jakub Kubacki resigned as Management Board Member effective at the end of 30 June 2026.

The Management Board manages the Company, operates under Management Board Regulations, and meets at least monthly at the registered office or elsewhere in Poland if all members consent. It may have three to six members, including the President and two Vice Presidents. At least two members should have higher education, at least three years of experience in financial-market institutions, and good reputation. Members are appointed and dismissed by the Supervisory Board, which determines their number; a member may also be dismissed or suspended by the General Meeting. Members serve a three-year joint term. The Management Board manages Company affairs, represents the Company, acts in the Company's interest, prepares development strategy drafts and defines main objectives. The President convenes and chairs meetings, or authorizes another member; if absent, the oldest member convenes meetings.

For the current term expiring 2 July 2028, the Management Board consisted of Omar Arnaout, President, first appointed 10 January 2017; Filip Kaczmarzyk, member, first appointed 10 January 2017; Pawel Szejko, member, first appointed 28 January 2015; Jakub Kubacki, member, first appointed 10 July 2018; and Bartosz Osinski, member, first appointed 26 August 2025 with KNF approval received on 1 December 2025. The previous term expiring 1 July 2025 included Omar Arnaout, Filip Kaczmarzyk, Pawel Szejko, Jakub Kubacki and Andrzej Przybylski. From 2 July to 1 December 2025, before Bartosz Osinski's approval, the Management Board comprised Omar Arnaout, Filip Kaczmarzyk, Pawel Szejko and Jakub Kubacki.
