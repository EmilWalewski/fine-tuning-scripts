Fair value hierarchy definitions follow standard levels: Level 1 uses unadjusted quoted prices in active markets, Level 2 uses directly or indirectly observable inputs other than Level 1 prices, and Level 3 uses unobservable inputs. Level 1 fair value of debt securities liabilities is determined from Catalyst market quotations at period end. Level 2 comprised IRS, CIRS and FORWARD derivatives; fair values are based on future cash flows calculated from the difference between forecast WIBOR 3M and WIBOR 3M at transaction date, using an external WIBOR 3M forecast. Level 3 at 30 June 2025 showed debt portfolio investments PLN 10,797.3M carrying and PLN 10,505.0M fair value, loans granted PLN 561.4M carrying and PLN 560.8M fair value, receivables PLN 41.8M, secured loans and borrowings negative PLN 3,083.4M carrying and negative PLN 3,101.5M fair value, and payables negative PLN 174.0M.

Debt portfolio investment fair value is based on expected future cash flows discounted by a rate reflecting portfolio-specific credit risk, calculated as the acquisition internal rate of return and reviewed for current risk-free rate and credit-risk premium. Differences between fair value and amortised-cost carrying value result from different methodologies: carrying value reflects expected portfolio repayments and balance-sheet FX rate, while fair value also reflects forecast collection costs and risk-free rate. Loan fair value is based on expected cash-flow forecasts; financial liabilities from loans and borrowings are valued from future transaction cash flows. Short-term trade and other non-tax receivables and payables are treated as carrying value equal to fair value under Level 3 inputs.
