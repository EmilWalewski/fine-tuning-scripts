XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025 consolidated annual report excerpt covers remuneration governance. The ability to freely dispose of subsequent tranches of granted shares arises after publication on the Warsaw Stock Exchange of the annual report containing consolidated data. Under the internal remuneration system, employees receive compensation for work corresponding to the type of work performed and required qualifications, with regard to the quality and quantity of work delivered. Remuneration principles for Management Board and Supervisory Board members are described in the Policy for Granting Fixed Remuneration to Members of the Management Board and Supervisory Board of XTB S.A. dated 31 July 2023, the Policy on Variable Remuneration Components dated 12 April 2024, and the Incentive Programme Regulations dated 12 April 2024, jointly referred to as the Remuneration Policy; these documents are available on the company investor-relations website in the Corporate Governance section at https://ir.xtb.com/lad-korporacyjny/regulaminy-i-polityki/. The remuneration model for XTB S.A. Management Board members comprises fixed remuneration, variable remuneration, and non-cash benefits. The rules and amounts of Management Board remuneration are set by the Supervisory Board. Management Board members are remunerated under employment contracts for an indefinite term. Their remuneration is determined appropriately to the function performed and the scale of the company’s operations, and includes fixed remuneration as part of total remuneration, reflecting the type of work performed by each Management Board member, the qualifications required, and the quantity, assessment, and quality of work performed; the organizational responsibility scope of each Management Board member is also a significant factor. When determining fixed remuneration, Supervisory Board members take into account the local labour-market situation.
