The condensed interim consolidated financial statements were prepared using measurement bases of amortized cost determined with the effective interest method, including impairment allowances for credit-impaired financial assets exposed to credit risk, for financial assets held in a business model aimed at collecting contractual cash flows and for other financial liabilities; fair value for derivatives and loans whose contractual cash flows are not solely payments of principal and interest on outstanding principal; and historical cost for non-financial assets and liabilities. The same accounting policies were applied as in the latest annual consolidated financial statements as at and for the year ended 31 December 2024, except for presentation changes made to improve comparability. The Group applied EU-endorsed amendments effective from 1 January 2025, namely amendments to IAS 21 The Effects of Changes in Foreign Exchange Rates concerning lack of exchangeability; these changes had no significant impact on the condensed interim consolidated financial statements. The statements incorporate EU-endorsed IAS, IFRS and related interpretations issued and effective for annual periods beginning on 1 January 2025. To better reflect economic substance and improve usefulness of data in the consolidated statement of financial position, the Group changed presentation of Investments by separating Investments in debt portfolios measured at amortized cost and Loans granted, and changed presentation of liabilities from loans, borrowings, debt securities and leases by separating Liabilities from bank loans and borrowings, Liabilities from debt securities and Lease liabilities. In the consolidated statement of cash flows, the Group split interest received and paid on hedging instruments into Interest received on hedging instruments and Interest paid on hedging instruments. Data published in the consolidated financial statements as at 31 December 2024 and for 1 January 2024-31 December 2024 were restated for comparability. The presentation change in the consolidated statement of financial position reclassified total liabilities from loans, borrowings, debt securities and leases: as at 30 September 2025 the previously published 7,082.9M PLN was reduced by 7,082.9M PLN to nil and presented as 3,506.0M PLN liabilities from bank loans and borrowings, 3,467.5M PLN liabilities from debt securities and 109.4M PLN lease liabilities; as at 31 December 2024 the previously published 6,626.6M PLN was reduced by 6,626.6M PLN to nil and presented as 3,458.6M PLN liabilities from bank loans and borrowings, 3,109.7M PLN liabilities from debt securities and 58.2M PLN lease liabilities. Forthcoming EU-approved standards and interpretations listed with effective dates from 1 January 2026 include the Annual Improvements to IFRS Accounting Standards, Volume 11, which introduce minor amendments to IFRS 1, IFRS 7, IFRS 9, IFRS 10 and IAS 7, with the Group analyzing possible effects and not expecting a material impact; amendments to IFRS 9 and IFRS 7 on classification and measurement of financial instruments, published by the IASB in May 2024, clarifying recognition and derecognition dates for certain financial assets and liabilities, adding an exemption for some financial liabilities settled through an electronic cash transfer system, clarifying and expanding SPPI assessment guidance, adding disclosures for instruments whose contractual terms may change cash flows, and updating FVOCI equity instrument disclosures, with the Group analyzing possible effects and not expecting a material impact; and amendments to IFRS 9 and IFRS 7 on nature-dependent electricity contracts, intended to better reflect physical and virtual nature-dependent power purchase agreements, with no expected impact on the Group consolidated financial statements. Standards and interpretations awaiting EU approval listed with effective dates from 1 January 2027 include IFRS 19 Subsidiaries without Public Accountability: Disclosures and amendments to IFRS 19, establishing reduced disclosure requirements for eligible entities and expected to have no impact on the Group consolidated financial statements; IFRS 18 Presentation and Disclosure in Financial Statements, published in April 2024 to replace IAS 1 Presentation of Financial Statements and improve comparability and transparency, with Group analysis indicating an impact on presentation and disclosure scope in KRUK Group consolidated financial statements; and IAS 21 amendments on translation to a presentation currency in hyperinflationary conditions, intended to ensure consistent translation rules when an entity presents financial statements in the currency of a hyperinflationary economy while its functional currency or the currency of a foreign operation comes from a non-hyperinflationary economy, with no expected impact on the Group consolidated financial statements.
