XTB Group recognizes deferred tax assets only to the extent probable future taxable income will allow settlement of unused tax losses; the asset is based on an assumption of future taxable profit, and deterioration of future tax results could make that assumption unjustified. Deferred tax assets mainly relate to capitalized losses generated by foreign branches in their initial development phase; recoverability is assessed under local tax rules and future tax budgets. Fair value estimation disclosures are in note 33, Risk Management; the Group uses valuation techniques appropriate to circumstances and available data, maximizing observable inputs, minimizing unobservable inputs, and tailoring the fair value model to the characteristics of each financial asset. Other estimates include provisions for retirement, disability and post-mortem severance obligations, calculated actuarially by an independent actuary as present value of future employee obligations based on employment and payroll at the reporting date, with macroeconomic, employee turnover, mortality and other assumptions; unused leave provisions based on estimated leave-benefit payout from unused leave days and salary at the reporting date; and legal risk provisions set individually for each dispute after assessing win probability and the need for a loss provision for all court cases. Accounting policies for the interim condensed consolidated financial statements are consistent with the annual consolidated financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2024, except for new or amended standards and interpretations effective for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2025. The Group's activity is not seasonal.
