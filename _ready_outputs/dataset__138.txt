XTB S.A. Group 2025 annual report excerpt sets out GHG accounting methodology, emission-factor sources. Scope 3 sources include the GHG Protocol Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Accounting and Reporting Standard, its 2011 supplement, and GHG Protocol Technical Guidance for Calculating Scope 3 Emissions, version 1.0, 2013, by the World Resources Institute and World Business Council for Sustainable Development. Emission-factor references cover German district-heating location-based, WTT and transmission-loss factors; supplier factors for electricity and district heat; Polish district-heating factors for 2024; Lebanon 2022 transmission losses; Umweltbundesamt 2024 renewable-energy emissions balance; Unimot Energia i Gaz Sp. z o.o., Energy Regulatory Office, Vattenfall, VEOLIA ENERGIA WARSZAWA S.A., World Bank Group, and Zapadoslovenska energetika, a.s.; access date: 06.02.2026. For Scope 3 categories 4, 6, 7 and 9, full life-cycle fuel GHG calculations were impossible because direct-combustion factors are split by gas, while WTT factors are only in CO2e. EXIOBASE 2022 applies average methane GWP of 27.9; because fossil/non-fossil allocation was difficult, XTB used the average value from table 7.SM.7 of the Sixth IPCC Report. Based on operations, business model, financial materiality and reputational risks, XTB selected Scope 3 categories: 1 purchased goods and services, 2 capital goods, 3 fuel- and energy-related emissions outside Scope 1 and 2, 4 upstream transport and distribution, 5 operational waste, 6 business travel, 7 employee commuting, and 11 use of sold products. The footprint includes all GHG Protocol gases: CO2, CH4, N2O, SF6, PFCs, HFCs and NF3. 100-year GWP values came from the Sixth IPCC Report, except DEFRA 2025 Scope 3 factors, footnoted to UK conversion factors 2024, where Fifth IPCC Report values were used and should be recalculated to Sixth IPCC Report values. Electricity assumptions: one XTB S.A. location lacked supplier-name data, so a location-based factor was used; missing electricity consumption was estimated from climate zone and heating type using a similar company, previous reporting-period data or partial 2025 data; missing partial-period data were extrapolated from analogous prior-year periods; absent supplier factors led to country location-based factors. EMBER national factors include full life-cycle electricity emissions, including direct and upstream WTT emissions assigned by GHG Protocol to Scope 3 Category 3; WTT electricity-generation emissions could not be separated and were included in Scope 2. Because supplier and national factors lacked non-CO2 data, market-based and location-based electricity calculations included only CO2. Stationary combustion covers emergency-generator fuel and building-heating fuel. Some locations had no emergency-generator fuel use or no information; non-responding locations were excluded from the 2025 Group carbon footprint. Two XTB S.A. branches lacked 2025 natural-gas heat data, so consumption was estimated from available data. Several branches and subsidiaries had no fuel-consuming equipment. Most branches and subsidiaries had no vehicles or car fleet. Some locations reported no refrigerant leakage, no air-conditioning system or gave no information; missing-data locations were excluded from the 2025 footprint. No Group company owned or controlled renewable electricity-generation installations. Direct emissions from transmission losses were not re-included in Scope 3 Category 3 because already covered by the supplier factor. Scope 3 primary data are direct measurements from specific sources or processes and are more accurate than secondary data or general factors; primary-data calculations represented 1.09% of total Scope 3 emissions, up 0.05 p.p. year on year. Category 1 purchased goods and services calculations used expenditure cost-account lists from accounting records. Because product origin countries were unavailable, Exiobase factors were matched to each company or branch country of operation. GHG Protocol spend-based calculations should use net amounts, but XTB S.A. and subsidiaries, as financial entities mainly providing VAT-exempt services, partially deduct input VAT by structure or do not deduct it, so costs include non-deductible input VAT and expenditure data may use different amount types. Non-emitting items and items already included in other reporting scopes were excluded. Missing or incomplete water-consumption data were estimated from other XTB S.A. units or by extrapolation.
