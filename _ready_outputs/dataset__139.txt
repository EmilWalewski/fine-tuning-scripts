Supervisory Board operated under the Polish Commercial Companies Code, the Company Statute, its publicly available Supervisory Board Rules defining organization and procedures, and the Best Practice for WSE Listed Companies. It functioned as the Company’s permanent supervisory body across all areas of activity, adopting resolutions or issuing opinions on matters reserved to its competence under the Statute and applicable law. Supervisory Board meetings were held regularly with Management Board members participating; in 2025 the Supervisory Board held 8 meetings. The Management Board provided the Supervisory Board with comprehensive information on all material matters concerning the Company’s operations. The Supervisory Board met the requirement to include at least two Independent Members under independence criteria adopted in the Company Statute. Resolutions on consent for benefits of any title by the Company or related entities to Management Board members, consent for the Company or its subsidiary to conclude a material agreement with a Company related party, Supervisory Board or Management Board member, or their related entities, and selection of the statutory auditor for the audit of the Company’s financial statements required approval by a majority of Independent Supervisory Board Members. Supervisory Board remuneration was determined transparently, did not constitute a material cost item affecting the Company’s financial result, was approved by General Meeting resolution, and was disclosed in the annual report. The Annual General Meeting held on 12 June 2025 approved the Supervisory Board’s 2024 activity report, prepared under the Commercial Companies Code and Best Practice for WSE Listed Companies 2021. Two committees operated within the Supervisory Board: the Audit Committee and the Remuneration Committee.
