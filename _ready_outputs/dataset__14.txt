In 2025, XTB Group continued developing and strengthening its institutional client segment through X Open Hub by offering liquidity and transaction technology services. These technologies allow institutional clients to build trading environments capable of offering the same or similar products and services as XTB, meaning such clients may potentially compete with XTB. Growth in the institutional segment depends materially on acquiring new clients, although onboarding an institutional client is described as a relatively long process, typically lasting up to one year. As of 31 December 2025, XTB had 32 institutional clients, compared with 31 a year earlier. The Group remains open to acquisitions, mergers and joint ventures involving companies with attractive business profiles that could complement XTB’s product and service offering or support further geographic expansion, but it assumes organic growth will remain the main driver of development; no such acquisition, merger or joint venture events occurred in 2025. XTB also continued expanding client services, including an eWallet available in 25 currencies such as EUR, USD, GBP, PLN, HUF, RON, CZK, CHF, JPY and CAD, with fast and free transfers between accounts, instant access to funds, and card payments using a Mastercard-licensed card issued by DiPocket UAB, an electronic money institution registered by the Bank of Lithuania. The eWallet service is currently available to clients in Poland, Czechia, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Germany, France, Spain and Italy. Additional product initiatives include IKZE retirement-security accounts for clients in Poland and PEA long-term investment accounts for clients in France. In technology, the company focused on improving security for its platform and mobile application, including a gradual rollout of automatic 2FA login for existing and new clients; more than 98% of clients now have this feature enabled and can no longer disable it. The XTB mobile application received a refreshed, more user-friendly home view, including added instrument-sorting functionality in response to client demand, and the news module was also modernized. XTB introduced changes to tools dedicated to institutional client support, enabling smoother handling of client requests related to trading-session configuration changes. In ESG strategy, XTB’s environmental objective is responsible e-waste management, employee environmental education, efficient resource management and actions enabling the Group to set greenhouse gas emission reduction targets. Its social objective is to provide a safe and inspiring work environment, build lasting social relationships, maintain active stakeholder dialogue and offer products and services meeting client expectations. Its corporate governance objective is to build long-term value through transparent and responsible management of environmental, social and governance aspects across XTB Group.
