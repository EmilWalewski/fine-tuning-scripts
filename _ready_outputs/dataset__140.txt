In Q3 2025, XTB Group's operating revenue was PLN 375.8M, down 20.1% year on year and 54.5% quarter on quarter, mainly because CFD trading profitability declined. Profitability per lot fell to PLN 152 versus PLN 272 in Q3 2024 and PLN 229 in Q2 2025, while profitability per USD 1M of nominal CFD turnover fell to USD 84 versus USD 167 in Q3 2024 and USD 128 in Q2 2025. The decline reflected low financial and commodity market activity in Q3 2025: most popular client instruments traded in more predictable, narrow price ranges, supporting range trading, increasing the number of profitable client trades and reducing, or potentially turning negative, the market-making result. Index markets were very calm: the German index moved in a very narrow sideways trend despite new highs, while US indices had wider but still strongly limited ranges. Gold began a rally in September and reached further record prices, but the prior two months were marked by market stagnation. Commodity markets, especially natural gas, cocoa and wheat, were more volatile, while oil also traded in a very limited range. Despite the unfavorable market environment and limited ability to use the potential of XTB's client base, active clients increased 71.5% year on year, transaction activity remained high, CFD contracts in lots increased 28.6% year on year, and nominal CFD turnover in USD increased 60.8% year on year. CFD derivative turnover reached 2,094.3 thousand lots versus 1,628.0 thousand lots in Q3 2024 and 2,321.6 thousand lots in Q2 2025; nominal CFD turnover was USD 1,118.3bn versus USD 695.3bn in Q3 2024 and USD 1,144.6bn in Q2 2025. In the three-month periods ended 30 September 2025, 30 June 2025, 31 March 2025, 31 December 2024, 30 September 2024, 30 June 2024, 31 March 2024 and 31 December 2023, total operating revenue was PLN 375.8M, PLN 580.6M, PLN 580.3M, PLN 465.4M, PLN 470.2M, PLN 381.8M, PLN 555.9M and PLN 506.7M, respectively. CFD derivative turnover in lots for the same periods was 2,094,296, 2,321,584, 1,907,974, 1,657,390, 1,627,978, 1,461,670, 1,527,139 and 1,497,241; profitability per lot was PLN 152, PLN 229, PLN 277, PLN 253, PLN 272, PLN 232, PLN 344 and PLN 322; nominal CFD turnover was USD 1,118.3bn, USD 1,144.6bn, USD 937.9bn, USD 727.9bn, USD 695.3bn, USD 621.5bn, USD 581.9bn and USD 548.9bn; profitability per USD 1M of nominal CFD turnover was USD 84, USD 128, USD 144, USD 147, USD 167, USD 142, USD 229 and USD 219. Lot is the transaction unit for financial instruments, with size varying by instrument; for CFDs based on currencies, including cryptocurrencies, one lot equals 100,000 units of base currency, while other lot values are defined in the instrument specification table. The presented turnover excludes CFDs on shares and ETPs, where one lot equals one share, and comparative periods were adjusted after unifying the lot definition for cryptocurrency CFDs with currency CFDs. Profitability per lot is net result on financial instruments adjusted for shares and ETP result and share/ETP CFD result divided by CFD derivative turnover in lots. Profitability per USD 1M of nominal CFD turnover is net result on financial instruments adjusted for shares and ETP result, translated into USD using the arithmetic average of National Bank of Poland month-end average exchange rates for the reporting period, divided by nominal CFD turnover in USD millions. The subsequent aggregate table contains revenue, CFD turnover and profitability rows, but its period headers are not visible in the supplied text, so the column-period mapping is not determinable. By financial-instrument revenue structure, commodity-based CFDs led Q3 2025 with a 48.5% revenue share versus 38.2% in Q3 2024, driven by high profitability on CFDs based on gold, natural gas and cocoa prices. Index-based CFDs were the second most profitable asset class, with a 32.4% revenue share versus 44.9% a year earlier, reflecting high profitability on CFDs based on the US 100 index, VIX Volatility Index and US 500 index. Currency-based CFDs generated 10.8% of total revenue versus 14.6% a year earlier; the most profitable instruments in this class were cryptocurrency-based CFDs on ethereum, bitcoin and ripple.
