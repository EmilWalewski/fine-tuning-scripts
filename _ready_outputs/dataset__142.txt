Dom Development S.A. Group management report for H1 2025 shows a materially expanded residential and service-unit offer, a broadly stable construction pipeline, and handovers in line with the Management Board's assumptions despite a slight year-on-year decline. As at 30 June 2025, the Group had 3,778 residential units available for sale, up 30% year on year from 2,915, reflecting execution of a supply-securing strategy amid stronger housing demand. Warsaw, the Group's key market, had the largest offer at 1,332 units versus 1,020, up 31%. Tricity Euro Styl S.A. recorded the strongest regional offer expansion, up 71% to 1,041 units from 610, driven by increased investment activity and the growing importance of Tricity as a high-demand market, supporting geographic diversification and market-position strengthening. Wroclaw Dom Development Wroclaw Sp. z o.o. had 753 units in offer versus 648, up 16%, while Krakow Dom Development Krakow Sp. z o.o. remained broadly stable at 652 units versus 637, up 2%. Management assessed the level and structure of the offer at the end of H1 2025 as safe and adapted to current market realities; the Group's offer, despite growth, remained below the sector's balanced supply benchmark defined as volume corresponding to forecast annual sales, confirming high market absorption and justifying further investment activity.

In H1 2025 the Group conducted construction of 8,270 residential and service units, compared with 8,371 a year earlier, a 1% decline, while starting 19 new projects covering 1,942 units. In Warsaw, prolonged administrative procedures and limited availability of attractive investment land led Dom Development S.A. to adjust activity to market conditions; construction in the capital covered 3,424 units at end-June 2025 versus 3,585, down 4%. Tricity showed increased investment activity, with 2,303 units under construction versus 1,994, up 15%. Krakow maintained a stable construction scale, with 1,173 units versus 1,176, or 0% change. Wroclaw had 1,370 units under construction versus 1,616, down 15%. All projects were implemented according to adopted schedules and cost assumptions, supporting satisfactory project profitability. The Group's own general contractors remained an operational advantage, delivering projects to Dom Development quality standards while maintaining competitive costs and reinforcing the Group's competitive position in the development market.

In H1 2025 the Group handed over 1,595 units to clients, down 3% from 1,637 in H1 2024. Wroclaw had the largest number of handovers, 624 units versus 48, an increase of 1,200%. Warsaw handed over 497 units versus 1,088, down 54%; Tricity handed over 392 apartments versus 356, up 10%; and Krakow handed over 82 units versus 145, down 43%. Efficient and timely project execution allowed the Group to deliver handovers consistent with Management Board assumptions and supported the annual plan, while a significant part of handovers was scheduled for Q4 2025, potentially materially affecting Group results later in 2025. Handover volumes were directly dependent on individual project schedules. In H1 2025 the Group completed construction of 9 projects comprising 1,249 units. As at 30 June 2025, the Group portfolio included 5,418 sold but not yet handed-over apartments with a total value of PLN 4,065m.
