Creotech Instruments S.A. reported no change since the previous periodic report in shares held by managing and supervisory persons. Under EGM Resolution No. 4 of 30 January 2025 establishing the Incentive Program, eligible participants were indicated: Management Board members on 2 October 2025 and employees on 13 October 2025. The Program covers up to 107,000 rights; 80,661 were granted, including 61,441 to employees who are not managing or supervisory persons. Management Board grants were 4,805 rights each to Grzegorz Brona, President, Pawel Gornicki, Vice-President for Finance, Maciej Misiura, Vice-President, and Jacek Mandas, Vice-President. Exercise depends on positive completion during the Program of SSOZ “Mikroglob” manufacture and delivery, phases B, C, D and E1, disclosed in current report No. 42/2024. On 9 January 2025 the Management Board signed an ESA contract for “Space Based Observation Mission (SBOM) Feasibility Study”, with the Company as leader; phase 0-A prepares a satellite and mission to detect space debris from near-Earth orbit, with Creotech designing a satellite based on its proprietary HyperSat platform. Phase 0-A value is about EUR 125k, over EUR 100k for the Company, over 12 months; the project proceeds and further ESA talks are planned on satellite construction. On 22 January 2025 the Management Board signed an ESA contract for “Starterkit for a base station of a private 5G mmWave network with ultra-precise time synchronization”, with the Company as leader, verifying ultra-precise time/frequency synchronization of 5G mmWave components and integration with 5G Non-Terrestrial Networks; solutions come from quantum-technology activity. Project value is about EUR 600k, about EUR 480k ESA-financed and the rest contractor-funded, including technological contribution; Creotech has about 50% of total value and 50% of ESA financing. The project ends at TRL 7, enabling production deployment. Microamp Solutions Sp. z o.o. is subcontractor. Testing at ESA’s 5G/6G Hub in Harwell Science and Innovation Campus will synchronize terrestrial 5G mmWave networks with satellite components for an NTN base-station system enabling multi-gigabit data transmission and civil/government research. Series K shares were issued under EGM Resolution No. 3 of 30 January 2025; subscription agreements were concluded on 14-19 February 2025 by private subscription under Art. 431 § 2 item 1 of the Commercial Companies Code, through Management Board offers and investor acceptances, without subscriptions or allotment under Art. 434 et seq. On 19 February 2025 the Management Board set the final subscriber list. The subscription covered 475,000 Series K shares at PLN 160 each. Series K shares were admitted and introduced to GPW main-market trading on 30 April 2025, after rights to those shares were admitted and introduced on 12 March 2025. On 12 March 2025 the Management Board announced signing on 11 March 2025 of an ESA contract for “DTM Tools”, with the Company as sole contractor. Deliverables are the DTM Tools toolkit and internet portal for drone users and infrastructure, supporting pre-flight risk analysis using terrain, population and infrastructure data, multidimensional planning and real-time mission management using direct and indirect satellite communication. The portal will provide analytics and consolidated management of drone operations. Project value is EUR 1,849,642, including EUR 1,479,713 ESA-financed, over 36 months. On 30 April 2025 the Management Board signed a subcontract with Germany’s OHB Digital Connect GmbH for BLAM, “BACKGROUND LIGHT AND ATMOSPHERE METROLOGY FOR QKD AT URBAN LOCATIONS”, implemented by OHB Digital Connect GmbH for ESA; Company tasks are worth EUR 175,514 in a 60-month project and cover design/manufacture of a subsystem measuring atmospheric background light for satellite quantum key distribution, monitoring turbulence and urban light-pollution background, with measurements used to plan ground-station deployment in SAGA and IRIS2. On 17 July 2025 the Management Board reported Annex No. 1 to the agreement with the State Treasury - Armament Agency for SSOZ “Mikroglob”; it changes advances paid to the Company to an escrow-account mechanism, enabling advances to finance not-yet-incurred expenditure for selected components, subassemblies and services up to about PLN 101.54m net. Advances will be settled by deductions from delivery invoices. Together with proceeds from the early-2025 Series K issue, the advances should, in the Management Board’s opinion, finance “Mikroglob” for the contract term, so it currently sees no need for additional financing instruments, in particular another share issue for “Mikroglob”.
