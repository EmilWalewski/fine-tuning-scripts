FVTPL financial assets arise from financial instrument transactions with Group clients and related hedging transactions; related credit risk is client or counterparty default risk. For OTC client transactions, the Group mitigates counterparty credit risk through a stop-out mechanism secured by client cash deposited with the brokerage house: if the current client account balance is equal to or below 50% of deposited collateral blocked in the trading system, the position generating the largest loss is automatically closed at the current market price. Initial margin depends on instrument type, client account, account currency, and cash account balance in the trading system as a percentage of transaction notional value; selected client agreements also include clauses such as minimum cash account balance requirements. Given these mitigation mechanisms, FVTPL asset credit quality was high and did not show material differentiation.
