Dom Development S.A., year ended 31 December 2025. Development-project revenue from sales of residential units, commercial space or similar real estate on third-party land is recognized like projects on Company-owned or perpetual-usufruct land when the Company performs marketing, sales, customer service and design/construction handling and bears project risks. Construction service revenue is recognized over time by percentage of completion, measured as incurred costs versus estimated total contract costs. If the contract result cannot be reliably estimated, revenue is limited to recoverable incurred costs and contract costs are expensed as incurred. Expected total costs above revenues create an expected loss charged to period costs and cost of sales/cost of services sold. Completion revenue above invoiced revenue is recognized in sales revenue/service revenue and other current assets; below invoiced revenue, in sales revenue/service revenue and deferred income. Other service revenue, including estate administration, is recognized when provided. Current tax receivables/liabilities use expected payments/refunds and tax rates/laws effective at the balance sheet date. Deferred tax uses the balance-sheet liability method on temporary differences between tax bases and carrying amounts. Deferred tax assets cover deductible temporary differences, unused tax credits and tax losses carried forward when probable taxable income permits use; they are reviewed each balance sheet date, reduced when recovery is no longer probable, and unrecognized assets are reassessed. Deferred tax liabilities cover future tax payable from taxable temporary differences increasing the tax base. Deferred tax uses expected realization/settlement rates based on legally or substantively enacted rates/laws at the balance sheet date. Tax on items outside profit or loss goes to other comprehensive income or directly to equity according to the item. Deferred tax offsetting requires an enforceable right to offset current tax receivables against liabilities/provisions and the same taxpayer and authority. Dividends are recognized when shareholder or unitholder rights to receive them are established. Net EPS equals period net profit divided by weighted average shares. For leases over 12 months, except low-value assets, the Company recognizes lease assets/liabilities and separates right-of-use asset depreciation from lease-liability interest in profit or loss. Office-space right-of-use assets are depreciated, lease finance costs recognized, the asset shown in property, plant and equipment, and the liability in non-current or current liabilities. For perpetual usufruct land rights, lease costs during development go to inventories, specifically semi-finished goods and work in progress; the asset is shown in inventories or short-term receivables, and the liability wholly in current liabilities. Because these rights relate to development properties, costs move from inventories/work in progress to profit or loss with cost of finished goods sold when finished goods are handed to customers and sale revenue is recognized. On 20 July 2018 the Act transforming perpetual usufruct rights to land developed for residential purposes into ownership rights entered into force; affected land is treated like previous perpetual usufruct land, and transformation fees like perpetual usufruct fees. Perpetual usufruct rights to land under development projects are generally inventories. Payment obligations are settled by transfer to apartment buyers to whom the rights relate. Related liabilities are current, consistent with current inventories, because unit sale and liability transfer occur within the Company’s operating cycle, the period from project start to realization of inventories into cash. The Company is legally released from perpetual usufruct or transformation-fee debt only on legal, notarial transfer to the buyer of the land share attached to the sold unit; until then, the land lease liability remains on the balance sheet. On unit handover, also the revenue-recognition moment, the related lease asset portion is transferred from inventories to buyer receivables in the amount corresponding to the land lease liability. Until ownership transfer, both receivable and liability are current because settlement occurs by buyer transfer within the operating cycle. On ownership transfer, the Company derecognizes the land lease liability and related apartment-buyer receivable.
