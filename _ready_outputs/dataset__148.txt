Q1 2025 Asseco Poland segment revenue from external customers and inter-segment sales was PLN 544.1M versus PLN 508.7M a year earlier, equal to 12% of Group sales. EBIT was PLN 104.0M versus PLN 86.0M in Q1 2024; EBITDA was PLN 130.3M versus PLN 110.8M. Segment metrics: EBIT is operating profit; EBIT non-IFRS excludes PPA intangible amortization, SBP equity-settled employee transaction costs and income/costs from company acquisition/sale transactions; CFO BT is operating cash flow before income tax paid; CAPEX is segment fixed-asset expenditure; FCF equals absolute CFO BT minus absolute CAPEX and lease payments; cash conversion is FCF divided by EBIT non-IFRS less real-estate sale proceeds. In finance and banking, Asseco Poland, the largest Polish-segment contributor, continued work with existing clients, including long-term service contracts producing about 40% of area revenue. It continued Bank Gospodarstwa Krajowego's central transaction-system transformation and, under a 4-year 2022 contract, maintained, developed and transformed def2000 into an open form enabling further maintenance and development without material technological and legal limits. For PKO BP, the largest regional bank, it developed and serviced the core customer-transaction IT system, interbank settlement system and Google-cloud debit-card management and settlement system. It supported Allegro Pay: Asseco systems process all contracts under Allegro's 2020 BNPL service; Asseco Core Banking supplies banking-tested solutions and Allegro accounting schemes, prepares repayment schedules, calculates and posts interest, registers loan disbursement, handles repayments, calculates arrears and processes accounting, while sellers receive full amounts immediately. Allegro Pay services run 24/7, including mass day-closing operations, support post-sale customer service and are delivered as subscription SaaS. Asseco developed Asseco BooX, its banking platform for Poland and global markets: Poland's first tech/process platform for selling and servicing financial products, combining banking systems with Asseco IT BPO within a subscription. First used by cooperative banks, it is offered to commercial banks and fintechs; as a cloud-capable subscription platform it targets neobanks, consolidating banks, test/regulatory sandboxes and e-commerce marketplace solutions. BooX is available by subscription or licence and operates on-premise, in cloud or hybrid. Asseco continued selling DORA compliance support, including AI-based digital-resilience gap analysis and DORA compliance control using DORA.ai, which checks gaps between internal documentation and regulator requirements; banks may receive templates for 9 critical areas, including ICT incident strategy, ICT system testing/control, business continuity, risk management, security and ICT audits. Asseco Group companies in Poland serve over 80% of cooperative banks, many in internet banking SaaS. In Q1 2025 Asseco worked on solutions for cooperative banks of both Polish associations, including System Uslug SGB with SGB-Bank as key systems supplier: a banking-system module package covering core transaction system, internet banking, analytical tools and accounting/IT services, operating in SGB-Bank-owned private cloud and used by 62 cooperative banks. It developed Wspolna Platforma Informatyczna, a BooX implementation with BPS Group institutions, combining transaction system, omnichannel internet/mobile/process front-end, reporting and analytics in SaaS to reduce infrastructure and resource costs. It implemented Kids and Junior mobile applications with parent and child/teen dashboards for children under 13 and older youth; BSGo Kids automatically converts to Junior at age 13 and later to the full adult application. It signed further cooperative-bank orders to modify Asseco Authority Requests and Data Services for automated PESEL-number restriction verification to limit identity-theft effects and handle dormant accounts; signed agreements for Klient 360 seller console giving fast customer and contract information; extended def3000/cIRM for IRRBB interest-rate-risk measurement; supported ISO20022 implementation; and worked on Asseco Enterprise Banking Platform integration with the National e-Invoice System (KSeF). On the Polish capital market, Asseco continued adapting PROMAK systems to GPW WATS, planned for production launch in November 2025, and delivered/implemented PROMAK NEXT core system and ePROMAK NEXT trading platform in new technology for a brokerage office of a large commercial bank, adjusted to the new GPW system. It pursued PROMAK NEXT as a new line of modern, highly automated cloud solutions for brokerage offices and houses, based on miniapplications enabling easy expansion and rapid integration with client products/services. In Business Intelligence, Asseco ran obligatory-reporting projects for financial-sector clients, data-warehouse development for banking clients, AML and fraud-detection compliance for banking and lending clients, and customer analytics/communication using AI for financial and medical clients.
