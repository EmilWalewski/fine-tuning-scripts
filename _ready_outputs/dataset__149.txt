In Q1 2025 Asseco International revenue rose 10.8% to PLN 1,058.3M, 23% of consolidated sales. Operating profit was stable at PLN 101.3M. EBITDA was PLN 152.3M versus PLN 151.6M in Q1 2024, up 0.5%. Asseco Central Europe Group (ACE) companies in Czechia and Slovakia delivered public-sector projects. For the Czech Ministry of the Interior, ACE is implementing eSeL, comprising e-Collection and e-Legislation platforms for electronic access to legal regulations; it is unique in EU countries and linked with EUR-Lex and N-Lex. ACE is developing National Integrated Ticketing (NICL), enabling nationwide one-ticket travel. From July 2026 Slovak passengers are to use one ticket for railways, urban public transport, and suburban buses. NICL will use Metropolis, a scalable, cloud-based, multilingual platform with one account for all transport modes and new-service integration. ACE signed a 5-year maintenance agreement. ACE implemented AI at the Supreme Audit Office of the Slovak Republic (NKU), automating internal processes including audit and report generation. In healthcare ACE cooperated with Slovakia's National Health Information Centre, General Health Insurance Company, and the Czech Social Security Administration, and delivered Czech Ministry of Health projects. ACE continued insurance digitalisation through Asseco Digital Insurance Platform and served J&T Finance Group, Slovenská sporiteľňa, Raiffeisen stavební spořitelna, and Modrá pyramida stavební spořitelna. ACE company Asseco BERIT is implementing Asseco Strategic Asset Management & Operations (Asseco SAMO) in Munich. SAMO integrates infrastructure, fixed-asset, and maintenance data, analyses strategic equipment wear, and will support Munich street-lighting management and traffic control. Asseco CEIT, focused on industrial digitalisation and Industry 4.0, continued automotive projects for Volkswagen Slovakia and AUDI Hungaria, including fleet replacement, intralogistics automation, and AGV deliveries. R&D is performed in its own R&D centre. CEIT uses a private 5G campus network for logistics and manufacturing innovations, developed Twiserion Design Manager for production, logistics, and warehouse planning/optimisation, and expanded its AGV fleet. Asseco Enterprise Solutions Group (AES), ACE's ERP competence centre, delivered strong Q1 2025 sales. Proprietary software and related services increased and formed 93% of revenue, supported by higher ERP spending across all main AES markets. AES products serve over 70,000 customers and over 700,000 active users. Asseco ERP companies are adding specialised AI functions. Asseco Business Solutions (ABS) revenue rose 8% year on year in Q1 2025. ABS offers ERP for medium and large enterprises, small-business applications, HR software, Europe-wide mobile SFA tools, data-exchange platforms, and factoring software. Domestic sales rose 7%. ABS systems operate in several dozen countries. Foreign sales were 13% of revenue; Austria, Switzerland, Germany, France, the Netherlands, Romania, and the United Kingdom generated 87% of foreign-market revenue. ABS follows one ERP strategy: Anywhere, Genius, Paperless. More ERP and SFA products and services are cloud-based. Full-outsourcing projects are rising, with ABS responsible for software, infrastructure, communication, and integration supervision. ABS continued AI implementation via Genius by Asseco, generating recommendations/notifications, adapting systems to user work styles, automating repetitive tasks, and providing predictive analyses. ABS expanded Image Recognition for FMCG and pharmaceutical clients, reporting product-display standards in shops and pharmacies. ABS developed products and services for planned KSeF National e-Invoice System implementation; ERP products support and comply with KSeF. The KSeF ecosystem includes ERP systems, Businesslink, electronic document workflow, Businesscheck, and SimplySign. ABS implemented paperless functions for internal employee communication/service and external document exchange with contractors and authorities. ABS acquired Białystok-based Tax Order, specialising in accounting-office software and comprehensive accounting training. Asseco Solutions AG, operating in Germany, Austria, Switzerland, and Italy, expanded partner cooperation and won new customers. APplus manufacturing ERP is used by over 80,000 users in over 1,000 companies in 25 countries. The latest APplus version addresses innovation, efficiency, rapid adaptation, and ease of use, and adds AI dashboards for individual control centres.
