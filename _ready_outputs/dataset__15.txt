At 30 June 2025, the Group’s credit risk exposure from purchased debt portfolios was PLN 10,797.330 M, up from PLN 10,500.278 M at 31 December 2024 and PLN 9,143.005 M at 30 June 2024, driven mainly by unsecured retail portfolios of PLN 9,724.660 M. Other portfolio exposures were secured retail PLN 229.804 M, unsecured corporate PLN 294.002 M, and secured corporate PLN 548.864 M. Gross loans at 30 June 2025 amounted to PLN 925.334 M, with expected credit loss allowances of PLN 366.564 M and net value of PLN 558.770 M; low-risk loans represented PLN 694.092 M gross and PLN 455.851 M net, medium-risk PLN 164.007 M gross and PLN 91.351 M net, high-risk PLN 22.013 M gross and PLN 6.406 M net, and unclassified PLN 45.222 M gross and PLN 5.162 M net. Loan risk classification is made when the loan is granted, based on delayed principal repayment shares of about 5% for low risk, 10% for medium risk and about 20% for high risk, with subsequent classification by baskets during the loan life. Liquidity risk is defined as the risk that the Group cannot settle obligations on time; liquidity management aims to preserve repayment capacity, fund current operations and development, and manage available funding sources efficiently. The Group’s liquidity rules cover debt financing, liquidity forecasts and analyses, and monitoring of covenant compliance, with cyclical sensitivity analysis against forecast recoveries from debt portfolios. New debt may be incurred only if repayment is possible from the Group’s own assets, intercompany transfers are feasible in terms of timing and cost, and financial ratios in credit agreements and bond terms are not breached. In the liquidity exposure analysis at 30 June 2025, purchased debt portfolios had a carrying amount of PLN 10,797.330 M and estimated cash flows of PLN 23,892.657 M, while granted loans had PLN 561.413 M carrying amount and PLN 881.988 M cash flows; secured bank loans and borrowings were PLN 3,083.411 M carrying amount with PLN 3,742.179 M contractual outflows, unsecured bonds PLN 3,387.014 M with PLN 4,461.896 M outflows, and lease liabilities PLN 71.024 M with PLN 85.861 M outflows. The corresponding net total was PLN 4,951.255 M carrying amount and PLN 16,618.670 M cash flows. At 31 December 2024, the comparable net total was PLN 4,427.881 M carrying amount and PLN 15,230.405 M cash flows. The maturity analysis excludes future purchases and collections from portfolios not yet acquired and future operating costs needed to generate inflows from financial assets; contractual cash flows were based on interest rates applicable at 30 June 2025 and 31 December 2024, and the Group does not expect material acceleration or significant changes in those flows. At 30 June 2025, the Group had an unused revolving credit limit of PLN 1,182.877 M, compared with PLN 475.189 M at 31 December 2024, available until 31 December 2029.
