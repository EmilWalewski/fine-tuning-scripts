LPP is a Polish family-owned apparel company designing, producing and distributing clothing, with more than 30 years of experience. The Group operates an omnichannel model on over forty markets across Poland, Europe, Central Asia and the Middle East, with five brands: Sinsay, Reserved, Cropp, House and Mohito. Collections are developed in Poland, while production of designed models is outsourced to external companies, mainly in Asia. LPP has distribution centres in Poland and abroad, invests in technologies improving distribution to physical stores and e-commerce, is headquartered in Gdansk, has offices in Krakow and Warsaw, and foreign offices in Shanghai, Dhaka, Istanbul and Tiruppur, India. The LPP team totals about 54 thousand people in offices, sales and distribution structures in Poland, European countries and Asia. Brand portfolio: Reserved, Cropp, House and Mohito operate in the moderate price segment, while Sinsay is Design&Value; all brands sell through physical and online channels. In 9M 2025 sales mix by brand was Sinsay 54%, Reserved 28%, House 6%, Mohito 6%, Cropp 5% and other 0.4%. The Group offered products in physical and online stores in 44 countries on 3 continents; physical network totalled 3,426 stores in 33 countries with total area of 2,850 thousand m2, and online presence covered 35 markets. Regional store footprint shown in the document was Poland 1,284 (+125), Baltics 151 (+41), WE 89 (+19), EE and ME 567 (+292), CEE excluding Poland 500 (+135) and SEE 835 (+240). The capital group comprised LPP SA as parent in Poland, 5 domestic subsidiaries and 31 foreign subsidiaries; foreign subsidiaries mainly distribute LPP brands abroad, while Polish companies handle Polish stores, promotional clothing sales, logistics and IT project management, including LPP Retail Sp. z o.o., Printable Sp. z o.o., LPP Logistics Sp. z o.o., Silky Coders Sp. z o.o. and Dock IT Sp. z o.o. In Q3 2025 the Group structure changed through liquidation of Spanish company LPP Clothing Retail Spain, s.l. and sale of Polish subsidiary Veviera Investments Sp. z o.o., which performed construction works related to logistics centres. Consolidated financial statements for 1 August 2025-31 October 2025 covered standalone results of LPP SA and results of foreign and Polish subsidiaries.
