Creotech Instruments Spółka Akcyjna, joint-stock company at ul. Jana Pawła II 66, 05-500 Piaseczno, registered by the District Court for the Capital City of Warsaw, XIV Commercial Division, REGON 141246690, was formed from Creotech Spółka z ograniczoną odpowiedzialnością on 2 January 2012 under the Extraordinary Shareholders Meeting resolution of 22 November 2011, notarial deed Repertory A No. 12116/2011, notary Krzysztof Borawski. It is not a subsidiary and has indefinite duration. Operations cover design, production and sale of space-industry subsystems and systems, including satellites; drone-operation management and satellite or drone data generation, processing or sharing systems; subsystems and systems for quantum computers, quantum cryptography, time synchronisation and other scientific uses; and contract manufacturing of industrial electronics. The interim condensed financial statements use historical cost and EU-endorsed IFRS, especially IAS 34; no accounting-policy differences exist between effective IFRS and EU-endorsed IFRS. Comprehensive income covers 9 months ended 30 September 2025, comparative 9 months ended 30 September 2024; financial position is as at 30 September 2025, comparative 31 December 2024; cash-flow and changes-in-equity statements cover 9 months ended 30 September 2025, comparative 9 months ended 30 September 2024. Amounts are in PLN thousand unless stated otherwise. The statements assume going concern for at least 12 months from the reporting date. In Q2 2025 the Company established and controlled Creotech Quantum S.A. and Creotech Geo P.S.A., creating a capital group; as both subsidiaries have limited operations, no operating revenue, minor costs and no employees, the parent deemed them immaterial under IAS 1 par. 29-31 and IAS 8 par. 5 and par. 8, prepared only separate interim financial statements as at 30 September 2025 and did not prepare consolidated statements. Separate-statement subsidiary disclosures are useful, relevant and faithful. Management analysed going-concern risks and identified no circumstances threatening continued operations. Functional and presentation currency is PLN; possible PLN 1k note summation differences arise from rounding, with amounts above PLN 500 rounded up and below PLN 500 rounded down. EU-endorsed IASB amendments effective from 1 January 2024 include IFRS 16 sale-and-leaseback lease liabilities, published 21 November 2023; IAS 1 current/non-current liability classification, published 20 December 2023; IAS 7 and IFRS 7 supplier-finance disclosures; Annual Improvements Volume 11 amending IFRS 1, IFRS 7, IFRS 9, IFRS 10 and IAS 7; and IFRS 9/IFRS 7 contracts referencing electricity dependent on natural factors. Not-yet EU-endorsed standards or amendments comprise IFRS 9/IFRS 7 classification and measurement, applicable from 1 January 2026 or later; IFRS 18 Presentation and Disclosure in Financial Statements, applicable from 1 January 2027 or later; IFRS 19 Subsidiaries without Public Accountability: Disclosures, applicable from 1 January 2027 or later; and IAS 21 lack of exchangeability, applicable from 1 January 2025 or later. These changes have no material effect on the interim condensed statements. Accounting policies changed in 2025 versus 31 December 2024. For property, plant and equipment, the minimum useful life of other fixed assets changed from 1 year to 13 months; useful-life ranges are 7-67 years for buildings and structures, 3-10 years for technical equipment and machinery, 5-15 years for vehicles, and 13 months-10 years for other fixed assets. Inventory issue value previously used FIFO except for non-interchangeable items and goods or services produced for specific projects, where specific identification of purchase prices or production costs applied. After the change, issue value uses FIFO or specific identification when required by project or contract terms, the actual price of each consumed asset; purchase price or production cost for non-interchangeable items and project-specific goods or services is set by specific identification. These changes have no material effect on current or previous financial statements.
