Dom Development S.A. Group reported safe, stable debt indicators at 31 December 2025, reflecting proper operating policy, including appropriate project launches, investment pace and controlled new real-estate purchases, and financial policy, including funding structure and sources. Management assessed the debt ratio levels as safe for operations and supportive of the Group’s creditworthiness in the financial market. Year-end 2025 debt ratios were very close to 31 December 2024 and reflected Management Board’s unchanged conservative financing approach. The largest change was net interest-bearing debt ratio, rising from an exceptionally low 1.9% in 2024 to a still low and comfortable 8.8% in 2025. The Group’s debt structure was driven mainly by bonds issued with total value of PLN 645M, including a May 2025 five-year bond issue of PLN 135M. Equity debt ratio decreased from 210.8% in 2024 to 200.8% in 2025. Equity coverage of assets was 33.2% versus 32.2%; equity debt ratio 200.8% versus 210.8%; total debt ratio 66.8% versus 67.8%; interest-bearing debt ratio 35.1% versus 35.8%; net interest-bearing debt ratio 8.8% versus 1.9%. Cash used in net interest-bearing debt includes funds on escrow accounts. The safe and stable debt indicators enabled the decision to pay, for the third time in the Company’s history, an interim dividend against profit for the current year while maintaining Management Board financial policy prioritising balanced proportions of debt, including interest-bearing debt, net assets, current assets and safe liquidity ratios. In 2025 Dom Development S.A. Group generated PLN 213M net cash flows from operating activities despite stable growth in outlays on current and future development projects adjusted to current and forecast market conditions. Management assessed this as a very good result confirming the Group’s healthy and mature business model. The decrease versus 2024 mainly reflected higher outlays on development projects and land acquisitions, including acquisition of a company currently operating as Dom Development Myśliborska Sp. z o.o., classified as an asset acquisition, namely land; total expenditure on this transaction was PLN 114M. Land prices continued to increase relative to other costs, visible also in inventories, whose carrying amount increased by PLN 778M in 2025. This reflected growth of the Group’s activity, with sales volume up 4% year on year. Cash outflows on development projects were balanced by high customer inflows generated by scheduled and timely project delivery, enabled by a record number of units sold in 2025. Balance-sheet deferred income and accruals increased by PLN 160M in 2025. Investing activities generated PLN 28M net cash outflow in 2025, mainly related to expenditure on intangible assets, chiefly software, and property, plant and equipment. Financing activities generated a significant PLN 277M net cash outflow, mainly due to payment of the remaining part of the dividend from 2024 profit and the interim dividend for 2025, totalling PLN 363M. At the same time bond debt increased by PLN 35M and bank loan debt by PLN 53M. Consolidated Group cash decreased by PLN 92M in 2025 to PLN 269M. The operating cash flow chart shows PLN 394M in 2021, PLN 378M in 2022, PLN 268M in 2023, PLN 204M in 2024 and PLN 213M in 2025.
