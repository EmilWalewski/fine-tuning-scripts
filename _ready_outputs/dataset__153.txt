Asseco Group is a capital group headed by Asseco Poland S.A., the parent, company and issuer, headquartered in Rzeszow, ul. Olchowa 14, REGON 010334578, with software production as its core business. The parent was established on 18 January 1989, changed name from Softbank Spolka Akcyjna to Asseco Poland Spolka Akcyjna on 4 January 2007, has indefinite duration, and since 1998 has been listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange main market. Asseco Poland S.A. leads an international group present through subsidiaries in 62 countries, mainly Europe, Israel and the USA, and described as one of Europe's leading software producers and the largest provider of modern IT solutions in Central and Eastern Europe. As group leader, it conducts domestic and foreign acquisitions to strengthen its European and global position, investing in IT companies that add local market and customer knowledge and access to new, unique IT solutions. The group's offering serves the financial sector, public institutions, industry, trade and services; it has proprietary products, complex IT-project experience and customers including major financial institutions, leading enterprises and public administration units. The interim condensed consolidated financial statements were prepared on a historical-cost basis, except for financial assets at fair value through profit or loss or other comprehensive income, financial liabilities at fair value through profit or loss, and investment properties at fair value. Subsidiaries in a hyperinflationary economy, Turkey, restated financial data for purchasing-power changes using the general price index. The statements assume going concern for at least 12 months from 31 March 2025; at preparation date no circumstances indicated a going-concern threat. They do not include all annual disclosures and should be read with the group's consolidated financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2024, published on 27 March 2025.
