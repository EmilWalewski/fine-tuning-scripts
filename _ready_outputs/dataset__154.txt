Dom Development S.A. Group delivered a record 2025: consolidated net profit reached 654.1M PLN, +15% versus 569.2M PLN in 2024, with prior chart values of 460.3M PLN in 2023 and 410.3M PLN in 2022; net profit per share was 25.36 PLN. Consolidated sales revenue was 3,256M PLN, +3% y/y, while gross profit on sales rose 10% to 1,121M PLN and gross margin increased to 34.4%, +2.2 p.p. y/y. The gross profit and margin improvement mainly reflected a 7% increase in finished-goods sales revenue, a lower share of service revenue, chiefly construction services for an institutional PRS buyer, and a lower share of goods revenue from land. Operating profit was 801M PLN, +15% y/y, with operating margin of 24.6%, +2.6 p.p.; management linked the result to operating efficiency, timely residential project delivery, effective sales policy, high-quality projects, stable construction costs and sustained demand. The Group recorded net sales of 4,448 units, +4% versus the previous record, the highest result in its 30-year history and described as the best annual result ever reported by a Polish retail residential developer; activity grew across all markets. Strong contracted sales not yet handed over created a high revenue-recognition potential for future periods, while provisions policy remained conservative. ROE was 38.4% despite dividend distributions of a significant part of consolidated profit for 2012-2024 and the December 2025 payment of a third historical interim dividend for the current financial year of 181M PLN; ROA remained 12.4%. Assets grew proportionally with scale, mainly due to inventories +18% y/y, comprising largely properties and design/construction outlays on sold but not yet transferred units not yet recognized as revenue. Market demand slightly revived versus 2024 as the Monetary Policy Council cut interest rates six times by a total 1.75 p.p.; rising wages improved customer credit capacity and increased sales-office traffic. The new apartment-price transparency law changed sales and marketing practices, especially for smaller developers, potentially supporting sector consolidation. Dom Development S.A. standalone results for 2025 were assessed by management as very good. In the source table units, sales revenue fell 9.8% to 1,608,862 from 1,782,984, gross profit on sales rose 3.4% to 517,918 from 501,069, EBIT rose 6.3% to 334,223 from 314,284, net profit rose 38.6% to 580,809 from 419,020, and basic EPS rose 38.5% to 22.51 from 16.25. Standalone profitability improved: gross margin 32.2% versus 28.1%, +4.1 p.p.; operating margin 20.8% versus 17.6%, +3.2 p.p.; net margin 36.1% versus 23.5%, +12.6 p.p.; ROE 39.5% versus 30.6%, +8.9 p.p.; ROA 16.4% versus 12.7%, +3.7 p.p. The standalone revenue decline mainly reflected lower service revenue after completion of a PRS institutional construction project and lower land-sales revenue; the Company delivered 1,915 retail units, -3% y/y, reducing finished-goods sales revenue by 4%, but increased sales profitability, confirming its position in higher-standard apartments. In 2025 Dom Development S.A. continued geographic diversification through subsidiaries active in the Tri-City market, Euro Styl S.A., Wroclaw, Dom Development Wroclaw Sp. z o.o., and Krakow, Dom Development Krakow Sp. z o.o. Group unit sales reached a record 4,448, +4% y/y, and dividends received from subsidiaries, including development companies and complementary construction and credit-intermediation businesses, nearly doubled to 300M PLN. The Warsaw leadership position and successful expansion into other major Polish agglomerations supported standalone net profit growth to 581M PLN and a very high 36.1% net margin. The 2025 asset position of the Company and Group remained very good and sector-distinctive: total Group assets rose 13% to 5,981M PLN from 5,289M PLN at 31 December 2024.
