XTB is presented as a FinTech technology entity operating in financial services, continuously developing highly innovative, comprehensive solutions for online transactions and investments in financial instruments, including proprietary technologies and the modern xStation trading platform, with work aimed particularly at further product-offering development. In the reporting period the technology team focused on improving security of the platform and mobile app: gradual activation of automatic 2FA login for existing and new clients began, further activities in this area were analyzed and planned, the XTB mobile app received a refreshed and more user-friendly main view, and additional instrument-view sorting functions were added in response to client demand. Tools for institutional-client service were also changed; implemented solutions enabled smoother handling of requests for trading-session configuration changes, and further work enabling smooth price-session configuration changes was planned for Q4. Ongoing technology work supports efficient operation of XTB trading systems, effective order execution, efficient new-client onboarding, and development of internal-process tools based on identified needs; key reporting-period work included system-architecture modernization to improve efficiency and transaction security. Research areas focus on functionality and security of systems, processes, and databases, with R&D also directed at new electronic trading systems. Because XTB's business strategy is based on new-technology development, the Product and Technology Department was separated within the organization; a substantial part of its personnel performs R&D work, which has significant, almost strategic impact on XTB's business, revenue generation, and ability to build and maintain a highly competitive position on the global capital market. The Product and Technology Department table presents cost series of 184.4M PLN, 165.8M PLN, 94.8M PLN, 58.4M PLN, 36.6M PLN, 27.2M PLN, 21.2M PLN, and 19.0M PLN, and headcount series of 614, 498, 429, 282, 176, 129, 116, and 106 people; headcount includes employees, mandate-contract workers, and B2B service providers. XTB's dividend policy assumes that the Management Board recommends dividends to the General Meeting based on standalone annual net profit and multiple company factors, including business outlook, future earnings, cash needs, financial position, capital-adequacy ratios, expansion plans, legal requirements, and KNF guidelines; dividend proposals are to ensure adequate capital-adequacy ratios and capital needed for Group development. The Management Board maintains its intention to recommend future dividend resolutions at 50% to 100% of the Company's standalone net profit for a given financial year, subject to the above factors; standalone net profit for the 9 months of 2025 was 462.8M PLN. XTB's total capital ratio under IFR indicates own funds relative to risk-weighted assets and whether a brokerage house can cover minimum capital requirements for market, credit, operational, and other risks; at the end of Q3 2025 the Company's total capital ratio was 169.9%. XTB invests part of its own cash in bank deposits and financial instruments, including Treasury bonds, bonds guaranteed by the State Treasury, and corporate bonds guaranteed by banks. As at 30 September 2025, total own cash and bonds in the XTB Group amounted to 1,805.8M PLN, representing 21.7% of total assets, of which 1,785.8M PLN was cash and 20.0M PLN was bonds.
