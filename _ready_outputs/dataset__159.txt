In 2025 the Management Board of XTB S.A. held 62 meetings and adopted 322 resolutions covering all areas of the XTB Group's operations. Resolutions addressed, among other matters, adoption of client documentation and numerous internal regulations and procedures. The Management Board participated in the audit firm selection process, discussed offers submitted by audit firms, and issued an assessment for the Supervisory Board and Audit Committee regarding selection of an audit firm for subsidiaries. It reviewed periodic reports from the risk management system, legal compliance system, internal audit system, reports on information technology and ICT environment security, and AML/CFT management reports. It also discussed and approved the Company's periodic reports. At monthly meetings the Management Board reviewed the status of court cases and analyzed customer complaints. In 2025 it convened one general meeting and presented the proposed agenda. The section describes competencies and professional experience of XTB S.A. Management Board members in 2025. Omar Arnaout, President of the Management Board, obtained a master's degree from the Warsaw School of Economics in Banking and Finance in 2005 and has been associated with XTB since January 2007. He served as Sales Dealer in 2007-2009, deputy director of XTB's Romanian branch in 2009-2010, deputy director of XTB's Italian branch in 2010-2012, director of the foreign branches office at Noble Securities Dom Maklerski S.A. in 2012-2014, sales director and President of the Management Board of xStore sp. z o.o. in 2014, retail sales director at XTB Limited in the United Kingdom in 2014-2016, and in 2016 became XTB regional director for Poland, Hungary, Germany and Romania. Since 10.01.2017 he has continuously served as President of the Management Board of XTB S.A. Filip Kaczmarzyk, Management Board Member for Trading, graduated from the Warsaw School of Economics in Quantitative Methods in Economics and Information Systems. He began his career at XTB S.A. in 2007 in the Trading Department as Junior Trader, became Deputy Chief Trader in April 2009, joined CFH Markets in London in November 2010 in the Customer Service Department, and from May 2011 to May 2015 cooperated with Noble Securities S.A., initially as Director of the OTC Instruments Trading Office and from November 2012 as Director of the Foreign Markets Department. He returned to XTB in May 2015 as Director of the Trading Department. Since 10.01.2017 he has continuously served as Management Board Member of XTB S.A.
