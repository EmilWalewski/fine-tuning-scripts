Dom Development S.A.’s Supervisory Board, based on Audit Committee recommendations, selected PricewaterhouseCoopers Polska sp. z o.o. Audyt sp.k. in Warsaw to audit the annual standalone and consolidated financial statements for the years ending 31 December 2025, 2026 and 2027 and to review the interim standalone and consolidated financial statements for the six-month periods ending 30 June 2025, 2026 and 2027; a separate June 2025 recommendation also covered the audit of the interim condensed standalone financial statements for the six months ended 30 June 2025. The agreement with PwC was signed on 22 July 2025 and amended on 23 July 2025 for the period required to perform the relevant reviews and audits; PwC is registered as an authorized audit firm under number 144. The company disclosed prior use of PwC and related entities for training, legal and permitted non-audit services, and noted that PwC or related entities audited financial statements for 2018-2024 and interim condensed financial statements for the six-month periods ended 30 June 2023, 2024 and 2025, while auditor remuneration for 2025 and 2024 is presented in note 7.53 to the 2025 consolidated financial statements. Management, citing the Supervisory Board’s statement, confirmed that the 2025 consolidated auditor selection complied with applicable rules, that the audit firm and engagement team met impartiality and independence requirements, that rotation and cooling-off regulations were observed, and that the company maintained policies on auditor selection and permitted non-audit services. Separately, on 3 September 2025 the Supervisory Board, based on an Audit Committee recommendation dated 27 August 2025, selected Ernst & Young Audyt Polska sp. z o.o. sp.k. in Warsaw to provide assurance over the Group’s 2025 sustainability reporting; the assurance agreement was signed on 22 December 2025 and the fee was 0.190 M PLN. EY had previously audited standalone and consolidated financial statements for 2010-2017 and provided consulting, digital reporting, CSRD preparation and training services in earlier years. The Management Board’s report on the activities of Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group in 2025 was prepared and approved by the Management Board on 16 March 2026, with the Board stating that it presents a true picture of the development, achievements and position of the Company and Group, including key threats and risks. The approving Management Board members were Mikołaj Konopka as President, Monika Dobosz as Vice President, and Justyna Wilk, Grzegorz Smoliński and Terry R. Roydon as Management Board Members.
