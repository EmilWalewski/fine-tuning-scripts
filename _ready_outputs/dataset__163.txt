Asseco Poland’s public-sector work in Q1 2025 included multiple contracts for ZUS: support, operation and maintenance of the Comprehensive IT System KSI ZUS under a 2022 agreement amended in 2023-2025; a 2022 framework agreement for KSI ZUS software modification and expansion, with 20 executive agreements active at end-March 2025, including 3 concluded in Q1 2025; a 2018 framework agreement for KSI ZUS modification and expansion, with 2 executive agreements from earlier reporting periods active at end-March 2025; IT preparation of KSI and the PUE Portal for the new supporting benefit for disabled persons and carers under a 2023 agreement amended in 2024-2025, “Active Parent” benefit applications under an August 2024 agreement amended in 2024 and 2025, “Contribution Holidays” applications for exemption from paying contributions for a specified month under an August 2024 agreement amended in 2024 and 2025, and “Widow’s Pension” concurrence-of-benefits applications under a December 2024 agreement; adaptation of KSI ZUS and PUE Portal to health insurance contribution changes under a January 2025 agreement; and support, operation and maintenance of the ZUS ICT Security System under a November 2024 agreement in consortium with NASK. In April 2025 Asseco Poland signed a new 4-year agreement with ZUS for support, operation and maintenance of KSI ZUS after completion of the current 2022 agreement. For the Ministry of Justice, Asseco performed contracts for construction, maintenance and development of the National Register of Debtors system KRZ, improving restructuring and bankruptcy proceedings, under a 2020 contract amended in 2020-2021 and 2023; development and maintenance of Electronic Land and Mortgage Registers EKW under a January 2024 contract; maintenance and development of the National Court Register KRS under a 2023 contract; and maintenance and development of the Random Case Allocation System SLPS under a 2023 contract. In Q1 2025 the company executed a 2-year 2024 agreement with the National Support Centre for Agriculture for technical assistance and maintenance services for Integrated IT System software. For the Chief Inspectorate of Road Transport GITD, Asseco continued a 6-year contract effective from March 2023 for maintenance and development of the CPD CANARD ICT system, whose central processing system supports detected road-offence handling, related case processing, ticketing, payment and debt-collection processes; since June 2023 it has also performed three 5-year support agreements for technical infrastructure elements and third-party software used in CPD CANARD. Under a separate 2022 agreement for GITD, Asseco has been building, maintaining and developing Central Register of Violations 2.0, which replaced Central Register of Violations 1.0 in March 2024 and supports road and enterprise inspection services and resulting cases under the Road Transport Act. For the Finance Ministry sector, Asseco maintained and developed customs and tax systems under the EU-co-financed Electronic Tax and Customs Services Platform PUESC programme. A key project with T4B Sp. z o.o. was maintenance and development of Digital Border, an advanced system supporting efficient service for persons and entities at border crossings through automatic data exchange, including with the Border Guard System, automatic vehicle identification and traffic control; Digital Border operates in 53 locations covering road and railway border crossings plus border air and maritime traffic, with maintenance and development support to be provided until end-2025. In Q1 2025 Asseco started ODAT work for mandatory advance notification in outbound road freight traffic, intended to give National Revenue Administration clients, especially carriers, forwarders and drivers, a tool for reserving export border-clearance slots for trucks at the EU external border; e-Notification will be made available through Digital Border development. In consortium with Pentacomp, Asseco executed for the Ministry of Finance represented by the Tax Administration Chamber in Krakow a contract to develop, modernise and maintain components of the Tax and Customs Information System SISC in Goods Trade with Third Countries and Excise Movements. For the same client, under a 2021 base agreement and a 2023 supplementary agreement, Asseco expanded, modernised and developed ZEFIR2, which handles collection and financial settlement of customs and tax receivables for the State Treasury and EU budget; in Q1 it also performed a 21-month July 2024 contract for ZEFIR2 maintenance services. In both contracts Asseco expands and maintains systems it previously built, mainly to adapt them to new EU Customs Code regulations and national rules, including excise tax and VAT settlement changes. With the Tax Administration Chamber in Szczecin, Asseco implemented PKWD Single Window, the Data Coordination and Exchange Platform, designed to exchange data between the National Revenue Administration, entrepreneurs or clients importing or exporting through Polish customs clearances, and inspections checking goods before release for circulation; under a 2024 annex, work adapted the system to the latest EU Customs Single Window-CERTEX specification enabling verification of required EU certificates. Asseco also expanded functionality of its PLANUS customs-procedure system, which automates and integrates with the National Revenue Administration, supports import, export, transit and special customs procedures, rapid declaration processing, real-time reporting and secure data exchange. In healthcare, Asseco Poland was one of the largest IT solution and service providers, cooperating with over 450 hospitals in Poland and serving 9.2 thousand locations and 39.7 thousand users in medical offices, medium and small clinics and medical practices, about 40% of that market. In Q1 2025 Asseco acted as main contractor or subcontractor in tender-based hospital and medical-centre projects covering delivery, implementation and production support for launching e-services or applications across Poland; delivered solutions improve appointment registration, patient access to test results and online medical records, fully integrated with hospital HIS and unit-management ERP systems. Maintenance agreements for already implemented hospital systems generated over 50% of revenues in the healthcare-provider area. Asseco supplied the Bank Krwi IT system to 18 Blood Donation and Blood Treatment Centres and the Military Blood Donation and Blood Treatment Centre; the system supports donor registration, blood collection organisation, laboratory service, production and distribution of blood components, donor and patient safety, and handling of more than one blood bank within one hospital. In consortium with Kamsoft, Asseco continued the 2019 National Health Fund contract for maintenance and development of the NFZ Activity Support IT System SIWDzNFZ. Asseco continued developing hospital, clinic and medical-practice solutions and, in Q1 2025, the Asseco Medical Patient Desktop application within Asseco Medical Management Solutions AMMS, giving doctors one-screen access to current patient disease history, test results, procedures and health-status data without browsing multiple AMMS modules; it also developed patient-facing functionalities.
