The Group is exposed to credit risk, liquidity risk, and market risk from financial instruments. The note presents abbreviated information on exposures, objectives, principles, risk measurement and management procedures, and capital management; full risk-management description is in the latest annual consolidated financial statements. The Parent Company Management Board is responsible for establishing and supervising Group risk management. Risk management principles are designed to identify and analyze exposures, define adequate limits and controls, and monitor risks and limit alignment; policies and systems are regularly reviewed for market-condition and business changes. Through training, adopted standards, and management procedures, the Group seeks a mobilizing and constructive control environment in which employees understand their roles and responsibilities. Credit risk is the risk of financial loss if a business partner, debtor, or other financial-instrument counterparty fails to meet contractual obligations; it mainly relates to Group loans, receivables from services, and acquired debt portfolios. Financial asset carrying amounts equal maximum credit-risk exposure.
