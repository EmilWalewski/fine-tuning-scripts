XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025 Consolidated Annual Report, Section 35 (Risk Management), detailing Group own funds, the risk management framework, and fair value disclosures. Group own funds table (in PLN thousands) reports Common Equity Tier I capital before deductions of 1,336,899k PLN (1,336.9M PLN) at 31.12.2025 versus 1,122,449k PLN (1,122.4M PLN) at 31.12.2024; Additional Tier I capital nil in both periods; deductions from CET I of (15,919)k PLN ((15.9M PLN)) at 31.12.2025 versus (11,352)k PLN ((11.4M PLN)) at 31.12.2024; Total Group own funds 1,320,980k PLN (1,321.0M PLN) at 31.12.2025 versus 1,111,097k PLN (1,111.1M PLN) at 31.12.2024 — a year-over-year increase of approximately 18.9% in CET I capital and total own funds. No breach of statutory capital adequacy norms occurred during the reporting periods. The Group operates a risk management system comprising policies, procedures, mechanisms, and tools matched to risk materiality. Principal objectives: identification and materiality determination of individual risk types; proper measurement/estimation of risk levels (including hard-to-measure risks); risk-level control via limit monitoring and response to limit/warning-level breaches; and support of business objectives through risk control and risk-appetite compliance. The system is founded on internal regulations "Risk Management System at XTB S.A." and "Risk Management Strategy at XTB S.A.", elaborated by further detailed regulations per risk category.

Risk governance follows the market-standard Three Lines model, dividing responsibility among business, control functions, and audit. First Line (Trading, Marketing & Sales, Customer Service, IT) — the "risk owners" making operational decisions and identifying risks in their processes. Second Line (Risk Control Department, Legal & Compliance Department) — independent units monitoring and supporting the first line, creating policies, setting limits, and ensuring regulatory compliance across multiple jurisdictions, assessing whether operations remain within risk-appetite boundaries. Third Line (Internal Audit Department) — independent monitoring and periodic assessment of the entire risk management and internal control system, reporting directly to the Management Board and Supervisory Board to reinforce independence. The Management Board is responsible at the strategic level for setting and monitoring risk management policy. The parent entity operates a Risk Management Committee composed of Supervisory Board members; its tasks include drafting the brokerage house's risk appetite document, opining on the Management Board's risk management strategy, supporting the Supervisory Board in oversight of strategy execution, and verifying the remuneration policy and its implementation for alignment of the remuneration system with risk, capital, liquidity profile, and the probability and horizon of income.

The Risk Control Department and Legal & Compliance Department hold special daily roles. The Risk Control Department implements and operates the organizational risk management system, supports the Management Board in shaping, reviewing, and updating risk management principles, monitors system adequacy and effectiveness, identifies and monitors risks of the Group's own investments, determines the total capital requirement, and estimates internal capital; it is headed by a Director who is simultaneously a permanent Management Board member, strengthening the risk function and embedding the risk perspective in strategic decisions. The Legal & Compliance Department handles legal and compliance risks; the Compliance Officer (Inspektor Nadzoru) proactively manages non-compliance risk, providing advisory support on the regulatory environment (including KNF and ESMA requirements), ensuring brokerage activity complies with law and internal regulations, examining compliance-oversight adequacy and effectiveness, conducting investigations, and monitoring sensitive areas such as anti-money-laundering (AML/CFT) and conflict-of-interest management. This department is managed and supervised by the Management Board Member for Legal Affairs.

The risk management process involves Group bodies and all employees, supporting both strategy-setting and daily operations and varying by risk type, with common elements: identification of threats and risk factors; risk analysis and measurement via analytical decomposition and valuation across probability and impact scales using quantitative and qualitative methods; risk assessment against Company-defined acceptable thresholds; risk treatment via controls, actions, or programs to keep risk within appetite; risk monitoring by both owners (mainly First Line) and Second Line functions (Risk Control and/or Legal & Compliance); and reporting whose scope and frequency reflect risk magnitude and type, with all material periodic risk-management, internal-control, and internal-audit reports discussed at Management Board and Supervisory Board meetings.

Fair value (Section 35.1) is defined as the price receivable on sale of an asset or payable on transfer of a liability in an orderly transaction between market participants at the measurement date. Section 35.1.1 (comparison of carrying and fair value): fair value of cash is estimated as approximating its carrying value; fair value of financial assets measured at fair value through profit or loss, liabilities to clients, and other liabilities is estimated as approximating their carrying value owing to the short maturities of these balance-sheet items.
