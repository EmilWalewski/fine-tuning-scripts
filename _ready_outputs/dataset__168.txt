LPP SA Capital Group interim condensed financial report for the period ended 31 October 2025 (nine months from 1 February to 31 October 2025). Bank credits and liabilities: during the balance-sheet period the Group signed and drew no new credit agreement; at the balance-sheet date it held investment-credit debt of 196M PLN. Total Group liabilities (including trade) at 31 October 2025 stood at 5.7bn PLN, practically unchanged versus 31 January 2025. The largest portion of trade liabilities was payables to suppliers; the Group uses supplier-financing programs (reverse factoring) offered by HSBC Polska SA, Santander Polska SA, Bank Pekao SA, PKO BP SA, ING Bank Slaski SA and BNP Paribas, under which factoring banks settle supplier obligations per pre-agreed terms after invoice presentation. Total trade liabilities under this arrangement at 31 October 2025 were 3.8bn PLN (total limit 6.2bn PLN).

Dividends: on 11 July 2025 the LPP SA General Meeting (Resolution No. 19) allocated part of profit for the year ended 31 January 2025 to a dividend totaling 1,225M PLN, i.e. 660 PLN per share, split into two parts. An interim dividend advance of 612M PLN (330 PLN per share) was paid 28 April 2025; the second tranche of 612M PLN (330 PLN per share) was paid 31 October 2025; dividend record date set at 10 October 2025. Comparative period: on 12 July 2024 the General Meeting (Resolution No. 21) allocated profit for the year ended 31 January 2024 to a dividend totaling 1,132M PLN, i.e. 610 PLN per share, in two parts; advance of 529M PLN (285 PLN per share) paid 30 April 2024; second tranche of 603M PLN (325 PLN per share) paid 30 October 2024; record date 11 October 2024.

Contingent liabilities and assets: in Q3 2025 Group companies used bank guarantees securing rental payment for company stores, office and warehouse space. Neither the Issuer nor a subsidiary granted credit/loan guarantees or sureties to a single entity exceeding 10.0% of the Issuer's equity. At 31 October 2025 total bank guarantees issued on LPP's order and responsibility were 387M PLN, comprising: guarantees securing contracts concluded by LPP SA of 90M PLN; guarantees securing contracts of consolidated related entities of 268M PLN; guarantees securing warehouse/office lease agreements concluded by LPP SA of 29M PLN. The Company also received guarantees securing counterparty payment, valued at 21M PLN at 31 October 2025. The value of sureties granted by LPP SA at 31 October 2025 was 275M PLN; the Management Board deems outflow of these off-balance-sheet/contingent amounts of negligible probability.

Related-party transactions (per IAS 24): related parties include key management personnel of the LPP Group and their close family members, and entities controlled or significantly influenced by them. Key management comprises Management Board and Supervisory Board members of the parent. Short-term benefits of parent Management Board members paid 01.02–31.10.2025 totaled 4,298 thousand PLN (4.3M PLN), versus 4,303 thousand PLN (4.3M PLN) in the nine months ended 31 October 2024. Short-term benefits of parent Supervisory Board members paid 01.02–31.10.2025 totaled 281 thousand PLN (0.3M PLN), versus 81 thousand PLN (0.1M PLN) in the comparative period.

Litigation: in Q3 2025 no material court, arbitration or administrative proceedings concerning LPP or subsidiary obligations/receivables were pending, and no material litigation settlements occurred. LPP SA is party to an administrative proceeding by the Polish Financial Supervision Authority (KNF) regarding a potential administrative penalty under Art. 96(1e) of the 29 July 2005 Public Offering Act, in connection with suspected breach of Art. 56(1)(2)(a) over improper disclosure of periodic information relating to the consolidated annual reports for financial years 2021 and 2022; the Company and Board members actively participate. A separate explanatory proceeding initiated by the Office of Competition and Consumer Protection (UOKiK) examines whether eco-related marketing breached collective consumer interests; part of coordinated European antitrust action on clothing-industry use of ECO labeling. LPP SA submitted extensive explanations and evidence; no charges currently raised; if practices were attributed, the maximum statutory monetary penalty is up to 10% of turnover achieved in the financial year preceding imposition; the Office has shown no activity for many months.

Supplementary information: no issuance, redemption or repayment of debt or equity securities occurred during the reporting period.

Post-balance-sheet events: on 28 November 2025 LPP SA received a letter from shareholders of Re Trading (sold in 2022) regarding investors' inability to pay remaining purchase-price installments for shares and to settle the Russian company's trade liabilities for purchased goods (Current Report RB 23/2025). Per IAS 10 para. 8 (Events after the Reporting Period), LPP SA treated this as an adjusting event in the interim consolidated financial statements for Q3 2025; detailed changes described in Note 6.

Report signed at Gdansk by Marcin Piechocki (Vice-President of the Management Board) and Mikolaj Wezdecki (Vice-President of the Management Board).
