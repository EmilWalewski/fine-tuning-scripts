Statements prepared on a going-concern basis with no adjustments for alternative asset/liability valuation or classification. No changes to estimates or assumptions occurred in the current period relative to those adopted and disclosed in the year ended 31 January 2025. Neither changes in accounting policies nor error corrections occurred.

Seasonality affects the entire apparel market in Poland and abroad: gross margin on new-collection sales at regular prices typically exceeds clearance-sale margins, producing inter-quarter margin disparities (highest in Q2 and Q4, lowest in Q1 and Q3); the Group changed its financial year to align with the collection calendar, neutralizing the impact of clearance and seasonality on quarterly margins.
