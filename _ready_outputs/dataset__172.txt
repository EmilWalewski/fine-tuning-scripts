Section 7 "Additional Information and Notes" of the consolidated financial statements of Grupa Kapitałowa Dom Development S.A. for the year ended 31 December 2025. Certain Group entities maintain statutory books under Polish GAAP (Accounting Act of 29 September 1994, as amended); the consolidated statements incorporate adjustments not recorded in those entities' books to achieve IFRS conformity. Accounting policies are consistent with those applied for the year ended 31 December 2024, except for adoption of amendments to IAS 21 (Effects of Changes in Foreign Exchange Rates), effective for annual periods beginning 1 January 2025. The IAS 21 amendments, issued by the IASB in August 2023, assist entities in assessing whether a currency is exchangeable into another and in estimating the spot exchange rate when a currency lacks exchangeability, and introduce additional disclosures on how an alternative rate is determined. Management analyzed these amendments and concluded they have no material impact on the Group's financial position, results, or scope of disclosures.

Published standards/interpretations not yet effective and not early-adopted by the Group: (1) Amendments to IFRS 10 and IAS 28 on the sale or contribution of assets between an investor and its associates or joint ventures, resolving the existing inconsistency between the two standards; accounting treatment depends on whether the non-monetary assets sold/contributed constitute a "business" (full gain/loss recognized if a business; partial gain/loss limited to other investors' interests if not) — EU endorsement of this amendment is deferred. (2) IFRS 18 Presentation and Disclosure in Financial Statements, issued by the IASB in April 2024, replacing IAS 1, effective 1 January 2027; principal changes concern the statement of profit or loss, required disclosures on performance measures, and aggregation/disaggregation of information. (3) Annual IFRS improvements amending IFRS 1, IFRS 7, IFRS 9, IFRS 10, and IAS 7, providing clarifications on recognition and measurement, effective for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2026. (4) Amendments to IFRS 9 and IFRS 7 on classification and measurement of financial instruments, issued by the IASB in May 2024, clarifying derecognition/recognition timing of certain financial assets and liabilities (with relief for liabilities settled via electronic payment transfer systems), adding guidance on the SPPI assessment, introducing new disclosures for instruments whose contractual terms may alter cash flows, and updating disclosures for equity instruments measured at FVOCI; effective for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2026. Management is assessing the impact of these standards but does not expect a material effect on the consolidated financial statements in the period of first application.

Note 7.4 Material Accounting Policies — Consolidation: The consolidated statements comprise the standalone statements of Dom Development S.A. and its subsidiaries, prepared for the same reporting period using consistent accounting policies after IFRS-conformity adjustments. All significant intra-group balances, transactions, and unrealized intra-group gains are fully eliminated; unrealized losses are eliminated unless they evidence impairment. Subsidiaries are consolidated from the date control is obtained until control ceases; control exists where the parent holds, directly or indirectly through subsidiaries, more than half of voting rights (unless demonstrably not constituting control) or can govern the entity's financial and operating policies. Changes in the parent's ownership interest not resulting in loss of control are recognized as equity transactions, with carrying-value adjustments of controlling and non-controlling interests; any difference between the non-controlling interest adjustment and the fair value of consideration paid/received is recognized in equity and attributed to owners of the parent. Investments in associates and joint ventures — entities over which the parent exercises significant influence (directly or via subsidiaries) without control — are accounted for using the equity method; such entities apply Polish Accounting Act policies, with adjustments to IFRS conformity made before computing the share of net assets. These investments are carried at cost plus subsequent changes in the parent's share of net assets, less any impairment write-downs; the Group's share of profit/loss is reflected in consolidated profit or loss, and its share of changes in the investee's other comprehensive income is recognized in the Group's OCI. Impairment of associate/joint-venture investments is assessed when indicators suggest impairment or that a prior-year write-down is no longer required. Property, Plant and Equipment: stated at cost less accumulated depreciation (except land) and accumulated impairment write-downs; cost includes the cost of replacing component parts when incurred, if material; depreciation is charged on a straight-line basis over the asset's useful life.
