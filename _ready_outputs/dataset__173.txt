Excerpt from the XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025 Consolidated Annual Report, covering Supervisory Board / Audit Committee member biographies and corporate governance disclosures on auditor independence. Grzegorz Grabowicz: served as financial controller and CFO, held positions on management boards and supervisory boards of companies across varied business profiles, including entities listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange (GPW); his tenure within XTB built sector-specific knowledge of the Company's operations. From 2014 to October 2018 he was a member of the Supervisory Board of Skarbiec Holding S.A.; from October 2017 to August 2020 a member of the Supervisory Board of Develia S.A. (formerly LC Corp S.A.); from June 2018 to May 2019 a member of the Supervisory Board of Medicalgorithmics S.A.; since May 2020 a member of the Supervisory Board of PRAGMAGO S.A.; and from 2 January 2019 to 5 September 2025 a Management Board member of Mabion S.A. These accumulated functions provided finance and management experience enabling him to meet the independence criteria referenced in the relevant Act (statutory independence requirements).

Ewa Stefaniak: member of the Supervisory Board and Audit Committee; holds higher education and experience in accounting and bookkeeping, plus a qualification certificate authorizing the keeping of accounting books issued by the Minister of Finance of the Republic of Poland. Professionally associated with the Company for 13 years, including service as a Management Board member, affording detailed familiarity with the financial sector and XTB's brokerage activities. During 1991-1995 she served as chief accountant at Kontrakt sp. z o.o., Hotel and Lodging sp. z o.o., and Contingsult sp. z o.o. From September 1996 to May 2006 she worked at ING Investment Management (Polska) S.A. as chief accountant / portfolio controller. From September 2006 to October 2019 she was employed at X-Trade Brokers Dom Maklerski S.A. (now XTB S.A.) as chief accountant. During 2008-2015 she held the position of Management Board member at XTB S.A.

Bartosz Zabłocki: member of the Supervisory Board and Committee; holds higher legal education. Has served as a member of the Company's Supervisory Board for over 10 years. From 2005 to 2022 he was a partner at the law firm Kancelaria Prawna P. Grzelka & Wspólnicy sp. k.; during 2012-2022 a Management Board member of XX ZW Investment Group S.A.; since March 2021 a Management Board member of Kamienica sp. z o.o.; and since November 2023 a partner in Contigate sp. z o.o.
