Operational and growth highlights for 2025 (per CEO letter and introduction): record breakthrough year reflecting prior years' strategic decisions in scale, growth pace, and rising global client trust. Over 864 thousand new clients joined the community. The active investor base on the XTB app and platform exceeded 1.19 million, reaching a record 1.19M active clients (+69.7% versus 2024). The total investor community grew to over 2.1 million persons at year-end, up 59.0% year-over-year. Operating revenues reached a record PLN 2.15 billion. Value of client-entrusted assets exceeded PLN 45.8 billion at end-2025.

Strategic product developments in 2025 emphasized making investing mainstream and broadening the product offering with education and support for new investors as priorities. In France, XTB introduced the PEA (Plan d'Épargne en Actions) investment account, opening access to long-term investment in European equities with tax benefits, significantly increasing offering attractiveness in one of Europe's most important markets. In the United Kingdom, XTB enabled clients to transfer ISA accounts to XTB, allowing investors to move portfolios from other institutions without losing tax benefits—deemed key to building durable client relationships. The Group continuously develops its mobile application and investment platform, focusing on user experience refinement, functionality enrichment, and introduction of new investment products. Systematic user growth is presented as evidence of rising XTB brand awareness and product attractiveness.
