XTB S.A. Capital Group consolidated annual report 2025, ESG/sustainability due diligence disclosure (Section 4.6 "Statement on due diligence", GOV-4). XTB, a financial institution listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange (GPW) since 2016, has implemented due diligence processes for years via its Legal and Compliance department, driven by its regulated financial-institution status, business development needs, responsible organizational culture, and competitive advantage. The Group has conducted non-financial (ESG) reporting since 2022, progressively building awareness, ordering processes, and achieving successive milestones. Due diligence is defined as a continuous sequence of actions for monitoring, improving, and verifying the effectiveness of implemented solutions, operating in parallel with the business throughout its functioning and supporting development by enhancing strategic and operational processes. The Group conducts all activity in line with the United Nations (UN) Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights and the OECD Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises, and complies with recommendations of the Polish Financial Supervision Authority (KNF) and local market regulators, ensuring respect for human rights, fair practices, and transparency.

Table 37 maps the core elements of the due diligence process to ESG areas and report chapters: embedding due diligence in corporate governance, strategy, and the business model (Ch. 1.3 Sustainability management; 1.6 Material impacts, opportunities and risks; 3.1 Our employees; 3.2 Consumers and end users); cooperation with affected stakeholders (Ch. 1.3; 1.5 Key stakeholders; 3.2; 1.6; 2.1.1 XTB Group Climate Policy; 4.3 Corporate and ethics culture; 4.5 Supplier relationship management); identification and assessment of negative impacts on people and environment (Ch. 1.6; 3.1; 3.2); taking action to limit identified negative impacts (Ch. 2.1.1 Climate Policy); and monitoring effectiveness of efforts and communicating relevant information (Ch. 2.1.2 Fuel and energy consumption; 2.1.3 Greenhouse gas emissions; 3.1.5 Policies and regulations).

In early 2024 these actions enabled the Group to conduct a Double Materiality Assessment and identify key ESG impacts, opportunities, and risks; it reviewed policies, identified areas for further improvement and negative impacts it intends to manage and mitigate, with stated intent for continued due diligence improvement in subsequent reporting periods. The due diligence framework is determined by: the XTB Group Business Strategy together with the ESG/Sustainability Strategy, treated as key, complementary documents whose mutually reinforcing objectives support ESG-criteria integration for stakeholder benefit; umbrella governance documents comprising the Code of Ethics, Anti-Mobbing and Anti-Discrimination Policy, Diversity Policy, AML/CFT Policy, and Anti-Corruption Policy, establishing XTB's operating principles and commitment to business ethics, improvement, and condemnation of unfair practices, with zero tolerance for human-rights violations, unethical conduct, corruption, or bribery; an internal and external breach-reporting procedure offering stakeholders commonly available, fully anonymous communication channels, with reporters granted statutory whistleblower protection prohibiting any retaliatory actions; and other procedures, policies, and instructions.
