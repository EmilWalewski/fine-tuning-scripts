XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025 — corporate governance and Supervisory Board disclosures. Ownership/control: Jakub Zabłocki, member of the Supervisory Board, directly controls and holds 81.97% of the share capital of XX ZW Investment Group S.A. (registered in Luxembourg), which in turn commands 35.78% of total shares and votes at the General Meeting, equating to 29.33% of the Company's shares; Jakub Zabłocki holds no direct shares in the Company. Remaining supervisory persons held no Company shares and no shares/interests in related entities. Members of the dominant company's Supervisory Board did not concurrently hold positions in the governing bodies of subordinated entities during the reporting period.

Supervisory Board committees: Audit Committee; Remuneration Committee; Risk Management Committee. By collegial resolution, the Supervisory Board itself performs the duties of the Remuneration Committee and the Risk Management Committee. Since 13 October 2017 the Audit Committee operates as a separate committee; prior to that date its tasks were performed collegially by the Supervisory Board.

Audit Committee composition (1 January–31 December 2025), no changes occurred during the period: Grzegorz Grabowicz — Chairman (Independence YES, Financial Knowledge YES, Industry Knowledge YES); Ewa Stefaniak — Member (Independence YES, Financial Knowledge YES, Industry Knowledge YES); Bartosz Zabłocki — Member (Independence NO, Financial Knowledge YES, Industry Knowledge YES). In 2025 the Audit Committee held 10 meetings, of which six included review of the sustainability area; 22 resolutions were adopted at meetings and by circular procedure. Average attendance of Audit Committee members was 100%; individual attendance — Grzegorz Grabowicz 100%, Bartosz Zabłocki 100%, Ewa Stefaniak 100%.

Audit Committee responsibilities (per text) include: monitoring the financial-reporting and sustainability-reporting processes; monitoring effectiveness of compliance (SLC), internal control (SIC), internal audit (SIA) and risk management (SRM) systems, including for financial and sustainability reporting, and receiving/assessing reports on system effectiveness; monitoring the conduct of statutory audit activities, including findings from inspections at the audit firm; controlling and monitoring independence of the statutory auditor and audit firm, particularly where non-audit services are provided; presenting audit-firm offers to the Supervisory Board and recommending selection of the firm for financial-statement audits and sustainability-reporting assurance; informing the Supervisory Board of audit/assurance results and explaining the audit's contribution to reporting reliability and the Committee's role; assessing auditor independence and approving permitted non-audit services; developing and regularly reviewing the audit-firm selection policy and the policy on permitted non-audit services provided by the audit firm, its network members and related entities; determining the firm-selection procedure and reviewing it regularly; presenting recommendations on auditor/audit-firm selection; submitting recommendations to ensure financial-reporting reliability; and adopting the prior-year Audit Committee activity report.

Independence criteria: in 2025 the Audit Committee members meeting the independence conditions under Art. 129(3) of the Act of 11 May 2017 on Statutory Auditors, Audit Firms and Public Oversight (Journal of Laws 2017, item 1089, the "Auditors Act") were Grzegorz Grabowicz (Chairman) and Ewa Stefaniak (Audit Committee member from 27 November 2024).

2025 Audit Committee meeting topics included: discussion of quarterly reports; opinion on the annual financial statements with a recommendation to the Supervisory Board; review of internal-control, risk-management and internal-audit system reports and hearings with responsible persons; meetings with representatives of the audit firm conducting the financial-statement audit; adoption of the 2024 Audit Committee activity report; verification of Audit Committee policies, procedures and rules; verification of audit-firm independence and remuneration; review of the sustainability area; and adoption of the Committee's work plan for the following year.
