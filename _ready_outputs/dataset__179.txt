LPP Group Q3 2025 (III kwartał 2025) results were materially impacted by a one-off event within other operating income and expenses. Following notification from shareholders of Re Trading (who acquired the Russian-business shares in 2022) regarding liquidity risk and inability of investors and their purchasing agents to settle obligations, the Company recognized an impairment write-down on the entire outstanding receivables totaling PLN 789M (PLN 548M on goods receivables from purchasing agents; PLN 241M on receivables from the sale of Re Trading shares to investors), booked in Q3 2025 within other operating expenses in the statement of comprehensive income. As a result, reported Q3 2025 operating profit reached PLN 388M, down 46.9% versus PLN 731M a year earlier, with EBIT margin at 6.3%. Adjusted (cleaned of the write-down), Q3 2025 operating profit was PLN 1.2bn, up 61.0% versus PLN 731M, with adjusted EBIT margin of 19.2%.

Financial income in Q3 2025 comprised valuation of fund participation units and interest earned; financial costs reflected higher year-over-year interest on loans and lease liabilities plus FX differences, yielding a negative net financial balance. Consequently, LPP Group recorded a Q3 2025 net loss of PLN 16M versus net profit of PLN 577M a year earlier, with net margin of -0.3% (vs 11.1%). On an adjusted basis, Q3 2025 net profit reached PLN 800M, up 38.7% versus PLN 577M, with adjusted net margin of 13.0%.

Operational/expansion milestones: In November Reserved opened its 18th store in Germany, a 2,748 m2 unit (women's, men's, children's) at Westfield Centro Oberhausen, the country's largest shopping-entertainment center. In early August Sinsay opened its first store in Tashkent, Uzbekistan, the Group's 43rd market. In early October the first Sinsay opened in Baku, Azerbaijan, the Group's 44th market, with further openings planned. In Q3, in Turkestan (Kazakhstan), Sinsay opened its 2,000th store, surpassing the 2,000-store milestone under the Group's Sinsay growth strategy. In December the brand opened its first store in Moldova, the 45th market in LPP's portfolio, completing the Group's regional coverage; further Moldova openings are planned from northern Briceni to southern Cahul. LPP Logistics launched a highly automated e-commerce warehouse near Bucharest, Romania, of 65,000 m2, capable of fulfilling over 80,000 orders daily, serving Romania, Bulgaria, Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Greece and Hungary. The Company signed a financing agreement with a consortium of Polish and international banks totaling approximately PLN 13.5bn, intended primarily to refinance existing debt, finance/refinance Group capital expenditures, and finance suppliers—responding to accelerated sales-network expansion, increased supplier financing, and logistics-infrastructure investment under the Group's growth strategy.
