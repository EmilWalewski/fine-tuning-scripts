Dom Development Group describes compliance as a key support mechanism for strategic execution, focused on preventing legal risk arising from non-compliance with laws, internal regulations and adopted standards. The compliance system is continuously adjusted to regulatory changes and covers internal rules concerning employee, ethical, social, environmental, corporate and whistleblowing matters. Compliance risk management is defined as managing the risk that the Company or Group may fail to adapt to or breach legal provisions, internal regulations or standards of conduct, potentially leading to legal sanctions, financial losses, reputational harm or loss of credibility. The Group seeks adequate and uniform compliance standards across its entities while allowing local systems to reflect each entity’s profile and scale. A compliance team composed of local representatives of Group companies supports business areas, assesses and measures compliance risk, implements mitigating actions, shares best practices and provides advice aligned with market practice and legal requirements. Group entities must report identified compliance risks to the Company’s Chief Risk and Compliance Officer, who coordinates such risks at Group level; entities may either appoint a local risk and compliance coordinator or assign this function to the Group-level officer. The compliance team, supervised by the Chief Risk and Compliance Officer, analyzes reported information to assess risks at company and Group level, prepare management reports for Group company management boards and the Company’s Supervisory Board, develop good practices, issue guidance and recommendations, and provide substantive compliance support. Compliance risks are identified by business process owners and through registers of conflicts of interest, gifts and irregularities, then monitored through risk-management registers, regulatory monitoring, staff awareness-building and consistent Group standards. Responses include accepting risks related to legal and regulatory changes, limiting risks by adjusting procedures and processes, reviewing and designing internal regulations for compliance, and avoiding risks through market best practices and prevention of non-compliant conduct. The internal audit function is independent from operational functions and objective, with the purpose of adding value and improving Dom Development Group’s operations through systematic assessment of risk management, controls and governance. Its main responsibilities include preparing and executing an internal audit plan, issuing recommendations and verifying corrective actions. The audit plan is based on annual risk identification and assessment across Group activity areas, then reviewed by the Management Board and Audit Committee. Implementation of audit recommendations rests with responsible Management Board members or Group Directors, while Internal Audit confirms completion based on information from relevant units. The Assurance Lead supervises internal audit, reporting functionally to the Audit Committee and organizationally to the President of the Management Board of Dom Development S.A., supporting independence and objectivity. Internal audit reports to the Management Board and Audit Committee on audit plan execution, audit results and recommendation implementation status, and operates in line with the Internal Audit Strategy, standards and good practices of the Institute of Internal Auditors and the Best Practice for GPW Listed Companies. As of 31 December 2025, Group companies were not party to any material court proceedings.
