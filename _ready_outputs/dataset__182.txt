KRUK Capital Group half-year report (Skrócone śródroczne skonsolidowane sprawozdanie finansowe), covering the consolidated statement of changes in equity and consolidated statement of cash flows; all figures in thousands PLN (w tysiącach złotych), normalized below to millions.

Consolidated statement of changes in equity, period ending 30 June 2025 (unaudited). Retained earnings (zyski zatrzymane) opened at 4,009.4M PLN and closed at 4,244.2M PLN, driven by net profit for the period of 584.0M PLN and reduced by a dividend payment (wypłata dywidendy) of 349.3M PLN. Other reserve capitals rose from 188.7M to 199.6M PLN on 10.9M PLN of share-based payments (płatności w formie akcji). FX translation differences of foreign operations widened from (130.7M) to (154.6M) PLN, a movement of (23.9M) PLN. Defined-benefit-program valuation reserve increased from 3.4M to 3.5M PLN (+0.1M). Hedging instruments valuation reserve rose from 64.8M to 72.0M PLN (+7.3M). Share premium above nominal (kapitał z emisji akcji powyżej wartości nominalnej) grew from 374.1M to 379.4M PLN (+5.3M, own-share issue). Share capital increased from 19.382M to 19.403M PLN (+0.021M). Non-controlling interests (udziały niekontrolujące) moved from (0.329M) to (0.167M) PLN.
