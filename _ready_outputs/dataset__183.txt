Consolidated Annual Report 2025 of the XTB S.A. Capital Group, corporate governance statement section. The Management Board of XTB S.A., acting under §72 sec. 7 lit. pt 5 of the Minister of Finance Regulation of 6 June 2025 on current and periodic information of securities issuers (consolidated text Journal of Laws 2025, item 755), presents its statement on application of corporate governance principles in 2025. Governance codes applied: Best Practices of WSE-Listed Companies 2021 (DPSN) and the Corporate Governance Principles for Supervised Institutions (KNF).

Internal systems and functions: XTB S.A. operates an internal control system and a risk management system tailored to its organizational structure. The internal control system monitors Company activity via permanent and periodic control actions, identifying risks tied to key operations, financial transactions, product management, and services offered by the XTB Capital Group. Responsibility for managing individual risks is allocated across three mutually independent lines of responsibility (Three Lines model): sales/client-contact units, product-offering units, and control units responsible for compliance verification against legal regulations and internal rules, plus internal audit as the third line guaranteeing independent assessment of the entire system. The risk management system aims at identification, analysis, measurement, evaluation, treatment, monitoring, and reporting of risk types; adopted strategies, policies, procedures, and processes support stability and security of operations.

Investor relations: dedicated web pages publish information for clients (service/product regulations, product news, informational and educational materials), investors and shareholders (financial and operating results, current and periodic reports, Group/Company strategy, analyst reports), and sustainability stakeholders (climate impact, social and labor matters). The Management Board holds four conferences per year presenting financial results and target realization. The Company participates in external investor conferences and market events such as ForFin (Forum of Finance and Investment). Investor relations contact: relacje.inwestorskie@xtb.com.

Management and Supervisory Board (Chapter 2): the Company has implemented a diversity policy for the Management Board, focused on diversity of education, age, gender, and origin, selecting candidates based on knowledge, experience, and skills with diversity criteria; applied on appointment of new members, assignment of new roles, or doubts regarding compliance. Members must report changes of circumstances to the Supervisory Board. A deviation from DPSN principle 2.1 is noted regarding the diversity policy for Supervisory Board members and minority participation in the managing body at a level not lower than 30%.

Conflict of interest and related-party transactions (Chapters 4-5): the Company maintains Conflict of Interest Management Rules governing prevention of conflicts in XTB S.A. and related employee duties, binding on all XTB employees, addressing factors arising from organizational structure and other capital group members' activity. Under the Management Board Rules, a Board Member must immediately notify the Board of any actual or potential conflict and abstain from discussion and voting on related resolutions; analogous rules apply to Supervisory Board members under the Supervisory Board Rules. A Related-Party Transactions Procedure governs material related-party transactions, defining identification of related parties, materiality criteria, approval mode, and corporate-body duties; such transactions are concluded on arm's-length market terms after analysis and, where specified, after approval of the competent body, with conflicted persons excluded from voting.

General Meeting and shareholder relations (Chapter 4): the Company convenes general meetings per applicable law, providing publicly available real-time broadcast of proceedings and answering shareholder questions per legal norms. A Dividend Policy is published at https://ir.xtb.com. The Management Board intends to recommend future dividend payouts at 50% to 100% of the Company's standalone annual net profit, subject to capital adequacy ratios, capital required for Group development, and KNF guidelines. The Ordinary General Meeting on 14 May 2025 resolved to distribute a dividend from 2024 net profit totaling PLN 640,752,417.95, i.e. PLN 5.45 per share, covering all 117,569,251 shares. Dividend record date: 16 June 2025; payment date: 25 June 2025.
