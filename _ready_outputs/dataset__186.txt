Consolidated sustainability reporting (Section 3, disclosure BP-1 General basis for preparation of sustainability statements) covering the Dom Development S.A. Group, whose parent entity is Dom Development S.A. The statement's formal basis comprises: the Polish Accounting Act of 29 September 1994 (Dz.U. 2024 item 1863), which transposes the EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD), Directive (EU) 2022/2464 of 14 December 2022 amending Regulation (EU) No 537/2014, Directive 2004/109/EC, Directive 2006/43/EC and Directive 2013/34/EU; Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2772 of 31 July 2023 supplementing Directive 2013/34/EU with respect to sustainability reporting standards (ESRS Standards); Regulation (EU) 2020/852 of 18 June 2020 establishing a framework to facilitate sustainable investment and amending Regulation (EU) 2019/2088 (EU Taxonomy); and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1416 of 11 July 2025 amending Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2772 regarding deferral of the start date for applying disclosure requirements for certain entities.

The sustainability statement underwent a limited-assurance attestation engagement performed by Ernst & Young Audyt Polska Sp. z o.o., sp.k., conducted in accordance with the National Standard for Sustainability Reporting Assurance Services 3002PL (KSUA 3002PL, limited-assurance engagement) and, correspondingly, the National Standard on Assurance Engagements Other Than Audits and Reviews 3000 (Z), reflecting International Standard on Assurance Engagements 3000 (revised), as adopted by resolutions of the National Council of Statutory Auditors (Krajowa Rada Biegłych Rewidentów).

The report covers both the upstream and downstream value chain of the Group. The value chain encompasses the Group's suppliers, producers and subcontractors and their activities related to supplying raw materials, materials and services to the Group, as well as customers and end users of the Group's products (downstream tier); a detailed value-chain description is provided in disclosure SBM-1.

No confidential Group information related to ESRS E5-3 was disclosed. In the remainder of the report, no other information relating to intellectual property, know-how or innovation outcomes, nor information on anticipated developments or matters under ongoing negotiation, was omitted. No exemption from the obligation to disclose information on anticipated developments or matters subject to ongoing negotiation was applied during preparation.

Results of the stakeholder dialogue conducted during the double-materiality analysis were presented to the Company's Management Board and Supervisory Board. Where the Group has objectives, actions or metrics not defined in accordance with ESRS, this is explicitly indicated, and where necessary the steps taken to achieve future alignment are added. Information, data, indicators and statements in the report refer to the Group unless stated otherwise.

The report covers Group sustainability information for the period from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025. The consolidation scope is identical to that of the consolidated financial statements for the financial year ended 31 December 2025. The sustainability statement is prepared on an annual cycle. The document forms part of the Management Board Report on the activities of Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group in 2025 (Capital Group Dom Development S.A.).
