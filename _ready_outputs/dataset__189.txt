XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025 Consolidated Annual Report excerpt addressing EU Taxonomy disclosures for financial undertakings under Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/2178. As an investment firm, the Group invokes Art. 7(2), excluding derivatives from the KPI numerator (derivatives transfer risk, not capital, deriving value from underlying shares, bonds, currency, or stock indices, and do not constitute investments in companies per Art. 2 Annex VII). Ancillary purchases of shares and ETFs executed to fill retail client orders are not treated as own-account transactions, being mere position-taking on clients' behalf. Consequently, asset-related KPIs (Template 1, Annex VIII) are not computed. The principal KPI "total environmentally sustainable assets" equals 0%, since both numerator and denominator exclude relevant items and derivatives are excluded from the numerator; in 2025 no own-account transactions occurred other than those described. Revenue-based KPIs apply to non-own-account transactions, i.e., client-order-related transactions. Revenues from executing orders on clients' accounts were not included among Taxonomy-eligible revenues because that category contains only individual (retail) clients, not institutional clients, leaving no means to obtain Taxonomy-alignment information from client activity reports. Fractional-rights revenues concerned exclusively individual clients (no institutional-client fractional-rights transactions occurred), so they were excluded from Taxonomy-eligible revenues. Given the assumption that no assets qualify under the taxonomy, the Issuer also assumed the percentage share in total assets of exposures referenced in Art. 7(3) of Regulation 2021/2178 (exposures to undertakings not required to publish non-financial information under Art. 19a or 29a of Directive 2013/34/EU) equals 0%. The XTB Group made no modifications to the presentation of these indicators versus the prior financial year. The Group does not finance third-party activities. XTB Group companies neither issued green bonds or green debt securities to finance specific identified activities nor invested in green bonds/green debt securities issued by third parties, and held no special-purpose loans. The principal KPI "total revenues from environmentally sustainable activities and services" equals 0%, per Art. 7(2) excluding derivatives from financial-undertaking KPI numerators. Revenues from executing orders on clients' accounts amounted to 0 PLN from institutional clients, so the indicator cannot be calculated under the method in Art. 2.4 Annex VII (numerator defined as the weighted average of revenues — fees, commissions, other monetary benefits — earned by the investment firm relative to total Taxonomy-aligned economic activity within client activities). Absent institutional clients in the order-execution revenue category (exclusively retail clients), no Taxonomy-alignment information can be obtained from client activity. In the own-account transaction revenue category, only derivatives revenues arise, excluded from the principal KPI by Art. 7(2), alongside ancillary purchases of shares and ETFs for retail order execution and bond purchases, all excluded from financial-undertaking KPI calculations. Assignment of Taxonomy-eligible revenues to the appropriate environmental objective in the own-account transaction row (Template 2, Annex VIII) is based on taxonomy disclosures of four institutional clients with published ESG reports containing taxonomy disclosures, plus one client not subject to the publication obligation that completed the taxonomy disclosure tables. Own-account transaction revenues, approx. PLN 37M at year-end 2025, constitute the sum of Taxonomy-eligible but not Taxonomy-aligned revenues under Template 2, Annex VIII (owing to derivative exclusion from the numerator). Section 2.2.2 Minimum Safeguards: regarding human and labor rights, XTB S.A. implements a due-diligence process resting on due diligence in management, operational, and strategic processes and implemented corporate documents; stakeholder cooperation (stakeholder opinion surveys, employee satisfaction surveys); identification of adverse impact and mitigation actions, risk management, internal audit, and compliance; monitoring effectiveness of efforts and processes, updating corporate documents, analyzing received reports; and undertaking adequate remedial actions. The Group operates per the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights and the OECD Guidelines, committing to respect all human rights recognized in internationally acknowledged instruments including the 10 principles of the UN Global Compact, the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights, the OECD Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises, and fundamental workplace rights in the 8 core ILO Conventions per the Declaration on Fundamental Principles and Rights at Work. EU Non-Financial Taxonomy: Group companies operate on a single business model of parent XTB S.A. conducting financial and investment activity; not being a non-financial undertaking, the Group identifies no obligation to assess non-financial taxonomy alignment under applicable regulations, including Regulation (EU) 2020/852 and related delegated acts, and no obligation to report non-financial-undertaking indicators for financial year 2025. All subsidiaries play a strictly supporting role to XTB S.A.'s financial business model and core objective by providing marketing/advertising, sales, and technology back-office support, and their revenues do not exceed 10% of total XTB Group revenues. XTB S.A. makes no statement in external communications or public disclosures that its activity is EU Taxonomy-aligned, nor claims engagement in taxonomy-aligned activities.
