XTB S.A. is the parent of XTB Group and fully consolidates a broad set of subsidiaries, mostly 100% held, including brokerage and service entities in the UK, Cyprus, Belize, the UAE, Chile, Indonesia, Poland, Seychelles, South Africa and Turkey; PT XTB Indonesia Berjangka is 90% owned, while XTB S.C. Limited and XTB Africa have not yet started operations and the Turkish entity is non-operating and in liquidation. The liquidation of Tasfiye Halinde XTB Yönetim Danışmanlığı A.Ş. began on 15 September 2020, with negative foreign-exchange translation differences of -3.580 M PLN at 31 December 2025 versus -3.627 M PLN at 31 December 2024, to be recognized in consolidated profit or loss upon liquidation completion. Key 2025 group developments included XTB Agente de Valores SpA in Chile receiving CMF brokerage licence no. 216 on 11 February 2025, a USD 1.557 M share-capital increase in PT XTB Indonesia Berjangka on 30 July 2025 while maintaining the 90% stake, completion of XTB Digital Ltd.’s liquidation in Cyprus on 23 September 2025, and the 18 December 2025 renaming of XTB Financial Consultation L.L.C to XTB Financial Services L.L.C together with an AED 24.500 M share-capital increase. As of 31 December 2025 and the financial-statement approval date, the Parent Management Board consisted of President Omar Arnaout and members Paweł Szejko, Filip Kaczmarzyk, Jakub Kubacki and Bartosz Osiński; Omar Arnaout, Paweł Szejko, Filip Kaczmarzyk and Jakub Kubacki were reappointed from 2 July 2025 to 2 July 2028 after their prior terms expired on 1 July 2025, Bartosz Osiński was appointed from 1 December 2025 to 2 July 2028, and Andrzej Przybylski did not seek reappointment after his term expired. The 2025 consolidated financial statements cover 1 January to 31 December 2025 with 2024 comparatives, were signed by the Management Board on 19 March 2026, were prepared under EU-approved IFRS with no early adoption of standards, use PLN as measurement and presentation currency with figures generally in PLN thousands, apply historical cost except for fair-valued financial assets and other assets and liabilities measured under accounting policies, present assets by liquidity and liabilities by maturity, and include adjustments to align locally prepared subsidiary accounts with Group policies. The statements were prepared on a going-concern basis, and as of signing the Management Board identified no circumstances threatening continuation for at least 12 months, except for the Turkish subsidiary in liquidation; the data are presented as comparable and based on consistent accounting policies except for new standards effective from 1 January 2025.
