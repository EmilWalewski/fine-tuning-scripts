XTB S.A. Capital Group composition (Section 1.2) and parent-company Management Board composition (Section 1.3), per the Q3 2025 report. XTB S.A. is the parent (Jednostka dominująca); all subsidiaries are fully consolidated (pełna). Subsidiaries and capital interests: XTB Limited (UK), Great Britain, brokerage, 100%; XTB Limited (CY), Cyprus, brokerage, 100%; XTB International Limited, Belize, brokerage, 100%; XTB MENA Limited, UAE, brokerage, 100%; PT XTB Indonesia Berjangka, Indonesia, brokerage, 90%; XTB Financial Consultation L.L.C, UAE, brokerage, 100%; XTB Agente de Valores SpA, Chile, brokerage, 100%; XTB Services Limited, Cyprus, partner acquisition/relationship-management and contract negotiation, 100%; X Open Hub Sp. z o.o., Poland, electronic-application and transactional-technology provision, 100%; XTB S.C. Limited, Seychelles, not yet operational, 100%; XTB Africa (PTY) Ltd., South Africa, not yet operational, 100%; Tasfiye Halinde XTB Yönetim Danışmanlığı A.Ş., Turkey, non-operational (in liquidation), 100%.

Liquidation of the Turkish entity Tasfiye Halinde XTB Yönetim Danışmanlığı A.Ş. commenced 15 September 2020. Negative FX differences from translating the Turkish subsidiary's foreign-currency balances were (3.6M PLN) at 30 September 2025 versus (3.6M PLN) at 31 December 2024 (note 23); FX differences on translating the Turkish entity's equity will be recognized in the consolidated result upon its liquidation.

On 5 April 2024 the parent allocated USD 1.5M to a further share-capital increase in subsidiary XTB MENA Limited, retaining 100%. On 17 July 2024 a total of USD 250k was allocated to a share-capital increase in subsidiary XTB S.C. Limited, retaining existing proportions: XTB S.A. holds 99.9% and XTB Services Limited 0.1%; as of the reporting date the entity was not yet operational. On 23 September 2025 the liquidation of XTB Digital Ltd. (Cyprus) was completed, effective that date.

Indonesia: on 17 January 2024 the parent acquired 90% of PT Rajawali Kapital Berjangka (Indonesia), a derivatives broker regulated by BAPPEBTI (Commodity Futures Trading Supervisory Agency). On 16 February 2024 the parent allocated USD 315k to a share-capital increase in PT Rajawali Kapital Berjangka. On 29 April 2024 the subsidiary was renamed PT XTB Indonesia Berjangka. On 1 October 2024 the parent allocated EUR 351k to a further share-capital increase, retaining 90%. On 17 December 2024 PT XTB Indonesia Berjangka received a PALN license from local regulator Bappebti Indonesia, granting Indonesian residents access to equities and ETPs offered by XTB. On 30 July 2025 the parent allocated USD 1,557k (USD 1.557M) to a further share-capital increase, retaining 90%.

UAE: on 25 July 2024 XTB Financial Consultation L.L.C (UAE) was registered in the local commercial register, with XTB S.A. taking 100%. On 26 July 2024 shares were paid up; contributed capital was AED 13k. The entity will provide brokerage services — financial advisory. On 23 December 2024 XTB Financial Consultation received a Securities and Commodities Authority (SCA) license in the UAE; the 5th-category license enabled improved cost and operational efficiency, expanded client services in the region, increased headcount, and the opening of a new office outside Dubai's special economic zone.

Chile: on 11 February 2025 XTB Agente de Valores SpA (Chile) received license no. 216 from the CMF (La Comisión para el Mercado Financiero / Chilean Financial Market Commission) to conduct brokerage activity in Chile, significantly strengthening XTB's presence in the region.
