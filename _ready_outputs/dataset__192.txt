Principal activity of the Parent and most subsidiaries is investment in acquired debt portfolios; under IAS 7.15 the Group treats these as core operating activity, presenting acquisition outlays within operating cash flows as "Change in debt-portfolio investments." The Group occasionally disposes of single cases from portfolios to increase core operating revenue. In the current period KRUK Group sold debt portfolios in the Czech and Slovak markets as part of curtailing operations there; under IAS 7.14 proceeds from such sales are presented within operating activity as "Change in debt-portfolio investments."
