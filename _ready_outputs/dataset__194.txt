Creotech Instruments S.A. interim condensed financial statements for the six months ended 30 June 2025 (all amounts in thousands PLN unless otherwise stated). Revenue reached PLN 67.9M, up PLN 56.1M year-over-year, driven by progress on the Company's key "Mikroglob" project. Total operating costs rose PLN 44.2M to PLN 70.5M, with the largest impact from increased external services (+PLN 32.2M), chiefly subcontractor costs on "Mikroglob," and higher wages and social insurance (+PLN 6.7M) tied to growing operational scale. A table of significant profit-and-loss items (period 01.01.2025–30.06.2025 versus 01.01.2024–30.06.2024) reports: revenue from sale of products PLN 67.943M vs PLN 11.814M; grant/subsidy income PLN 6.507M vs PLN 5.286M; consumption of materials and energy PLN 3.970M vs PLN 4.023M; external services PLN 37.110M vs PLN 4.848M; wages, social insurance and other benefits PLN 20.816M vs PLN 14.161M; depreciation/amortization PLN 6.918M vs PLN 4.035M; and net profit/(loss) from continuing operations PLN 3.541M profit vs PLN 7.548M loss prior-period.

Total assets at 30 June 2025 were PLN 300.0M, up PLN 137.7M versus 31 December 2024. Non-current assets reached PLN 116.5M, up PLN 9.0M, primarily from a PLN 6.5M increase in intangible assets driven by higher capitalized development expenditures. Current assets rose PLN 128.7M, mainly from a PLN 110.5M increase in cash from proceeds of the Series K share issue and "Mikroglob" project revenues. Equity at 30 June 2025 was PLN 147.3M, up PLN 76.3M versus end-2024 due to Series K issue proceeds. Trade and other payables increased PLN 42.5M in connection with commencement of key purchases for the strategic project.

The Company reported no changes in operating conditions or the economic environment during the period ended 30 June 2025 affecting the fair value of its financial assets and liabilities.

Segment description: the Company is principally engaged in development of space technologies, scientific apparatus, and control-and-measurement instrumentation; contract manufacturing is a supplementary revenue source. Management establishes operating segments based on reports used for strategic decisions, consistent with internal reporting to operational decision-makers. Segments are divided by primary product assortment groups (titles generating or planned to generate sales revenue), yielding four segments: Space (design, production, sale of subsystems and systems used in the space industry, including satellites); Geospatial — satellite data and UAV technologies (subsystems and systems for drone operations management and for generating, processing, or providing satellite and drone data); Quantum Systems (subsystems and systems for quantum computers, quantum cryptography, time-synchronization systems, and other systems used in scientific activity); and Manufacturing (contract production of industrial electronics to customer order). Operating segments are not combined. The Company does not present assets and liabilities, other operating income and costs, or financial income and costs per reportable segment because such amounts are not regularly presented to the chief operating decision-maker.
