RISKS — MACROECONOMIC AND GEOPOLITICAL FACTORS (Section 1.8): the residential real-estate market is cyclical and tightly linked to the economic cycle, depending on GDP growth pace, unemployment, wage levels, interest rates, market-participant sentiment and demographic cyclicality; geopolitical conditions are now added as a cyclicality driver. The Polish residential market is strongly dependent on geopolitical developments amid the war in Ukraine and escalation of the Middle East conflict, with scenarios ranging from relatively positive (rapid resolution on terms accepted by all parties) to highly pessimistic (cyclical collapse from uncontrolled escalation and incidents at NATO borders). Adverse changes could materially negatively affect the Company's and Group's operations and financial position. Mitigants: in-house general contracting, durable subcontractor relations, scale effects enabling resource optimization, a strong balance sheet and low net debt, with Management monitoring impacts. INTEREST-RATE RISK: housing-market conditions are inversely correlated with interest rates — rate increases raise mortgage costs, cut demand and pressure prices downward, while rate decreases boost demand and prices and encourage greater investment risk-taking, directly affecting developer profitability, financing costs and buyer purchasing power. The Company finances itself and its Group via issued bonds and variable-rate loans, exposing it to financial-result deterioration on rate increases. In 2025 the NBP cut interest rates by a total of 1.75 percentage points, bringing the reference rate to 4.00% at year-end, in response to disinflation approaching the 2.5% target. Management considers the Group resilient given its premium-standard housing position (clients seeking inflation protection, lower credit-cost sensitivity) and applies a Group-wide interest-rate hedging policy using hedge ratios and derivative instruments solely to hedge identified exposure; Management continuously monitors hedging policy execution and rate-risk levels.
