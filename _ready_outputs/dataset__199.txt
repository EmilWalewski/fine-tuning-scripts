Excerpt comprises accounting-policy and professional-judgment notes (sections 2.1–3.2) from the interim condensed consolidated financial statements of Grupa Kapitałowa XTB S.A. for the period 1 January 2025 to 30 September 2025, with comparatives for 1 January–30 September 2024 and balance-sheet position as at 31 December 2024 ("Report for Q3 2025"). Statements prepared in accordance with IAS 34 as endorsed by the European Union, covering the parent and its subsidiaries (the "Group"), and approved by the parent's Management Board on 17 November 2025.

Statement of compliance: prepared on the historical-cost basis, except for financial assets measured at fair value and other assets/liabilities whose measurement methods are set out in the accounting policy. Assets presented in the statement of financial position by liquidity; liabilities by maturity. Accounting policies consistent with the prior financial year, except for income-tax charge, calculated per IAS 34.30c and new standards effective from 1 January 2025. Group companies keep books per accounting rules of their countries of domicile; adjustments are made to align with Group policies. The parent elected not to early-adopt any standard. IFRS comprise standards and interpretations approved by the IASB ("RMSR") and IFRS Interpretations Committee ("KIMSF").

Functional and presentation currency: Polish złoty (PLN); all values stated in thousands of PLN unless otherwise indicated.

Going concern: prepared on a going-concern basis for the foreseeable future. As at the reporting date, the Management Board identifies no circumstances threatening continuation of operations by Group companies for at least 12 months from signing, except the Turkish subsidiary Tasfiye Halinde XTB Yönetim Danışmanlığı A.Ş. (in liquidation), described in note 1.2.

Comparability/consistency: data comparable and prepared on consistent policies across all periods presented.

Changes in accounting policies (section 2.5): policies consistent with the consolidated financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2024, except for new or amended standards and interpretations effective for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2025. Cited: Amendments to IAS 21 "The Effects of Changes in Foreign Exchange Rates" — lack of exchangeability — not yet EU-endorsed as at approval date, applicable for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2025. The Group did not early-adopt any published-but-not-yet-effective standard, interpretation, or amendment. New/amended standards first applied in 2025 had no material impact on the Group's interim financial statements.

New standards published but not yet effective (section 2.6), none yet EU-endorsed at approval date: Amendments to IFRS 9 "Financial Instruments" and IFRS 7 "Financial Instruments: Disclosures" — classification and measurement of financial instruments — applicable for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2026; IFRS 18 "Presentation and Disclosure in Financial Statements" — applicable on or after 1 January 2027; IFRS 19 "Subsidiaries without Public Accountability: Disclosures" — applicable on or after 1 January 2027; IFRS 14 "Regulatory Deferral Accounts" — EU endorsement work suspended indefinitely, IASB effective date deferred indefinitely; Amendments to IFRS 10 "Consolidated Financial Statements" and IAS 28 "Investments in Associates" regarding sale or contribution of assets between an investor and its associates or joint ventures — EU endorsement work suspended indefinitely, IASB effective date deferred indefinitely. The Group anticipates no material impact from these published, not-yet-effective standards and interpretations.

Professional judgment (section 3): in applying accounting policies, the parent's Management Board made judgments materially affecting carrying amounts of assets and liabilities.

Significant estimates and valuations (section 3.1): preparation under IFRS requires estimates and assumptions affecting reported amounts; estimates are continuously assessed by management based on historical experience and other factors, including expectations of future events. Actual results may differ from estimates. Changes in estimates are recognized in the period of revision (and future periods where applicable).

Expected credit losses and impairment (section 3.2): the Group recognizes an expected-credit-loss allowance under IFRS 9 for all assets measured at amortized cost, incorporating forecasts and expected future economic conditions in credit-risk assessment. Where objective evidence of impairment arises from post-recognition events reducing expected future cash flows, allowances are charged to current-period costs. The Group assesses impairment of overdue receivables and provides allowances for the estimated value of doubtful and uncollectible receivables. At the annual reporting date, non-current assets, including intangibles, are reviewed for impairment indicators (e.g., licence expiry or withdrawal from use); where indicators exist, a formal estimate of recoverable amount is performed. Where carrying amount exceeds recoverable amount, impairment is recognized and the asset written down to its recoverable value.
