Bodies competent to handle reports (by subject matter) comprise the Inspector of Supervision, MLRO (Money Laundering Reporting Officer), the Management Board, the Supervisory Board, or members of these organs. Under the Procedure for Reporting Irregularities at XTB S.A. dated 24.09.2024, investigators must be separate from the management chain involved in the case; upon receipt, the competent entity opens an investigation that should not exceed 2 months. External reports of suspected corrupt practices are submitted via the electronic form at https://www.xtb.com/pl/zawiadomienie or by post to XTB S.A.'s registered office in Warsaw. Reports must contain at minimum: date, place and circumstances of the event; nature of the event; and evidence of violation (documents, e-mails, other). All reports are treated as confidential and stored in a dedicated register; a reporter who provided contact details receives acknowledgment within 14 business days of reporting, and upon conclusion of proceedings is informed of the outcome within one month of closure. The Management Board Member for Legal Affairs is responsible for reporting investigation results to the management and supervisory organs under the violation-reporting procedure and cyclically informs the Supervisory Board of received reports. Anti-corruption and bribery prevention, detection and response measures in the reporting year: an AML (Anti-Money Laundering) team operates within the Legal and Compliance department and Group XTB employees undergo training on anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorist-financing; the XTB S.A. Anti-Corruption Policy was applied; rules on accepting gifts and presents were in force; client conversations were recorded in line with applicable law; anti-corruption training was conducted for new employees, members of management and supervisory organs, and persons holding functions particularly exposed to corruption risk; and quarterly alerts were sent to persons in such high-risk functions and to all employees. The violation-reporting mechanism rests on the internal "Procedure for Anonymous Reporting of Irregularities," setting the procedure for reporting actual or potential legal violations, including breaches of anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorist-financing rules, internal procedures, and ethical standards. It covers headquarters and branch employees and other persons performing activities for XTB S.A., including suppliers, provides whistleblower protection against retaliation, and implements obligations introduced by the Act of 14 June 2024 on the protection of whistleblowers; every employee witnessing or aware of a violation is obliged to report it. Reports are made via the electronic form at https://www.xtb.com/pl/zawiadomienie.

Governance and internal control: the Internal Audit function audits the ESG area; the internal auditor reports organizationally to the President of the Management Board and functionally to the Chair of the Audit Committee, per the Internal Audit Regulations. Internal Audit primarily assesses the correctness and effectiveness of processes, regulations and internal procedures, and provides an advisory role to other units, including those responsible for risk management, ESG and reporting. A Risk Control Department implements the risk-management system covering the Company's operating activity, with particular focus on ESG-derived risks; it is managed by the Management Board Member for Risk, while legal and compliance matters are overseen by a separate legal team under the Management Board Member for Legal Affairs. The XTB Risk Management System aims to ensure conscious and controlled risk-taking across Group XTB; risk-management policies serve identification, measurement and mitigation of risk and set limits constraining exposure. Within the Supervisory Board operate a Risk Management Committee and an Audit Committee, the latter functioning as a separate supervisory body.

Section 4.4 Risk management and internal control (GOV-5): in 2025 the sustainability area was developed by refining existing solutions, ordering ESG processes, identifying material impacts, opportunities and risks, and setting strategic directions for coming years. A mechanism for gathering data from XTB S.A. Capital Group was implemented and reporting was aligned with the CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive). The Management Board of XTB S.A. is the highest decision-making instance on ESG risks; ESG risks identified through the double-materiality process, per the 2024-2027 strategy, were incorporated into the internal risk-management system. Experts across Group XTB areas bear responsibility for completeness, reliability and correctness of reporting data; the Sustainability Team coordinates the whole ESG reporting process — collecting, consolidating, analyzing and disclosing information — supporting all contributing employees using common, company-wide tools available across Group XTB. Oversight of reporting correctness is exercised personally by the Management Board Member for Finance and by Management Board members. From 2025 the internal auditor from XTB S.A.'s internal audit unit will regularly review published sustainability statements and, at least once every two years, audit the ESG area.
