Section 3 (Consolidated Sustainability Statement), subsection BP-2 (Disclosures in relation to specific circumstances) of the Management Board Report on the activities of Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group for 2025 (Grupa Kapitałowa Dom Development S.A.). In 2025 no specific circumstances affected preparation of the sustainability statement. The Group adopted the following time horizons for identifying and assessing material impacts, risks and opportunities (IROs): short-term (business perspective) = 1 year; medium-term (business-model perspective, matching investment planning, execution and sales duration) = 2-3 years; long-term (planning perspective) = 3-6 years. Given the sector's specifics, any horizon beyond three years is always long-term, extending past the standard investment steps (planning, execution, sales period). The 3-year framing does not exempt Group companies from addressing climate risks, building life cycle, or circular-economy (GOZ) implementation, whose materialization beyond three years is considered obvious; planned actions in this scope remain implemented within up to three years (i.e. within the maximum medium-term horizon).

The Group reports no upstream/downstream value-chain metrics estimated via indirect sources. Most metrics rely on actual data from direct sources; where access to full actual data was limited, estimates ensuring completeness were applied using best available methods. The sole metric estimated from indirect sources is the quantity of municipal waste destined for recycling and/or other recovery processes (methodology explained in section E5-5). The Group reports no quantitative metrics or amounts subject to high uncertainty. Planned accuracy-improvement actions: pursuing effective acquisition of direct data from materials and services suppliers, continued use of recognized databases referenced to the geographic region of business activity, and continued cooperation with contractors to obtain comprehensive actual data and reduce reliance on estimates.

The Group discloses no information based on other regulations or generally accepted sustainability-reporting interpretations and standards. No material changes are reported in the method of preparation or presentation of the sustainability statement. The Group identified prior-period (2024) errors in the areas of materials consumption and water consumption. In 2025 the Group omits data based on permissible exemptions under Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1416 of 11 July 2025.
