XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025 accounting-policy notes (sections 3.3-4.4). Fair value measurement (3.3): valuation techniques appropriate to circumstances, maximizing observable inputs and minimizing unobservable inputs; fair-value methodology adapted to characteristics of each financial asset; estimates detailed in Note 35 (risk management). Other estimates (3.4): provisions for retirement, disability and post-mortem employee benefits computed actuarially by an independent actuary as present value of future obligations based on headcount/wages at the balance-sheet date, using macroeconomic, staff-turnover and mortality-risk assumptions; provision for unused holidays based on estimated leave entitlement (unused days x balance-sheet-date remuneration); legal-risk provision set individually per dispute by assessing likelihood of winning each court case. Consolidation principles (4.1): consolidated statements cover parent plus subsidiaries at 31 December 2025 and 31 December 2024; subsidiary statements adjusted to IFRS conformity, same reporting period, consistent accounting policies. Business combinations (4.1.1): acquisitions and separated business lines settled via acquisition method; consideration measured at aggregate fair value (at payment date) of assets transferred, liabilities incurred/assumed and equity instruments issued for control; directly related combination costs expensed when incurred; identifiable assets, liabilities and contingent liabilities recognized at acquisition-date fair value per IFRS 3 (with IFRS 3 exceptions); acquisition method also applied to common-control transactions; step acquisitions remeasure previously held interests to fair value through profit or loss, with amounts previously in other comprehensive income reclassified to P&L. Investments in subsidiaries (4.1.2): subsidiaries are entities controlled by the parent (including special-purpose entities); control via exposure/rights to variable returns and power over the investee; results of subsidiaries acquired/disposed during the year recognized from/to date control obtained/lost; all intragroup transactions, balances, income and costs fully eliminated. Functional/presentation currency (4.2): functional and presentation currency of parent is Polish zloty (PLN); foreign-currency monetary assets/liabilities translated at NBP mid-rate at balance-sheet date; non-monetary items at historical cost; FX differences recognized in P&L when arising. Cash and equivalents (4.3): cash comprises demand bank deposits; equivalents are investments readily convertible to known cash amounts, low value-change risk, maturity three months or less from acquisition; indirect cash-flow method used; interest received on cash/equivalents and interest paid to clients classified as operating, finance-lease interest as financing; cash includes Group own funds plus client funds, the latter held on segregated bank accounts and excluded from the Group cash-flow statement. Financial assets and liabilities (4.4): investments recognized at purchase/derecognized at sale (trade-date accounting) at fair value; transaction costs for fair-value-through-P&L instruments expensed, otherwise capitalized in initial value; financial assets classified as amortized cost, FVTPL (incl. held-for-trading), or FVOCI; financial liabilities as amortized cost or FVTPL (incl. held-for-trading); classification based on business model and SPPI contractual cash-flow criterion; debt-instrument reclassification only when the management model changes.
