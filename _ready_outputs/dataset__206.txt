Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group H1 2025 financial-results review (Management Report for H1 2025), covering company-level income statement, balance sheet, liquidity and leverage ratios.

Liquidity (Group): current ratio fell to 4.81 from 5.37 at 31.12.2024, driven by higher short-term liabilities, notably recognition of a dividend payable of 180.6M PLN as at 30.06.2025; quick ratio rose to 0.85 from 0.82; cash ratio rose to 0.61 from 0.38 (short-term liabilities exclude deferred income). Improvement in quick/cash ratios reflects a significant increase in cash of 374.8M PLN to 735.6M PLN, alongside the dividend-driven rise in short-term liabilities. Management assesses Group liquidity as consistently very good, citing a strong balance sheet enabling investment execution and rapid market opportunism.

Leverage ratios (Group, 30.06.2025 vs 31.12.2024): equity-to-total-assets coverage 29.4% vs 32.2%; debt-to-equity 240.0% vs 210.8%; total debt ratio (total liabilities/total assets) 70.6% vs 67.8%; interest-bearing debt to equity 45.4% vs 35.8%; net interest-bearing debt to equity (interest debt less cash, incl. escrow) (4.0)% vs 1.9%. Management characterizes leverage as remaining at safe levels, confirming credit standing and reflecting a conservative financing-structure approach, with values qualitatively similar to end-2024.

Company income statement (H1 2025 vs H1 2024, PLN, % change): revenue 474.4M vs 915.6M, down 48%; gross profit on sales 156.2M vs 261.8M, down 40%; EBIT (operating profit) 70.8M vs 165.6M, down 57%; net profit 361.7M vs 291.6M, up 24%; basic EPS 14.02 vs 11.31, up 24%. Revenue decline driven primarily by far fewer unit handovers—594 units in H1 2025, down 45%—consistent with project handover schedules concentrated in H2 2025 given long development cycles; annual target unaffected. Company recognized 414.3M PLN revenue from finished goods (594 units handed over) plus 50.7M PLN from services. Handovers significantly featured popular-segment projects Osiedle przy Ryżowej and Osiedle Urbino, and premium-segment Apartamenty Literacka. Gross margin reached 32.9%, assessed by Management as very good. EBIT fell 94.7M PLN due to lower revenue with stable general administrative and selling costs. Net profit up 24% to 361.7M PLN despite operating decline, supported by materially higher financial income, chiefly dividends received from subsidiaries: 299.9M PLN in H1 2025 vs 155.7M PLN in H1 2024. Business is non-seasonal; inter-quarter fluctuations tied to development-construction cycle and handover clustering after occupancy permits (revenue recognized on unit handover per IFRS 15) and to timing/amount of subsidiary dividends; sub-annual results should be read with operating data. Company also reports current sales volume net of cancellations based on preliminary and paid reservation agreements (operating results in note 2.2).

Company balance sheet (as at 30.06.2025): total assets 4,156.5M PLN, up 17% vs end-2024, driven mainly by a 351.1M PLN rise in cash and growth in long-term loans granted. Inventories—the dominant current asset—amounted to 2,458.1M PLN, 59% of the balance sheet total. Cash stood at 481.0M PLN, plus 64.1M PLN held on open residential escrow accounts. Investments in subsidiaries, associates and a joint venture totaled 435.8M PLN (10.5% of assets); loans granted to subsidiaries totaled 593.3M PLN (14% of assets). Largest subsidiary investment carrying values were development companies Euro Styl S.A. (265.5M PLN) and Dom Development Kraków sp. z o.o. (162.7M PLN). Management describes the Company's asset position in H1 2025 as remaining very good and positively distinguishing it within the sector.
