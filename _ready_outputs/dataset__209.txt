Consolidated sustainability report (ESRS S1-14, Occupational Health & Safety) for Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group, FY2025. All own employees of Group companies are covered by a legally-based OHS management system, equating to 100% coverage of own workforce (UoP) under legal requirements/recognized standards (100% in 2025 and 2024). The OHS system has NOT been subjected to internal audit or external audit/certification. The Group does not record cases of work-related ill health detected among former employees. The recordable-accident rate calculation assumed 1,992 working hours as the annual work norm for 2025.

Key OHS indicators presented as 2025 vs 2024 for own employees (UoP): work-related fatalities from work injuries and ill health 0.00 vs 0.00; recordable work-related accident rate 2.82 vs 2.08 (year-over-year increase); number of recordable reportable work-related accidents 4.00 vs 3.00; recorded cases of reportable work-related ill health 2.00 vs 2.00; days lost due to work-related injuries, fatalities and ill health 57.00 vs 13.00 (sharp rise). Value-chain workers (subcontractors): fatalities from work injuries/ill health 0.00 vs 2.00; recorded cases of work-related ill health 9.00 vs no data; recorded accidents 12.00 vs no data. Other workers on company premises (co-workers, other employment forms): fatalities 0.00 vs no data; recorded ill-health cases 0.00 vs no data.

Data on accidents, near-misses, potentially dangerous situations, ill health and confirmed occupational diseases are gathered via a dedicated OHS application "eBHP" plus internally maintained registers. Any employee or co-worker may report an event through the application; reports route directly to the OHS Department for incident analysis and handling. The app enables reporting of accidents and health-related events, data completion, document generation/storage, and case monitoring. Accidents of both own staff and subcontractor staff are logged in a dedicated accident module. On construction sites, subcontractor accidents are reported via the app by the Site Manager, who forwards them to the system; for own staff, any employee, co-worker or unit manager may file.

Group companies execute priorities from the OHS and Environmental Protection Policy (described in S1-1) and are currently working on monitoring implementation of the OHS Policy and Standard, applied through specific clauses in contracts, particularly long-term framework agreements with partners. Additional measures: start-of-work meetings/training (site manager discusses project risks, work methods, OHS hazards and PPE before work commences); safety alerts and "safety tasks" distributed by email and intranet; environmental alerts warning own and subcontractor staff of forecast extreme events (heavy rain, intense snowfall, strong winds, heatwaves) affecting site work; workplace inspections/controls at least weekly by OHS specialists checking safety, health and environmental aspects plus staff safety behaviors and PPE compliance, followed by reports with preventive recommendations; weekly (un)safe-behavior observations by the OHS Department, discussed directly with employees, feeding an improvement program.

OHS and first-aid training delivered to all Dom Construction employees; all Dom Construction site offices equipped with AED defibrillators; Health Days with preventive examinations organized on Dom Construction sites. In 2025 the "Wednesday with Good Practice" communication cycle continued, promoting best OHS practices on sites. A 2025 rescue competition tested emergency-response skills; year-end practical training with VR-technology elements covered firefighting and evacuation. Firefighters from the Specialist Search-and-Rescue Group in Warsaw (Rescue & Firefighting Unit No. 15, State Fire Service) conducted exercises on a Dom Construction site covering landslide securing and extracting persons buried in excavations; Dom Construction provided the site and specially prepared excavations, plus training for staff on conduct and first aid in burial cases. Health Days were again held at all Dom Construction locations for subcontractor employees (nutrition, health prevention, blood-pressure/blood-sugar measurement, first-aid instruction); investor companies organized pre-medical first-aid training. All Group employees are obliged to attend OHS training (initial on first work day, periodic on expiry). In 2025 an in-house e-learning periodic OHS course for administrative-office staff and managers was implemented (training now delivered internally), and the proprietary "Construction, a safe adventure" project targeting engineering-technical staff was created to raise OHS awareness among site supervisors.

Group OHS expenditure in 2025 (nearly PLN 4M) rose 9% versus 2024. For the second time in Dom Construction's history, three Company-executed sites entered the State Labour Inspectorate's "Build Safely" competition; at the regional stage, Osiedle przy Forcie (Warsaw) placed third, Osiedle Hubska (Wrocław) placed second, and Apartamenty Matecznego (Kraków) placed first in the Małopolska voivodeship.
