Excerpt from XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025, covering market-risk/liquidity-risk management methodology and corporate governance. Given trading-portfolio volatility, XTB performs market-risk tests every business day. Stress testing constitutes a core risk-management tool, examining the impact of low-probability, high-negative-effect scenarios on the Company's operations; all stress tests are run at minimum annually. Tests cover both internal threats and external factors, including consequences of partner/counterparty problems and failures and potential penalties imposed by administrative authorities supervising XTB across various areas. Both hypothetical scenarios (price-shock sets defined by expert method) and historical scenarios (replication of past crises) are applied. Given significant open positions dependent on individual market-risk factors, XTB places special emphasis on market-risk stress tests, including single-factor tests assessing the effect of an abrupt material price change of one specific market parameter. Stress-test results serve multiple uses: as a criterion for recognizing individual risk types as material (a material loss outcome triggers a risk type being deemed material, intensifying risk-management, monitoring and measurement activities); for assessing whether all risk factors are adequately captured in ICAAP and ILAAP processes; and for reviewing the level of limits constraining individual risk types. XTB aims for full coverage of risks with capital and liquid assets at both Company and Group level; where stress tests indicate possible underestimation of risk, internal capital and liquid-asset requirements may be reviewed. XTB does not apply hedge accounting. Warning limits for quantitative elements of the risk-appetite declaration are set at a safer level than the declaration itself; breaches trigger actions defined in contingency capital plans and contingency liquidity plans, aimed at reducing risk exposure and improving the capital/liquidity situation before risk appetite is exceeded. Breaches of risk appetite intensify these actions, and the Supervisory Board (Rada Nadzorcza) is immediately informed of any breach of the acceptable risk level. The procedure permits deviations from its application only with approval of at least two Management Board members of the parent entity; deviation information is forwarded to the parent entity's Risk Control Department. Contingency liquidity-maintenance plans implemented in the parent entity were not used in the reporting period or comparative period, as the most liquid assets (own cash) substantially exceed liabilities. Contractual payment terms of financial assets and liabilities are presented in notes 36.3 (consolidated) and 37.3 (separate statements), with partial and cumulative contractual liquidity gaps stated per maturity bucket, calculated as total assets minus total liabilities. The Group manages financial resources via ongoing monitoring of asset-financing capacity and timely settlement of liabilities without loss. The parent entity uses a liquidity model for certain financial-instrument-related bank accounts, defining a safe free-cash zone requiring no corrective action; on reaching the upper limit, surplus above optimal level is transferred to the relevant current account, and on reaching the lower limit, funds are transferred back to restore optimal cash. The Risk Control Department performs daily oversight of cash account balances; liquidity operations are also handled by the Trading Department and the Finance and Accounting Department. Subsidiaries manage liquidity by analyzing expected cash flows and matching asset maturities to liability maturities, do not apply liquidity models, and reported no liquidity incidents or inability to meet obligations. On corporate governance: in 2025 XTB S.A. complied with the "Best Practices of WSE Listed Companies 2021" (Dobre Praktyki Spółek Notowanych na GPW 2021), adopted by WSE Council Resolution No. 13/1834/2021 of 29 March 2021. A statement on the Company's application of DPSN 2021 principles is published in the Corporate Governance section of XTB's investor-relations site (https://ir.xtb.com/). The Company maintains and regularly updates corporate (www.xtb.com), investor-relations (ir.xtb.com) and sustainability (esg.xtb.com) websites, guided by reliability and equal access to information for investors and shareholders. XTB's corporate governance is grounded in applicable law, including the Minister of Finance Regulation of 6 June 2025 on current and periodic information transmitted by securities issuers (Journal of Laws 2025).
