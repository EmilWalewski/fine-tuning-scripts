Excerpt from XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025, presenting the ESG double-materiality assessment and management risk/opportunity register (Table 7: "Istotne szanse i ryzyka zarządcze"/Material managerial opportunities and risks), structured by topic, impact/risk-opportunity description, linkage to strategy and business model, current and expected effects, risk/opportunity management, current and projected financial effects, and value-chain location of occurrence. The register reports no current financial effects for the listed items and states the Group is unable to estimate their financial impact.

Consumers and end users (own operations, downstream): identified as an opportunity to acquire new clients, improve the XTB Group's image, and build competitive advantage by enhancing investment products with ESG solutions, and to activate clients by combining investment activity with charitable and social initiatives; noted as not arising from XTB's strategy or business model, with no impact on strategy/business model but possible influence on value-chain stakeholders and organizational decision-making. Management measures cited: Sustainalytics (Morningstar) rating, planned launch of an ESG website service, and activity of the XTB Foundation.

Business conduct risks (own operations): (1) Misconduct/corruption risk regarding possible corruption cases within the XTB Group, not arising from strategy/business model; managed via internal anti-corruption regulations, an Anti-Corruption Policy, anti-corruption training, and building a responsible values- and best-practice-based organizational culture. (2) Security risk of personal-data leakage of XTB Group stakeholders (for whom it acts as data administrator) resulting from cybercrime, human error, or system error; managed via implemented safeguards and network/data hardening, a breach-reporting procedure, internal information campaigns, and GDPR (RODO) compliance.

Additional business-conduct risks (own operations): (3) Legal and reporting risk arising from failure to adapt Group processes to a highly volatile legislative environment and regulator decisions on environmental, social, and governance matters, partners' unfamiliarity with ESG practices and ethics, and improper fulfillment of reporting obligations; managed via conducting the double-materiality assessment, aligning reporting to EU ESRS standards, tracking regulations and legal changes, selecting reputable partners, and ongoing partner assessment. (4) Operational risk from various court and supervisory-authority proceedings (e.g., data leaks and personal-data protection breaches, inspections and potential penalties imposed by KNF and UOKiK, adverse litigation outcomes); managed via implemented data safeguards, legally and best-practice-compliant operations, transparent communication with the supervisor, and timely fulfillment of obligations.

(5) Product/complaint and reputation risk tied to the nature of XTB's investment products and their negative market perception (e.g., leveraged financial instruments) and negative online/forum commentary, given the high investment risk and high probability of client losses they carry; this risk arises from the characteristics of XTB S.A.'s investment products, which form part of the Company's business model; managed via provision of informational materials, refined transparent client documents, and the MiFID questionnaire assessing suitability of services to clients' knowledge, experience, preferences, and risk appetite.

(6) Security risk covering all actions by criminals to gain access to client accounts and assets, execute unauthorized transactions and unauthorized fund transfers, steal confidential data, or conduct DDoS attacks, a material factor in the risk of resource loss to the firm and its clients; this risk is directly linked to the XTB Group's business model and activity, and upon materialization would affect internal operational processes, XTB's reputation, the Group's financial-statement positions, and clients' situations. Reported as having no significant current financial effects, with the Group unable to estimate the financial impact. Management measures: default two-factor authentication (2FA) for clients, continuous enhancement of XTB safeguards, cybersecurity awareness campaigns for stakeholders, cooperation with supervisory authorities on cybersecurity and reporting of irregularities, cybersecurity education for employees, and monitoring of client transactions for atypical features.
