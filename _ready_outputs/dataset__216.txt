Asseco Group quarterly report for the three-month period ended 31 March 2025 (all figures in millions of PLN), Section B commentary and supplementary information. Q1 2025 was affected by a loss recognized on the disposal of shares in the Turkish subsidiary Mobven (part of the ASEE Group), which reduced consolidated operating profit by 6.5M PLN and consolidated net profit attributable to parent shareholders by 3.3M PLN, and by remeasurement of balance sheet items for hyperinflation relating to Turkish ASEE subsidiaries, where the Group recognized 7.0M PLN in financial income, increasing consolidated net profit attributable to parent shareholders by 3.6M PLN. In Q1 2024 Asseco Poland sold a property, increasing consolidated operating profit by 14.0M PLN and consolidated net profit attributable to parent shareholders by 11.3M PLN; additionally, hyperinflation remeasurement of Turkish ASEE subsidiaries generated 10.2M PLN of financial income, increasing parent-shareholder net profit by 5.2M PLN.
