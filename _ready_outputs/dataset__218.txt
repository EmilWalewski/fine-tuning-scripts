Dom Development S.A. Capital Group consolidated sustainability reporting (2025 management report), covering climate-change mitigation and adaptation disclosures under ESRS E1-1 through E1-4. The Group holds no climate transition plan; development of a transition plan is scheduled no earlier than 2030. The Group likewise holds no dedicated policy on energy efficiency, climate-change mitigation, or adaptation; climate-related objectives and actions are instead set out in the ESG Strategy DOM 2030, with a separate standalone policy planned no earlier than 2030.

On emissions-reduction actions (E1-3), Group companies in 2025 purchased 12,385 MWh of renewable-source energy, used to deliver development projects. Under the ESG Strategy, through at least 2030 Group companies plan continued greenhouse-gas reduction actions focused on the construction process and later building life-cycle stages, including increased purchase of renewable-energy guarantees of origin, improved building energy efficiency, and gradual vehicle-fleet transition, driven primarily by higher utility-use efficiency in project delivery. These actions support the emissions-reduction target in ESG Strategy DOM 2030, though their scale and pace are currently difficult to estimate. The Group does not plan to deploy new GHG-reduction technologies. Achieved GHG reduction resulted from lower electricity and heat consumption. The Group applies adaptive solutions in own operations to reduce key identified physical climate hazards at design and construction stages, in line with national law, technical guidelines, and applicable standards.

Identified physical climate hazards and adaptation measures include: flooding (plot flood-risk assessment, increased permeable surfaces, urban infiltration strips and linear rain gardens, sustainable urban drainage systems (SUDS), foundation waterproofing, basement pumps, anti-backflow devices, rooftop boilers/chillers, flood-resistant materials, tree planting, raised electrical/mechanical/utility systems, EPS/XPS waterproof insulation, flood insurance); storms and torrential rain (reinforced roof structures, raised lowest residential floor, wind-performance design, roof drainage, lightning protection, dense hedging/shrubs, rain-cladding systems, strong connections between external building elements, backup generators for fire-protection systems only, surge protection, passive landslide controls such as retaining walls/gabions/geotextiles, inverted and extensive green roofs, rainwater tanks); water stress and drought (building at least 500m from forest areas, rainwater harvesting, increased fire-resistant materials); heat stress (thermal mass and phase-change materials, light reflective materials, external wall/window/roof insulation, temperature zones, rooftop photovoltaic (PV) installations, green roofs, geothermal and heat pumps in Warsaw and Tri-City projects, double facades only in Tri-City); and settlement/subsidence (peripheral drainage at least 2m from building, deep/semi-deep foundations, uniform foundations, structural reinforcement). These measures are funded from own resources, not directly linked to EU Taxonomy delegated-act activities, executed within development-project budgets; costs appear in note 7.35 of the consolidated financial statements under "consumption of materials and energy" and "external services."

On targets (E1-4), the Group's ESG Strategy DOM 2030 sets a reduction target of 30% in Scope 1 and 2 CO2e emissions per dwelling under construction by 2030 versus 2021 base year; no Scope 3 target set. The target is not absolute, given changing operational scale; it covers operational activity across all Group companies relating to electricity, heat, gas, and vehicle-fleet purchasing. It was developed internally and approved by the Management Board, is a gross target (excluding GHG removals, carbon credits, avoided emissions), is not science-based, and is not aligned with the 1.5°C warming-limit goal. Reported emissions are not subject to regulated emissions-trading systems; the location-based GHG Protocol method was used. Target table (Scope 1 and 2, location-based): 2021 base year 2.67 tCO2e/dwelling; 2025 1.81 tCO2e/dwelling; year-on-year change +5.8%; 2030 target 1.87 tCO2e/dwelling. Effectiveness is monitored via automated data collection enabling regular utility-consumption monitoring, GHG Protocol reporting with annual sustainability disclosures, and data-driven operational optimization.
