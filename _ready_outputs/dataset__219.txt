XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025 Consolidated Annual Report excerpt; competitive landscape analysis, no financial figures or tables. XTB identifies competitors across categories: fintech firms, transaction-platform providers, and international CFD-specialist brokers. Fintech segment: Trade Republic, German online broker founded 2015 in Munich, offering equities, bonds, derivatives, and crypto via mobile app, plus interest-bearing current accounts and planned savings plans/products such as PEA and Livret A in France. Scalable Capital, German automated-investment-management firm operating since 2014, leading European robo-advisory provider, offering ETF investing and algorithmic portfolio management; available in UK, Austria, Switzerland and other EU countries via mobile app and web platform. Revolut, British fintech founded 2015, operating in 40-50+ countries (UK, EU, US, Australia, Singapore, Brazil, Japan), offering multicurrency accounts, international payments, payment cards, commission-free FX, savings accounts, equities, ETFs and CFDs. Transaction-platform category: eToro, social-trading investment platform offering equities, currencies, commodities, crypto and ETFs, with CopyTrader real-time copy-trading; operating in US, UK, Israel, Cyprus, Australia, Germany and UAE. Trading 212, founded 2006, offering equities, ETFs, CFDs and crypto with commission-free equity/ETF/fractional-CFD trading, access to NYSE, NASDAQ, LSE; Android/iOS and web platforms; FCA-regulated (UK); provides educational materials. Robinhood Markets, Inc., US firm offering commission-free trading of equities, ETFs, options and crypto, primarily online via mobile app and website. Saxo Bank, Danish investment bank and online broker established 1992, offering equities, FX, CFDs, bonds, investment funds and portfolio management across 180+ countries via SaxoTraderGO (mobile/desktop) and SaxoTraderPRO; regulated by FCA (UK), Danish FSA and other regional authorities; provides educational materials. International CFD-specialist brokers: Plus500, CFD platform founded 2008, covering equities, forex, commodities, indices, crypto and other instruments across 50+ countries; listed on London Stock Exchange; regulated by FCA (UK), ASIC (Australia) and others; offers desktop/mobile platform with charting, technical indicators, risk-management and educational tools. IG Group, UK-headquartered global broker offering forex, CFDs, equities, commodities and crypto across 17+ countries (Europe, US, Australia, Canada, Asia); FCA-regulated; provides webinars, guides and analytical tools. CMC Markets, British financial-services firm specializing in online derivatives/CFD trading, with operational centres in Sydney and Singapore, present internationally including Poland; regulated by FCA (UK) and BaFin (Germany). XTB strengths: global presence in 15+ countries; regulated by 9 financial supervisory bodies including FCA (UK) and KNF (Poland) plus authorities in Europe, Middle East and Latin America; client-fund segregation for security; professional support 24 hours, 5 days weekly via phone and live chat; education via Akademia XTB platform (webinars, video courses, articles, guides) plus regular educational sessions and market analyses. In Poland, XTB Group is brokerage-market leader through broad investment-product offering and proprietary trading platform and mobile app; domestic competition includes bank-affiliated brokerage houses covering equities, bonds, funds, ETFs, commodities and crypto. Leading Polish bank brokers: mBank Biuro Maklerskie (equities, bonds, ETFs, CFDs, crypto via mTrader web platform and mInwestor mobile app); Santander Biuro Maklerskie (retail and institutional services: GPW and foreign equities, bonds, ETFs, investment funds via Santander Makler platform); Dom Maklerski Banku Ochrony Srodowiska (equities, bonds, investment funds, derivatives via BOSSA.pl online platform and BOSSAFX forex platform); PKO BP Biuro Maklerskie (retail and institutional services: GPW and foreign equities, bonds, ETFs, investment funds via IBI Trader investment platform).
