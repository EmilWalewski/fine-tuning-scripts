XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025 Consolidated Annual Report excerpt detailing prudential capital adequacy, regulatory supervisory assessment, and dividend policy. Own funds and total capital requirement components (in thousands PLN, expressed below in millions) are disclosed as at 31.12.2025, as average monthly value over the period (estimated from daily balances), and as at 31.12.2024. Own funds (Fundusze własne / Kapitały podstawowe) stood at 1,330.5M PLN at 31.12.2025, averaged 1,249.7M PLN over the period, and totaled 1,114.5M PLN at 31.12.2024, evidencing year-over-year growth in the regulatory capital base. Common Equity Tier I before deductions (Kapitały podstawowe Tier I bez pomniejszeń) reached 1,336.9M PLN at 31.12.2025, with a period average of 1,258.0M PLN, up from 1,122.4M PLN at 31.12.2024. Additional Tier I capital (Kapitały dodatkowe Tier I) was nil across all periods. Items reducing Common Equity Tier I (Pozycje pomniejszające kapitały podstawowe Tier I) were negative 6.4M PLN at 31.12.2025, averaged negative 8.3M PLN, versus negative 7.9M PLN at 31.12.2024. Client risk requirement (Ryzyko dla klienta) was 26.7M PLN at 31.12.2025, with a period average of 21.3M PLN, rising from 15.5M PLN at 31.12.2024. On 23 December 2025 the Company received from the Investment Firms Department of the Polish Financial Supervision Authority (KNF) a supervisory BION assessment of 2 [2.63], assigned as at 30 June 2025; a level-2 rating signifies a low threat to the brokerage house's stability and falls within KNF-recommended criteria, which should permit the Company to potentially pay a dividend for 2025 in line with those criteria. The total capital ratio (IFR), measuring own funds relative to risk-weighted assets and indicating capacity to cover the minimum capital requirement for market, credit, operational, and other risks, reached 190.5% at year-end 2025. Standalone (jednostkowy) net profit for 2025 amounted to 638.9M PLN, the basis disclosed in the Company's separate annual financial statements. The Management Board intends to recommend to the General Meeting payment of a dividend in an amount reflecting standalone net profit and the Company's financial capacity, guided by the need to maintain adequate capital adequacy ratios, capital required for Group development, and KNF guidelines. The Board's stated intention is to recommend future dividend resolutions at a level of 50% to 100% of the Company's standalone net profit for the given financial year. The dividend policy may be amended by the Board as needed, with decisions accounting for continuing operations prospects, future profits, cash needs, financial position, capital adequacy ratio levels, expansion plans, and legal requirements. Each dividend resolution will additionally be considered by the General Meeting, and payment and the dividend amount ultimately depend on shareholders' decision at the Ordinary General Meeting, with shareholders not bound by any Board recommendation. Taking into account the criteria set by KNF in its position published on 17 December 2025, particularly regarding the total capital ratio level and the BION assessment, a dividend payout by XTB of up to a maximum of 75% of 2025 profit is possible in 2026.
