Grupa Asseco quarterly report, Q1 2025 (three months ended 31 March 2025; figures in PLN millions), Section B commentary 3.3 Formula Systems segment. Defined metrics: EBIT = operating profit; EBIT non-IFRS = EBIT adjusted for PPA amortization of acquired intangibles, equity-settled share-based payment (SBP) costs, and gains/costs from company acquisitions/disposals; EBITDA = EBIT + depreciation/amortization; CFO BT = operating cash flow before income tax paid; CAPEX = segment fixed-asset expenditure; FCF = |CFO BT| - |CAPEX| - |lease payments|; cash conversion ratio = FCF / EBIT non-IFRS. Segment revenue notes cover external-customer plus inter-segment sales and external (off-Group) debt.
