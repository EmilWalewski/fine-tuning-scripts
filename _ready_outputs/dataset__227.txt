Section 7 (supplementary notes) of the consolidated financial statements of the Dom Development S.A. Capital Group for the year ended 31 December 2025. Parent company Dom Development S.A. is a joint-stock company seated at Plac Piłsudskiego 3, 00-078 Warsaw, Poland, registered in the National Court Register under number 0000031483 (District Court for the Capital City of Warsaw, XII Commercial Division), country of registration Poland, principal place of business Warsaw. Core activity per Polish Classification of Activities: realization of construction projects connected with the erection of buildings (PKD 41102). The Company is majority-owned by Groupe Belleforét S.a r.l., seated in Luxembourg, which held 54.81% of the Company's shares as at 31 December 2025. Ultimate parent of the Group is SCOP 2003 Trust, established under the law of England and Wales; SCOP 2003 Trust is sole shareholder of SCOP Luxembourg 2007 S.a r.l., which in turn is majority owner of Groupe Belleforet S.a r.l. and prepares the highest-level consolidated statements.

Group structure and parent's share in subscribed capital as at 31 December 2025 (all subsidiaries Polish, full consolidation method): Dom Development Grunty sp. z o.o. (46% capital, 100% voting rights); Dom Development Kredyty sp. z o.o. (100%/100%); Dom Development Wrocław sp. z o.o. (100%/100%); Dom Land sp. z o.o. (no direct stake, controlled via board members); Euro Styl S.A. (100%/100%, itself parent of Euro Styl S.A. Capital Group); Dom Construction sp. z o.o. (100%/100%); Dom Development Kraków sp. z o.o. (100%/100%); Dom Development Myśliborska sp. z o.o. (100%/100%); Fundacja Nasz Dom (100%/100%). Group's principal activity is construction and sale of residential real estate. Dom Development Grunty sp. z o.o. is fully consolidated despite 46% capital share because its financial and operating policy is directed by management board members appointed by Dom Development S.A.; its activity is purchase of real estate for the Group's development operations. Dom Land sp. z o.o. holds 54% of Dom Development Grunty sp. z o.o. All Group companies operate in Poland under the Commercial Companies Code for an unlimited duration.

In 2024 Euro Styl S.A. concluded two investment (joint-venture) agreements to undertake development projects via special-purpose companies, building on land acquired directly by these joint ventures with business partners (detailed in note 7.39). During the twelve months ended 31 December 2025 the Group did not discontinue any line of business.

Material structural changes: On 15 December 2025 the Company acquired 100% of shares in GH Development 9 Sp. z o.o. (now Dom Development Myśliborska Sp. z o.o.) for PLN 27.0M (27,012 thousand). Purpose: realization of a multi-stage development project on land at ul. Myśliborska in Warsaw (carrying value with incurred outlays PLN 85.8M / 85,838 thousand) held by that company. The company also held a loan taken to acquire the land, with carrying value including accrued interest of PLN 87.3M (87,313 thousand). On the acquisition date Dom Development S.A. entered, under a subrogation agreement, as lender in place of former lender GH INTERNATIONAL INVESTMENTS 2 NV. GH Development 9 had no employees, no prior operating activity, no material contracts, and no identified operational processes; management therefore classified the transaction as an asset acquisition rather than a business combination. Resulting changes in the Group's consolidated balance sheet at transaction date: inventories (acquired property) +PLN 113.6M (113,613 thousand); receivables (VAT) +PLN 0.7M (725 thousand); cash (payment for shares and loan subrogation) -PLN 114.3M (114,325 thousand); deferred tax provision -PLN 16 thousand.

On 12 May 2025 the Company received from Dom Development Wrocław Sp. z o.o. a return of capital contribution of PLN 13.5M (13,500 thousand). Aside from the above, no other material changes to investments in subsidiaries, associates, or joint ventures occurred in the twelve months ended 31 December 2025.

Basis of preparation (note 7.2): statements prepared under the historical cost convention on a going-concern basis. Management notes the 24 February 2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine and the escalation of the Middle East conflict as destabilizing factors that may affect housing demand, construction costs, and subcontractor availability; the Group operates solely in Poland and, as at the reporting date, all investments progressed per plan or ahead of schedule, supporting the going-concern assumption. Functional and presentation currency is the Polish zloty (PLN); figures are in thousands of PLN unless stated otherwise. Statements comply with IFRS as adopted by the EU; for the financial year ended 31 December 2025 there are no differences between effective IFRS and EU-endorsed IFRS for the Group's accounting policies.
