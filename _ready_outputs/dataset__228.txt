XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025 Consolidated Annual Report, ESG/MDR-T disclosure (section 2.1.3, datapoint E1-4) detailing greenhouse gas emissions accounting methodology and reporting boundaries. Reported emissions cover financial year 2025 (period from 1 January to 31 December 2025), with all dependent entities consolidated under operational and financial control (100% of each unit's emissions). Three reporting scopes defined: Scope 1 (direct emissions from stationary or mobile sources owned or controlled by the company, technological processes, and refrigerant leakage); Scope 2 (indirect emissions from consumption of electrical and thermal energy — district heating, arising at the point of media generation); and Scope 3 (other indirect emissions across the entire value chain). The 2025 reporting represents the second year XTB Group calculated its carbon footprint; the base year remained 2024, with no recalculation of 2024 figures and no identification of material changes versus prior reduction periods. Methodology was developed in collaboration with an external advisor/expert, applying international standards and best practices, based on operational data; the calculation model underwent additional verification by XTB Group's GHG calculation team, including double verification of computations. Advisor assumptions concerned electricity consumption, solid fuels, purchases, transport, and waste. Process steps included a kick-off meeting, workshop, employee education, GHG emissions profile verification, scopes and Scope 3 categories definition, organizational data gathering via customized data-collection templates, verification, and double-checked calculations. Scope 3 categories assessed: Category 1 (Purchased goods and services) — emissions from production and transport of purchased goods/services per XTB accounting principles, primarily technology/IT services, operational and administrative services, telecommunications and infrastructure services, office space maintenance, cleaning, security, repairs, equipment servicing, plus purchase of office and computer materials, office furnishings, industry subscriptions/publications, marketing and advertising services (online and offline), UX research, and other operational support services. Category 2 (Capital goods) — upstream emissions from 2025 purchases of IT and telecommunications equipment, office furnishings/furniture, and other technical equipment and office infrastructure. Category 3 (Energy- and fuel-related emissions not in Scope 1 and 2) — upstream emissions of purchased fuels (extraction, production, transport) consumed in stationary and mobile XTB-owned/controlled sources; upstream emissions of purchased energy; and direct and upstream emissions from transmission and distribution losses of electrical and thermal (district heating) energy. Category 4 (Upstream transportation and distribution) — emissions from transport and distribution of products purchased in the reporting year in vehicles not owned or controlled by XTB Group, including postal and courier services. Category 5 (Waste generated in operations) — emissions from wastewater, municipal waste from XTB-occupied facilities, and electronic waste. Category 6 (Business travel) — emissions from employee business travel and accommodation. Category 7 (Employee commuting) — emissions from employee commuting plus remote-work-generated emissions. Category 11 (Use of sold products) — emissions from end-user application usage estimated on current-year data without full application life-cycle assumptions, covering energy consumption by various electronic devices and emissions from data transmission during application use.
