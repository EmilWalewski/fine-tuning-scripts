Effective tax rate reconciliation and deferred income tax disclosure for Grupa KRUK, drawn from the condensed interim consolidated financial statements (half-year report). The group's effective tax rate diverges from statutory rates principally because consolidation includes entities whose activity is subject to deferred income taxation upon income realization or dividend distribution. Grupa KRUK does not recognize CIT based on a projected average annual effective rate, as this would not eliminate in-year tax irregularities.

Deferred tax movement narrative: net deferred income tax value on 01.01.2024 was negative PLN 142.6M; on 01.01.2025 negative PLN 51.6M; and on 30.06.2025 negative PLN 40.7M (per the asset/provision reconciliation column totals). A parallel hedging-derivatives deferred tax line moved from negative PLN 13.8M (01.01.2024) to negative PLN 17.8M (01.01.2025) to negative PLN 19.97M (30.06.2025). Period changes in temporary differences recognized in profit or loss and in other comprehensive income are disclosed across multiple line items spanning property, plant and equipment; intangible assets; tax losses deductible in future periods; trade and other receivables; liabilities from credits, loans and other debt instruments; employee benefit liabilities; provisions and liabilities; investments in debt portfolios; investments in granted loans; hedging derivatives; and assumed future income outflows from investments in subsidiaries. Figures are stated in thousands of zloty (tys. zł). Selected clearly mapped period-end totals only are cited above; the flattened multi-column matrix rows containing orphaned numeric sequences without clear metric-period mapping are not reconstructed.

Deferred tax provision driver: the provision level is affected by changes in assumed future cash flows from investment companies to KRUK S.A. in the foreseeable future, dependent on KRUK S.A. liquidity needs and obtained/forecast available new debt financing; obtained/forecast available new debt financing within investment companies; planned expenditures on debt portfolios (which influence forecast liquidity in investment companies); and planned collections from purchased debt portfolios in investment companies. Consequently, the deferred tax provision item "Assumed future cash flows from subsidiaries" may be subject to material change across reporting periods.

IAS 12 exemption: the group applies the IAS 12 exemption and does not create a deferred tax provision on retained earnings in related companies where it controls the timing of reversal of temporary differences and it is probable they will not reverse in the foreseeable future. The aggregate temporary differences forming the basis of the unrecognized deferred tax provision on retained earnings totaled PLN 5,908,104 thousand (PLN 5,908.1M) as at 30 June 2025, versus PLN 5,081,798 thousand (PLN 5,081.8M) as at 31 December 2024, an increase of PLN 826.3M year-over-period.

OECD Pillar Two: the group is within the scope of OECD Pillar Two model rules. Pillar Two provisions were enacted in Poland — the jurisdiction in which Kruk S.A. is registered — effective 1 January 2025 under the Act of 6 November 2024 on top-up taxation of constituent entities of international and domestic groups. The group applies the IAS 12 exception regarding recognition and disclosure of deferred tax assets and liabilities related to Pillar Two income taxes. Under the rules, the group will be liable for top-up tax equal to the difference between the jurisdiction's effective tax rate and the 15% minimum rate, once the revenue condition is met — namely consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million in two of the four tax years immediately preceding the tax year. The group estimates Grupa KRUK consolidated revenues may reach at least EUR 750 million at the earliest in 2025 and 2026; in that case Grupa KRUK could be obliged to pay top-up tax for the first time for 2027.

Safe Harbour: if additional statutory conditions are met, Grupa KRUK may use the Act's "Safe Harbour" institution, exempting it from calculating and paying top-up tax in the Polish jurisdiction for a maximum of five years, counted from the first year the revenue condition is met (at the earliest from 2027).

Pillar Two exposure assessment: the group is assessing its exposure. The assessment indicates Grupa KRUK has subsidiaries operating in jurisdictions (Polish, Maltese and Luxembourg) for which the estimated weighted-average effective tax rate based on gross profit for the reporting period ended 30 June 2025 is below 15%. However, despite the sub-15% average effective rate, the group's exposure to paying income taxes under Pillar Two may not reflect the full tax-rate difference for those jurisdictions, owing to the availability of statutory Safe Harbours and the effect of specific adjustments under Pillar Two rules, which create differences between effective tax rates and those computed under IAS 12. Based on current assessment, application of Pillar Two rules is expected to increase the group's effective annual tax rate not earlier than 2027.
