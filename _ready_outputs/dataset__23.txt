Dom Development Group’s internal control and risk management framework assigns the Management Board responsibility for the effectiveness of internal control in financial reporting, while the Supervisory Board, mainly through the Audit Committee, oversees risk management and compliance and reports to the General Meeting on the adequacy and effectiveness of internal control, risk management, compliance and internal audit. The Group defines risk as a potential event that could negatively affect operations, financial results or reputation, and operates a formalized risk management process in place since 2000, updated cyclically to reflect external conditions and the Group’s scale. The system is embedded in organizational culture and corporate governance, based on three lines of defence, and designed to identify material business risks early, assign accountability, translate strategy into operational objectives, support sustainable and responsible business, strengthen operational capability, and assess performance and efficiency across organizational levels. All Group companies participate in identifying risks, assessing probability and impact across core and support activities, and defining mitigation or elimination actions through procedures, processes and internal controls; the system also covers reliability and quality of data relevant to financial statement credibility. In 2025, the Chief Risk and Compliance Officer and Assurance Lead supported Group companies in risk identification and mitigation, effective implementation and updating of procedures, continuity processes, best-practice sharing, scalable process design, legal compliance, management control and compliance culture. The framework is governed by the Internal Standard for Risk Management, Compliance, Audit and Quality of Operations - Dom 2030 and the related Risk Management and Compliance Policy, with Group-level supervision ensuring that entity-level risks are collected, processed, analyzed and assessed using consistent methodology and standards. Responsibilities are structured so that each entity’s Management Board sets acceptable risk levels with business owners, the Group Chief Risk and Compliance Officer coordinates Group-level review of risk management, and the Assurance Lead heads internal audit and independently assesses the adequacy and effectiveness of risk management. Risk, compliance and audit supervisors report directly to the President of Dom Development S.A.’s Management Board, have direct access to Supervisory Board and Audit Committee members, and attend selected management and supervisory meetings. The Group’s risk management objectives include creating and protecting shareholder value, improving efficiency, protecting reputation, minimizing losses, identifying material events early, strengthening financial stability, maintaining risk within defined limits, ensuring internal controls, promoting proactive reporting and multi-horizon risk assessment, ensuring legal and best-practice compliance, continuous improvement through analysis and recommendations, and increasing trust among investors and stakeholders. The process consists of five stages: identification, measurement and assessment using a risk register and probability-impact scales for financial and non-financial effects, monitoring and control, reporting and communication, and management actions including effective internal audit. Oversight operates on three levels: Supervisory Board and Audit Committee supervision, management-board responsibility at Group company level with consolidation by the Company Management Board, and operational actions within coordinated Group and company internal control systems.
