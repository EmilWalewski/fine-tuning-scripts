Excerpt from XTB S.A. Consolidated Annual Report 2025, comprising the Supervisory Board's assessment and the independent statutory auditor's report (PwC, Polna 11, Warsaw, +48 22 746 4000) addressed to the General Meeting and Supervisory Board. The Supervisory Board of XTB S.A. reviewed and assessed the 2025 standalone financial statements of XTB S.A., the 2025 consolidated financial statements of the XTB Capital Group, and the 2025 Management Board activity report for the Group and Company. The Board issued a positive assessment, concluding the statements present reliably and clearly all information material to evaluating the asset and financial position of the Company and Group, and are consistent with books, documents, and actual state. The assessment was based on: the statements submitted by the Management Board; the independent auditor's reports on the 2025 standalone and consolidated audits plus the additional report for the Audit Committee prepared under EU Regulation 537/2014 (16 April 2014) and the Polish Act on Statutory Auditors, Audit Firms and Public Oversight (11 May 2017); the independent auditor's limited-assurance attestation report on sustainability reporting; meetings with the audit firm including the key statutory auditor; meetings with the Management Board; and other verification procedures in selected financial and operational areas. Legal basis: Minister of Finance Regulation of 6 June 2025 on current and periodic information by securities issuers (§72(1)(16); §73(1)(14)). Signed Warsaw, 19 March 2026, with qualified electronic signature, by Supervisory Board Chairman Aleksander Chłopecki. A separate Supervisory Board statement concerning the Audit Committee (legal basis §72(1)(8); §73(1)(8)) confirms compliance with rules on appointment, composition, and functioning of the audit committee, including member independence and required sector, accounting, and audit knowledge; that conditions for entrusting audit-committee functions to the Supervisory Board are met; that conditions for entrusting sustainability-reporting and attestation audit-committee functions to a separate committee are met; and that the Supervisory Board's Audit Committee performed its statutory duties; signed 19 March 2026 by Chairman Aleksander Chłopecki. The auditor's opinion section confirms the consolidated financial statements as of 31 December 2025 and is consistent with the additional report to the Audit Committee. Audit materiality was set using a base of pre-tax profit over 2023-2025, given that pre-tax profit is subject to material fluctuations across individual years. The report covers the audited consolidated income/comprehensive income statements and notes (referencing notes 4.4, 4.11, 5.1, 15, 21). A key audit matter is recognition of result on financial instruments (realized and unrealized gains/losses), involving mass client transaction processing, derivative valuation, expert knowledge, and IT systems. The report also addresses ESEF compliance (Single European Electronic Format; file XTB-2025-12-31-1-pl.xbrl), XBRL tagging verification under ISAE 3000 (revised), KSUA 3001PL and KSUA 3000(Z) standards.
