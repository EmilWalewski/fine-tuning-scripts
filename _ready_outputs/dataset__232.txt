Excerpt from the 2025 Consolidated Sustainability Report (Management Board Report on the activities of Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group for 2025) covering Tax Practices and EU Taxonomy disclosures. The Group adheres to tax law compliance and timely settlement of tax liabilities as a core principle, factoring tax consequences into business decisions based on existing statutory interpretation plus general and individual tax rulings. It cooperates with tax authorities, providing legally required information for correct determination of tax liabilities, and maintains relationships with tax authorities founded on mutual respect and trust while filing all returns and making payments on time. Internal tax teams work to minimize tax risk to an acceptable level, monitor new and changing tax regulations, assess their impact on the Group post-enactment, take proactive compliance measures, and collaborate continuously with professional tax and reporting advisors to adapt systems and software. Group companies transact with both related and unrelated parties at arm's-length (market) prices; prior to each transaction an assessment is made whether transfer-pricing regulations apply, whether market terms are met, and whether transfer-pricing documentation is required. Companies holding tax strategies under applicable law: Dom Development S.A., Dom Construction Sp. z o.o., Dom Development Wroclaw Sp. z o.o., Euro Styl S.A., and Euro Styl Construction Sp. z o.o. The Group concludes its activity complies with the OECD Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises and the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights, does not breach environmental or social objectives, and notes that no proceedings were conducted against the Group in 2025, indicating operation in accordance with minimum safeguards. For the third time the Group discloses the share of taxonomy-eligible activity for the reporting period 01.01.2025-31.12.2025, this year applying new table templates per Regulation 2026/73.
