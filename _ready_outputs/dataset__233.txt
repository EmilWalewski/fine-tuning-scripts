XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025 Consolidated Annual Report excerpt detailing greenhouse-gas (GHG) emissions methodology and ESG climate disclosures, structured by GHG Protocol Scope 3 categories. GHG calculations performed at entity level where survey frequency sufficed; otherwise aggregated Group-wide XTB figures applied. Methodology incorporates direct combustion emissions, well-to-tank (WTT) emissions for electricity production, and direct plus WTT emissions from transmission losses; per GHG Protocol such Category WTT emissions are optional but were voluntarily included for the XTB Group.

Category 6 (Business travel): some entities derived figures from cost accounts, so reported amounts are not uniformly net as required by GHG Protocol. Category 7 (Employee commuting): emissions calculated from a 2025 commuting and remote-work survey completed by XTB Group employees; 51% response rate, with collected data extrapolated to the full workforce where the response level for a given entity/branch reached at least 20%. Category 5 (Generated waste): where data was missing, estimates were based on archival data or comparable waste-fraction data from other subsidiaries; average waste per employee was multiplied by headcount. Absent disposal-method information for municipal bio-waste and mixed municipal waste fractions, a conservative landfill assumption was applied. E-waste values reported in liters were converted to kilograms using e-waste density from the SEPA database. For electronic equipment, laptops, monitors, and PCs, average disposal-emission factors were taken from EPD cards of electronic devices purchased in 2025 across all XTB S.A. branches and subsidiaries; remaining e-waste used DEFRA 2025 emission factors.

Category 8: emissions from operation of leased assets already captured under Scope 1 and Scope 2 given the organizational boundaries adopted. Category 9: not applicable, Group conducts no product-distribution-to-customers transport activity. Category 10: not applicable, firm produces/sells no goods requiring further customer processing. Category 11 (Use of sold products): emissions calculated per international ICT7 standard using actual 2025 application-usage data; inputs include average user login time per country split by interface (mobile application and xStation web platform), computed separately for individual and retail clients and limited to the annual period. For Linux, Macintosh, or Windows devices, a conservative assumption of desktop PC plus monitor usage was applied (laptops omitted due to lack of market-share data and lower energy use). For phones and tablets, average operating-system energy consumption was weighted by global market share. Application energy consumption was assumed equal to total device energy consumption; data-transmission emissions were included, with average per-session data transfer determined via test. Per the Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Accounting and Reporting Standard, Category 11 emissions from end-user product use cover end-users' Scope 1 and Scope 2; WTT emissions excluded from this category.

Category 12: not applicable, XTB Group does not distribute software on physical media (per ICT Sector Guidance built on GHG Protocol Product Life Cycle Accounting and Reporting Standard, end-of-life software emissions apply only to physical media). Category 13: not applicable, no asset-leasing activity. Category 14: not applicable, no franchise activity. Category 15 (Investments): XTB Group purchases company shares on a fiduciary/custodial basis, allocating fractional rights to clients; per agreement the Group accepts fractional-rights purchase orders and undertakes to acquire whole shares of the client-selected company, allowing clients to derive benefits proportionally. Consequently the Group cannot select companies or manage portfolios, as holdings result from client transactions. ICT software emissions (operating systems, applications, web platforms) are estimated on an ongoing basis; hardware emissions (servers, computers, laptops, smartphones, tablets) are calculated over average useful life (typically 2-5 years) per physical lifespan and replacement cycle.

Estimation scopes for XTB Group emissions: Scope 1 (stationary combustion, fugitive emissions), Scope 2 (electricity, district heat), Scope 3 cat. 1 (water consumption), Scope 3 cat. 5 (generated waste), and Scope 3 cat. 7 (employee commuting and remote work). GHG calculations are not subject to independent third-party approval except for sustainability assurance; calculations were executed with an external expert who certified them as confirmation of correctness.

E1-6 / MDR-M energy disclosures: in 2025, 100% of electricity consumed at XTB Central Office derived from renewable sources (wind energy), confirmed by Polish Power Exchange (Towarowa Gielda Energii) guarantees of origin. The share of externally purchased renewable-source electricity reached 11.3% of total externally purchased electricity Group-wide in 2025, down 2.9 percentage points year-over-year. Both purchased electricity and renewable energy originated from the same supplier. Neither the manager of the Skyliner office building housing XTB S.A. Central Office nor the XTB Group resells energy to third parties. Electricity-consumption data collected for 2025 GHG calculations was also used to monitor the Group's identified material climate and environmental impact associated with energy-intensive IT infrastructure.
