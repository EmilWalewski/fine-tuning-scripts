Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group profile, covering full-year 2025 sales and units in construction/land bank as at 31 December 2025; market shares per Rynekpierwotny.pl estimates and headcount per employment contracts as at 31.12.2025. Warszawa: Group is undisputed leader of the Warsaw residential market, current sales market share 13.2%; Warsaw is Poland's principal business and academic hub, attracting domestic and foreign residents, with the highest residential prices among Polish cities, yet among the most demanding development markets due to limited land availability, complex legal land status, and lengthening administrative procedures; Group present in the capital since inception, building a strong brand and know-how providing competitive advantage. Wrocław: Group active since 2008 via Dom Development Wrocław Sp. z o.o., now the No. 1 developer on this market with 12.4% market share on 816 units sold, 1,033 units under construction, 1,711 units in land bank, 116 employees; held land resources for future projects enable further growth of the Wrocław contribution to Group results. A separate Wrocław data block reports 13.2% market share, 2,015 units sold, 2,861 units under construction, and 9,783 units in land bank. Trójmiasto (Tri-City): 2017 was pivotal as the Group entered the Tri-City market through the acquisition of Euro Styl S.A., materially increasing scale; efficient organization, similar corporate culture and business approach made the acquisition a success; in 2025 Euro Styl sold 1,082 units in the Tri-City area and surroundings, 12.5% of total sales in the Tri-City Agglomeration, its second-highest result in history, ranking first among local developers by annual sales; acquisition gave Euro Styl easier access to capital, used effectively, substantially expanding activity and future-project potential while remaining highly profitable. Tri-City data block: 12.5% market share, 1,082 units sold, 2,193 units under construction, 5,439 units in land bank, 146 employees. Kraków: in 2025 the Group sold a record 535 apartments, 7.8% of total regional sales, ranking second among developers; Kraków data block confirms 7.8% market share, 535 units sold, 1,079 units under construction, 1,899 units in land bank, 83 employees. Strategy of prudent expansion ("rozważna ekspansja") in selected agglomerations only: Group operates exclusively in Poland's leading agglomerations with stable residential-market growth prospects enabling attractive margins; market strength confirmed by key macroeconomic indicators including unemployment rate, average wage level, and relatively high average primary-market transaction prices for apartments. Source notes: transaction prices for Q4 2025 per NBP; data for December 2025 per GUS; status as at 31.12.2025 per GUS.
