Excerpt from the XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025 (Sustainability Statement / General Information), describing core products, services, and 2025 product-offering developments. The Group's investment offering comprises over 11,000 investment products, classified into three groups: equities/ETFs, Exchange Traded Products (ETPs), and CFDs. The offering includes shares listed on the 16 largest securities exchanges worldwide; over 7,000 listed company shares; and ETFs (Exchange Traded Funds). ETPs encompass ETC and ETN instruments, whose essence is replicating the rate of return of a reference index via physical or synthetic replication; XTB offers clients over 1,800 ETPs as well as ESG index-based ETFs. CFDs (Contracts for Difference) are over-the-counter derivatives between two parties ("buyer" and "seller") referencing the price movement of an underlying asset (covering indices, shares, commodities, precious metals, and cryptocurrencies); XTB also offers ESG index-based CFDs. Listed corporate shares are characterized by high price volatility over both short- and long-term horizons.

Long-term/retirement savings products: In 2025 XTB expanded its tax-advantaged savings range. In Poland, building on 2024 launches of Individual Retirement Accounts (IKE) and Individual Retirement Security Accounts (IKZE), the IKZE account was added to the Polish offering in Q3 2025, and pending orders were added to the IKE account. In the United Kingdom, XTB enabled full or partial transfer of an existing ISA (Individual Savings Account) to XTB, and implemented a Cash ISA / Flexible Cash and Stocks & Shares ISA variant allowing tax-free saving of interest income within the UK annual ISA contribution limit (permitting re-deposit without affecting the annual allowance). In France, in Q2 2025 XTB launched PEA (Plan d'Epargne en Actions) accounts, enabling investment in shares and ETFs under favorable tax conditions.

Free-cash interest: XTB pays variable interest on clients' uninvested free funds; rates are variable and may change daily, interest is calculated daily and paid cyclically during the first five business days; the feature can be activated automatically, with no minimum or maximum free-funds thresholds applied to the investor.

Investment Plans: ETF-based passive investing solution allowing investors to build a diversified portfolio with low engagement and low cost, from as little as PLN 50, providing market exposure across broad markets, sectors, or geographic regions via funds available in XTB's offering.

eWallet (multi-currency card / virtual wallet): XTB offers clients a virtual wallet with a card supporting cashless in-store payments, mobile and contactless transactions, and ATM cash withdrawals worldwide. The eWallet supports accounts in 25 currencies, including EUR, USD, GBP, PLN, HUF, RON, CZK, CHF, JPY, and CAD, plus fast and free transfers between accounts, providing instant access to funds. The card is issued under Mastercard license by DiPocket UAB, an Electronic Money Institution registered by the Bank of Lithuania. The eWallet service is currently available to clients in Poland, the Czech Republic, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Germany, France, Spain, and Italy. XTB launched eWallet for clients in Poland at the start of 2025; nearly 22,000 clients activated the virtual wallet in that period, of which 43% in Poland and 57% from abroad.

XTB Social service: No decisions were made in 2025 regarding the future of the XTB Social community service launched in March 2024, which was intended to allow users in selected markets to receive notifications of selected investors' transactions and view their portfolios and key trading statistics with full anonymization. In June 2024 the Company suspended this service in the European Union following the Polish Financial Supervision Authority (KNF) position of 29 May concerning conditions for investment firms providing "social trading" type services.
