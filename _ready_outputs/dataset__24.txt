In 2025 Dom Development Group delivered record commercial performance, with Q4 net sales of 1,232 units, including 586 in Warsaw, 325 in Tricity, 168 in Wrocław and 153 in Kraków, marking its best quarterly result and the sixth consecutive quarter above 1,000 units sold. Full-year net sales reached 4,448 units, up 4% from the previous record of 4,269 units in 2024 and described as the strongest annual retail-segment sales result ever reported by a developer in Poland. Demand was supported by six Monetary Policy Council rate cuts totaling 1.75 p.p., higher wages, improved mortgage affordability and increased sales-office traffic, while Q4 also reflected the new apartment price transparency regime, which required changes in sales and marketing communication and favored larger developers with in-house marketing capacity. The Group’s diversified offer attracted both mortgage and cash buyers, with sell-out indicators below market averages across all markets. Retail handovers rose 8% y/y to 4,228 units from 3,916 in 2024, while PRS handovers totaled 97 units versus 300 in 2024; including PRS, total handovers reached a record 4,325 units, supporting results alongside stable construction costs. Shareholder returns were also record-high: on 12 June 2025 the General Meeting approved a PLN 13.00 per share dividend for 2024, totaling PLN 335.380 M, including PLN 154.791 M paid as an interim dividend in December 2024 and PLN 180.589 M paid on 3 July 2025, with dividend day on 25 June 2025. The payout represented 59% of Group 2024 net profit and 80% of Company 2024 net profit, while PLN 83.640 M of remaining Company profit was transferred to supplementary capital. In 2025 the Company also approved its third-ever interim dividend for the current year, PLN 7.00 per share or PLN 180.589 M in total, with dividend day on 2 December and payment on 9 December, consistent with its policy of increasing dividends over time while maintaining liquidity, balance-sheet strength and funding for development, particularly the land bank. In financing, on 14 April 2025 the Management Board terminated the prior bond program capped at PLN 400.000 M outstanding nominal value, while continuing to service PLN 260.000 M of series DOMDEM1280928 unsecured bearer bonds maturing on 28 September 2028 and PLN 140.000 M of series DOMDEM2051229 bonds maturing on 5 December 2029. The same day it approved a new bond issuance program of up to PLN 400.000 M outstanding nominal value with mBank S.A., allowing ordinary unsecured dematerialized bearer bonds in multiple series for an indefinite period, with maturities up to 60 months, fixed or floating interest, public offers exempt from a prospectus or non-public offers to one investor, potential mBank subscription guarantees, and introduction to trading in the Warsaw Stock Exchange alternative trading system.
