Excerpt from Dom Development S.A. Group's 2025 Consolidated Sustainability Report (Section 3, SBM-2: Interests and Views of Stakeholders), part of the Management Board Report on the activities of Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group for 2025. Establishes that stakeholder interests, views, and rights materially shape the Group's strategy and business model, determining action directions, management approach, and external relations. Four stakeholder categories profiled. Own-workforce employees: their interests, views, and rights influence strategy via organizational culture, operational efficiency, and customer relations; expectations of fair remuneration, development opportunities, and safe working conditions drive Group investment in training and motivational systems; their engagement affects quality of customer service and effectiveness of project delivery, while respect for employee rights builds team loyalty and positive corporate image, enhancing competitiveness and long-term success. Value-chain workers (subcontractor employees engaged in investment execution): their work quality determines timeliness and quality of project delivery; respect for their rights, including decent remuneration and safe working conditions, minimizes risk of investment interruptions; cooperation with responsible subcontractors strengthens corporate image, reduces operational risks, and increases long-term business stability. Local communities: residents' opinions are significant for project acceptance; Group companies conduct public consultations and incorporate local perspectives; observance of community rights, including regulations protecting residents' interests, is key to avoiding conflicts and building positive relations, reinforcing corporate image and supporting long-term market success. Customers and end users: expect transparency, fairness, and high service quality, shaping the firm's operating standards and product offering; their preferences regarding location, finishing standard, and sustainable solutions influence investment design and market offering; customer rights, such as personal data protection and warranties, enforce regulatory compliance and trust-building; incorporating customer needs into strategy enables competitive advantage and long-term success.
