Consolidated Sustainability Statement of the Dom Development S.A. Capital Group within the Management Board Report on operations for 2025 (Section E1-5 Energy Consumption and Energy Mix; Section E1-6 Gross GHG Emissions Scopes 1, 2, 3 and Total GHG Emissions). The Group operates in a high climate-impact sector; under its business model and activities, 100% of revenue is classified as derived from high climate-impact sectors. Energy demand arises principally from purchased electricity and heat during construction works, administrative activity, and use of company vehicles. Fuel and energy data are sourced from settlement invoices indicating actual consumption; conversion factors applied: 1 MWh = 1,000 kWh = 3.6 GJ = 0.086 toe. Baseline year 2021 was selected reflecting external factors (future legal changes) and internal factors (change in operating scale and ESG approach) and was independently verified.

In 2025 total energy consumption declined slightly versus 2024, directly linked to a minimal decrease in Group production output. Purchased RES energy share rose to 39% (from 18% prior year), confirmed by guarantees of origin received; the renewable share in the overall mix remains modest but is expected to grow successively in coming years. Group companies already implement measures to reduce energy use and emissions, including procurement of renewable-source energy and progressive replacement of the vehicle fleet with lower-emission vehicles.

E1-6 GHG Emissions: Scope 1 covers direct emissions from sources owned or controlled by the Group, including stationary combustion (natural gas, heating oil), fuel in mobile equipment and generators (diesel), vehicle fleet (diesel, petrol), and fugitive sources (e.g., air-conditioner leaks). Biogenic CO2 emissions from fuel combustion identified within Scope 1 amounted to 43.969 tCO2. No purchased, sold, or transferred carbon units or GHG allowances are included in Scope 1 calculations. Scope 2 covers indirect emissions from purchased electricity, heat, steam, and cooling; reported under two methodologies, market-based and location-based. Location-based quantifies Scope 2 emissions using average emission factors for energy production in specific locations including local boundaries; market-based quantifies emissions based on emissions from generators from which the entity makes contractual electricity purchases combined with instruments such as guarantees of origin or renewable energy certificates. The Group has no biogenic CO2 emissions from biomass combustion or biodegradation, no process emissions, and no fugitive Scope 2 emissions; indicators to calculate biogenic emissions from purchased electricity and heat are currently unavailable. No removals or purchased/sold/transferred carbon units or GHG allowances are included in Scope 2 calculations. Scope 3 covers other indirect emissions resulting from Group activity but originating from sources not owned or controlled by the Group. In 2025 there were no significant changes to the definition of the reporting entity and its upstream and downstream value chain that could affect year-over-year comparability of GHG emissions.

GHG intensity (Scope 1+2 emissions per net revenue) reported figures: location-based 3.98 tCO2e/PLN million, market-based 4.12 tCO2e/PLN million, with further reported values 8.36, 1.93, 2.98, and 8.51 tCO2e/PLN million; the precise metric-period mapping of these additional values is not unambiguous in the source and is therefore not attributed to specific periods.
