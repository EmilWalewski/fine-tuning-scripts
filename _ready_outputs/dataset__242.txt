XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025 (Management Board report on operations / Business model, Strategy and operations). Technology and product development positioned as near-strategic drivers of revenue and competitive global capital-market positioning. During 2025 XTB enhanced tools dedicated to institutional clients, enabling smoother handling of trading-session configuration change requests, and modernized system architecture to improve efficiency and raise transaction security. R&D focus areas: functionality and security of systems, processes and databases, plus development of new electronic-trading systems; for 2026 the technology team prioritizes continuous analysis and deployment of security-related functionality. A dedicated Product and Technology Division was carved out within XTB's structure, with a substantial share of staff performing R&D work.

Security initiatives in 2025: phased rollout of automatic 2FA login for existing and new clients, now enabled for over 98% of clients, with client-side disabling no longer permitted. The Company launched a web-security awareness site (https://pl.xtb.com/bezpieczenstwo-w-sieci) covering cybercriminal fraud methods. In Q4 2025 the technology team began work on an in-app emergency account-blocking and logout feature for suspected unauthorized access; this functionality was deployed in Q1 2026. Planned security measures include verification of inbound calls from XTB to clients to reduce data-phishing attempts by impersonators (a practice common in banking). The mobile app received a refreshed, more user-friendly main view, added instrument-view sorting functionality per client demand, and a modernized news module.

Marketing executed in coherent phases. Q1: out-of-home campaigns using external advertising media (billboards, public-space posters); in Poland promoting the IKE account under the slogan "Inwestowanie zaczynam od IKE"; foreign markets covered Czech Republic, Slovakia, Germany, Portugal and Romania with both outdoor and television campaigns. Q2 2025: marketing expanded toward new-product promotion and global brand recognition. Early April, tied to introducing PEA accounts for clients in France, a promotional campaign featured brand ambassador Zlatan Ibrahimovic, with outdoor ads on Paris streets and non-standard locations including select golf courses. In the same period XTB engaged in sports sponsorship, displaying its logo on athletes' apparel at two tennis Grand Slam tournaments, Roland Garros and Wimbledon.

Product and Technology Division headcount and costs (costs in thousands PLN; headcount includes employment contracts, mandate contracts and B2B service contracts):
- Division costs: FY2025 255,162k PLN (255.2M PLN); FY2024 165,839k (165.8M); FY2023 94,770k (94.8M); FY2022 58,381k (58.4M); FY2021 36,616k (36.6M); FY2020 27,159k (27.2M); FY2019 21,151k (21.2M).
- Division headcount: 2025: 616; 2024: 498; 2023: 429; 2022: 282; 2021: 176; 2020: 129; 2019: 116.
Trend: division costs rose ~53.9% year-over-year (165.8M to 255.2M PLN) and headcount grew from 498 to 616 (+118), continuing a sustained multi-year expansion from 21.2M PLN/116 persons (2019).

Product offering development cited as a key strategic area expanding the Group's reach, addressing both safer-style investors and those accepting higher market risk (details referenced in "Produkty" within Business model and "Plan produktowy 2026" within Strategy and operations). Marketing channels are tied to strategic goals: continually increasing share of the global fintech market, pursuing a leading position in international investment services, and acquiring the mass-market client.

2025 business-objective execution: (1) Strengthening positions in Western and Central-Eastern European markets via continual client-base expansion, introducing newly anticipated products — IKZE account in Poland and PEA for clients in France — supported by large marketing campaigns across traditional media (television, VOD), outdoor media (billboards, public-space advertising) and the internet (portals, social media). (2) Further geographic expansion into new markets in Latin America and Asia; in 2025 the Group focused non-European efforts on Indonesia and Brazil, obtaining requisite licenses and permits from local financial-market regulators. The Indonesian subsidiary acquired its first clients in 2025, offering equities and ETF funds; in Brazil, given local brokerage-sector conditions and protectionism, XTB is considering all business options including ceasing further activity in that market. (3) Expansion of the Group's products and services and development of technological innovation; in 2025 the Group added the eWallet — a virtual wallet with a multicurrency card supporting cashless in-store payments, mobile and contactless transactions, and worldwide ATM cash withdrawals, enabling clients to maintain accounts.

XTB's growth strategy rests on five pillars aimed at building a strong, globally valued brand, with the Group intending to systematically reinforce its market position through a diversified product offering attractive to investors of differing preferences, financial resources, knowledge and experience.
