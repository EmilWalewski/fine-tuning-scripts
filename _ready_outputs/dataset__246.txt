XTB S.A. Capital Group Q3 2025 report (interim condensed consolidated financials), all figures in thousands PLN unless noted. Property, plant and equipment movements for FY2024: gross value rose from 94.7M PLN (1 Jan 2024) to 121.1M PLN (31 Dec 2024), driven by acquisitions (nabycie) of 19.3M PLN (17.3M PLN computer equipment; 1.6M PLN other PP&E; 0.3M PLN PP&E under construction), lease additions of 14.9M PLN (right-of-use office premises), offset by sales/disposals of 7.2M PLN and net FX differences of -0.6M PLN. Accumulated depreciation deepened from -44.3M PLN to -55.7M PLN (current-period amortization -19.4M PLN; disposals +7.8M PLN; FX +0.2M PLN). Net book value increased from 50.4M PLN (1 Jan 2024) to 65.3M PLN (31 Dec 2024); components at year-end: computer equipment 23.6M PLN, other PP&E 8.6M PLN, right-of-use office premises 32.4M PLN, right-of-use vehicles 0.12M PLN, PP&E under construction 0.6M PLN.

Non-current assets by geography (Razem): 68.0M PLN at 30.09.2025 vs 67.3M PLN at 31.12.2024. Breakdown 30.09.2025: Central-Eastern Europe 43.7M PLN (of which Poland 38.7M PLN), Western Europe 14.3M PLN, Latin America 0.5M PLN, Middle East 8.3M PLN, Asia 1.1M PLN.

Liabilities to clients (Note 18) rose sharply to 5,933.7M PLN at 30.09.2025 from 4,164.9M PLN at 31.12.2024: retail clients 5,847.4M PLN (up from 4,082.8M PLN), institutional clients 86.4M PLN (from 82.1M PLN); these arise from client transactions including cash deposited on client accounts.

Financial liabilities at fair value through profit or loss (Note 19, CFD derivatives) totaled 259.7M PLN at 30.09.2025 vs 208.2M PLN at 31.12.2024: CFD on shares/ETP 84.6M PLN, on commodities 90.7M PLN, on currencies 56.3M PLN, on indices 28.1M PLN, on bonds 0.005M PLN.

Lease liabilities (Note 20) totaled 28.1M PLN at 30.09.2025 (short-term 11.3M PLN; long-term 16.7M PLN) vs 33.9M PLN at 31.12.2024. Short-term lease costs recognized in comprehensive income: 0.433M PLN (Jan-Sep 2025) and 0.399M PLN (Jan-Sep 2024); no low-value asset lease costs. Group is lessee for office space and vehicle rentals.

Other liabilities (Note 21) totaled 198.2M PLN at 30.09.2025 vs 156.9M PLN at 31.12.2024: trade payables 69.7M PLN, payables to brokers 66.9M PLN, provisions for other employee benefits 29.8M PLN, public-law liabilities 21.8M PLN, liabilities to KDPW 8.9M PLN, payroll liabilities 1.1M PLN.

Variable Remuneration Components Program: persons materially affecting the parent's risk profile receive annual variable remuneration paid in XTB S.A. shares; share-based payment costs recognized in Group equity.

Provisions (Note 22.1) totaled 3.5M PLN at 30.09.2025 vs 3.5M PLN at 31.12.2024: pension provisions 0.504M PLN, legal-risk provisions 2.982M PLN. Movements Jan-Sep 2025 (from 3.530M PLN to 3.486M PLN): utilization 0.044M PLN total (pension -0.014M PLN, legal-risk -0.030M PLN); no increases or releases. Pension provisions are actuarially valued. Legal-risk provisions cover expected disputed-case payouts; Group cannot determine expected payment timing.

Contingent liabilities (Note 22.2): parent and Group companies are defendants in several lawsuits, mainly employee and client claims. At 30.09.2025 total claims pursued against the Group were ~16.5M PLN (16,540k PLN), of which ~16.0M PLN (16,039k PLN) unprovisioned; at 31.12.2024 these were 16,134k PLN and ~14,924k PLN respectively. No provisions created; Group assesses low probability of losing. On 9 May 2014 the parent issued a guarantee of 0.054M PLN (54k PLN) securing a contract between subsidiary XTB Limited (UK) and PayPal (Europe) Sarl & Cie SCA (Luxembourg), for the term of the indefinite main agreement.
