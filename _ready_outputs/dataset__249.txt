XTB S.A. Capital Group Q3 2025 Report excerpt detailing factors affecting financial/operational results, development strategy, and group structure. XTB's business model features high revenue volatility quarter-to-quarter, driven principally by: number of active clients, deposit volumes, transaction volumes on FX/CFD financial instruments, market competition, general market conditions, financial/commodity market volatility, geopolitical and economic environment, and the regulatory environment. The model combines agency and market-maker characteristics, with XTB acting as counterparty to client-initiated transactions; it does not engage in proprietary trading in the strict sense. For most crypto-based CFD instruments, XTB hedges transactions with external partners, effectively ceasing to be the counterparty (though legally remaining XTB). A fully automated risk-management process limits exposure to market changes and enforces position hedging to maintain proper capital requirement levels. All equity and ETP transactions, and CFDs based on these assets, are executed directly on regulated markets or alternative trading systems; XTB is not a market maker for this instrument class. Higher financial/commodity market activity generally benefits Group revenue via higher client turnover and per-lot profitability; clear, prolonged market trends generate peak revenues. Declining market activity reduces operating revenues. Range trading conditions (predictable, narrow price ranges) produce more profitable client transactions, lowering the Group's market-making result. Market volatility stems from external, market-specific and macroeconomic factors materially affecting future-quarter revenues.

Development strategy rests on five pillars aimed at building a strong global brand, systematically strengthening market position through a diversified product offering for investors of varying preferences, financial resources, knowledge, and experience. As a dynamically developing fintech, XTB integrates ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) criteria into its business strategy; in 2024 XTB adopted an ESG Strategy for 2024–2027. Sustainability commitments include: responsible management practices, regulated operations, risk management, further geographic expansion into new markets including Latin America and Asia, responsible payment practices, technology and product portfolio development, further development and strengthening of the institutional client segment via X Open Hub, expansion of products/services and technological innovation, responsible marketing, transparent communication, financial education, business ethics, fair competition, mature organizational culture, growth through acquisitions/mergers/joint ventures, human rights and competitive employment conditions, employee development and competence building, and diversity and flexibility.

Group structure: parent company XTB S.A., 7 foreign branches, and 12 subsidiaries. Branches and subsidiaries support international expansion and operations, located in strategic financial centers of Europe, Latin America, and the Middle East. Under the MiFID II single European passport, XTB operates as a branch under KNF authorization in the following EU member states: Czech Republic, Spain, Slovakia, Romania, Germany, France, and Portugal. As of 30 September 2025, the Group comprised 12 subsidiaries. On 23 September 2025, the liquidation of XTB Digital Ltd. (Cyprus) was completed effective that date.
