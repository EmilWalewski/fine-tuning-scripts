Semi-annual consolidated financial report presenting selected financial data for the condensed interim consolidated financial statements covering 01.01.2025-30.06.2025, with comparatives for the prior-year period (01.01.2024-30.06.2024) and balance-sheet positions as at 30.06.2025 versus 31.12.2024; all figures unaudited (niebadane) and stated in PLN thousands (converted below to millions PLN). Income statement and cash flow figures translated to EUR at NBP arithmetic average of month-end rates: current period 4.2208, comparative period 4.3109; statement of financial position items translated at NBP closing rate: current period-end 4.2419, comparative period-end 4.2730.

Sales revenue rose to 1,599.7M PLN (H1 2025) from 1,480.6M PLN (H1 2024), a year-over-year increase (EUR 379.0M vs EUR 343.4M). Operating profit increased to 804.1M PLN from 770.7M PLN (EUR 190.5M vs EUR 178.8M). Profit before tax was essentially flat at 585.4M PLN versus 586.0M PLN (EUR 138.7M vs EUR 135.9M). Net profit attributable to shareholders of the Parent Entity declined to 584.0M PLN from 602.7M PLN (EUR 138.4M vs EUR 139.8M). Net cash from operating activities improved to 398.5M PLN from 333.5M PLN (EUR 94.4M vs EUR 77.4M). Purchase of portfolios at contractual prices ("zakup pakietów wg cen wynikających z umowy") decreased to 805.5M PLN from 899.7M PLN (EUR 190.8M vs EUR 208.7M). Collections from indebted persons ("wpłaty od osób zadłużonych") rose to 1,910.3M PLN from 1,737.5M PLN (EUR 452.6M vs EUR 403.1M), indicating a debt-collection / receivables-portfolio business model. Net cash from investing activities was an outflow of (40.8M) PLN versus (5.4M) PLN prior year (EUR (9.7M) vs EUR (1.3M)), a larger investing outflow. Net cash from financing activities was an outflow of (306.3M) PLN versus (535.4M) PLN (EUR (72.6M) vs EUR (124.2M)), a reduced financing outflow. Net change in cash was a positive 51.3M PLN versus a negative (207.4M) PLN in the comparative period (EUR 12.2M vs EUR (48.1M)), a swing to net cash inflow.

Diluted earnings per share were 28.71 PLN (H1 2025) versus 29.46 PLN (H1 2024) (EUR 6.80 vs EUR 6.83). Basic earnings per share were 30.12 PLN versus 31.20 PLN (EUR 7.14 vs EUR 7.24). Weighted average number of shares was 19,392 thousand versus 19,319 thousand.

Statement of financial position (as at 30.06.2025 vs 31.12.2024): Total assets grew to 12,098.9M PLN from 11,648.9M PLN (EUR 2,852.2M vs EUR 2,726.2M). Long-term liabilities were broadly stable at 6,491.3M PLN versus 6,504.5M PLN (EUR 1,530.3M vs EUR 1,522.2M). Short-term liabilities increased to 844.4M PLN from 615.7M PLN (EUR 199.1M vs EUR 144.1M). Equity (kapitał własny) rose to 4,763.3M PLN from 4,528.7M PLN (EUR 1,122.9M vs EUR 1,059.8M). Share capital (kapitał akcyjny) was 19.4M PLN versus 19.4M PLN (PLN 19,403k vs PLN 19,382k; EUR 4,574k vs EUR 4,536k). Book value per ordinary share increased to 245.49 PLN from 233.65 PLN (EUR 57.87 vs EUR 54.68).
