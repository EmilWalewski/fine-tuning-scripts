Regarding factors expected to affect results over at least the coming quarter (item 7.11), the Management Board of Asseco Poland assesses that the Group's current financial position, production potential, and market position pose no threats to its continued operation and development for a period of at least 12 months from the end of the reporting period. The Board identifies multiple internal and external factors that may directly or indirectly influence the Group's financial results. External factors include: developments in the economic and political situation of Poland, the European Union, and other countries where the Group operates, including the impact of the geopolitical situation in Ukraine and the Gaza Strip (described in point 2.2 of the interim condensed consolidated financial statements); inflation and currency exchange-rate fluctuations (primarily the US dollar and euro, but also currencies of countries where the Group operates); increases or decreases in demand for IT solutions in the financial-banking sector, public administration, and the enterprise sector; shortage of qualified IT experts in a demanding labor market (risk of rising labor costs); intensifying competition from both Polish and foreign IT companies, particularly when competing for large and prestigious contracts; changes in clients' credit situation, financial liquidity, and financing capacity; changes in interest rates and bank margins; opportunities and risks tied to relatively rapid technological change and innovation in the IT market; and the risk of potential clients deferring IT investment decisions. Internal factors include: execution of complex IT projects conducted under long-term contracts; results of ongoing commercial activities in both the domestic and foreign markets; the need to retain the most qualified and key employees; results of work on new products; and execution of the Group's operating strategy assuming organic growth and expansion into new foreign markets.

No other material information is known beyond that listed above whose disclosure could materially affect the assessment of the Group's personnel, asset, or financial situation (item 7.12).
