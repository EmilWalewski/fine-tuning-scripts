Operating segment disclosure for a debt-collection group reporting under consolidated profit-and-loss materiality criteria, with management reports reviewed at least quarterly by the Parent's Management Board (President of the Management Board). Three reportable business segments: purchased debt portfolios (recovery of acquired receivables portfolios); debt-collection services (recovery performed on commission, in the client's name); and other (financial intermediation, lending, provision of economic/credit information). Primary segment performance measures are indirect margin (Marża pośrednia = revenue less direct and indirect costs) and EBITDA (= indirect margin less general costs), per internal management reporting. Geographic operations span Poland, Romania, Czechia, Slovakia, Germany, Spain, Italy and France; reporting geographies are Poland, Romania, Italy, Spain and Other foreign markets, with area revenue based on geographic location of collection branches. Debt-collection services and other-product revenues derive from business partners; no inter-segment revenues occurred.
