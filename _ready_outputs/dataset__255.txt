XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025, financial results and dividend section. Group revenue reached 8,866M PLN in 2025, up from 6,274M PLN in 2024, continuing a multi-year ascent: 1,639M PLN (2019), 3,113M PLN (2020), 4,046M PLN (2021), 6,593M PLN (2022), 6,780M PLN (2023). The 2025 increase was driven by CFD instruments based on indices, characterized by high profitability. CFD turnover (in thousands of lots) is presented across the same 2019-2025 horizon.

By instrument class, CFDs (contracts for difference) led the 2025 revenue structure. Commodity-based CFDs were the most profitable asset class, contributing 43.7% of revenue (2024: 48.0%), reflecting strong profitability on CFDs linked to gold, natural gas, and cocoa prices. Index-based CFDs formed the second revenue contributor, rising to 36.0% from 33.3% the prior year, based on the US 100, German DAX (DE40), and US 500 indices. Currency-based CFDs accounted for 13.7% of total revenue, down from 14.6% a year earlier; within this class the most profitable instruments were CFDs on cryptocurrencies bitcoin and ethereum.

Composition of the gross result on operations on financial instruments by revenue category: in 2025, Spread 48%, Swaps 25%, Market making 25%, and Commissions and fees 2% (total 100%). In 2024 the corresponding split was Spread 53%, Swaps 41%, Market making 5%, and Commissions and fees 1% (total 100%). Year-over-year, market making rose sharply (5% to 25%), swaps declined (41% to 25%), and spread declined (53% to 48%), indicating a marked shift toward market-making contribution within the financial-instrument result.
