Additional explanatory notes to the interim condensed consolidated financial statements of the Asseco Group, Section 5.1, detail operating revenue structure for the three months ended 31 March 2025 versus the comparative three months ended 31 March 2024, reported in millions PLN. Total operating revenue rose to 4,576.9M PLN from 4,249.6M PLN year-over-year. By revenue type, own software and services reached 3,617.6M PLN (vs 3,298.1M), third-party software and services 417.9M PLN (vs 420.3M), and hardware and infrastructure 541.4M PLN (vs 531.2M). By product group: financial sector solutions 1,428.0M PLN (vs 1,322.9M), public institution solutions 975.5M PLN (vs 820.5M), ERP solutions 402.7M PLN (vs 372.1M), other IT solutions 730.8M PLN (vs 715.8M), infrastructure 852.7M PLN (vs 825.7M), and other non-IT 187.2M PLN (vs 192.6M).

Segment operating revenue by type (Q1 2025): Asseco Poland 544.1M PLN (own software/services 498.8M, third-party 36.1M, hardware/infrastructure 9.2M); Asseco International 1,058.3M PLN (732.2M; 125.6M; 200.5M); Formula Systems 2,990.3M PLN (2,392.3M; 266.3M; 331.7M); eliminations (15.8M); total 4,576.9M PLN.
