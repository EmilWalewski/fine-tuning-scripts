ECL for amortized-cost loans uses PD (probability of default), PPS (prepayment probability), LGD (loss given default), and EAD (exposure at default). LGD depends on recoveries post-default via receivables sale or amicable then judicial collection; forecast recoveries draw on historical payment curves for comparable receivables. Wonga updated its payment curve in 2025 reflecting observed higher long-horizon recoveries. Wonga portfolio macroeconomic variables analyzed: consumer bankruptcy, unemployment levels (total registered, newly registered, re-registered), registered unemployment rate, new job offers in period; variables added to the loss model where they improve forecast accuracy beyond behavioral scoring. Novum portfolio incorporates macroeconomic estimates into PD and LGD parameters; analyzed variables: GDP index dynamics, CPI change, retail sales index change, average wage dynamics in the national economy, unemployment rate. At end-2025 wage dynamics was the statistically significant variable, correlated with historical payments feeding LGD. The baseline macroeconomic forecast scenario in LGD estimates relies on available forecasts, notably National Bank of Poland (NBP) inflation and GDP projections.
