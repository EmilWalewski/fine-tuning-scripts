Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group, 2025 Management Report (Section 1.4, Flagship Residential Estates), details flagship multi-stage, above-standard, city-forming residential projects leveraging brand recognition, financial backing, experience, and quality, combining large sales scale with above-average profitability; most projects include participation in local technical infrastructure.

Dzielnica Mieszkaniowa Metro Zachód (Warsaw Bemowo), the Company's flagship multi-stage investment underway since 2019, currently building over 590 units, the Group's largest construction site. In 2025 it recorded the highest sales of all projects at 466 units; handovers reached 430 units (top-tier within the Group), of which 97 units transferred to a professional institutional-rental (PRS) investor.

Osiedle Wilno (Warsaw Targówek): a long-standing contributor to Group results; approximately 4,000 units built over 15 years. In 2025 buyers were found for 352 units, construction started on a further 248 units plus a linear park; including prior stages, 542 units remained under construction at year-end 2025. The Company built the Warszawa Zacisze-Wilno railway station providing transit to the city center—the first such undertaking in Poland by a residential developer.

Osiedle Urbino (Warsaw Włochy, near Ochota): modern-modernist buildings with light facades; among the highest resident-satisfaction ratings of the Group's Warsaw multi-stage projects. In 2025 the Company handed over 236 units, with a further 507 units under construction; later stages planned on adjacent secured land.

Mokotów Sportowy (Warsaw Augustówka): part of a new district with potential of approximately 10,000 apartments; the Dom Development estate on Antoniewska St. will ultimately comprise at least 2,500 units, located between Jeziorko Czerniakowskie and the Vistula bend. In 2025 the Company began construction of two further stages; first residents receive keys in Q1 2026.

Apartamenty Literacka (border of Bielany, Żoliborze, Bemowo): a comprehensive revitalization project on degraded land replacing an unfinished office building; in 2025 the Company delivered 89 units and started construction of 115 more.

Subsidiaries also build flagship multi-stage projects. For Euro Styl S.A., DOKI in Gdańsk is a comprehensive revitalization of former shipyard land (historic Gdańsk Shipyard), a multifunctional urban space comprising the new DOKI residential quarter and the hotel-services MONTOWNIA building (hotel lofts, conference center, northern Poland's largest food hall). MONTOWNIA, the historic former submarine assembly hall, holds 114 fully equipped serviced hotel lofts. DOKI and MONTOWNIA received multiple awards: three European Property Awards 2021-2022, Building of the Year 2024 Prime Property Prize, Baltic Real Estate Awards 2023 and 2025, Art in Architecture, and the Ministry of Culture's "Zabytek Zadbany" title in 2024. Gdańsk City Council approved a residential-function change for the next stage DOKI V (via the housing special-act), with accompanying primary school and linear park. DOKI and MONTOWNIA will total over 1,200 units (apartments, hotel lofts, service premises); 246 units were under construction at year-end 2025.

In 2025 Euro Styl S.A. continued the Public-Private Partnership (PPP) project in Dolne Miasto, Gdańsk, revitalizing the historic downtown. Its newest stage, Zajezdnia Motława (residential-services, near Wyskok and Wilk bastions and the Opływ Motławy greenery), first stage: two buildings with 113 apartments plus ground-floor service premises, under conservation supervision, including revitalization of the historic former tram depot hall and a new public square. Earlier PPP stages: Jaskółcza (apartments, investment apartments, service premises, near Motława) and Esencja (distinctive ornamented facade, on Nowa Motława).

Euro Styl S.A. also builds multi-stage Tri-City projects. Konstelacja (Pogórze near Gdynia) adjoins Beauforta and Beauforta 2 estates; above-standard, 4-storey buildings with recreational features. In 2025 two further stages with 192 units went on sale; 89 units were handed over, with more planned for 2026-2027.

Osiedle przy Błoniach (Rumia): family-oriented, multi-storey buildings, greenery, shops, walking paths, playground, bike facilities. Euro Styl S.A., with the City of Rumia, delivered a Municipal Recreation Zone forming stage I of the new Błonia Janowskie City Park.
