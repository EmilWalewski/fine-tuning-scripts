In Q1 2025, Asseco Poland continued work in the public administration segment with major Polish public-sector institutions including ZUS, NFZ, the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Justice, ARiMR, KRUS and GITD. The company carried out activities under an April 2024 agreement with ARiMR covering development and maintenance of the Agency’s IT Systems, described as one of Poland’s largest computerized databases made available to external entities and institutions and used to support aid mechanisms. During the period, Asseco also implemented the expansion of the Central Beneficiary Service System together with maintenance services. The implementation and development of this system was presented as a key stage in ARiMR’s digitalization, because integration with the developing online Electronic Services Platform for electronic communication with beneficiaries enables aid application handling to be carried out electronically. The reporting-period text also states that Asseco Poland continued cooperation with ZUS and was performing contracts in that area, but the provided excerpt cuts off before listing those contracts.
