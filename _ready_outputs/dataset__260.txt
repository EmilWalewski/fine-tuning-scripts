Excerpt from the Consolidated Annual Report of the XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025, addressing the firm's risk management and internal control framework. Two units hold central roles in daily operation of this system: the Risk Control Department and the Legal and Compliance Department. The Risk Control Department is responsible for implementing and operating the organizational risk management system, supporting the Management Board in shaping, reviewing, and updating risk management principles when new risk types emerge or when significant changes occur in strategy and action plans. It monitors the adequacy and effectiveness of the implemented risk management system, identifies and monitors risks of the Group's proprietary investments, determines the total capital requirement, and estimates internal capital. The department is headed by a Director who simultaneously serves as a permanent member of the Management Board—a structure that strengthens the risk management function's standing within the organization and ensures the risk perspective is incorporated into key strategic decision-making. The Legal and Compliance Department is responsible primarily for legal and compliance risks, with the Compliance Officer (Inspektor Nadzoru) playing a key role in proactively managing non-compliance risk. Beyond control functions, the unit performs a substantial advisory role, supporting business units in interpreting the complex regulatory environment (including KNF and ESMA requirements) and ensuring brokerage activity is conducted in accordance with legal provisions and internal regulations. It is further responsible for regular examination of the adequacy and effectiveness of the adopted compliance oversight system, conducting investigations, and monitoring particularly sensitive areas such as anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing (AML/CFT) and conflict-of-interest management. The Legal and Compliance Department is managed and supervised by the Management Board Member for Legal Affairs.

The firm emphasizes that procedures are only as effective as the people observing them; accordingly XTB builds risk awareness through training and knowledge exchange (e.g., within the ICT security team), promotes ethics and transparency via a whistleblowing system enabling any employee to report irregularities, and integrates ESG (Environment, Society, Corporate Governance) considerations as an integral part of its sustainable development strategy.

BION supervisory assessment: XTB's management system, including its risk management system, undergoes annual evaluation by the Polish Financial Supervision Authority (KNF) under the Supervisory Review and Evaluation Process (BION). The assessment obtained at the end of 2025 indicates: regarding the business model—the business model and strategy create medium-low risk to the investment firm's capacity to operate effectively; regarding governance—deficiencies in governance mechanisms indicate a medium level of threat to the brokerage house's stability; regarding adequacy of liquidity and funding resources—the liquidity position and funding profile indicate a low level of threat to the brokerage house's stability, considering management and control mechanisms for liquidity and funding risk; regarding capital adequacy—the level of own funds indicates a low level of threat to the brokerage house's stability, considering the level, management, and control mechanisms for the individual risk types to which the brokerage house is exposed.

Participants in the Group's risk management process comprise the Company's governing bodies and all of its employees; the process supports both strategy-setting and the Company's day-to-day operations.
