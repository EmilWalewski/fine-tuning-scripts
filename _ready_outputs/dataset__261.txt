Creotech Instruments S.A. interim report for the nine-month period ended 30 September 2025 (Q1–Q3 2025), dated 26 November 2025, comprising condensed interim financial statements prepared under International Financial Reporting Standards as endorsed by the European Union; all amounts in PLN thousands unless stated otherwise. Selected financial data (Section A) presented in both PLN and EUR thousands, comparing the nine months ended 30.09.2025 against the comparative nine months ended 30.09.2024 for income statement and cash flow items, and the position as at 30.09.2025 against 31.12.2024 for balance sheet items.

Revenue from sale of products reached 84.6M PLN for the nine months to 30.09.2025, up sharply from 15.5M PLN in the comparable 2024 period (EUR 20.0M vs EUR 3.6M), a more than fivefold year-over-year increase. Revenue from grants/subsidies (dotacje) was 10.5M PLN versus 7.5M PLN prior year (EUR 2.5M vs EUR 1.7M). Operating result was a loss of 4.7M PLN, a marked narrowing from the prior-year operating loss of 17.2M PLN (EUR -1.1M vs EUR -4.0M). Pre-tax result was a loss of 3.8M PLN against a 17.3M PLN loss in 2024 (EUR -0.9M vs EUR -4.0M). Net result was a loss of 3.0M PLN, improving from a net loss of 14.5M PLN year over year (EUR -0.7M vs EUR -3.4M), reflecting substantial reduction of losses driven by revenue growth.

Cash flow: net cash from operating activities turned strongly positive at 25.8M PLN versus an outflow of 5.1M PLN in 2024 (EUR 6.1M vs EUR -1.2M). Cash flow from investing activities was an outflow of 21.0M PLN, widening from a 14.6M PLN outflow prior year (EUR -5.0M vs EUR -3.4M). Cash flow from financing activities was a 70.4M PLN inflow against a 2.6M PLN outflow in 2024 (EUR 16.6M vs EUR -0.6M), indicating significant capital raising/financing during the period. Total net cash flow was a positive 75.2M PLN versus a negative 22.2M PLN year over year (EUR 17.7M vs EUR -5.2M).

Balance sheet as at 30.09.2025 versus 31.12.2024: total assets nearly doubled to 317.0M PLN from 162.3M PLN (EUR 74.2M vs EUR 38.0M). Long-term liabilities were 55.8M PLN, down from 61.7M PLN (EUR 13.1M vs EUR 14.4M). Short-term liabilities rose substantially to 120.4M PLN from 29.6M PLN (EUR 28.2M vs EUR 6.9M), a roughly fourfold increase. Equity rose to 140.7M PLN from 71.0M PLN (EUR 33.0M vs EUR 16.6M), nearly doubling. Share capital (kapitał podstawowy) was 0.285M PLN versus 0.238M PLN (EUR 0.067M vs EUR 0.056M). Number of shares stood at 2,854,347 versus 2,379,347, an increase consistent with the equity raise and financing inflow reflected in the cash flow statement.
