Dom Development S.A. Group H1 2025 Management Report (Section 3) covering financial results discussion: liquidity ratios, debt ratios, forecasts, loans/guarantees, related-party transactions, litigation, and Management Board approval. Liquidity ratios (30.06.2025 vs 31.12.2024; short-term liabilities exclude deferred income): current ratio 3.87 vs 4.55; quick ratio 0.77 vs 1.20; cash ratio 0.61 vs 0.20. Current ratio declined from a high 4.55 to 3.87 due to increased short-term liabilities, notably recognition of a dividend payable of 180.6M PLN (180,589 tys. zł) as at 30 June 2025. Despite this, cash ratio rose markedly, driven by a 351.1M PLN (351,140 tys. zł) increase in cash to 481.0M PLN (481,014 tys. zł). Management characterizes liquidity as very strong, citing a robust balance sheet enabling efficient investment delivery and rapid response to market opportunities, supported by the developer sector's long production cycle and financing constraints. Debt ratios (30.06.2025 vs 31.12.2024): equity-to-total-assets coverage 39.6% vs 41.5%; debt-to-equity (total liabilities/equity) 152.4% vs 141.1%; total debt (total liabilities/total assets) 60.4% vs 58.5%; interest-bearing debt-to-equity 45.2% vs 41.5%; net interest-bearing debt-to-equity (interest-bearing liabilities less cash, including escrow account funds) 12.1% vs 23.9%. Management attributes safe debt levels to disciplined operating policy (appropriate investment commencement/pace, controlled land acquisitions) and financing structure, confirming credit reliability; ratios near year-end 2024 levels reflect a conservative financing approach. Forecasts (3.4): the Board publishes no financial forecasts for the parent or Group, except that, per the Bond Act of 15 January 2015 (Art. 35), until full bond redemption the Company publishes on its website projected financial liabilities of the Company and Group. Loans, sureties, guarantees (3.5): in H1 2025 Group companies extended no loans to non-Group entities and granted no loan sureties or guarantees to a single entity (or its subsidiary) material to the Group or equal to at least 10% of the Company's equity; contingent liabilities detailed in note 7.21 of the interim condensed consolidated financial statements for the six months ended 30 June 2025. Related-party transactions (3.6): all transactions among Group companies and related parties were on arm's-length market terms; described in note 7.19 of the interim condensed consolidated statements for the period ended 30 June 2025. Material litigation (3.7): as at 30 June 2025 no Group company was party to material court proceedings. Management Report approval (Section 4): the H1 2025 Management Report on activities of Dom Development S.A. and its Group was prepared and approved by the Management Board on 27 August 2025, with the Board declaring it presents a true picture of development, achievements, situation, and principal threats/risks.
