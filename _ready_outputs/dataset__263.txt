Creotech Instruments S.A. interim condensed financial statements for the six-month period ended 30 June 2025 (all amounts in thousands PLN unless stated). Business shows high sales volatility but no pronounced seasonality or cyclicality, mitigated by operating across multiple market segments and a high share of long-term, stage-billed projects.

Series K share issuance: authorized under Resolution No. 3 of the Extraordinary General Meeting dated 30 January 2025; subscription agreements concluded 14–19 February 2025 via private subscription under Art. 431 §2(1) of the Commercial Companies Code, executed through Management Board offers accepted by investors (no subscription registration or allotment under Art. 434). On 19 February 2025 the Management Board adopted a resolution setting the final subscriber list. Subscription covered 475,000 Series K shares at an issue price of PLN 160 per share (gross proceeds PLN 76M, per Section B). Proceeds finance expenditures for the contract signed 20 December 2024 with the State Treasury – Armament Agency (Agencja Uzbrojenia) for production and delivery of the "Mikroglob" Satellite Earth Observation System (SSOZ), phases B, C, D, E1; remaining financing covered by contract advances per the 17 July 2025 contract annex, and potentially supplemented by debt financing available in later contract stages after first counterparty payments are recognized in revenue. On 16 April 2025 the District Court for the Capital City of Warsaw, XIV Commercial Division of the National Court Register (KRS), registered statute amendments raising share capital in connection with the Series K issuance.

Dividends: none paid during the period or up to publication approval.

Property, plant and equipment: PLN 4.5M expenditure on a leasehold improvement (investment in third-party fixed asset) linked to the Company's production facility investment in Piaseczno; no other material PP&E acquisitions or disposals. No contractual commitments to acquire PP&E.

Litigation: no court, arbitration, or public-administration proceedings concerning Company liabilities or receivables as of the reporting date. No material changes in estimates.

Structural changes (Section 12.11): in Q2 2025 the Company established subsidiaries Creotech Quantum S.A. and Creotech Geo P.S.A., acquiring 100% stakes in each via share capital payments of PLN 150K and PLN 100K respectively.

Publication approval: interim condensed financial statements approved for publication by the Management Board on 25 September 2025. Management Board: Grzegorz Brona (President), Paweł Górnicki (Vice President for Finance), Maciej Misiura (Vice President), Jacek Mandas (Vice President); accounting books maintained by Piotr Kazimierczak.

Basis of preparation: statements prepared in accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards as endorsed by the European Union, on the historical-cost basis. The statement of comprehensive income covers the six months ended 30 June 2025 with comparatives for the six months ended 30 June 2024; the statement of financial position covers 30 June 2025 with comparatives at 31 December 2025 (as stated in source); cash flow and changes-in-equity statements cover the six months ended 30 June 2025 with 30 June 2024 comparatives. Going-concern assumption applied for at least 12 months from period-end. Going-concern factors analyzed by the Board: R&D expenditures and commercialization preparation of developed (including satellite) technologies, operating losses, secured and expected contracts, and ability to raise financing. In December 2024 the Company signed the State Treasury – Armament Agency contract for the "Mikroglob" SSOZ at a net value of PLN 452.6M, leveraging prior R&D and capacity while radically improving financial results. In April 2025 the Company concluded a contract with the European Space Agency for delivery of an Earth-observation satellite constellation (CAMILA), valued at EUR 51.9M (EUR 25.6M attributable to the Company after subcontractor remuneration). In Q1 2025 the Company completed a successful Series K share issuance raising PLN 76M. The Company holds secured contracts ensuring near-term operations, and the successful issuance demonstrates access to financial markets.
