Excerpt from XTB S.A. Consolidated Annual Report 2025, presenting the Group's operational, regulatory, conduct, and counterparty risk register (risk description, impact, and mitigating controls). No financials, dates, or governance data provided; content is qualitative risk-matrix narrative.

Operational/human-error risk on transfers to correspondent brokers: insufficient funds to cover trading could prevent position hedging and undermine capital adequacy or order execution; mitigated by precise transfer-amount estimation, broker transaction limits, broker margin-call warnings, automated transfer-status checks, and daily monitoring of unexecuted transfers.

Improper product implementation or onboarding of new suppliers: inadequate data integration risks gaps/errors in regulatory reporting, the capital-adequacy ratio calculation system, and accounting, plus increased manual workload; immature products or unverified suppliers (market data, cloud) introduce failure points and compliance gaps that may cause asset mispricing, quotation outages, mis-execution, or misselling, eroding platform trust, provoking mass complaints, and exposing the Group to supervisory sanctions (KNF, FCA) over deficient due diligence; mitigated by formal product-introduction procedures with preliminary alignment, active inter-team communication channels, and cyclical all-staff meetings sharing production changes, research results, and solution mockups.

Failure at the executing broker: loss of effective hedging of market exposure, exposing the balance sheet to uncontrolled losses in high volatility; for clients, trading blockage (notably equities/ETFs and specific CFDs) and adverse price gaps triggering complaints and loss of confidence; regulatory counterparty-concentration risk and possible administrative proceedings; mitigated by business-continuity procedures and diversification of order-routing partners and own-account hedging counterparties.

Bad-tick (incorrect price) from quotation suppliers: cost of client complaints on positions closed at erroneous/non-market prices; mass occurrence risks reputational materialization and loss of client trust; mitigated by price-source validation against backup source, verification on material deviation from prior quote, spread monitoring/limitation, execution and price-continuity mechanisms, quotation controls by Trading Department and DKR, and trading-system optimization.

Incorrect MiFID client classification: client may acquire instruments unsuitable or too risky, causing losses, plus regulatory sanction exposure for poorly organized classification processes; mitigated by internal controls, Compliance controls, and internal procedures.

Offering financial instruments inconsistent with the target group: client losses from improper target-market designation or eligibility cause dissatisfaction, attrition, claims against the Company, and regulatory sanctions; mitigated by internal controls, Compliance controls, internal audit, internal procedures, and client education.

Unauthorized investment advice or misleading clients: XTB or staff providing services meeting the definition of investment advice without the requisite authorization exposes the Company to client claims and administrative proceedings with monetary penalties; mitigated by verification of selected client phone calls for legal compliance, internal controls, Compliance controls, and internal procedures.

Unclear contract terms / abusive clauses in client agreements: improperly constructed terms may trigger high administrative penalties (e.g., UOKiK), orders for retrospective cost refunds, and mass class actions; for cost- and security-sensitive retail clients, opaque provisions (e.g., on rollover) erode perceived fairness and, amplified by social media, may cause reputational loss and mass account closures; systemic violation of consumer interests undermines the business model, depresses market valuation, and threatens licence retention in key jurisdictions; mitigated by the internal control system, legal-compliance supervision system, internal audit, language simplification, and additional client mailings.

Non-performance or improper execution of client agreements: risk of individual or class litigation, obligation to compensate client losses, loss of trust and client attrition, and significant reputational damage in collective disputes; mitigated by periodic analysis of GTC terms, Compliance controls, and the internal control system.

Errors in reporting to the supervisor and to the market: systemic reporting errors undermine XTB's credibility as a GPW-listed entity, drawing administrative sanctions and remedial plans that hinder business development; for investors, unreliable data signals corporate-governance and internal-control failure, prompting share-price declines and, in extreme cases, trading suspension or accusations of information manipulation; long-term loss of transparency reputation in a multi-jurisdictional regime may impede foreign expansion and licence acquisition, constraining client-base scaling; mitigated by the internal control system, legal-compliance supervision system, and internal audit.
