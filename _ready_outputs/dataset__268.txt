Consolidated financial statement note 7.16 (additional equity disclosures) and related risk/capital notes for Grupa Kapitałowa Dom Development S.A. for the year ended 31 December 2025 (FY2025 vs FY2024 comparatives), figures in thousand PLN. Interest-bearing financial assets (bank deposits, loans granted) totaled 587.9M PLN (FY2024: 646.3M PLN); interest-bearing financial liabilities (loans, own bonds) totaled 703.8M PLN (FY2024: 616.9M PLN), yielding a net financial position of -115.9M PLN versus +29.4M PLN prior year, a swing from net positive to net negative.

Interest-rate sensitivity analysis assumes a 100 basis-point shift, deemed reasonable given market environment, holding all other variables constant, computed over the twelve-month periods ended 31 December 2025 and 2024. FY2025: a +100bp move impacts variable-rate assets by 4.762M PLN and variable-rate liabilities by -4.561M PLN, for net sensitivity of 0.201M PLN on both profit/loss and net assets (post-tax); a -100bp move reverses signs symmetrically. FY2024: variable-rate assets impact 5.234M PLN, variable-rate liabilities -3.997M PLN, net sensitivity 1.237M PLN, indicating reduced net rate sensitivity year-over-year. Finance costs on loans and bonds are capitalized into work-in-progress and gradually transferred to profit/loss with the cost of inventories sold.

Credit risk: principal financial assets are bank balances, cash, loans granted, and trade/other receivables; credit risk is mainly attributable to trade receivables, carried net of impairment allowances estimated by the Management Board. Liquidity-fund counterparties are reputable, highly-rated banks. Cash and cash equivalents plus short-term financial assets by rating, FY2025: Moody's Aa 142.7M PLN; Fitch AA- 27.7M PLN; Fitch A 23.5M PLN; Fitch BBB 217.5M PLN; Fitch BBB- 59.5M PLN; unrated bank 57.2M PLN; cash on hand 0.18M PLN; total 528.2M PLN. FY2024 total 591.0M PLN (Moody's A2 186.3M PLN; Fitch A+ 23.8M PLN; Fitch A 124.2M PLN; Fitch BBB- 163.4M PLN; Fitch BB+ 92.8M PLN; Fitch BB- 0.15M PLN; cash on hand 0.29M PLN), reflecting lower rated-cash balances year-over-year. Loans granted carry limited credit risk: counterparties are jointly-controlled entities (operational and financial joint control), secured on borrowers' real estate whose project profitability is continuously monitored. No significant credit-risk concentration; risk spread across many partners/clients; receivables from core activity (sale of apartments, commercial premises, garages) fully secured as delivery follows full payment per preliminary sale agreements.

Liquidity risk: undiscounted contractual future cash flows from financial liabilities by maturity band, FY2025 total 1,796.9M PLN — loans 52.6M PLN; own bonds issued 753.0M PLN (128.1M PLN nearest band); trade and other payables 842.5M PLN (702.9M PLN nearest band); lease liabilities 148.8M PLN. FY2024 total 1,626.7M PLN — loans nil; own bonds 730.1M PLN; trade/other payables 771.5M PLN; lease liabilities 125.0M PLN; aggregate liability cash flows rose year-over-year, driven by loans and longer-dated maturities. Liquidity managed via short/medium/long-term cash-flow planning (short-term plans updated at least monthly), selection of financing sources, monitoring of bank-covenant indicators, diversification of development financing, and cooperation with reputable financial institutions.

Capital management: Management policy maintains a strong capital base to preserve investor/creditor/market confidence and fund growth. Return on equity (net profit to average annual equity) was 35.5% in FY2025 versus 36.1% in FY2024. Weighted-average cost of debt interest was 5.96% in 2025 versus 5.87% in 2024. Net financial leverage ratio (loan and bond debt less cash, equivalents and short-term financial assets, divided by equity) was 8.8% at 31 December 2025 versus 1.5% at 31 December 2024, a marked increase. The Company has no defined own-share buyback plan; Group companies are subject to no externally imposed capital requirements beyond statutory Commercial Companies Code requirements.
