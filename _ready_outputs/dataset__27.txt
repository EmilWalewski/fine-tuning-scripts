Dom Development S.A. describes a governance structure in which the Management Board has 4 to 8 members, including the President, appointed for a joint three-year term, with its size determined by the Supervisory Board. A shareholder holding at least 50.1% of shares has a personal right to appoint and dismiss half of the Management Board, including the President and the Vice President responsible for finance, with specified rounding for odd-sized boards; other members are appointed and dismissed by the Supervisory Board. The Management Board represents the Company judicially and extra-judicially, with valid declarations requiring joint action by two Management Board members, one Management Board member with a commercial proxy, or two commercial proxies. Under the Statute, the Management Board may increase share capital within authorized capital by issuing new shares with total nominal value up to PLN 1.000 M, in one or several increases, for three years from registration of the Statute amendment adopted on 12 June 2025, including related subscription warrants expiring within that period. With Supervisory Board consent it may exclude shareholders’ pre-emptive rights in whole or in part for authorized-capital issues, while any such increase requires a positive Supervisory Board opinion. Acquisition of treasury shares for redemption does not require General Meeting consent, subject to the Commercial Companies Code, but requires Supervisory Board consent. From 1 January to 28 February 2025 the Management Board comprised Mikołaj Konopka as President, Leszek Stankiewicz as Vice President, and members Justyna Wilk, Terry Roydon and Grzegorz Smoliński. After Leszek Stankiewicz resigned effective 28 February 2025, Groupe Belleforêt S.à r.l., as the shareholder holding at least 50.1% of shares, appointed Monika Dobosz as Management Board Member and Vice President responsible for finance from 1 March 2025 for the joint three-year term; from 1 March to 31 December 2025 the Management Board comprised Mikołaj Konopka, Monika Dobosz, Justyna Wilk, Terry Roydon and Grzegorz Smoliński. The Management Board operates under the Commercial Companies Code, the Statute, publicly available Management Board Rules approved by the Supervisory Board, and Best Practice for GPW Listed Companies. It manages day-to-day operations, represents the Company externally, and decides matters not reserved to the General Meeting or Supervisory Board; resolutions require an absolute majority, with the President’s vote decisive in a tie. In strategy and current tasks it follows the Company’s overriding interest, law, and the interests of shareholders, employees and creditors, acting professionally within justified business risk based on available information, analyses and opinions. Management Board remuneration is set by the Supervisory Board under the remuneration policy and transparent procedures, reflecting responsibility, competence, economic results and market-comparable levels. The Supervisory Board has 5 to 9 members appointed for a joint three-year term, with size set by the General Meeting. The General Meeting appoints and dismisses its members, except that a shareholder holding at least 50.1% of shares may appoint and dismiss half of the Supervisory Board, including one Vice-Chair, with specified numbers for five-, seven- and nine-person boards. At least two Supervisory Board members appointed by the General Meeting should meet independence criteria. Throughout 2025 the Supervisory Board had eight members: Jarosław Szanajca as Chair; Janusz Zalewski and Dorota Podedworna-Tarnowska as Vice-Chairs, with Dorota Podedworna-Tarnowska independent; Philippe Bonavero; Anna Maria Panasiuk as independent member; Mark Spiteri; Edyta Wojtkiewicz as independent member; and Grzegorz Kiełpsz.
