Excerpt from the Management Board Report on operations of Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group for 2025, within the Consolidated Sustainability Reporting section, addressing the Audit Committee and auditor independence governing the assurance of sustainability reporting. Per the Company's policy on services provided by the audit firm performing assurance (atestacja) of sustainability reporting, the audit firm conducting assurance for this public-interest entity, and any member of its network, neither directly nor indirectly provide to Dom Development S.A. or related entities the prohibited non-audit services specified in Article 5(1) second subparagraph, points (b), (c) and (e)-(k) of Regulation (EU) No 537/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 April 2014 on specific requirements regarding statutory audit of public-interest entities and repealing Commission Decision 2005/909/EC. The enumerated prohibited services comprise: any participation in management or the decision-making process of the audited entity; bookkeeping and preparation of accounting records and financial statements, as well as preparation of sustainability reporting; designing and implementing internal control or risk-management procedures related to the preparation or control of financial information, or designing and implementing financial-information technology systems; valuation services, including valuations performed in connection with actuarial services or litigation-support services; legal services covering (i) provision of general legal advice, (ii) negotiation on behalf of the audited entity, and (iii) acting as an advocate in the resolution of disputes; services linked to the audited entity's internal audit function; services tied to financing, capital structure and capital allocation, and the investment strategy of the audit client, excluding assurance services in connection with financial statements such as the issuance of comfort letters in connection with the entity's prospectuses; promotional activities and dealing in or trading the shares or interests of the audited entity for own account, or underwriting issues of the entity's shares or interests; and human-resources services relating to management personnel able to exert significant influence over the preparation of accounting records or financial statements subject to statutory audit, where such services involve searching for or selecting candidates for such positions, conducting reference checks of such candidates, designing organizational structure, or cost control. The audit firm performing the sustainability-reporting assurance and, where it belongs to a network, each network member may provide non-audit services other than the prohibited non-audit services described above to Dom Development S.A. or related entities, provided such services are approved by the Audit Committee following its proper assessment of threats to independence and of safeguards applied in accordance with Article 74 of the Act of 11 May 2017 on Statutory Auditors, Audit Firms and Public Oversight. The Audit Committee's recommendation of 25 April 2025 concerning the selection of the audit firm satisfies the applicable conditions set out in Article 130(2) of the Act of 11 May 2017 on Statutory Auditors, Audit Firms and Public Oversight.
