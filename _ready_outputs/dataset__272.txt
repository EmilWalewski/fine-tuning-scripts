Excerpt from XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025, covering treasury/investment policy and greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions accounting under ESRS E1 disclosures. XTB S.A. allocates part of its cash into bank deposits and financial instruments—Treasury bonds, State Treasury-guaranteed bonds, and corporate bonds guaranteed by banks—held as capital placement protecting Company funds against inflation; these bonds are measured at fair value through profit or loss. Shares held from fractional-share transactions are characterized as immaterial in scale per Group analysis.

Applying the "Financed Emissions—The Global GHG Accounting & Reporting Standard Part A" (Partnership for Carbon Accounting Financials, GHG Protocol-approved and consistent with the GHG Protocol Corporate Accounting and Reporting Standard), the Group concludes it is NOT obligated to report GHG emissions from held shares and bonds on the XTB S.A. Capital Group balance sheet as of 31.12.2025 under Scope 3 Category 15. Rationale: the entity should have decision-making authority over which companies' shares it buys; immaterial positions, short-held or held-for-sale assets, and fair-value-measured assets may be excluded from financed-emissions calculations.

Disclosure E1-6: From 2024 XTB Group began calculating full GHG emissions from operational activity. All scope calculations relied exclusively on publicly available spreadsheets (MS Excel). Emissions-intensity assessment uses a GHG emissions indicator per PLN 1 million net revenue at Group level.

Base-year 2024 recalculation (Table 16): Following changes recorded after publication of the 2024 Statement and to ensure due diligence and improve data quality/comparability, the Group recalculated scopes and categories where justified. After corrections and re-computation, total carbon footprint of XTB S.A. Capital Group for 2024 decreased 6.17% versus originally reported value; most adjustments were minor (under 10%). Largest changes: Scope 3 Category 5 (-56.05%), driven by updated mixed-waste quantities reflecting revised waste-allocation per current office-space lease structure, plus an updated formula estimating emissions from IT-equipment disposal based on Environmental Product Declaration (EPD) cards used in Scope 3 Category 2 calculations. Scope 3 Category 7 (-54.44%): corrected user responses on commuting days so commuting plus remote-work days summed to 5 working days/week (accounting for combined/alternative transport), corrected remote-work emissions formula, methodology updated by excluding Well-to-Tank (WTT) emissions for electricity in countries computed from EMBER database data, and improved extrapolation for employees lacking survey data. Scope 3 Category 11 (-45.97%): recalculation due to changed methodology for the average platform-usage-time indicator, stemming from updated event-triggering rules improving reporting consistency, yielding more realistic usage measurement and better cross-platform data comparability.
