Grupa Kęty S.A. consolidated Q3 2025 quarterly report (nine months ended 30 September 2025) covering employee share-option programs, liabilities, and explanations of material revenue/cost movements. The Group operates two share-option programs over Grupa Kęty S.A. shares (2023 program and 2020 program as amended in 2023), addressed to the Group's key management personnel. During 9M 2025 key management exercised rights from Tranches I and II of the 2020 program, taking up 87,607 Grupa Kęty S.A. shares. On 1 October 2025, on completion of the vesting period, key management received the right to acquire 34,000 shares under Tranche III of the 2020 program at 381.99 PLN per share. On 29 September 2025, 97,000 share options were granted to key management under Tranche III of the 2023 program, exercisable after a 3-year period subject to program conditions. No changes were made to estimates or assumptions versus those published in the 2024 consolidated financial statements. Program parameters: 2023 program tranches (2025/2024/2023) each carry 97,000 options; 2020 program 2022 tranche 90,000 options. Explanations of material revenue/cost changes (21), 9M 2025 vs comparable prior-period: sales revenue rose 347M PLN, mainly higher sales volumes, of which 221M PLN from the Q3 acquisition of SELT Sp. z o.o.; total operating costs rose 285M PLN, including: depreciation/amortization up 26M PLN reflecting investments (incl. 16M PLN from the Q3 2024 SELT acquisition); materials and energy consumption up 243M PLN on greater production scale (incl. 130M PLN from SELT), while aluminum quotations in PLN were up ~1% year-on-year; external-services costs up 21M PLN (incl. 18M PLN from SELT); employee-benefit costs up 73M PLN, mainly pay rises and headcount growth (incl. 32M PLN from SELT); finished-goods/work-in-progress inventory rose 67M PLN in 9M 2025 versus a 20M PLN decrease in 9M 2024, reflecting higher 2025 production (incl. 11M PLN from SELT). As a result, gross profit on sales reached 632M PLN, up 62M PLN year-on-year. Operating profit was 639M PLN, up 60M PLN, including 30M PLN from recognition of SELT's operating result for 8 months of the current year. Net finance costs were 15M PLN higher, mainly interest costs on increased debt. After income-tax expense 43M PLN higher (mainly non-recognition of deferred-tax assets following 2024 completion of key investments covered by tax reliefs, note 11), net profit reached 466M PLN, up 2M PLN year-on-year.
