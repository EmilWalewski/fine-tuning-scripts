Excerpt from XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025, detailing human-capital and labour-related operational risks together with their mitigation measures. Improper termination of employment contracts ("Niewłaściwe rozwiązanie umowy o pracę") exposes the Company to costly litigation, compensation payouts, and legal-service costs; for clients, materialization may temporarily destabilize operational and servicing processes where it concerns staff critical to service continuity, while at market level such cases damage the Company's reputation as a stable, credible employer and may negatively affect shareholders' assessment of corporate governance (ESG). Mitigation comprises cooperation with a labour-law firm and rigorous application of legal regulations. Inadequate working conditions ("Nieodpowiednie warunki pracy") — breaches of ergonomics, hygiene, or workplace-culture standards — drive declining motivation, higher turnover, and risk of sanctions from labour-supervision authorities; for clients this translates into lower service quality and reduced trading-platform innovation stemming from weak staff engagement, while at market level it poses material reputational risk, undermining the Company's credibility with shareholders and impeding execution of a strategy reliant on high-class human capital, potentially including loss of a key employee. Mitigation rests on HR policy ensuring role substitutability, organizational structures with dedicated deputy-director positions for given units, preferential remuneration systems for key employees, and an extensive package of non-wage benefits. Workplace accidents ("Wypadki przy pracy") generate the risk of sudden staffing shortages in critical teams, potentially delaying key projects and triggering sanctions from labour-supervision authorities; for clients these events indirectly raise operational risk by weakening the stability of trading systems and support quality. Mitigation includes training, a remote-work regulation (occupational health and safety, BHP), company-provided equipment, and employer liability insurance covering workplace accidents.
