Current report RB 10/2026, dated Rzeszów, 18 March 2026, issued by the Management Board of Asseco Poland S.A. (registered office Rzeszów; "the Company"). The report transmits, as attachments, the text of resolutions adopted by the Company's Extraordinary General Meeting ("EGM") held on 18 March 2026 in Warsaw, together with the text of draft resolutions that were put to a vote during the EGM proceedings but were not adopted. An objection ("sprzeciw") to the minutes was raised against Resolution No. 3 concerning adoption of the EGM agenda. The Company further notifies that the EGM adopted a resolution to refrain from passing a resolution under agenda item 8—concerning a reduction of the share capital and amendment of the Company's Statute—deeming it moot ("bezprzedmiotowa"), in connection with the non-adoption of resolutions on the redemption of the Company's own shares ("umorzenie akcji własnych") under item 7 of the adopted agenda. Legal basis: § 20 sec. 1 points 6, 7 and 8 of the Regulation of the Minister of Finance on current and periodic information provided by issuers of securities and on conditions for recognizing as equivalent information required under the laws of a non-EU member state, dated 6 June 2025 (Journal of Laws of 2025, item 755).
