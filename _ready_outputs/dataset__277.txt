XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025 (Management Board report, "Other information" section) addresses anti-corruption governance, auditor engagement/remuneration, and litigation. Anti-corruption policy, anchored partly on the UN Convention Against Corruption, integrates a violation-reporting system with the internal Procedure for reporting violations to ensure efficient, independent, objective incident investigation. Identified high corruption-risk areas: XTB client servicing; IT; selection of suppliers/business partners; commercial contracting; sponsorship and charitable actions; employee hiring, remuneration and bonusing; processing of information including confidential data and trade secrets; and judicial/administrative rulings. In 2025, XTB S.A. headquarters rolled out anti-corruption and anti-bribery training for newly hired employees and decided to send cyclical alerts to employees identified as particularly exposed; these supplement existing Anti-Money Laundering (AML) training. Training covers persons identified as particularly exposed to corruption/bribery risk plus members of management and supervisory bodies. The function-identification and training area is new for XTB; 2025 marked the first time persons holding particularly risk-exposed functions in XTB S.A. and its foreign branches, plus Supervisory Board and Management Board members, were trained.

Auditor: PricewaterhouseCoopers Polska sp. z o.o. Audyt sp.k. (PwC), Warsaw, served as Company auditor in 2024-2025. On 21 February 2024 the Supervisory Board (per Statute §19(2)(h) and Supervisory Board Regulations §8(2)(h)) entrusted PwC with: audits of standalone and consolidated financial statements for 2024-2025; reviews of abridged standalone and consolidated half-year statements for the six months ended 30 June 2024 and 30 June 2025; assurance on safekeeping/protection of client assets (per the Minister of Finance regulation of 22 February 2019) with reports for years ended 31 December 2024 and 2025; and assurance on the Supervisory Board remuneration report (per the Public Offering Act of 29 July 2005) with reports for the same years. PwC selection complied with the Act on Statutory Auditors of 11 May 2017 and internal policies. Financial statement audit engagement signed 8 July 2024. Client-asset safekeeping assurance and remuneration-report assurance engagements both signed 30 July 2024. Sustainability reporting assurance engagement signed 25 November 2025 (per Accounting Act art. 64(7)), providing limited assurance on sustainability reporting conformity for 1 January-31 December 2025.

Litigation: a lawsuit filed July 2020, served on the Company November 2020, alleges failure to apply security measures; dispute value approx. 2.8M PLN. Alleged harm: XTB's failure to apply financial security measures purportedly enabling misappropriation of funds by the plaintiff's employee (who was also an XTB client). Management deems the claim entirely groundless. Roland Berger analysis confirmed the client's transactions were not delayed and order execution was even faster than the average for other clients. June 2023: court of first instance dismissed the claim, finding no material breaches by XTB. 22 August 2023: plaintiff appealed. September 2023: case files transferred to the Warsaw Court of Appeal, which served the appeal on XTB's attorney in March 2024. 9 April 2024: response to appeal filed.
