XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025 Consolidated Annual Report, ESG section covering Environment/Climate, Social Responsibility, and Governance areas. Environment/Climate strategic direction: sustainable development via responsible e-waste management, employee environmental education, efficient resource management, and enabling future greenhouse-gas emission reduction targets across Grupo XTB. Reporting-period actions: updated material environmental/climate impacts, opportunities and risks; followed the Climate Policy adopted in 2024; held GHG-emissions training for XTB Head Office staff; for the first time calculated and published the full carbon footprint of Grupo XTB; more precisely monitored municipal waste and e-waste via a newly introduced weighing system at Head Office through the building manager; applied 2024-implemented office-space signage reminding staff of rational resource use; ran educational mailings under the "Naturalna Inwestycja" (Natural Investment) program; strengthened internal communication via the xPress newsletter; organized clean-up of the Narew riverbanks near Warsaw, participated in beach clean-ups and tree planting in Cyprus, and for the second time collected e-waste and batteries with Elektroeko and UNEP/GRID Warszawa. Stated environmental goals: responsible e-waste management; ultimately calculating GHG emissions across all three scopes; implementing a system to manage Grupo XTB's climate-change impact.

Social Responsibility area: executing the ESG Strategy for 2024–2027, with stakeholder dialogue under a double-materiality assessment (survey, in-depth interviews, workshops) forming the basis for the Group's ESG strategic directions and goals. Objective: sustainable social development through a safe and inspiring work environment, durable social relations, active stakeholder dialogue, and client-expectation-meeting services/products. Actions: via the XTB Foundation supported entrepreneurs operating in flood-affected areas (the "Pocztówki z powodzi"/Postcards from the Flood campaign) and participated in Szlachetna Paczka (Noble Gift); the XTB Foundation continued educational efforts in partnership with the "Trampki na giełdzie" Foundation and engaged in creating educational programs for high-school youth; the XTB Talents team (HR) again ran an employee satisfaction survey across the whole Group; ran an additional employee satisfaction survey and received the "Great Place to Work" certificate for the Head Office and the German branch of XTB S.A.; strengthened internal communication by introducing the xPress newsletter and quarterly "Town Hall" meetings.

Governance area strategic direction: sustainable development and long-term value via transparent, responsible management of environmental, social, and corporate-governance aspects in XTB S.A. Capital Group. Goals: implementing a comprehensive ESG approach within corporate governance covering environmental, social, and management aspects; integrating ESG risks into the Group's internal Risk Management System; updating Head Office remuneration rules while measuring and monitoring the Gender Pay Gap indicator for the entire Group; creating a safe and inclusive work environment; intensifying the XTB Foundation's educational and social activity. Reporting-period actions: reviewed material social-area impacts, opportunities and risks; launched the first ESG service portal to be closer to stakeholders; introduced uniform standards for quarterly employee evaluation to build a coherent Group-wide remuneration policy; expanded and strengthened the Talents department (formerly HR) to foster a high-performance culture; held employee-representative elections; for the second time identified and published Grupo XTB's pay gap; continued the "Inner Power" event series for employees to share passions; again organized preventive USG (ultrasound) screenings for staff during the recurring Health Day at Head Office; again organized flu vaccinations at Head Office; participated again in the "Two Hours for the Family" campaign, with all Group companies joining for the second time; organized first-aid training and a safety day for employees; conducted equality workshops under Diversity & Inclusion.

Clients are identified as a key stakeholder group; in 2025 XTB expanded its offering with long-term investing and saving products, developing its technology, app, and trading platform. Sustainability did not determine the product portfolio's shape but supported business development through: setting coherent strategic business directions with ESG's supporting role; strengthening dialogue (double-materiality assessment) and identifying stakeholder needs; supporting employee development and innovation via flexible work arrangements (where possible); employee well-being and comfort (cyclical Health Day, supporting employee passions, promoting a healthy lifestyle); developing XTB app functionality with ESG ratings and introducing rating-based instruments to the offering.

Governance/value-chain table actions: reviewed material governance-area impacts, opportunities and risks and identified a cybersecurity risk; introduced two-factor authentication (2FA) for clients; launched the first cybersecurity landing page; delivered anti-corruption training for members of XTB S.A. corporate bodies; updated the XTB investor-relations website; expanded the XTB product portfolio with new positions; aligned the sustainability reporting process with ESRS standards and published the first CSRD-compliant statement; expanded the ESG structure by creating two teams within the Sustainable Development Division; and implemented ESG risks into the internal risk management system of XTB S.A.
