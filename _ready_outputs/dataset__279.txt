Dom Development S.A. Capital Group 2025 Management Report (Section 1: Basic Information; sub-section 1.3 Business Model). The Group is a residential developer operating across Poland's four largest housing markets—Warsaw, Wrocław, Tri-City (Trójmiasto), and Kraków—chosen for highest housing demand, lowest unemployment, highest wages, and most favorable demographics (rising household counts), combining scale with strong regional structures and local market knowledge. In 2025 the Group held market leadership on 3 of its 4 markets. Dom Development S.A. remained Warsaw's largest developer with 13.2% market share, selling 2,015 units. Dom Development Wrocław Sp. z o.o. retained first position in Wrocław with 12.4% share and 816 transactions. Euro Styl S.A. led the Tri-City market with 12.5% share via 1,082 contracted units. In Kraków—a high-volume, high-price but more fragmented market—the Company ranked 2nd with 7.8% share, selling 535 units.

Strong balance sheet: Management prioritizes solid finances and stable liquidity for both Company and Group; substantial financial resources at low debt levels enable flexible response to cyclical real-estate/construction markets, while available capital and secured credit lines give an edge in land transactions requiring large multi-million payments on short notice. ROE maximization: long-term shareholder profitability via project-level returns, appropriate operating scale, and regular dividend payments from profits.

Business model: focus on the four largest Polish housing markets, primarily popular-segment units of elevated standard plus apartments, yielding above-average margins at high sales volumes. Sustainability-aligned approach per the "DOM 2030. ESG Strategy" document. Development companies—Dom Development S.A., Euro Styl S.A., Dom Development Wrocław Sp. z o.o., Dom Development Kraków Sp. z o.o.—operate near-identical models; other Group entities provide support. No changes in core management principles of the Company or Group occurred in 2025.

Land acquisition: basis is multi-stage projects meeting the 15-minute-city concept; the Group considers optimal land bank (land in preparation) to equal 4 years of sales, with increasing urban revitalization projects amid limited land availability. Design and permitting: cooperation with renowned architectural studios; high energy-efficiency standards; typical multi-family projects feature elevators, underground parking, ground-floor retail; all units have balconies, terraces, or gardens. Construction: executed by in-house general contractors using traditional technology, standard build time ~16-20 months, local material suppliers. Sales: conducted directly via on-site/HQ sales offices plus online/remote options; payments in tranches tied to construction progress, with funds held in open escrow accounts at leading Polish banks.

Mortgage advisory: in 2025, 56% of Group transactions used mortgage financing, up 7 p.p. vs 2024, mostly via Dom Development Kredyty Sp. z o.o., which intermediated 1,164 loans totaling PLN 659.72M. Apartment finishing: turnkey ("pod klucz") fit-out programs (bathroom, flooring, painting, internal doors; optional kitchen with appliances), with developer warranty and key handover possible at technical acceptance with fixed pricing.
