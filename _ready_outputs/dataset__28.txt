Dom Development Group has formal procedures for cooperation with direct employees across recruitment, onboarding, goal-setting, communication, development, feedback, wellbeing and improvement. Recruitment is designed to be transparent, with full information on role requirements, duties and working conditions, while onboarding covers company information, internal procedures, training options and social benefits. Employees receive measurable, achievable and time-bound periodic goals, supported by meetings on progress. Communication is maintained through regular team meetings, communication tools, knowledge-sharing platforms, transparent information flow and an open-door policy for direct contact with supervisors. Development is managed through annual training-needs identification, tailored training and workshops, webinars and e-learning, while feedback is provided through periodic evaluation meetings focused on performance, progress and improvement areas. Wellbeing measures include work-life balance support, Labour Code-compliant remote work, integration events, participation in health and cultural activities, psychological support, access to the Company Social Benefits Fund and adjustment of duties in difficult personal situations. Cooperation is monitored through annual Employee Opinion Barometer satisfaction surveys, where employees may freely share views, observations and ideas on workforce matters; results influence management-board decisions on managing negative impacts, with group company management boards responsible for employee cooperation. The Group has not implemented a Global Framework Agreement or other agreements concerning respect for employee rights. Its key irregularity-reporting regulation and due-diligence tool is the Procedure for Reporting Abuse and Protection of Whistleblowers, implemented in 2024. Employees may raise concerns directly with supervisors or anonymously report any irregularity concerning material actual or potential positive or negative impacts affecting or potentially affecting them. Internal reporting channels include dedicated email addresses, traditional correspondence, telephone and in-person reporting. Reports are confidential, with full access limited to the team handling breach cases, and all reports are logged in an internal irregularities register to monitor follow-up actions, investigation outcomes and recommendation effectiveness. The policy prohibits retaliation, attempted retaliation and threats against reporting persons. External reporting to the Ombudsman or a public authority is available to workforce members who qualify as whistleblowers. New hires are informed during onboarding about a satisfaction survey after three months, while other employees may provide feedback through the annual Employee Assessment Barometer and annual appraisals. Group companies assess remedy effectiveness by analyzing survey results, implementing proposed solutions and monitoring corrected areas, while HR tracks negative workforce impacts using turnover, absences and annual appraisal results, and department directors hold regular meetings for open feedback. In 2026 the Group plans an information and communication campaign on abuse-reporting channels and mandatory training to ensure own workforce and value-chain workers know and trust the structures and processes for reporting and resolving concerns or needs.
