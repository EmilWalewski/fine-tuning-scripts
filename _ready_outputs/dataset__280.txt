XTB S.A. Capital Group Q3 2025 report excerpt details marketing, product strategy, and the All-in-One investment application roadmap. In late October 2025, XTB received a gold Effie Awards 2025 statuette recognizing the effectiveness of its marketing activities; the award honored an advertising campaign run across the 2024/2025 turn that lifted the growth dynamic of new accounts to approximately 27%. Top-of-mind brand awareness (first brand recalled by respondents) rose from 8.4% to 33%, and spontaneous brand awareness (unprompted recognition) rose to 41%. Poland is one of over 55 Effie programs worldwide; nominated and awarded campaigns enter the Effie Index, a global marketing-effectiveness indicator ranking the most effective brands, networks, and holdings.

Management's ambition is for XTB to be recognized as Europe's leading All-in-One investment application, offering clients easy, intelligent, efficient ways to trade, invest, and save with instant access to their funds. Recent years mark XTB's transformation from a CFD broker into a modern FinTech delivering a universal investment application; this transformation continues in 2025 and subsequent years. Section 4.6 Product plan for 2025: across the first nine months of 2025 XTB continued the intensive new-client acquisition process begun the prior year, focusing on individuals interested in long-term passive investing and broad financial management. Growth is enabled by the universal investment application, new products and services, and changes improving mobile-app and platform usability. The company cites continuous development of its Product and Technology Department, including development and R&D work on responsible deployment of artificial-intelligence-based technologies.

Q1 2025 opened with the launch of the eWallet service for clients in Poland. The virtual wallet was activated by nearly 22 thousand clients, of whom 43% were in Poland and 57% abroad. eWallet provides a multi-currency card supporting cashless in-store payments, mobile transactions, and contactless ATM cash withdrawals worldwide, enabling accounts currently in 25 currencies (including EUR, USD, GBP, PLN, HUF, RON, CZK, CHF, JPY, CAD, BRL, INR, LKR) plus fast, free transfers between accounts. Clients have instant access to funds and can pay with a card issued under Mastercard license by DiPocket UAB, an Electronic Money Institution registered by the Bank of Lithuania. eWallet is currently available to clients from Poland, Czechia, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Germany, France, Spain, and Italy.

Further announced product changes were introduced in late March, covering products dedicated to long-term saving, including retirement. In Poland, pending orders were added within the IKE (Individual Retirement Account). For UK clients, the option to transfer an existing ISA (Individual Savings Account) to XTB was enabled, covering either the entire account or only part of the assets.

In Q2 2025, the PEA (Plan d'Epargne en Actions) account service launched for clients in France; holders may invest in stocks and ETP funds with favorable tax conditions such as exemption from capital-gains income tax if investments are held at least 5 years. The maximum PEA contribution limit is EUR 150 thousand per client.

In Q3 2025, XTB introduced in Poland the long-awaited IKZE (Individual Retirement Security Account). IKZE may be held by any natural person (non-shareable account), including a sole proprietor, aged 16 or older with Polish tax residency. IKZE gains are exempt from the 19% capital-gains tax, and accumulated capital is inheritable. The account carries an annual contribution limit deductible from income in the tax return. Withdrawals may be made after age 65 and after meeting a condition of contributions over at least 5 calendar years, then taxed at a flat 10% income tax rate; without meeting these conditions, funds may be returned subject to taxation under the applicable annual tax scale.

The Group continues its universal-investment-application strategy serving every investor managing funds short- and long-term, planning portfolio expansion. During the first three quarters of 2025 the XTB team focused on preparing to introduce cryptocurrencies and options. Cryptocurrency introduction is tied to and dependent on completion in Poland of the legislative process enacting law aligning Polish regulations with the MiCA Regulation (Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation). On 16 October 2025 the Senate reviewed the bill, submitted amendments, and referred it to the Public Finance Committee, which on 6 November adopted some of them and returned the bill to the Sejm. Pending legislation, XTB is preparing legal documents and technological changes to the app and platform to enable crypto-assets. Introduction of options depends on KNF (Polish Financial Supervision Authority) acceptance of the option-pricing model; the related process is ongoing, with KNF able to submit additional questions. The product plan reflects current knowledge and resources and depends on external factors beyond the company's control (cooperation with external suppliers, regulatory approvals, enactment of legislation), and may therefore change.
