XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025, corporate governance and group characteristics section. XTB is an international provider of products, services and technology solutions on global financial markets, specializing in investment activity for over 20 years, operating a universal global application enabling online investing anytime and anywhere; debuted on the Warsaw Stock Exchange (Giełda Papierów Wartościowych) regulated market in 2016. The Group conducts brokerage activity under a KNF (Polish Financial Supervision Authority) license, operating cross-border under the EU MiFID II single passport regime in member states including Poland, Spain, Czechia, Slovakia, Romania, Germany, France and Portugal. The Group is subject to regulatory supervision including KNF, FCA, DFSA, CySEC, Bappebti Indonesia and SCA. Group structure (as identified in the 2024 double-materiality assessment, chapter 1.6) comprises XTB S.A. and its 7 foreign branches plus 13 subsidiaries. Governing documents include the XTB S.A. Statute, Management Board Regulations, Supervisory Board Regulations and General Meeting Regulations, publicly available on the XTB ESG portal (https://esg.xtb.com).

Management Board (Zarząd) members are appointed and dismissed by the Supervisory Board for a three-year joint term; the Supervisory Board sets the number of members; members may also be dismissed or suspended by General Meeting resolution. The Board represents and manages the Company, prepares development strategy drafts and defines principal business objectives. The current Management Board was appointed on 25 June 2025 by Supervisory Board resolutions reappointing the existing five-member composition for a new joint three-year term (2 July 2025 to end of 2 July 2028).

On 26 August 2025, by Supervisory Board resolution, Bartosz Osiński was appointed as new Member of the Management Board; on 1 December 2025 the Company received the KNF decision approving his appointment as Member of the Management Board for Risk. Supervisory Board (Rada Nadzorcza) committees: Audit Committee (overseeing XTB sustainable development), Remuneration Committee, and Risk Management Committee. By collegial resolution the Supervisory Board itself performs the tasks of the Remuneration Committee and the Risk Management Committee; since 13 October 2017 the Audit Committee functions as a separate committee. The Supervisory Board may establish additional committees; detailed tasks and appointment/operating rules are set in the Supervisory Board Regulations available in XTB investor relations (Corporate Governance / Regulations section). Full board CVs, competencies, independence and operating principles are detailed in the Management Board Report, Corporate Governance, chapter "Statutory Bodies of XTB."

Consumer and complaint-handling practices: XTB emphasizes transparent complaint processes disclosed at contract conclusion via the Brokerage Services Regulations, which describe complaint channels, required content and maximum response timeframe. A dedicated "Complaints" website section includes the "Instruction on Submitting Complaints" and "Complaint Management Strategy"; complaint information is also in the website Education section. Available complaint channels: in-person (written and oral) at headquarters, by telephone via the helpline, by correspondence to XTB headquarters, and via an electronic form available in the Investor Room (Pokój Inwestora) — the latter being the most flexible, location-independent and continuously monitored channel. All complaints are registered in the CRM system with a unique identifier and status, visible in the Investor Room for client tracking. XTB states it does not tolerate any manifestations of discrimination.
