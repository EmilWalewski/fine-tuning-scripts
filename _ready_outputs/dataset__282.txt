Interim condensed consolidated financial statements (quarterly report) of Grupa KRUK, Section 5 (Judgments and Estimates), Section 6 (Financial Risk Management), and Operating Segments disclosure, with comparative figures at 31.12.2025 vs 31.12.2024 (amounts in thousands PLN, converted to millions). Management asserts that preparation of the interim consolidated financial statements requires judgments, estimates, and assumptions affecting accounting-policy application and reported asset, liability, revenue, and cost values; estimates undergo continuous reassessment based on historical experience and reasonable expectations of future events, with changes recognized prospectively from the period of revision.

Material estimate positions: Investments in debt portfolios (purchased receivable packages) of 11,632.7M PLN at 31.12.2025 vs 10,500.3M PLN at 31.12.2024 (note 9), an increase of ~1,132.4M PLN. Acquired portfolio carrying value at the valuation date is set using an estimation model based on discounted expected cash flows; expected cash inflows are estimated via analytical/portfolio analysis or via individual legal-economic analysis of receivables or debtors. KRUK Group prepares recovery projections independently per market, incorporating historical recovery realizations, current and planned legal regulations, receivable type/character and collateral, current collection strategy, and macroeconomic conditions. An effective interest rate equal to the internal rate of return (including a credit-risk component) is derived from the initial expected cash flow projection and remains unchanged throughout the holding period, used to discount estimated cash inflows.

Loans measured at amortized cost: 610.0M PLN at 31.12.2025 vs 499.6M PLN at 31.12.2024 (note 9), up ~110.4M PLN. Gross loan balance is based on expected cash flows discounted at the effective interest rate, determined for homogeneous loan groups using life tables built on historical early-repayment data; prepayment probability varies with time elapsed since loan origination. Gross balance is reduced by expected credit losses, set using probability of default, loss given default, and exposure at default.

Loans measured at fair value through profit or loss: 2.3M PLN at 31.12.2025 vs 3.3M PLN at 31.12.2024 (note 9), a decline of ~1.0M PLN. Loans failing the SPPI test are measured at fair value determined at Level III, based on projected expected cash flows; key parameters are the discount rate applied to present-value expected flows and expected portfolio credit losses.

Judgment positions: Deferred tax provision — KRUK Group controls the realization of temporary differences in subsidiaries and therefore recognizes deferred tax provisions equal to income tax expected to be payable in the future, based on 3-year plans/forecasts. The provision is affected by changes in assumed future cash flows from investment companies to KRUK S.A., which depend on KRUK S.A. liquidity needs, secured and projected new debt financing for both KRUK S.A. and investment companies, planned outlays on receivable portfolios, and planned collections from purchased portfolios. The "Assumed future cash flows from subsidiaries" deferred tax provision item may be subject to material changes across reporting periods; the Group verifies deferred tax asset recoverability against approved multi-year profit forecasts.

FX differences from translation of capital and profits of subsidiary InvestCapital: -148.9M PLN at 31.12.2025 vs -118.0M PLN at 31.12.2024, an increased negative balance (~30.9M PLN). InvestCapital conducts material transactions in three currencies — EUR, PLN, and RON; per IAS 21, KRUK Group assesses functional-currency alignment quarterly considering historical and planned transactions. Given the volume of planned and held debt-portfolio investments, InvestCapital's functional currency is EUR.

Financial risk management: Principles were presented in the most recent annual consolidated financial statements as at 31 December 2024; no material changes occurred in the financial risk management approach during 1 January–31 December 2025.

Operating segments: Based on revenue materiality in the consolidated statement of profit or loss, the Group distinguishes basic operating segments, reviewed by the Parent's Management Board no less than quarterly via internal management reports. Segments: (1) purchased receivable portfolios — collection of acquired receivable packages; (2) collection services — third-party collection on clients' behalf; (3) other — loan provision and financial intermediation. Primary segment performance measures are indirect margin and EBITDA, reviewed by the Parent's CEO. Operations during the period spanned Poland, Romania, Italy, Spain, Czechia, Slovakia, Germany, and France. Geographic areas distinguished: Poland, Romania, Italy, Spain, and Other foreign markets, with area revenue based on geographic distribution of collection branches. Collection-services revenue and other-product revenue originate from business partners; no inter-segment transaction revenue occurred within the Group.
