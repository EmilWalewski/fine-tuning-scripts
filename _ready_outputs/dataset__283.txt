Supplementary notes to the interim condensed consolidated financial statements of Grupa Asseco for the three-month period ended 31 March 2025 (presented in millions of PLN). On seasonality and cyclicality, the Group's quarterly revenue distribution is subject to minor seasonality, with fourth-quarter revenue typically slightly higher than other quarters because a large portion derives from IT services sales to large enterprises and state institutions, which often make larger investment purchases of equipment and licenses in the final months of the year.

Employment: total headcount across Group companies was 33,716 at 31 March 2025 versus 33,752 at 31 December 2024 (net decline of 36). By function: Parent Company Management Board 12 (vs 11); Management Boards of Group entities 217 (vs 209); production departments 29,229 (vs 29,315, a decrease); sales departments 1,776 (vs 1,767); administrative departments 2,482 (vs 2,450). By segment: Asseco Poland 3,886 (vs 3,822, increase); Asseco International 8,691 (vs 8,822, decrease of 131); Formula Systems 21,139 (vs 21,108, slight increase).

Significant post-balance-sheet events. Acquisition of Sycket Technologies S.L.: on 22 April 2025, Payten Holding S.A. acquired 70% of shares in Sycket Technologies S.L., headquartered in Spain. Dividend resolution: on 14 May 2025, the General Meeting of Shareholders of Asseco Poland S.A. resolved to distribute the net profit generated in financial year 2024 and pay a dividend, with details set out in note 5.7. Acquisition of Automated Workflow Private Limited: on 16 May 2025, Sapiens International Corp. NV acquired 100% of shares in Automated Workflow Private Limited, headquartered in India, together with its subsidiaries, for a purchase price of USD 21.8 million (PLN 82.4 million).

Significant events relating to prior years: through the date of preparation of these interim condensed consolidated financial statements for the three-month period ended 31 March 2025, no prior-year events occurred that should have been but were not recognized in these financial statements.
