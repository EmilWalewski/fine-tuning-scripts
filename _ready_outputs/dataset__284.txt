Dom Development S.A. consolidated sustainability report and Management Board report on the activities of Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group in 2025; section covers shareholder structure, control rights, and restrictions on share rights. Shareholder structure as at 31 December 2025: Groupe Belleforêt S.à r.l. (Luxembourg-domiciled) held 54.81% of shares and was the controlling/dominant shareholder; PTE Allianz Polska S.A. 9.70%; Jarosław Szanajca (Chairman of the Supervisory Board) 5.64%; remaining shareholders 29.86%. Table of holders directly/indirectly controlling at least 5% of total votes at the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS/WZA) as at 31 December 2025: Groupe Belleforêt S.à r.l. 14,140,441 shares = 54.81% of capital and votes; PTE Allianz Polska S.A. 2,501,493 shares = 9.70%; Jarosław Szanajca 1,454,050 shares = 5.64%. No change in number of shares held by any of these three shareholders since 31 December 2024 (all changes reported as "-"). PTE Allianz Polska S.A. holding stated per notification dated 15 May 2023 and comprises shares held by Allianz Polska Otwarty Fundusz Emerytalny (Allianz OFE). On 15 May 2023 the Company received notification from PTE Allianz Polska S.A., manager of Allianz OFE, that its share in total votes at the GMS exceeded 5%; following liquidation of the Second Allianz OFE, 2,501,493 shares (9.70% of share capital and 9.70% of total votes) were recorded in the Allianz OFE account.

Company characterized as having a stable shareholder structure; Groupe Belleforêt S.à r.l. representatives sit on both the Management Board and Supervisory Board, having co-founded Dom Development S.A. Management Board states the transparent, stable ownership structure and long-term shareholder commitment support sustainable development and value-building, suited to the developer industry's exceptionally long multi-year production cycle. Dom Development S.A. shares are held in numerous Open Pension Funds (OFE), with PTE Allianz Polska S.A. dominant among them. Management Board declares no knowledge of any agreements (including post-balance-sheet) that could change shareholding proportions of existing shareholders and bondholders in future, and no information on any insurance, cooperation, or collaboration agreements concluded between shareholders in 2025.

Special control rights: Per Article 6.2.2 of the Company's Statute, a shareholder holding at least 50.1% of shares holds a personal right to appoint and dismiss half of the Management Board members, including the President of the Management Board and the Vice-President responsible for Company finances under the Management Board Regulations; for an odd number of Management Board members, such shareholder appoints three (in a five-member Board) or four (in a seven-member Board) members. This right is exercised via written statement of appointment/dismissal delivered to the Company; remaining Management Board members are appointed and dismissed by the Supervisory Board. Per Article 7.4 of the Statute, a shareholder holding at least 50.1% holds a personal right to appoint and dismiss half of the Supervisory Board members, including one Deputy Chairman of the Supervisory Board; for an odd number, such shareholder appoints three (five-member Supervisory Board), four (seven-member), or five (nine-member) members, exercised via written statement delivered to the Company. As at 31 December 2025, the shareholder holding at least 50.1% of shares was Groupe Belleforêt S.à r.l., domiciled in Luxembourg.

Restrictions on share rights: Pledgees and usufructuaries of the Company's shares are not entitled to exercise voting rights. No restrictions exist on transfer of ownership of Dom Development S.A. shares. Under managerial option programs operated by the Company through 2025, transferability of subscription warrants issued by Dom Development S.A. in connection with those programs was restricted, as warrants could be exercised only by the participant of the given managerial option program, and in case of death could be exercised by heirs only with consent of the Supervisory Board of Dom Development S.A.
