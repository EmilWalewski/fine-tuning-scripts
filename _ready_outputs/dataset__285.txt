Dom Development S.A. Group H1 2025 management report excerpt covering non-financial reporting, dividend, and sales performance.

Non-financial/sustainability reporting: Under the ESG DOM 2030 Strategy, the Management Board of Dom Development S.A. Group committed to implementing a sustainability reporting system. In March 2025, the Capital Group published a sustainability statement within its consolidated management report. Legal basis: Polish Accounting Act of 29 September 1994 (Dz. U. 2023 item 120, as amended), implementing EU Directive 2022/2464 of 14 December 2022 (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive, CSRD), which amends Regulation (EU) No 537/2014 and Directives 2004/109/EC, 2006/43/EC and 2013/34/EU on corporate sustainability reporting. The CSRD was transposed into national law via the Act of 6 December 2024 amending the Accounting Act, the Act on statutory auditors, audit firms and public oversight, and certain other acts (Dz.U. 2024 item 1863). The 2024 materiality assessment identified material impacts, risks and opportunities for sustainability reporting, using qualitative and quantitative inputs from internal and external stakeholders, in line with CSRD requirements and European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS) and applying the double-materiality principle (impact materiality plus financial materiality across short-, medium- and long-term horizons). In H1 2025, no new material circumstances affecting Group sustainability matters were identified.

Record dividend: On 12 June 2025, the General Meeting of Dom Development S.A., following the Management Board recommendation, approved a record dividend of PLN 13.00 per share. Dividend record date set at 25 June 2025; payment date set at 3 July 2025. In 2024, the Company paid (for the second time in its history) an interim dividend on account of the current-year profit of PLN 6.00 per share, totaling PLN 155M. Thus for FY2024 the Company distributed to shareholders a total of PLN 335,379k (335.4M PLN), equal to 62% of the Group's FY2024 consolidated net profit and 84% of the Company's FY2024 net profit. Part of the Company's FY2024 net profit, PLN 335,379k (335.4M PLN), i.e. PLN 13.00 per share, was allocated to the shareholder dividend; accounting for the FY2024 interim dividend of PLN 154,791k (154.8M PLN), i.e. PLN 6.00 per share, paid on 18 December 2024, the FY2024 dividend paid in 2025 to shareholders amounted to PLN 180,589k (180.6M PLN). The remaining portion of net profit, PLN 83,640k (83.6M PLN), was allocated to the Company's supplementary capital. The distribution fully complies with the Dom Development S.A. dividend policy announced 3 April 2013, which provides for systematic growth in shareholder distributions; the Board reaffirms continuation of this strategy, maximizing shareholder benefit while maintaining solid financial condition and high liquidity. The Board views the high return on equity (ROE) achieved by both the Company and the Group as a basis for Dom Development's continued ranking among stock-market leaders for dividend attractiveness, while part of profit is directed to strengthening the balance sheet and financing further operating development. Reported dividend per share history (PLN): 2019 9.05; 2020 9.50; 2021 10.50; 2022 10.00; 2023 11.00; 2024 12.00; 2025 13.00.

Sales: Early 2025 continued late-2024 trends, notably price stabilization in the primary residential market driven by a well-balanced demand/supply ratio. Despite the start of an interest-rate-cutting cycle, the timing and scope of further decisions remain hard to predict, as does the strength and durability of any rebound in homebuying interest in coming quarters. Developer unit supply in Poland's largest cities reached a record level, lengthening time-to-sell and further stabilizing prices. In this environment the Dom Development Group delivered strong results, maintaining high sales dynamics. In H1 2025 the Group sold a total of 2,033 units, up 4% versus the comparable prior-year period (1,954 units in H1 prior year). Sales remained dominated by the Warsaw market, where Dom Development S.A. — leader of the capital's residential market — sold 869 apartments over six months. Euro Styl S.A. held its prior-year result, selling 499 units. Dom Development Wrocław Sp. z o.o. recorded a 15% year-over-year sales increase (437 units vs 379), while in Kraków, Dom Development Kraków Sp. z o.o. sold 228 units, down from 245 (a 7% decline). Unit figures cover apartments and commercial/service premises. The Board considers these results satisfactory given current economic and market conditions. In H1 2025 the Group strengthened its competitive position, increasing market share. Recognizable brand and stable financial standing distinguished the Group within the industry and were important for many clients buying property as an inflation hedge. In the Group's H1 2025 sales structure, 53% of transactions were financed by mortgage credit and 47% were cash purchases, indicating that improving credit conditions support purchasing decisions and increase primary-market activity.
