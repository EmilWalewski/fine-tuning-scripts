Excerpt from the XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025, detailing statutory shareholder rights and corporate governance procedures at General Meetings, with no financial data. Information disclosed to a shareholder must be made public via current report. A shareholder denied requested information during General Meeting proceedings who registered an objection to the minutes may petition the registry court to compel the Management Board to provide the information; such petition must be filed within one week of the close of the General Meeting at which disclosure was refused. A shareholder may also petition the registry court to compel the Company to publish information provided to another shareholder outside the General Meeting. Under the Reports Regulation, the Company is obligated to disclose, via current report, information provided to a shareholder following a registry court order compelling the Management Board in the aforementioned cases.

Right to demand copies of the annual financial statements: every shareholder may demand copies of the Management Board's report on Company operations and the financial statements, together with a copy of the Supervisory Board's report and the statutory auditor's opinion, no later than fifteen days before the General Meeting.

Right to demand election of the Supervisory Board by voting in separate groups: upon request of shareholders representing at least one-fifth of the Company's share capital, the election of the Supervisory Board must be carried out by the next General Meeting through voting in separate groups. The Company promptly publishes draft resolutions on its website.

Right to demand copies of motions: every shareholder may demand copies of motions on matters covered by the agenda of the next General Meeting; such demand must be submitted to the Management Board no later than one week before the General Meeting.

Right to demand verification of the attendance list of shareholders present at the General Meeting: immediately after the election of the chairman of the General Meeting, an attendance list must be prepared listing the participants of the General Meeting, specifying the number of Company shares each represents and the votes attached to them. The attendance list must be signed by the chairman of the General Meeting and displayed during the proceedings. Upon request of shareholders holding one-tenth of the share capital represented at the General Meeting, the attendance list must be verified by a committee elected for this purpose, composed of at least three persons; the applicants have the right to elect one member of the committee.

Right to obtain information: the Management Board is obligated to provide a shareholder, during General Meeting proceedings and upon request, information concerning the Company if justified for assessing a matter on the agenda. Where important reasons warrant, the Management Board may provide information in writing outside the General Meeting; in such case, it must provide the information no later than two weeks from the date the shareholder submitted the demand during the General Meeting.

A petition for a declaration of invalidity of a General Meeting resolution must be filed within thirty days of its announcement, but no later than one year from the date the resolution was adopted.

Entities entitled to challenge General Meeting resolutions: the right to bring an action for a declaration of invalidity or for the annulment of a General Meeting resolution belongs to the Management Board, the Supervisory Board, and individual members of those bodies; to a shareholder who voted against the resolution and, after its adoption, demanded that the objection be recorded in the minutes; to a shareholder unjustifiably barred from participating in the General Meeting; and to shareholders who were not present at the General Meeting, only in the case of defective convening of the General Meeting or adoption of a resolution on a matter not included in the agenda.

Amendment of shareholder rights: a change to the rights of shareholders in the form of an amendment to the Articles of Association requires a General Meeting resolution adopted by a three-fourths majority of votes and entry into the entrepreneurs' register of the National Court Register (KRS). Furthermore, a resolution amending the Articles of Association that increases shareholders' obligations or diminishes rights granted personally to shareholders requires the consent of all affected shareholders.

Redemption of shares: shares may be redeemed by way of a reduction of the Company's share capital, with redemption requiring the consent of the shareholder. The Articles of Association contain no provision on compulsory redemption of shares. The conditions, legal basis, and procedure for redemption of shares, as well as the amount of remuneration for redeemed shares or the justification for redemption without remuneration, are determined in each case by the General Meeting in the form of a resolution.
