In fiscal 2025 and to the report submission date there were no restrictions on exercising voting rights from the Company's securities, no restrictions on transferring ownership of securities, and the Company is aware of no agreements that could change existing shareholders' shareholding proportions.

Dividend: the ordinary General Meeting is the body authorized to decide on profit distribution and dividend payment, determining whether and what portion of audited profit to allocate, and setting the dividend date and payment term. Dividend rights accrue to persons holding dematerialized bearer shares on the dividend date and to entities entitled from dematerialized shares on collective accounts; dividend collection terms follow public-company rules. General Meeting competences (per Commercial Companies Code and Statute) include: review/approval of management report and financial statements; granting discharge to Management and Supervisory Board members; profit distribution or loss coverage; issuance of convertible/priority bonds and subscription warrants (art. 453 §2 KSH); Company liquidation; acquisition of treasury shares for redemption, redemption and share-capital reduction; merger, transformation and division; Statute amendment; approval of Management Board and Supervisory Board regulations; and setting Supervisory Board remuneration rules and amounts.
