Dom Development S.A. reported no management option costs in profit and loss and supplementary capital for H1 2025, versus PLN 0.708 M in H1 2024, with no share options outstanding at either the start or end of the 2025 six-month period; by comparison, H1 2024 began with 200,000 options outstanding, 100,000 exercised, and 100,000 remaining at period-end, with total exercise prices of PLN 10.000 M initially and PLN 5.000 M at the end and a weighted average exercise price of PLN 50.00 per share. Contingent liabilities declined to PLN 20.738 M as of 30 June 2025 from PLN 37.644 M at 31 December 2024, including guarantees down to PLN 18.988 M from PLN 29.744 M and sureties down to PLN 1.750 M from PLN 7.900 M. Obligations secured by promissory notes remained PLN 3.900 M at both dates, including notes representing other collateral and no leasing-contract promissory-note collateral. In H1 2025 the Company granted no material credit or loan sureties or guarantees to one entity or subsidiary meeting the stated materiality threshold, and it was not party to material court proceedings as of 30 June 2025. Operations continued without disruption, investments proceeded on schedule, and improving mortgage availability after the first interest-rate cut in more than 18 months and lower WIBOR rates near the end of Q2 2025 revived sales offices and supported results, while record developer apartment supply in Poland’s largest cities lengthened sell-out times and stabilized primary-market prices. In this environment, Dom Development and its Group maintained high sales of about 1,000 units per quarter, supported by stable construction costs, negative year-on-year building-material price dynamics, in-house general contracting, long-term subcontractor relationships and controlled execution risk, which helped margins, predictable project launches, satisfactory profitability and quality. The Company planned continued systematic offer expansion in later 2025 quarters. From 1 January to 30 June 2025, Dom Development S.A. started 829 apartments and commercial units in Warsaw projects, including 292 in Q1 and 537 in Q2, with the largest starts in Dzielnica Mieszkaniowa Metro Zachód and related Warsaw developments. Completed projects totaled 641 units in H1 2025, including 236 in Q1 and 405 in Q2, led by Osiedle Przy Ryżowej phases in Q2. Subsidiaries also conducted projects in Wrocław, Tricity and Kraków. Handovers of residential and commercial units to retail clients fell to 497 in H1 2025 from 1,088 in H1 2024, comprising 341 in Q1 and 156 in Q2, while total Q2 2025 handovers were 253 including 97 units transferred to a PRS investor.
