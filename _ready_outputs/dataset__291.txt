Independent auditor's review report issued by UHY ECA Audyt Sp. z o.o. (Połczyńska 31A, 01-377 Warsaw; member of UHY International network of legally independent audit and consulting firms) addressed to the General Meeting and Supervisory Board of CREOTECH INSTRUMENTS S.A. ("the Company"). The report concerns a review of the Company's interim condensed financial statements comprising: the interim condensed statement of financial position as at 30 June 2025; the interim condensed statement of comprehensive income; the interim condensed statement of changes in equity; and the interim condensed statement of cash flows for the period from 1 January to 30 June 2025; together with selected explanatory notes. Management of the Company is responsible for preparing and presenting the interim condensed financial statements in accordance with International Accounting Standard 34 "Interim Financial Reporting" as adopted via European Commission regulations. The auditor is responsible for formulating a conclusion on the interim condensed financial statements based on the review performed. The review was conducted in accordance with National Review Standard 2410, reflecting International Standard on Review Engagements 2410 "Review of Interim Financial Information Performed by the Independent Auditor of the Entity," adopted by resolution of the National Council of Statutory Auditors. A review consists principally of making inquiries, primarily of persons responsible for financial and accounting matters, and applying analytical and other review procedures. The review is substantially narrower in scope than an audit conducted in accordance with National Auditing Standards reflecting International Standards on Auditing (adopted by resolutions of the National Council of Statutory Auditors and the Council of the Polish Audit Oversight Agency); consequently it does not provide assurance that all significant matters that would be identified in an audit have been disclosed, and accordingly no audit opinion is expressed. Conclusion: based on the review performed, nothing came to the auditor's attention that would cause it to believe the interim condensed financial statements were not prepared, in all material respects, in accordance with IAS 34 "Interim Financial Reporting" as adopted via European Commission regulations.
