CEO letter from Mikołaj Konopka, President of the Management Board of Dom Development S.A., covering record sales, handovers, and financial results for the Dom Development Capital Group in 2025, framed within the ESG DOM 2030 strategy. 2025 was a record year across multiple dimensions in the company's 30-year history. The Group sold a record 4,448 net units, up 4% versus the prior record of 4,269 units in 2024. Total value of units sold in 2025 reached PLN 3.46bn, also a 4% year-over-year increase. Sales exceeded at least 1,000 units in every quarter of the year, confirming a sustained trend rather than a one-off and reinforcing the Group's leading market position. Record sales are attributed to structural advantages and exploitation of favorable market conditions, including indirect benefit from the price-transparency law (ustawa o jawności cen) in force since September of the prior year, which raised marketing and sales-professionalization costs but constrained smaller competitors; brand reputation; long market presence; and high customer trust amid growing emphasis on transaction security. In 2025, mortgage-cofinanced transactions represented 56% of total Group sales. The year closed with a record 4,228 units handed over to retail clients, 8% higher than 2024; including units delivered to a PRS-segment investor, total handovers reached 4,325 units, another all-time high. Net Promoter Score reached 69 points (scale -100 to 100), exceeding the prior year, reflecting rising customer satisfaction. Record net profit exceeded PLN 654m, up nearly 15% year-over-year, driven by higher handovers combined with stable construction costs, positively affecting both revenues and realized margins. Dom Development has paid dividends uninterruptedly since its Warsaw Stock Exchange debut in 2006. The General Meeting approved a record dividend for 2024 totaling over PLN 335m, i.e. PLN 13 per share; of this, nearly PLN 155m (PLN 6 per share) was paid as an advance in December 2024, with the remaining nearly PLN 181m (PLN 7 per share) distributed in July 2025. Additionally, in December 2025 shareholders received an advance on the 2025 dividend, also nearly PLN 181m (PLN 7 per share). On offer and land bank: at end-2025 the Group's offer comprised approximately 3,500 units available for sale, with a sell-out period of 2.5 to 3.2 quarters depending on city, significantly shorter than the market average, indicating sales efficiency and a market share by sales higher than implied by offer size. The company entered 2026 with projects under construction totaling nearly 7,200 units, of which 56% were already sold; while many competitors face oversupply, the majority of units sell during construction and the share of completed unsold units remains at a low single-digit level. At end-2025, cumulative net sales to be recognized in future periods (sold but not yet handed over) covered 5,200 units valued at PLN 4bn, versus just under 5,000 units valued at PLN 3.7bn at end-2024. The Group regularly launches new projects and replenishes its land bank across all markets of operation: Warsaw, Tricity (Trójmiasto), Wrocław, and Kraków. In 2025 it spent over PLN 840m on land acquisitions, versus over PLN 560m a year earlier. At end-December the land bank held potential to build nearly 19,000 units, enough to sustain current sales for about 4 years. The 2026 target is to further strengthen its leading market position and maintain sales significantly above 4,000 units, while preparing to achieve over 5,000 units sold annually in subsequent years. Identified challenges include regulatory-environment volatility and potential external geopolitical or macroeconomic risks; absent unforeseeable events, market prospects are assessed as positive. Despite unfavorable demographic trends, the rising number of households—especially one- and two-person—naturally generates housing demand; the Group concentrates activity in the largest, dynamically developing urban centers, which are more resilient to economic fluctuations, attract steady population inflow, and have aging housing stock that often fails to meet modern living standards, leaving room to capture demand. Under the ESG DOM 2030 strategy, the company continued the Miejska Zieleń (Urban Greenery) program, creating new publicly accessible green areas in each agglomeration and revitalizing neglected sites for local communities; in 2025, the program's second year, it allocated nearly PLN 8m and developed over 2 hectares. It also implemented a Sustainable Procurement Standard (Standard Zrównoważonych Zamówień) governing resource use and circular-economy principles within the Group, organizing existing solutions and setting new targets. The company enters its 30th year as a stable, responsible, well-established firm with an optimistic outlook on continued growth. Letter signed by Mikołaj Konopka, President of the Management Board of Dom Development S.A.
