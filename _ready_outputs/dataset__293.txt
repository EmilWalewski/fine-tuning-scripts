Excerpt from the Consolidated Annual Report of the XTB S.A. Capital Group for 2025, presenting the group's core product offering. The first segment details XTB's CFD (Contract for Difference) business: CFDs are described as over-the-counter (OTC) derivative contracts between a "buyer" and a "seller" tracking the price movement of an underlying asset, transacted directly between parties (hence OTC) and deriving value from underlying asset prices (hence derivative). Eligible underlyings include currency pairs (FOREX), equities, major stock indices, commodities, and increasingly popular cryptocurrencies. The report emphasizes that buying a CFD on a rising asset (e.g., EUR/USD or gold) does not constitute actual purchase or ownership of the currency or precious metal; the investor owns only the specific CFD contract and speculates purely on the price movement of the instrument, which may rise or fall—the same principle applying to equity CFDs. CFD trading allows investors to take a long position (buy) when anticipating a price increase or a short position (sell) when anticipating a price decrease, with all CFD contracts executed using financial leverage. XTB's CFD product range comprises over 60 CFDs based on currency pairs, over 20 based on stock indices, over 20 based on commodities, and 30 based on cryptocurrencies. The second segment covers XTB's Investment Plans (Plany Inwestycyjne), an intelligent solution dedicated to passive investing of growing popularity, enabling clients to build a low-cost investment portfolio based on ETF (Exchange-Traded Fund) funds. By offering broad market exposure, an investor can achieve a market rate of return with relatively little time and energy commitment. Investment Plans permit creation of a personalized ETF portfolio starting from as little as 50 PLN. A single client may run up to 10 Investment Plans simultaneously, with the client independently determining the duration of the investment. XTB's current offering includes over 1,900 different ETF funds available within Investment Plans, providing exposure to entire markets, industry sectors, or geographic regions.
