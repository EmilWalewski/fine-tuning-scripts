APplus features an AI-supported module recommending interface elements matching real usage, derived from anonymously observed individual APplus usage, similar roles, or industry-specific scenarios.

Czech Asseco Solutions holds a 25% share of the local SME-dedicated ERP market, offering 4 proprietary systems used by over 18 thousand clients across the Czech Republic and Slovakia in both enterprise and public sectors; it grows SME sales and offers a medium/large-enterprise solution under the Helios brand. Slovak Asseco Solutions, holding ~17% of the domestic market with ERP systems deployed at over 4 thousand clients, focused in Q1 2025 on acquiring new contractors and developed sales of next-generation proprietary products under the QASIDA brand.

Asseco South Eastern Europe Group (ASEE Group), operating mainly in South-Eastern Europe, grew Q1 2025 revenue 9% year-over-year; reported results were negatively affected by a decline in the average EUR/PLN rate, with EUR-denominated revenue 13% higher YoY. Geographically, South-Eastern Europe accounted for 59% of total ASEE sales, with material contributions from Central Europe (13%), Western Europe (13%), and Turkey (12%); ASEE also generates revenue in the Middle East, Africa, India, and Latin America. By segment: payments 55% of sales, dedicated solutions 26%, banking 19%. Payments revenue rose 24% (driven by nearly all business lines, especially eCommerce and payment-card processing, ATM line and services including an independent ATM network, plus independent payment terminal network/IPD and electronic cash registers/ECR directed at points of sale). Banking sales rose 7% (core banking systems, multichannel systems, and security solutions). Dedicated solutions were affected by slowdown in new contract signings, mainly the third-party solutions line in Serbia and Romania; the proprietary-solutions line grew, chiefly via call/contact center and business process management (BPM) solutions.

ASEE Group remains acquisition-active: in the period, Payten Holding S.A. acquired 51% of shares in Fawaterk for E-payments (Cairo-based), specializing in online payments for SMEs, enabling secure e-payment acceptance, purchase-report generation, and invoicing. After the reporting period, Spanish company Sycket Technologies joined ASEE, offering mobile/web application development and point-of-sale (POS) management systems. ASEE results were also affected by restatement under IAS 29 of Turkish subsidiaries' financial data to reflect purchasing power at period-end (Turkey's accounting status as a hyperinflationary economy); impact detailed in point B1 and note 2.11 of the interim condensed consolidated financial statements.

In Western Europe, Asseco PST operates in Portugal, Portuguese-speaking African countries (Angola, Mozambique, Cape Verde, East Timor, São Tomé and Príncipe), Namibia, and Malta, continuing and expanding cooperation with banks on maintenance and development of core banking systems. Its portfolio holds ~70 financial institutions; its solutions are used by ~80% of banks in Angola, ~50% in Cape Verde, ~30% in Namibia, ~25% in Portugal and Mozambique, and ~20% in East Timor and São Tomé and Príncipe. Clients access products via the cloud Core Banking System (CBS) platform—a complete, flexible, modular solution with shared components (centralized client catalog, product and price catalog). Asseco PST also provides a mobile-banking solution using USSD (Unstructured Supplementary Service Data) technology, enabling basic banking for clients lacking smartphones or broadband, and the ACSS (Asseco Customer Service Suite) CRM tool offered on Microsoft Marketplace with Microsoft integration.

Asseco PST subsidiary Finantech – Sistemas de Informação continued intensive Angola activity deploying its flagship capital-market platform SIFOX, supported Portuguese trading-platform clients, and presented a prototype of the new Sifox Trading Online solution. Second PST subsidiary CPI Angola (Consultoria de Projetos Informáticos), a provider of proprietary accounting and mandatory reporting systems for the financial sector, expanded in Portugal and Angola supporting bank digitalization.

Asseco Spain expanded competencies in artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and digital transformation; Mimecast selected Asseco Spain as its strategic partner in Spain, with Asseco Spain delivering AI-based cybersecurity covering all implementation/installation phases. It developed its proprietary AI Project Assistant (AIPA) tool, which migrates legacy application code to the latest programming languages with security emphasis, shortening project times, raising efficiency, and lowering costs; AIPA is used by public, financial, and utilities clients.

In Q1 2025 Asseco Lietuva continued a 3-year contract (signed 2023) for maintenance and development of a billing system with Ignitis Group, a major Baltic-region energy group; worked for Vilniaus Vandenys (largest Lithuanian water utility) on the 3-module FAVAKA system (CRM, billing, financial accounting) to be integrated with internal/external systems, running through 2026; executed a late-2023 contract with the Lithuanian Public Employment Service (Ministry of Social Security and Labour) to create, implement, maintain, and develop the UZTIS information system (main service channel for jobseekers/employers, deployment within 2 years); implemented the integrated university management support system (VUSIS) at Vilnius University (with later support/development responsibility); executed a December 2024 contract with the National Library of Lithuania to modernize the Virtual Electronic Heritage Information System (VEPIS) and create its eKultūra portal subsystem; and is responsible for implementing/developing the Integrated Criminal Process Information System (IISPP) and its EU systems integration.
