Dom Development S.A. interim condensed standalone financial statements for the six months ended 30 June 2025, covering bond issuance programs and interest liabilities. The company maintains two revolving bond issuance programs, each capped at PLN 400 million in aggregate nominal value of issued and outstanding bonds: one under an agreement with Trigon Dom Maklerski S.A. and Trigon Investment Banking Sp. z o.o. & Wspólnicy S.K. (bonds issuable in series until 17 November 2027), and one under a new issuance agreement signed 14 April 2025 with mBank S.A. (bonds issuable in series for an indefinite period from signing).

Material change in the period: on 14 April 2025 the Management Board resolved to terminate the prior bond issuance program (capped at PLN 400,000k nominal, established by Board resolution of 7 February 2023) and to establish a new program ("Program") with aggregate nominal value not exceeding PLN 400 million. Under the prior program, the company continues to service two outstanding bond series: (a) 260,000 unsecured bearer bonds series DOMDEM1280928, total nominal value PLN 260.0M, maturing 28 September 2028; and (b) 140,000 unsecured bearer bonds series DOMDEM2051229, total nominal value PLN 140.0M, maturing 5 December 2029. The new mBank S.A. Issuance Agreement (14 April 2025) provides for bonds that are: issued either via public offering under Art. 33(1) of the 15 January 2015 Bonds Act (with prospectus exemption per Art. 1(4)(a) or (b) of the EU Prospectus Regulation 2017/1129) or via non-public offering to a single investor under Art. 33(2); ordinary unsecured bearer bonds; maturity up to 60 months; fixed or variable interest; dematerialized and registered with Krajowy Depozyt Papierów Wartościowych S.A. and admitted to the alternative trading system run by the Warsaw Stock Exchange (GPW). The Issuance Agreement allows a guarantee agreement per series under which mBank S.A. may commit to subscribe a given series.

Early redemption for cancellation: on 12 May 2025 the company executed early redemption for cancellation of 663 unsecured bearer bonds series DOMDET4250925, nominal value PLN 1,000 each (maturity 25 September 2025), total nominal value PLN 663k. Buyback price per bond was PLN 669.58; total redemption price was PLN 670k.
