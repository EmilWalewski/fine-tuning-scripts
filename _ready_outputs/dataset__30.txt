Dom Development Group maintained a financially conservative funding structure in H1 2025, with consolidated equity of PLN 1,762.429M at 30 June 2025, equal to 29% of total assets, and deferred income liabilities of PLN 2,219.422M, or 37% of assets, reflecting customer prepayments before unit and parking-space handovers. Interest-bearing debt was PLN 799.366M, with only 26% short term. The balance sheet also included PLN 180.589M of dividend liabilities after the 12 June 2025 Ordinary General Meeting; the total 2024 profit dividend was PLN 335.380M, including PLN 154.791M paid in 2024 and PLN 180.589M paid on 3 July 2025. H1 2025 net operating cash inflow was PLN 201.631M, down 24% y/y, while investing cash outflow was PLN 14.580M, mainly PLN 9.737M for intangible assets and property, plant and equipment. Financing cash inflow rose sharply to PLN 187.744M from PLN 3.110M in H1 2024, driven by PLN 135.000M of debt securities issuance and PLN 55.029M of net credit proceeds. Cash and equivalents increased by PLN 374.795M in H1 2025 to PLN 735.641M, 35% above the H1 2024 closing level of PLN 544.921M, with opening cash of PLN 360.846M, up 26% y/y. Management assessed profitability as very satisfactory: gross sales margin improved to 35.2% from 32.0%, operating margin to 23.3% from 20.9%, net margin to 19.0% from 16.9%, ROE to 38.4% from 34.6%, and ROA to 11.4% from 10.7%. Management highlighted the 35.2% gross margin, up 3.1 p.p. y/y, as industry-distinguishing and attributed it to strong market reading, sales policy execution, cost control and operating-process optimization, while noting that despite somewhat lower handover volume the Group still lifted EBIT margin to 23.3%. It cautioned that interim ROE and ROA can be affected by dividend timing and uneven quarterly handovers and should mainly be assessed over full fiscal years.
