Excerpt from Grupa Kęty S.A. consolidated quarterly report for Q3 2025 (9 months ended 30.09.2025), covering accounting estimates, seasonality, and operating segment results; all figures in millions PLN.

Accounting estimates (Section 5): Principal estimates are disclosed in interim condensed consolidated financial statement notes — lease recognition/measurement (note 18); inventory write-downs (note 15); receivables impairment allowances (note 14); goodwill impairment (note 13); provisions and accrued liabilities (note 16); deferred tax assets/provisions (note 11); managerial option valuation (note 19); investment properties (note 37 of FY2024 consolidated statements); production-technology liability valuation (notes 10.8 and 28 of FY2024 statements); impairment of property, plant and equipment (note 12.2); asset impairment (notes 13.7 and 13.8 of FY2024 statements); assessment of control/significant influence over other entities (note 10.1 of FY2024 statements); and depreciation rates (notes 10.4 and 10.8 of FY2024 statements). Detailed assumptions are presented in the FY2024 consolidated statements. Over the nine months of 2025, no material changes in assumptions or estimates occurred relative to 2024, and no other changes in estimates arose in the reporting period.

Seasonality (Section 6): The Group operates in three operating segments, offering a broad product portfolio serving numerous industries across diverse economic sectors; approximately half of sales are realized in foreign markets. Product offering development and diversification in recent years have reduced the impact of seasonality. Seasonality is not material in the SOG segment. In the SWW and SSA segments, typically higher demand for products is observed in Q2 and Q3 versus Q1 and Q4, driven mainly by weather conditions and production cycles characteristic of the construction and industrial sectors, to which a significant share of these segments' products is directed. Management assesses that the Group's operations do not exhibit material seasonality within the meaning of IAS 34 para. 21.

Operating segments (Section 7): The Group is organized and managed by operating segments delineated by the type of products and services offered; each segment supplies distinct products and serves different markets, consistent with the management reporting structure used by the Management Board for operating and strategic decisions. Inter-segment transactions are settled as if between unrelated parties, at current market prices. Operating segments are identical to reportable segments. The Group operates and reports across: Segment of Extruded Products (SWW) — manufacture and sale of aluminium profiles; Segment of Aluminium Systems (SSA) — manufacture and sale of facade systems, window-and-door systems, and roller-shutter systems for construction, and since September 2024, following the acquisition of SELT Sp. z o.o., also sun-protection/shading systems for construction; Segment of Flexible Packaging (SOG) — manufacture and sale of plastic packaging and packaging materials; plus an "Other" item comprising the Grupa Kęty S.A. Group Centre (responsible for Finance, Reporting, IT, PR, Investor Relations, Risk Management and Compliance, Internal Audit, Development and Capital Investments, HR) and the subsidiary Dekret Centrum Rachunkowe Sp. z o.o. providing accounting and HR-payroll services to Group companies. Allocation of companies to segments is presented in note 2.

Segment financial results (Section 7.1, nine months 2025): Segment performance is assessed mainly on revenues, operating profit (EBIT), EBIT plus depreciation (EBITDA), and capital expenditure.

Consolidated totals (Razem) for the nine months ended 30.09.2025 / balance sheet at 30.09.2025: Revenue from contracts with customers 4,243M PLN; EBIT 639M PLN; depreciation 178M PLN; EBITDA 817M PLN; net interest result reflecting interest income 1M PLN against interest costs of 60M PLN; profit before tax 581M PLN; income tax (114M PLN); net profit 467M PLN. Balance sheet: total assets 4,378M PLN; liabilities 2,482M PLN; capital expenditure on property, plant and equipment 141M PLN. Intercompany dividends of 581M PLN recorded in the "Other" item are fully eliminated in consolidation (net nil), and consolidation eliminations reduce revenue by 499M PLN. Segment-level revenue from contracts with customers: SOG 875M PLN (entirely external) and SWW 1,573M PLN (of which 1,097M PLN external and 476M PLN from related entities); inter-segment revenues are eliminated on consolidation. Inventory write-downs net to a 2M PLN charge at the Group level.
