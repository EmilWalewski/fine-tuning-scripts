Excerpt from XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025 Consolidated Annual Report, ESG/sustainability section presenting double-materiality assessment tables (Table 5) of material risks and opportunities across environment/climate, own workforce, and consumers/end-users, mapped to impact type, strategy linkage, anticipated effects, risk/opportunity management, financial effects, and value-chain location. Climate change risk: external-event/business-continuity risk from sudden extreme weather (storms, earthquakes, hurricanes, floods) at certain XTB Group company and branch locations; locally material for employee safety and potential loss of IT infrastructure; not arising from strategy/business model; no impact on business model, value chain, or decision process; mitigations include flexible employment forms, diversification of office/branch locations, diversification of IT infrastructure locations, primary IT infrastructure sited in areas not exposed to extreme weather, securing IT equipment in higher-risk locations, and implementation of XTB Climate Policy; no current financial effects, Group unable to estimate financial impact; arises in own operations. Climate change opportunity: enhanced competitiveness, reputation, and resilience via climate-action measures; stems from ESG Strategy assumptions; no impact on strategy/business model/value chain/decision process; actions include implementing XTB Group Climate Policy, promoting awareness of rational resource use, document digitalization, use of renewable (OZE) energy, and responsible management of IT infrastructure and e-waste; no current financial effects, impact unquantifiable; own operations.

Own workforce risk: human-resources and reputational risk of losing qualified staff and increased turnover from insufficient employee well-being/work-life balance, inadequate training, or insufficient internal anti-mobbing/discrimination practices; not arising from strategy/business model; risk identification drove strategic goals — implementation of a transparent remuneration policy, cyclical first-aid training, and introduction of a DEI policy (completed); no impact on business model; possible impact on own workforce and organizational decision process; mitigations: competitive employment terms and benefits (including the "Helping-Hand" well-being platform), flexible employment forms, annual employee satisfaction survey, employee representatives advocating before the Management Board, pro-employee health-prevention and healthy-habit initiatives, and a whistleblowing procedure; no current financial effects, impact unquantifiable; own operations. Own workforce opportunity: attracting talent, strengthening organizational culture, and increasing XTB's employer attractiveness through ESG pro-employee actions; stems from ESG Strategy; no impact on strategy/business model/value chain/decision process; actions: creating a modern developmental work environment, competitive employment terms, flexible employment forms, HR procedures/policies, initiatives undertaken, and ESG strategy implementation; no current financial effects, impact unquantifiable; own operations.

Consumers and end-users product risk: product/customer-complaint and reputational risk from complaints due to XTB service outages, misunderstanding of XTB document/contract provisions (despite their completeness, availability, transparency), or clients taking on investment risk without sufficient preparation (e.g., investing in leveraged financial instruments); not arising from strategy/business model; no impact on strategy/business model; possible impact on value-chain stakeholders and decision process; mitigations: continuous monitoring of IT systems and infrastructure, clear/transparent/legally compliant document and contract provisions, educational materials building investment knowledge and product-risk awareness, and a MiFID questionnaire assessing service suitability to clients' knowledge, experience, preferences, and risk appetite; no current financial effects, impact unquantifiable; arises in own operations and downstream. Consumers and end-users reputational/client-loss risk: risk of losing reputation, clients, and key business partners from declining XTB service quality should employment conditions worsen — ineffective employee policy causing increased turnover, loss of talent and key staff, leading to cost pressure and reduced employer attractiveness; not arising from strategy/business model; no impact on strategy/business model; possible impact on value-chain stakeholders and decision process; mitigations mirror the workforce-risk controls (competitive terms/benefits incl. Helping-Hand, flexible employment, annual satisfaction survey, employee representatives before the Management Board, pro-employee health initiatives); no current financial effects, impact unquantifiable; own operations and downstream. Consumers and end-users opportunity: strengthening the XTB brand and competitive advantage by positioning XTB as a responsible entity ahead of market and regulatory trends, ensuring service quality and transaction security, and heeding critical client feedback; does not arise from strategy/business model; no impact on strategy/business model/value chain/decision process; actions: developing XTB technology and innovation, tracking trends and responsible management decisions, employing qualified experts, monitoring service and product quality, gathering client feedback, and sharing financial/investment knowledge; no current financial effects, impact unquantifiable; own operations and downstream. The excerpt also references reporting on reputational damage and penalties and the hiring/training of qualified employees as a mitigating action. No financial figures, monetary values, shareholders, board appointments, dividends, acquisitions, or tax data are present in this qualitative ESG materiality section.
