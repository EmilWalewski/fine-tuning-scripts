Excerpt from XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025 covering corporate governance and ethical standards. The Company maintains a Code of Ethics (Kodeks Etyki) defining fundamental norms, principles of conduct, and values to be observed by all employees. All Company employees are obligated to responsible and ethical behavior, adherence to the principles arising from the Code of Ethics, and honest conduct across all activities and interpersonal and business interactions. The Company makes every effort to conduct its business activity in compliance with the law and with adopted standards of business conduct, as reflected in applicable acts of universally binding law and internal regulations. Employees observe principles of social coexistence within mutual relations, loyalty, honesty, and management of conflicts of interest.
