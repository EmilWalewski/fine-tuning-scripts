Consolidated Annual Report of the XTB S.A. Capital Group for 2025 presents the Equity and Liabilities side of the statement of financial position, with explicit metric-period-value mapping for 31 December 2025 versus 31 December 2024 (all figures in thousands PLN, normalized here to millions). On the liabilities side, total liabilities reached 6,742.9M PLN at year-end 2025, up from 4,419.1M PLN at year-end 2024, an increase of 2,323.8M PLN. The dominant component was amounts due to customers (liabilities to clients), which rose to 6,253.9M PLN at 31 December 2025 from 3,992.1M PLN a year earlier, a year-over-year increase of 2,261.8M PLN. These customer liabilities arise from transactions executed by clients, including cash deposited on client accounts, and constituted the single most significant balance-sheet item. Per the narrative, amounts due to customers represented 92.8% of total liabilities and equity at the end of 2025, up from 90.3% at the end of 2024. Other liability components at 31 December 2025: financial liabilities measured at fair value through profit or loss 213.4M PLN (vs 171.8M PLN in 2024, +41.6M PLN); lease liabilities 16.8M PLN (vs 22.8M PLN, a decrease of 6.0M PLN); other liabilities 175.1M PLN (vs 156.4M PLN, +18.7M PLN); provisions for liabilities 6.1M PLN (vs 3.3M PLN, +2.9M PLN); current income tax liabilities 0.5M PLN (vs 12.8M PLN, a decline of 12.3M PLN); and deferred income tax provision (deferred tax liability) 77.0M PLN (vs 59.9M PLN, +17.1M PLN).

On the equity side, the Company's total equity amounted to 1,990.4M PLN at the end of December 2025, marginally lower than at 31 December 2024 by 0.1%, i.e., a decrease of 2.2M PLN (from 1,992.5M PLN). The share of equity in the Company's total balance-sheet sum fell to 22.8% at the end of December 2025, compared with 31.1% at the end of 2024. Equity composition at 31 December 2025: share capital (kapitał podstawowy) 5.9M PLN (unchanged from 2024 at 5.9M PLN); supplementary/reserve capital (kapitał zapasowy) 71.6M PLN (unchanged at 71.6M PLN); other reserve capitals (pozostałe kapitały rezerwowe) 1,274.3M PLN, up from 1,059.5M PLN in 2024 (an increase of 214.8M PLN); foreign exchange translation differences (różnice kursowe z przeliczenia) negative 0.3M PLN at 2025 year-end versus positive 0.4M PLN at 2024 year-end (a swing of approximately 0.7M PLN); and retained earnings (zyski zatrzymane) 638.9M PLN, down from 855.2M PLN in 2024, a decline of 216.3M PLN. Total equity and liabilities (total balance-sheet sum) stood at 8,733.3M PLN at 31 December 2025.

The narrative also reports the return on assets, computed as the ratio of the Company's net profit to the balance-sheet total: 7.3% as stated for one reporting date and 13.3% for the comparative date (the source text labels both percentage figures as relating to 31 December 2024; the underlying period attribution is ambiguous in the text and is therefore reported as stated without reconciliation). The overall structural shift over the year reflects rapid growth of customer deposit liabilities driving the balance-sheet expansion, a near-flat absolute equity base, and a corresponding decline in the equity-to-total-assets ratio, with retained earnings reduced and other reserve capitals materially increased.
