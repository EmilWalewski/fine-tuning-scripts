XTB S.A. Capital Group Q3 2025 Report, interim condensed standalone financial statements; section on regulatory-environment changes most material to the Company, covering recent and forthcoming measures.

DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act, EU regulation amending Regs (EC) 1060/2009, (EU) 648/2012, 600/2014, 909/2014): published in EU Official Journal 27 Dec 2022; targets financial-sector resilience to ICT threats via ICT risk management (incl. third-party tech providers), periodic digital-resilience testing, detailed incident classification/reporting, and information-sharing on defense techniques. Entered into force 16 Jan 2023; financial entities required to comply by 17 Jan 2025. Company exercised due diligence to adapt.

Act of 25 Jun 2025 amending laws to ensure financial-sector digital operational resilience: implements DORA into Polish law (draft published 18 Apr 2024). Confirms KNF as competent supervisory authority; requires submission of ICT-contract information to KNF by 31 January annually; amends bank outsourcing rules (expanded delegation grounds, added ICT obligations); empowers KNF to review operational-resilience test results. Penalties for breaches: up to ~PLN 21M (or 10% of revenue) for entities, up to ~PLN 3M for responsible individuals, plus up to 1-year ban from management/supervisory/managerial functions. Enacted 25 Jun 2025, signed by President 31 Jul 2025, effective 7 Aug 2025.

Minister of Finance Regulation of 20 Sep 2024 on information provided to KNF by investment firms, state banks conducting brokerage, banks under Art. 70(2) of the financial-instruments trading act, and custodian banks: effective 28 Sep 2024; more detailed, comprehensive reporting aligned with EU standards and DORA; new definitions, expanded risk-management requirements, stricter reporting.

Act of 6 Dec 2024 amending the Accounting Act, the Act on statutory auditors/audit firms/public oversight and other acts: implements Directive (EU) 2022/2464 (CSRD) and Delegated Directive (EU) 2023/2775; eases bookkeeping for small/micro entities, introduces sustainability reporting and assurance for large/listed entities and certain financial institutions, simplifies group reporting. Signed by President 12 Dec 2024, effective 14 days after promulgation; size-threshold changes effective 1 Jan 2025; sustainability-reporting provisions phased in 2024-2027.

Act of 26 Apr 2024 on accessibility requirements (implementing Directive (EU) 2019/882): effective 28 Jun 2025; pre-existing service contracts may continue until expiry but no later than 28 Jun 2030; non-compliant products permissible until 28 Jun 2030.

Act of 17 Oct 2025 on financial-market complaint handling, Financial Ombudsman and Financial Education Fund: mandates electronic complaint acceptance; passed by Sejm 12 Sep 2025, Senate amendments adopted 17 Oct 2025, sent for presidential signature; effective 3 months after promulgation.

Act of 26 Sep 2025 on the crypto-asset market: implements Regulation 2023/1114 (MiCA) for supervision and investor protection; enacted 26 Sep 2025, Senate filed 81 amendments 16 Oct 2025, Public Finance Committee adopted some 6 Nov, returned to Sejm.
