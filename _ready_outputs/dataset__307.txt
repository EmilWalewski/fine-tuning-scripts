Dom Development S.A. consolidated financial statements for year ended 31 December 2025, Section 7 supplementary notes covering parent-company management/supervisory board changes and operational portfolio activity. Management Board changes: Jarosław Szanajca submitted resignation as President of the Management Board on 28 August 2024, effective 31 December 2024, intending to join and support the company from the Supervisory Board; he recommended majority shareholder Groupe Belleforét S.a r.l. (Luxembourg) appoint then-Management Board Member Mikołaj Konopka as President. Acting under Statute point 6.2.2, Groupe Belleforét appointed Mikołaj Konopka President of the Management Board effective 1 January 2025 for a joint three-year term. Monika Perekitko resigned as Management Board Member on 29 October 2024, effective same day; shareholder holding at least 50.1% of shares (Groupe Belleforét) appointed Justyna Wilk as Management Board Member effective 1 January 2025. The Supervisory Board appointed Grzegorz Smoliński as Management Board Member from 29 October 2024; both appointed for a joint three-year term per Statute 6.2.3. Leszek Stankiewicz resigned as Member and Vice-President of the Management Board on 29 November 2024, effective 28 February 2025; on 29 November 2024 Groupe Belleforét appointed Monika Dobosz as Member and Vice-President effective 1 March 2025 for a joint three-year term, responsible for finance per Management Board Regulations. As at 31 December 2025 the five-member Management Board comprised: Mikołaj Konopka (President), Monika Dobosz (Vice-President), Justyna Wilk (Member), Grzegorz Smoliński (Member), Terry Roydon (Member). Supervisory Board changes: on 21 November 2024 the Extraordinary General Meeting resolved that from 1 January 2025 the Supervisory Board would comprise eight members (amending resolution no. 24 of the 30 June 2022 General Meeting) and appointed Jarosław Szanajca to the Supervisory Board effective 1 January 2025 for a joint three-year term. Chairman Grzegorz Kiełpsz resigned the chairmanship effective 31 December 2024, remaining a Member; on 29 November 2024 the Supervisory Board elected Jarosław Szanajca Chairman effective 1 January 2025. As at 31 December 2025 the eight-member Supervisory Board comprised: Jarosław Szanajca (Chairman), Janusz Zalewski (Vice-Chairman), Dorota Podedworna-Tarnowska (Vice-Chair, independent), Grzegorz Kiełpsz (Member), Mark Spiteri (Member), Philippe Bonavero (Member), Edyta Wojtkiewicz (independent Member), Anna Maria Panasiuk (independent Member).
