XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025 — ESG/human-capital disclosure on average training hours per employee, comparing reporting periods 31.12.2025 and 31.12.2024. Group XTB average training hours per employee (without breakdown by gender or job grade) reached 15.14 hours in 2025 versus 7.25 hours in 2024, a +108.8% year-over-year increase. By job grade, growth was broadly comparable across all levels: Senior management and directors rose to 21.00 hours (2025) from 10.38 hours (2024), +102.3%; Middle/managerial staff rose to 19.61 hours from 9.54 hours, +105.6%; Other employees rose to 13.86 hours from 6.72 hours, +106.1%. Within the 31.12.2025 period, grade-and-gender detail shows: Senior management/directors — women 27.87h, men 19.10h; Middle/managerial — women 14.88h, men 21.49h; Other employees — women 12.69h, men 14.51h, with 0.5h recorded under "Other." For the 31.12.2024 period: Senior management/directors — women 16.30h, men 9.07h; Middle/managerial — women 10.86h, men 8.96h; Other employees — women 7.23h, men 6.44h. Average training hours per employee by gender were 13.45h (women), 16.00h (men) and 0.5h (other) in 2025, against 7.87h (women) and 6.93h (men) in 2024. By gender the sharpest increase was for men (+130.9%), with women up +70.9% year-over-year, attributed to the male-dominated workforce (67% of employees) and their occupancy of roles requiring mandatory specialist training; in every case training-hours growth dynamics significantly exceeded headcount growth dynamics by gender. The overall indicator increase is attributed to improved monitoring of precise training time and a higher number of trainings relative to 2024.
