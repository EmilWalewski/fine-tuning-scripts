Dom Development S.A.’s total assets increased by 9% in 2025 to PLN 3,860 million. Inventories remained the largest asset category, reaching PLN 2,447 million at year-end and accounting for 63% of the balance sheet, up 15% year on year. Long-term loans granted, exclusively to subsidiaries, amounted to PLN 580 million, reflecting the reclassification or extension of loan arrangements to a long-term character. Total non-current assets rose to PLN 1,134 million, while current assets declined by 6% to PLN 2,726 million.

The company’s equity increased by 15% to PLN 1,684 million, representing 44% of total assets, despite the payment of the remaining dividend for 2024 of PLN 181 million and an interim dividend for 2025 of PLN 181 million. Total liabilities stood at PLN 2,177 million, or 56% of the balance sheet, up 5% year on year. Long-term liabilities were PLN 610 million, also up 5%, and mainly consisted of bonds issued by the company with a value of PLN 535 million. Short-term liabilities amounted to PLN 1,566 million and remained close to the prior-year level. The total value of bonds issued by the company increased by PLN 35 million compared with the end of 2024.

The company described its balance sheet structure as favorable. The current ratio remained high at 4.13, compared with 4.55 at the end of 2024. The quick ratio fell to 0.42 from 1.20, and the cash ratio declined to 0.10 from 0.20. The decreases were mainly linked to subsidiary loans becoming long-term, the replacement of an income tax receivable at the end of 2024 with an income tax liability at the end of 2025, and a lower cash balance.

Debt indicators remained at levels described as good and safe. Equity covered 43.6% of total assets, up from 41.5%. Total liabilities to equity declined to 129.3% from 141.1%, while the overall debt ratio decreased to 56.4% from 58.5%. Interest-bearing debt to equity fell to 38.9% from 41.5%, although net interest-bearing debt to equity increased to 26.4% from 23.9%. The company stated that its liquidity and debt ratios were monitored and managed by management and remained satisfactory and safe. As of 31 December 2025, Dom Development S.A. also had PLN 717 million of available undrawn credit lines at five of the largest banks in Poland.
