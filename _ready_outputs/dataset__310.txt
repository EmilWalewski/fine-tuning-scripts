KRUK Group's interest rate risk hedging policy (Section 52) covers: Group interest-rate-risk objectives; risk management principles for the KRUK Capital Group; permissible impact of interest rate risk on Group results (interest-rate-risk appetite); measurement and monitoring methods for interest rate risk and exposure; procedures upon breach of permissible risk appetite; and hedging principles. For interest-rate-risk management the Group enters into IRS and CIRS contracts. As of 30 June 2025, KRUK Group companies held open, unsettled IRS contracts with a total volume of EUR 432,500k (EUR 432.5M). The EUR contracts aimed to hedge cash-flow variability generated by EUR-denominated liabilities arising from changes in reference rates EURIBOR 1M and EURIBOR 3M, and to hedge interest payments on EUR-denominated credit and bonds. In H1 2025 the Group entered into cross-currency interest rate swap (CIRS) contracts with a total nominal of PLN 500,000k (PLN 500.0M), under which the Group pays a coupon based on a fixed rate on EUR-set debt per transaction terms and receives a coupon based on a floating rate plus margin calculated on the PLN debt covered by the transaction.
