Dom Development S.A. Capital Group H1 2025 Management Report excerpt covering land bank, risk factors, and interest-rate environment. Land bank comprises plots designated for future residential development across varying preparation stages, from acquisition and ownership securing through advanced design and administrative-decision procurement. Group strategy targets maintaining land bank sufficient for uninterrupted development activity over at least three years, enabling flexible supply adjustment to market conditions. Given diminishing availability of attractive investment land in largest agglomerations, Management increasingly utilizes already-urbanized sites, driving a rising number of revitalization projects aligned with sustainable-development goals and local zoning plans. Land-acquisition challenges are greatest in Warsaw, stemming from complex legal conditions tied to property-ownership history, limited buildable plots, high market competition, and time-consuming, regulatory-risk-laden administrative processes for zoning decisions and building permits. The Company employs a flexible, multi-stage land-acquisition model securing "controlled land" via preliminary or conditional agreements, enabling future acquisition upon meeting formal-legal conditions, advance location securing, and shortened time-to-market. Group holds high-quality land resources in Warsaw, Krakow, Wroclaw, and Tricity (Trojmiasto).

Interest-rate environment: Housing market strongly dependent on interest-rate levels, with historically lower rates supporting property-investment interest and mortgage-financing availability while higher rates dampen demand. After a long period of stable money cost beginning October 2023, the Monetary Policy Council (RPP) in May 2025 cut the reference rate by 0.5 percentage point to 5.25%, then in July 2025 by a further 0.25 percentage point to 5.00%. Consequently, the WIBOR 3M benchmark relevant to mortgage lending declined from 5.85% in June 2024 to 5.22% in June 2025. Nominal rate levels remain relatively high, but the gradual downward trend may improve credit conditions in H2.

H1 2025 mortgage market showed high activity despite no new borrower-support instruments being implemented. In June 2025, 37,470 persons (37.47 thousand) applied for mortgage loans, up 36.6% versus the same month prior year (27,440 / 27.44 thousand applicants), though down 3.0% versus May 2025. Average requested residential mortgage amount in June 2025 reached PLN 477,010 (477.01 thousand zloty), 7.7% higher than June 2024 and 2.0% higher than the prior month — a record-high level, the highest in history. Year-over-year the number of mortgage applicants rose by over one-third. Rising mortgage interest reflects the initiated rate-cut cycle and expectations of further monetary easing, which increases financing availability and stimulates borrower activity. Improved creditworthiness is driving more clients toward three- and four-room family apartments. Management anticipates a slow rise in apartment sales in coming months, especially in the popular segment, linked to growing mortgage availability, and views potential further rate cuts as positively affecting the housing market. Management considers that Dom Development S.A. and Group companies, given strong market position in the higher-class apartment segment, recognizable brand, and consistent strategy execution, possess stable foundations to adapt to economic-environment changes and maintain resilience to development-industry cyclical fluctuations.
