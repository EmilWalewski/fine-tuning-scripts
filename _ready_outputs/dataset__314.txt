Capital management: Management policy maintains a strong capital base to preserve investor, creditor, and market confidence and ensure continued development. Return on equity (net profit to average annual equity) was 36.8% in 2025 versus 29.5% in 2024. Weighted average cost of the Company's debt was 5.9% in both 2025 and 2024. Net financial leverage ratio (loan and bond debt less cash and equivalents and short-term financial assets, divided by equity) was 26.4% at 31 December 2025 versus 23.4% at 31 December 2024. The Company has no defined own-share buyback plan and is subject to no externally imposed capital requirements other than statutory requirements under the Commercial Companies Code.
