XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025 Consolidated Annual Report excerpt detailing the Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Process (ICAAP) and Internal Liquidity Adequacy Assessment Process (ILAAP) within the Company's risk management system. ICAAP is a risk-based mechanism covering all risk types XTB poses to others and is exposed to, encompassing both actual and potential risks and incorporating internal factors plus regulatory, economic and business environment factors; its outcome is determining the capital level necessary to cover all material risks and ensure operational safety. First ICAAP stage: risk identification via comprehensive review of internal and external threats tied to operations, services, processes, and the legal/macroeconomic/competitive environment, assessing probability of occurrence and potential losses on materialization. The risk management system identifies risks whose materialization could cause financial losses or threaten Company stability, covering internal factors (activity profile, services, products, resources) and external factors (macroeconomic situation, competitive environment).

Internal capital estimation undergoes regular annual reviews assessing adequacy of measurement methods to actual risk level and completeness of risk coverage; changes are considered whenever events materially affect the risk profile, e.g. expanded geographic reach or new products entailing new risk types. The ICAAP review is approved by the Management Board, presented to the Supervisory Board, and subject to independent internal audit assessment. Internal capital is allocated across risk types: credit risk, market risk, and operational risk together with hard-to-measure risks, the latter comprising risks deemed material: model risk, business risk, capital risk, management risk, and systemic risk. Internal capital structure was established as at 31.12.2025 (composition figures not provided in text). Due to the nature of liquidity risk, holding additional capital to cover it is not deemed purposeful; instead an adequate level of liquid assets must be maintained.

Risk materiality assessment feeds the internal capital estimation process. XTB continuously maintains capital sufficient to cover all material risks. ICAAP measurement methods ensure operational risks are adequately secured by own funds. Internal capital measurement results play a key role in risk management: coverage of internal capital by own funds is monitored on an ongoing basis, and when approaching warning levels, immediate decisions are taken to limit exposure to individual risk types, particularly market risk exposure. Ongoing monitoring enables constant risk-level control and timely preventive action against excessive risk. Warning limits aim to prevent breaches of the obligation to maintain own funds above the internal capital level. Mechanisms implemented to respond to excessive risk growth include remedial actions defined in contingency capital plans and the XTB Group Recovery Plan. Internal capital is a material element of the risk management system influencing business decisions, which should not lead to excessive risk growth relative to held capital. Capital planning forecasts internal capital level and degree of own-funds coverage to ensure adequately high future capital relative to projected risk levels. Internal capital level is reported to the Management Board every business day.

XTB defines a risk appetite, i.e. the maximum risk level accepted to execute its business strategy and financial objectives, providing concrete operating frameworks and preventing actions burdened with excessive risk for short-term profit maximization. Risk appetite contains quantitative elements that measurably limit acceptable risk and qualitative elements specifying conduct consistent with the risk profile and business strategy as well as risk factors unacceptable in operations. Objectives of setting acceptable risk level: maintaining regulatory requirements safely above required minimums; ensuring an adequate buffer of capital and liquid assets above levels from internal risk measurements; preserving consistency with the desired risk profile and business strategy. Risk appetite is developed based on findings of the Risk Committee, comprising Supervisory Board members, then adopted by the Management Board and Supervisory Board of XTB. The maximum acceptable risk level is monitored on an ongoing basis with daily information on risk-appetite indicator levels provided to the Management Board.

Under ILAAP the Company identifies external and internal factors affecting the risk of failing to meet current obligations due to cash-flow mismatch and sets the required liquid-asset level to cover these risks; individual risk factors associated with urgent coverage of sudden liquidity outflows are assessed against the liquid-asset value to be held. As with internal capital, the level of liquid assets and liquid-asset requirement is monitored on an ongoing basis with daily data provided to the Management Board. Ongoing monitoring plus appropriate warning levels and limits prevent liquid assets falling below the level needed to cover the liquid-asset requirement. Defined mechanisms include contingency liquidity plans and the Group Recovery Plan, establishing remedial actions in a liquidity crisis. The ILAAP process undergoes an annual review covering all liquidity risk factors and ensuring adequate liquid-asset resources; upon identifying new liquidity risk factors, changes to the liquid-asset-requirement measurement are considered. Review results are accepted by the Management Board, presented to the Supervisory Board, and subject to independent internal-auditor assessment.
