Glossary of defined terms (Słownik Pojęć) for KRUK S.A. consolidated/quarterly financial reporting, establishing the nomenclature used throughout the report. Group definitions: "Grupa, Grupa KRUK, Grupa Kapitałowa" denotes KRUK S.A. together with its subsidiaries and personally-controlled entities; "Jednostka Dominująca" (Parent) is KRUK S.A.; "Zarząd" is the Management Board of KRUK S.A. Functional currency of Grupa KRUK is the Polish złoty (PLN). Other currencies referenced: EUR (euro, used across most EU states), CZK (Czech koruna), RON (Romanian leu), USD (US dollar), evidencing multi-jurisdictional operations spanning Poland, Czechia, Romania, the eurozone, and the US.

Capital markets and debt instruments: Catalyst is the authorization and trading system for debt financial instruments operated by the Warsaw Stock Exchange (GPW) and BondSpot. NFW FIZ denotes non-standardized securitization closed-end investment funds (Niestandaryzowane Fundusze Wierzytelności Fundusze Inwestycyjne Zamknięte).

Hedging/derivative instruments employed by the Group: CCIRS (Cross-Currency Interest Rate Swap); CIRS (Currency Interest Rate Swap), used to hedge currency and interest-rate risk on future cash flows and net investment in foreign operations; IRS (Interest Rate Swap), used to hedge future cash flows against interest-rate risk; FORWARD, a derivative contract to buy or sell an underlying at a predetermined settlement date and strike price.

Reference rates: EURIBOR (Euro Interbank Offered Rate) and WIBOR (Warsaw Interbank Offered Rate). NBP is the National Bank of Poland (central bank).

Credit-risk / IFRS impairment terminology (consistent with expected-credit-loss modeling): Default (failure to perform/non-payment); EAD (Exposure At Default); LGD (Loss Given Default); PD (Probability of Default); PPS (Prepayment Possibility, probability of early repayment); POCI (Purchased or Originated Credit-Impaired assets, credit-impaired at initial recognition); SPPI test (Solely Payments of Principal and Interest), a qualitative test assessing whether financial-asset cash flows constitute solely principal and interest. OWSP denotes cash-generating units (ośrodki wypracowujące środki pieniężne).

Profitability metrics: "Marża pośrednia" (indirect margin) defined as operating-activity revenue less direct and indirect costs; EBITDA defined as indirect margin less general/overhead costs. Reporting frameworks: MSR (International Accounting Standards / IAS) and MSSF (International Financial Reporting Standards / IFRS); "Raport kwartalny" denotes the condensed interim consolidated financial statements.

Taxation and regulatory terms: CIT (Corporate Income Tax); PIT (Personal Income Tax); CFC tax (tax on income of foreign controlled companies held by Polish residents). Filar II (Pillar II) — regulations obligating taxpayers that are multinational groups to pay a top-up tax equal to the difference between the effective tax rate (computed per the Act's rules) for a given jurisdiction and the 15% minimum rate, indicating the Group's exposure to OECD global minimum tax rules. OECD (Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development). PFRON (State Fund for Rehabilitation of Disabled Persons). UE (European Union).

Macroeconomic indicators: CPI (Consumer Price Index); PKB (GDP, Gross Domestic Product). ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) defined as the set of principles and disclosure process covering enterprises' environmental, social, and governance impact.

The section is purely definitional (report glossary); it contains no period-specific financial figures, dates, transactions, dividends, board appointments, or shareholder-structure data — only the terminology framework underpinning the Group's financial statements, signaling KRUK S.A.'s use of derivative hedging, IFRS-based expected-credit-loss measurement, multi-currency cross-border operations, debt-securities financing via Catalyst, and Pillar II global-minimum-tax compliance.
