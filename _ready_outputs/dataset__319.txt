Independent auditor's review report on the interim condensed standalone (separate) financial statements of Dom Development S.A. ("the Company"), addressed to the Shareholders and Supervisory Board. The auditor reviewed the Company's interim condensed standalone balance sheet as at 30 June 2025 and the related interim condensed standalone statement of profit or loss, statement of comprehensive income, statement of changes in equity, and statement of cash flows for the six-month period then ended, together with related explanatory notes ("interim condensed standalone financial statements"). The Company's Management Board is responsible for preparing and presenting these statements in accordance with International Accounting Standard 34 Interim Financial Reporting as adopted by the European Union; the auditor is responsible for expressing a conclusion based on the review. The review was conducted in accordance with National Review Standard 2410 in the wording of International Standard on Review Engagements 2410 (Review of Interim Financial Information Performed by the Independent Auditor of the Entity), adopted by resolution of the National Council of Statutory Auditors. A review consists principally of making inquiries, primarily of persons responsible for financial and accounting matters, and applying analytical and other review procedures; it is substantially narrower in scope than an audit conducted under National Auditing Standards and consequently does not enable obtaining assurance that all significant matters that might be identified in an audit have been disclosed, so no audit opinion is expressed. Conclusion (unmodified): based on the review, nothing came to the auditor's attention that would cause it to believe the attached interim condensed standalone financial statements were not prepared, in all material respects, in accordance with IAS 34 as adopted by the EU.
