Grupa Kęty S.A. is the parent of a group made up of subsidiaries operating mainly in aluminium profiles, aluminium systems for construction, plastic packaging, and accounting services. The disclosed ownership interests are equal to the voting rights. As of 30 September 2025 and 31 December 2024, most subsidiaries were wholly owned, directly or indirectly. The main exceptions shown are Aluprof Netherlands B.V., in which Aluprof S.A. held 55% at both dates, and the parent company itself, for which ownership data is not applicable.

The group includes entities in Poland and several foreign jurisdictions, including Ukraine, Italy, Slovenia, Germany, Czechia, Hungary, Romania, the United Kingdom, the United States, Belgium, and the Netherlands. The operating segments identified in the table include SWW, SSA, SOG, and Inne. Grupa Kęty S.A. is described as a holding company and as conducting production and trading activity in aluminium profiles. Key subsidiaries include Aluform Sp. z o.o. and related aluminium profile businesses in the SWW segment, Aluprof S.A. and its sales and production subsidiaries in the SSA segment, Alupol Packaging S.A. and related packaging companies in the SOG segment, and Dekret Centrum Rachunkowe Sp. z o.o. in the Inne segment.

The interim condensed consolidated financial statements were prepared in accordance with IAS 34 Interim Financial Reporting as endorsed by the EU. They are presented in PLN, and unless otherwise indicated, values are stated in millions of PLN. The statements were approved for publication on 22 October 2025.

The financial statements were prepared on a going concern basis. Management assessed that, as of the date of preparation, there was no threat to the group’s ability to continue as a going concern for at least 12 months from the reporting date. This assessment included the impact of climate-related matters and the war in Ukraine. The group stated that no new material factors had arisen between the publication of the 2024 consolidated financial statements on 27 March 2025 and the publication date of the interim report that would make the previous climate analysis obsolete. It also stated that no new factors or events related to the war in Ukraine had occurred by the publication date that would have a material impact on the group’s operations.

The accounting policies applied in the interim report are consistent with those used in the annual consolidated financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2024, published on 27 March 2025. Amendments to IAS 21 concerning exchangeability of currencies and exchange rates became effective on 1 January 2025, were endorsed by the EU, and did not affect the interim financial statements.

Regarding the Pillar Two reform under BEPS 2.0, the text states that the reform introduced a global minimum tax. In Poland, it was implemented by the Act of 6 November 2024, effective from 1 January 2025, with an option for voluntary application from 1 January 2024. The group may become liable for a top-up tax if the effective tax rate in any jurisdiction falls below 15%. Because group entities are generally located in jurisdictions with high levels of taxation, the risk of a material top-up tax in the coming years was assessed as low. No material amounts arising from the Pillar Two reform were recognized.
