Excerpt from the Consolidated Annual Report of the XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025, covering risk management, ethics, anti-corruption, and ESG governance of the investment firm. XTB assesses negative impacts of its activities and undertakes or plans preventive measures grounded in internal communication, procedural-formal infrastructure, and an internal/external whistleblower (sygnalista) reporting procedure. Headquarters (Centrala) operates a Risk Management Strategy and Risk Management System covering employees and XTB subsidiaries, aimed at designing/implementing processes and internal documents for measuring, estimating, limiting, and controlling risk. XTB S.A. declares zero tolerance for discrimination and negative conduct toward co-workers; preventive measures include internal communication, procedural-regulatory infrastructure, and maintaining a positive work atmosphere. Reported violations trigger an internal investigation (witness interviews, evidence collection, documentation analysis) and remedial actions including additional employee training derived from collected findings.

The Supervisory Inspector (Inspektor Nadzoru) representing XTB's compliance area plays the key role in monitoring legal compliance, operating under implemented regulations establishing a compliance supervision system to detect and prevent breaches of XTB's legal obligations governing brokerage (działalność maklerska) activity. A Code of Ethics (Kodeks Etyki) has been implemented at XTB S.A. Headquarters since 2019, defining core norms, conduct rules, and values. An anonymous irregularity-reporting procedure was also implemented, setting rules for anonymous reporting of actual or potential legal violations, including breaches of anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorism-financing (AML/CFT) rules, internal procedures, and ethical standards; reports are submitted via a dedicated electronic form preserving anonymity.

The XTB management system is based on transparent rules with formal-legal backing ensuring transparent and responsible practices. A double materiality assessment conducted in 2024 — based on interviews, surveys, and stakeholder workshops — identified material risks, material opportunities, and material impacts, referencing EU sustainability reporting standard (ESRS) indicators to ensure completeness. The results enabled preparation of sustainability reporting and an update of XTB's strategic directions and goals reflecting organizational needs and plans within the assumed time horizon. XTB emphasizes legal compliance, quality, and responsibility consistent with its character as an investment firm, building relationships based on mutual trust.

Over the last 12 months, neither Headquarters nor Group companies received inquiries from the OECD National Contact Point (Krajowy Punkt Kontaktowy OECD) or the Business and Human Rights Resource Centre. The Company undergoes state institution inspections and complies with all post-inspection recommendations. XTB S.A. does not operate in the controversial weapons production sector.

Anti-corruption: the Company maintains anti-corruption procedures and rules, namely the XTB S.A. Anti-Corruption Policy (Polityka Antykorupcyjna) and the XTB S.A. Code of Ethics, addressing gift policy, conflict-of-interest management, and corporate ethics. XTB has additionally adopted an incentives management policy and conflict-of-interest management regulations. All centralized purchases (i.e., made in Poland) are conducted per a dedicated goods-and-services procurement procedure. XTB is an obligated institution under AML/CFT law; to ensure compliance it adopted an AML/CFT Procedure specifying detailed solutions, including duties of employees of individual departments in the AML/CFT area, with anonymous reporting rules (including AML/CFT breach reports) set in a dedicated procedure. XTB suppliers, per contracts concluded with XTB, are obliged to familiarize themselves with and act in accordance with the XTB S.A. Anti-Corruption Policy and to report any corruption suspicions and support clarification actions. No cases of corruption were identified in XTB S.A. companies and branches during the examined reporting period, and no proceedings related to corruption-type offenses are pending.
