Investor relations: XTB's entry onto the regulated GPW market created a new stakeholder group with which the company began active communication; XTB attracts strong interest from both individual and institutional stock-market investors, domestic and foreign. The communication function for analysts and stock-market investors falls within the competencies of the Management Board Member for Financial Affairs (CFO), supported by a dedicated investor relations team of senior- and mid-level specialists with diverse experience in finance, disclosure obligations, and public relations. XTB S.A. maintains regular, open contact with capital-market participants, guided by principles of reliability, equal access to information, comprehensibility, and transparency. The company adheres to the "Best Practice for GPW Listed Companies 2021" principle of providing company information without undue delay, no later than within 14 days; the investor relations team customarily responds as quickly as possible depending on question complexity and the need to prepare and aggregate additional data. In 2025 the company communicated with individual investors via reports, newsletter, email, and online results meetings.
