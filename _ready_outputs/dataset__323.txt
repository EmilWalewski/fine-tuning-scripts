XTB S.A. Consolidated Annual Report 2025 excerpt covering compliance, data protection, tax governance, cybersecurity, and ESG. Compliance oversight system aims to ensure the Company fulfills legal obligations governing brokerage activity, advises and assists staff performing brokerage functions, and conducts regular assessment of the adequacy and effectiveness of the compliance framework. Branch-level compliance is assigned to a designated person reporting directly to the Compliance Inspector (Inspektor Nadzoru). Compliance risk management covers identification, probability and consequence assessment, risk weighting, mitigation measures, and documentation. Control proceedings are conducted across XTB organizational units. In 2025, two labor-law proceedings were ongoing in one XTB S.A. branch, subject to XTB analysis and assessment; as of publication date these matters remain under clarification.

Given the Company's high level of technological development, a Specialist Team for IT Systems was established within the Product and Technology Department to support the Company, including the Data Protection Officer (DPO/Inspektor Ochrony Danych), in ensuring data-processing security adequate to the risk of infringement of individuals' rights and freedoms. The DPO is supported by a Deputy DPO. A DPO has been appointed at XTB S.A., performing continuous, long-term duties: gathering information to identify processing operations, analyzing and verifying processing compliance, informing, advising, and recommending actions. The DPO acts as the contact point for the supervisory authority (President of the Personal Data Protection Office) for breach notifications and consultations. XTB S.A. maintains its own Cookies Policy and Privacy Policy, the latter prepared per the information obligation under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR/RODO) of 27 April 2016.

Tax function: XTB S.A. maintains identified and documented processes and procedures for managing tax-law obligations, supported by adequate personnel, technical, and financial resources. The Tax Strategy targets an effective, efficient, and transparent tax function ensuring compliance and tax-risk control; tax risk management processes are implemented, with tax governance and compliance as key supervisory elements. In 2025 the XTB S.A. Tax Strategy for 2025-2028 was updated. The Company implemented management and control processes to ensure tax compliance and the correctness and timeliness of tax declarations, filings, and payments; accounting books are maintained reliably to enable proper fulfillment of all tax obligations.

Tax controls: matters relate to transactions between XTB S.A. and related foreign entities arising from an Advance Pricing Agreement (APA) decision received by the Company. On 14 January 2026, XTB S.A. submitted objections to KAS findings contained in the tax-audit protocol dated 17 December 2025; by letter dated 28 January 2026 the Head of KAS responded to those objections. On 28 May 2025, a customs-fiscal audit was initiated by the Head of the Masovian Customs and Fiscal Office in Warsaw against XTB S.A., covering compliance with the Company's obligations as remitter of flat-rate income tax on payments under Article 21(1) of the Corporate Income Tax Act of 15 February 1992 and Article 29(1) of the tax act of 26 July 1991. The audit covers the period 1 January 2021 to 31 December 2021 and is ongoing; the anticipated completion date was extended to 2 June 2026.

Cybersecurity and client protection: XTB places highest priority on data and systems security. Access to the xStation investment application is protected via access authorization, passwords, fingerprint, or code; server-application connections are fully encrypted. Client funds are protected, with withdrawals permitted only to the client's account and key information authenticated via multi-stage verification. After the balance-sheet date, a new "Emergency Lock" function was introduced, allowing immediate account-access restriction upon suspicious activity—disabling transactions, withdrawals, and eWallet services—to allow time for password change and securing of funds. XTB's website includes cybersecurity content informing on digital-risk threat types (https://pl.xtb.com/bezpieczenstwo-w-sieci).

Environment and climate: XTB Group implemented a Climate Policy defining climate-change challenges and targets for coming years. The Group has a published ESG Strategy and, since 2022, published sustainability reports compliant with the SIN reporting standard, including a carbon-dioxide emissions report. Since 2024 the Group publishes its full carbon footprint across three scopes, calculated with an external specialist expert and subject to limited assurance attestation. Reports and strategies are publicly available on the Company's websites.
