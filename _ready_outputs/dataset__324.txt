Biodiversity and ecosystems: Group has no transition plan and no dedicated biodiversity policy; both planned no earlier than 2030. No resilience analysis of business model/strategy against biodiversity performed. Operations and investments solely in Poland, providing legal stability; sustainable development is a business-model pillar. Group applies the sequential mitigation hierarchy: avoidance, minimization, restoration on-site, and compensation only as legally required last resort. Promotes revitalization of previously used (e.g. industrial) areas, curbing urban sprawl via residential reuse; DOM 2030 Strategy commits to revitalization projects. Example project since 2024: Rydlowka 5, Podgorze, Krakow. Over 1,100 dwellings delivered on degraded/reclaimed land. Nature-based actions per internal Green Investment Card standard: green roofs, rain gardens, urban heat-island reduction. Local-community knowledge and financial-impact analysis of remedial actions not incorporated; actions not part of broader collective/regional plans. "Miejska Zielen" (Urban Greenery) program officially launched 2024 under ESG DOM 2030 Strategy; annual practice across all Group markets, targeting at least one publicly accessible area per agglomeration. In 2025 Group allocated nearly PLN 8 mn (vs PLN 6 mn in 2024) to public green areas, creating over 1.5 ha of new greenery across four Polish agglomerations: Warsaw Chrzanow park (near Metro Zachod district); Wroclaw Gorka Tarnogajska and Zielna St plantings; Krakow Malborska St pocket park; Gdansk Lema St pocket park, Piekarnicza/Lema square, and new Motlawa waterfront on Dolne Miasto. Brownfield conversions also at Urbino, Bokserska and Literacka estates (Warsaw) and Gdansk (Kartuska St, Dolne Miasto: Jaskolcza, Jalmuznicza, Kurza). Remediation performed per administrative decisions of regional environmental authorities; contaminated soil removed off-site to authorized entities, replaced with clean soil. Compensatory plantings made per administrative decisions (point-based, not formal offsets). 2025 inventory found 25% of investments on low-risk floodplain areas; adaptation actions to be identified next year. No measurable biodiversity targets set (covered by ESG DOM 2030); targets no earlier than 2030, not based on global biodiversity frameworks or ecological thresholds.
