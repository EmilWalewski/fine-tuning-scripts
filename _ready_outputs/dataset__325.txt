XTB S.A. 2025 Consolidated Annual Report excerpt covering sustainability governance and business model. Governance allocation of ESG responsibilities between the Management Board (Zarząd) and Supervisory Board (Rada Nadzorcza) of XTB S.A.: the Management Board sets direction, priorities and objectives for the sustainability area; accepts, manages and oversees sustainability work; ensures compliance of sustainability reporting with legal requirements; adopts and monitors implementation of the ESG Strategy; approves sustainability policies and procedures; and performs other duties arising from law, the Statute and other XTB S.A. corporate documents. The Supervisory Board oversees sustainability development directions, priorities and objectives; supervises the sustainability reporting process; monitors XTB's sustainability activities; and fulfills other statutory and corporate-document duties.

Management Board and Supervisory Board engagement in sustainability during 2025 and through publication date: Management Board members adopted, by Board Resolution (Uchwała Zarządu), the updated results of the Double Materiality Assessment (Badanie Podwójnej Istotności); emerging policies and corporate documents were consulted with the Management Board; ESG topics were consulted directly with Board members per their competence and responsibility allocation; the Management Board Member for Finance (Członek Zarządu ds. Finansowych) completed training enhancing sustainability competencies; the Sustainability Manager (Kierownik ds. zrównoważonego rozwoju) conducted quarterly reviews of the area during meetings of the XTB S.A. Audit Committee (Komitet Audytu), summarizing 2025 ESG activities and the updated Double Materiality Assessment results.

GOV-3 Incentive programs for Company bodies: in 2025 XTB continued the practice introduced in 2024 of linking the variable portion of Management Board members' remuneration to delivery of the ESG Strategy for 2024-2027. Apart from this, no financial incentives tied to sustainability matters are applied to members of the Management Board or Supervisory Board.

XTB Foundation (Fundacja XTB) has operated within the XTB Group since 2020, supporting delivery of the ESG Strategy. Foundation activities include: raising economic, financial and new-technology awareness and knowledge; scientific-research activity and promotion of solutions developed within the XTB Capital Group; supporting and organizing initiatives promoting financial institutions and new technologies; corporate social responsibility and sustainable development activity; promotion of education and equalization of development opportunities; initiating and supporting XTB Group employee activity; and charitable and social activity.

Strategy, business model and value chain (Section 1.4, SBM-1): XTB S.A. is a Polish brokerage house (dom maklerski) operating in the FinTech sector, listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange (Giełda Papierów Wartościowych w Warszawie S.A.). It heads the international XTB S.A. capital group, which offers investors worldwide constant and immediate access to international financial markets via a universal investment application. For over 20 years the business model has been built on solutions enabling clients to achieve investment objectives through immediate access to global financial markets, centered on its key technological product, the investment application reaching clients worldwide. The human-centric business model aligns closely with ESG strategy directions and objectives. XTB emphasizes geographic diversification of revenues, consistently pursuing a global-brand strategy based on new capital-group entities. Its offering serves individual and institutional clients worldwide. XTB Group activity is regulated and supervised by competent authorities in the markets where it operates, including EU countries under the single European passport regime. No material changes occurred in the XTB Group's business model during the reporting period.
