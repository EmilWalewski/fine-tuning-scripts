Quarterly report excerpt (condensed interim consolidated financial statements) addressing seasonality/cyclicality (Section 8) and investments. Management states the Group's operations exhibit no signs of seasonality or cyclicality. Section 9 (referenced) concerns the nature and amounts of changes in accounting estimates previously disclosed in prior financial years that materially affect the current period.

The Investments disclosure details investments in debt-claim portfolios (pakiety wierzytelności) measured at amortized cost. A comparative table with clear metric-period-value mapping (figures in thousands PLN; as at 31.12.2025 unaudited vs. 31.12.2024) reports the value of acquired debt portfolios: unsecured portfolios (portfele niezabezpieczone) 10,804.2M PLN at 31.12.2025 versus 9,674.6M PLN at 31.12.2024, a year-over-year increase of approximately 1,129.6M PLN (~+11.7%); secured portfolios (portfele zabezpieczone) 828.5M PLN at 31.12.2025 versus 825.7M PLN at 31.12.2024, broadly flat (~+0.3%). Total acquired debt portfolios at amortized cost reached 11,632.7M PLN at 31.12.2025 versus 10,500.3M PLN at 31.12.2024, up approximately 1,132.4M PLN (~+10.8%), driven almost entirely by growth in unsecured portfolios.

Methodology: at the end of each quarter the Group updates, where justified, the parameters underlying the estimation of expected future cash flows for debt portfolios measured at amortized cost, namely: (a) the discount rate, applied where the outlay/cost basis of an acquired debt portfolio changes; (b) the period over which cash flows are estimated; and (c) the value of expected future cash flows, based on available current information and currently applied debt-collection (windykacja) processes. The Group performs analyses of the impact of macroeconomic factors on planned recoveries; however, historically no dependency of recoveries on acquired debt portfolios upon the macroeconomic situation has been demonstrated.
