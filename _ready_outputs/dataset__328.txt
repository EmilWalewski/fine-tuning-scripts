LPP Group Q3 2025 (III kwartał) and nine-month (I-III kwartał) operating review covering revenue by region, total revenue, gross margin, operating costs, capital expenditure, inventory, and debt; all figures in PLN, lease/debt under IFRS 16 (MSSF16).

Revenue by region (PLN M): 9M 2025 Poland 7,374 vs 6,298 (+17.1%), foreign 9,275 vs 7,558 (+22.7%), purchasing agents 0 vs 667 (-100.0%), total 16,649 vs 14,523 (+14.6%). Q3 2025 Poland 2,717 vs 2,208 (+23.1%), foreign 3,424 vs 2,843 (+20.5%), purchasing agents 0 vs 162 (-100.0%), total 6,141 vs 5,212 (+17.8%).

Total revenue: Q3 2025 Group revenue PLN 6.1bn, +17.8% y/y; on comparable basis (Q3 2024 cleaned of purchasing-agent transactions) +21.6% y/y, and +23.1% in constant currency. Growth driven by both traditional and online channels. Brick-and-mortar sales +22.3% y/y (vs 2024 cleaned for purchasing agents), supported by retail floor-space expansion (232 new stores opened in Q3) and positive LFL of 4.3% (highest, double-digit, in Reserved and House). Online sales +20.7% y/y, driven by all-brand mobile apps and broader online-dedicated product offer, especially Sinsay; SinsayClub loyalty program boosted e-commerce. Online channel 27.4% of total Group sales in Q3 2025 (26.7% in Q3 2024). Largest brand by nominal revenue and dynamic: Sinsay PLN 3.4bn, +25.1% y/y, driven by diversified, affordable, expanding product offer, ambitious new brick-and-mortar store openings, and economic conditions favoring budget-segment shopping. Reserved (second by Group revenue share) PLN 1.7bn, +23.3% y/y, from well-received collection and strong online dynamics. Geographically, Q3 2025 omnichannel foreign revenue exceeded Polish revenue at 55.8% of Group omnichannel sales; double-digit growth in Poland (+23.1% y/y) and abroad (+20.5% y/y) from successful collections across nearly all brands and dynamic network expansion; largest non-Poland sales in Ukraine, Romania, Czechia, and Hungary.

Gross margin: Q3 2025 gross margin 57.6%, +2.8 pp y/y; after cleaning for Q1 2024 purchasing-agent transactions, +1.0 pp y/y. Higher margin despite dilutive rising Sinsay share, achieved via favorable USD/PLN rate at which collections were ordered and fewer promotional campaigns y/y.

Operating costs (Koszty operacyjne, PLN M): 9M 2025 6,789 vs 5,928 (+14.5%); cost per m2/month 290 vs 312 (-7.0%); cost/sales 40.8% vs 40.8% (0.0 pp); cost/sales from core activity 40.8% vs 42.8% (-2.0 pp; 9M24 cleaned of purchasing-agent transitional transactions). Q3 2025 2,380 vs 2,096 (+13.5%); cost per m2/month 289 vs 317 (-9.0%); cost/sales 38.8% vs 40.2% (-1.5 pp); cost/sales from core activity 38.8% vs 41.5% (-2.7 pp). Q3 cost growth (+13.5%) slower than sales growth, driven by intensive expansion (new-store costs) and higher logistics costs from new Romania FC launch plus extra business-interruption logistics costs after June 2025 Romania warehouse fire (added transport and space-rental costs). Cost per m2 fell 9.0% y/y (317 to 289 PLN/m2) via logistics automation and lower in-store/online marketing spend. Q3 2025 store costs PLN 1,354M (+15.1% y/y); headquarters and e-commerce costs PLN 1,026M (+11.6% y/y). Own-store cost per m2 fell from 177 to 164 PLN/m2 on lower rent/m2 (more Sinsay openings at lower rents), lower personnel/m2 (efficient in-store work-time management), and lower other store costs from energy and external-service optimization. Operating cost share of revenue fell from 40.2% (Q3 2024) to 38.8% (Q3 2025); vs comparable sales (cleaned of purchasing agents) fell from 41.5% to 38.8%.

Capital expenditure (Nakłady inwestycyjne, PLN M): 9M 2025 stores 1,086 vs 782 (+38.8%), offices 42 vs 30 (+38.7%), logistics 937 vs 274 (+242.1%), IT & other 121 vs 120 (+1.1%), total 2,186 vs 1,206 (+81.3%). Q3 2025 stores 430 vs 297 (+44.8%), offices 13 vs 11 (+16.4%), logistics 452 vs 153 (+195.4%), IT & other 44 vs 58 (-25.0%), total 938 vs 519 (+80.8%).

Inventory (Zapasy, PLN M): at 31.10.2025 inventory 4,801 vs 4,094 at 31.10.2024 (+17.3%; +PLN 707M); inventory per m2 1,693 vs 1,827 PLN/m2 (-7.3%). Inventory rise from new Sinsay brick-and-mortar openings and broader Sinsay online offer; per-m2 decline mainly from time-phased goods receipts. Inventory turnover 173 days in Q3 2025 vs 153 days in Q3 2024.

Debt (Zadłużenie, PLN M, IFRS 16) at 31.10.2025 vs 31.10.2024: long-term loans 146 vs 443 (-67.0%), short-term loans 1,952 vs 494 (+295.1%), bonds 0 vs 308 (-100.0%), finance lease 5,078 vs 4,558 (+11.4%), cash 693 vs 1,134 (-38.9%), deposits and investment funds 849 vs 845 (+0.5%). Net debt 5,634 vs 3,824 (+47.3%); net debt/EBITDA (4Q) 1.1 vs 1.0 (+17.2%).
