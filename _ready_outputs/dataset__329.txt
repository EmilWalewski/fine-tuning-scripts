Dom Development S.A. interim condensed standalone financial statements for the six months ended 30 June 2025, covering dividend distribution from 2024 net profit and governance changes. On 6 November 2024, the Management Board (with Supervisory Board consent) resolved to pay shareholders an advance on the 2024 dividend of PLN 154,790,532.00 (PLN 6.00 per share); record date 12 December 2024, payment date 18 December 2024, covering all 25,798,422 shares; advance paid 18 December 2024. On 18 March 2025 the Management Board proposed dividing 2024 net profit of PLN 419,019,575.12 as follows: PLN 335,379,486.00 (PLN 13.00/share) to shareholder dividend—net of the PLN 154,790,532.00 (PLN 6.00/share) advance, leaving PLN 180,588,954.00 (PLN 7.00/share) outstanding; and PLN 83,640,089.12 to supplementary capital. Proposed dividend date 25 June 2025, payment date 3 July 2025. Supervisory Board approved the proposal 7 May 2025; Annual General Meeting adopted it 12 June 2025. On 3 July 2025 the Company paid the outstanding 2024 dividend of PLN 180,588,954.00 (PLN 7.00/share).

Governance: On 28 August 2024 Jarosław Szanajca resigned as Management Board President effective 31 December 2024, intending to join the Supervisory Board; majority shareholder Groupe Belleforêt S.à r.l. (Luxembourg, holding at least 50.1%), under Statute clause 6.2.2, appointed Mikołaj Konopka (then Board Member) as President effective 1 January 2025 for a joint three-year term. On 29 October 2024 Monika Perekitko resigned as Board Member (effective same day); Groupe Belleforêt appointed Justyna Wilk as Board Member from 1 January 2025, and the Supervisory Board appointed Grzegorz Smoliński from 29 October 2024 (joint three-year terms). On 29 November 2024 Leszek Stankiewicz resigned as Board Member/Vice-President effective 28 February 2025; Groupe Belleforêt appointed Monika Dobosz as Member/Vice-President (finance) effective 1 March 2025. At 30 June 2025 the five-member Board: Mikołaj Konopka (President), Monika Dobosz (Vice-President), Justyna Wilk, Grzegorz Smoliński, Terry Roydon.

On 21 November 2024 the Extraordinary General Meeting amended resolution no. 24 (30 June 2022), setting the Supervisory Board at eight members from 1 January 2025, and appointed Jarosław Szanajca to the Supervisory Board from that date (three-year term). On 22 November 2024 Chairman Grzegorz Kiełpsz resigned the chairmanship effective 31 December 2024, remaining a member; on 29 November 2024 the Board elected Szanajca Chairman from 1 January 2025. At 30 June 2025 the eight-member Supervisory Board comprised Szanajca (Chairman), Janusz Zalewski and Dorota Podedworna-Tarnowska (Vice-Chairs; latter independent), Kiełpsz, Mark Spiteri, Philippe Bonavero, and independents Edyta Wojtkiewicz and Anna Maria Panasiuk.
