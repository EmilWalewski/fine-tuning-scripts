Creotech Instruments S.A. interim condensed financial report for the nine months ended 30 September 2025 (all amounts in thousands PLN unless stated otherwise), covering subsequent events, seasonality, securities issuance, dividends, asset transactions, litigation, structural changes, and publication approval.

Subsequent events: On 2 October 2025 the Management Board reported (referencing current reports 28/2025 of 16 April 2025 and 48/2025 of 29 August 2025) the commencement of administrative proceedings to approve the prospectus of subsidiary Creotech Quantum S.A. (Warsaw, the "Acquiring Company") in connection with the public offering of 2,854,347 new ordinary bearer series B shares ("Division Shares") and their admission to trading on the GPW-operated regulated market. On 2 October 2025 the Company received from the European Space Agency (ESA) an approved contract amendment (Contract Change Notice) for the "CAMILA" (COUNTRY AWARENESS MISSION IN LAND ANALYSIS) project, disclosed via current report 51/2025 of 3 October 2025; the amendment activates two additional options: (i) delivery, launch and orbital commissioning of a fourth observation satellite, and (ii) access to a commercial ground station network for regular orbital operations. The change revises the schedule with new milestones covering standard satellite operations and sets a new completion date of November 2029. Contract value increases by EUR 7,095,783 to EUR 59,021,294 total; other material terms, including the maximum penalty cap, remain unchanged. On 7 November 2025 (current report 52/2025) the Company signed an ESA contract for the "TELESCOPE ARRAY FUNCTIONAL PROTOTYPE" project, part of ESA's Space Safety Programme, to develop a prototype validating a universal remote ground-based observatory architecture for detecting near-Earth objects; total contract value EUR 1,600,000, of which ESA funds EUR 1,280,000 and the remainder is covered by participating entities. Creotech is prime contractor, supplying sCMOS astronomical cameras with accessories and managing project execution; the Company's scope of work is EUR 502,778, with 73.6% ESA-funded and 26.4% from own resources, over an expected ~12-month period. On 26 November 2025 (current report 53/2025) ESA accepted the remaining deliverables under the CAMILA Requirements Key Point review, successfully closing the review; the Company is entitled to supplementary remuneration of EUR 1,159,055, bringing total Requirements Key Point remuneration to EUR 4,337,339, of which approximately 35% accrues directly to the Company and the balance to subcontractors.

Seasonality: Operations show high sales volatility but no pronounced seasonality or cyclicality, mitigated by activity across multiple market segments and a high share of long-term, stage-settled projects.

Securities issuance: Series K shares were issued under resolution no. 3 of the Extraordinary General Meeting of 30 January 2025; subscription agreements were concluded 14-19 February 2025 via private subscription under art. 431 §2(1) of the Commercial Companies Code (no public subscription or allotment per art. 434 et seq.). On 19 February 2025 the Management Board fixed the final subscriber list. Subscription covered 475,000 Series K shares at an issue price of PLN 160 per share. Proceeds finance part of the expenditures for the contract concluded 20 December 2024 with the State Treasury - Armament Agency for production and delivery of the "Mikroglob" Satellite Earth Observation System (SSOZ), phases B, C, D, E1; the remainder is funded by contract advances under the 17 July 2025 amendment, with potential later debt financing. On 16 April 2025 the District Court for Warsaw (XIV Commercial Division, National Court Register) registered the statutory share-capital increase related to the Series K issue.

Dividends: None paid during the period or up to publication approval.

Tangible fixed assets: PLN 5.9 million incurred on investment in a third-party fixed asset linked to the production facility investment in Piaseczno; no other material acquisitions or disposals. No contractual commitments to acquire tangible fixed assets exist.

Litigation: No court, arbitration, or public administration proceedings concerning the Company's liabilities or receivables were pending as of the report date.

Estimates: Not applicable. Structural changes: In Q2 2025 the Company established Creotech Quantum S.A. and Creotech Geo P.S.A., taking 100% stakes via share-capital contributions of PLN 150 thousand and PLN 100 thousand respectively. No other materially impactful information occurred.

Publication approval: The interim condensed financial statements were approved for publication by the Management Board on 26 November 2025. Board: Grzegorz Brona (President), Paweł Górnicki (VP Finance), Maciej Misiura (VP), Jacek Mandas (VP); accounting books entrusted to Piotr Kazimierczak.
