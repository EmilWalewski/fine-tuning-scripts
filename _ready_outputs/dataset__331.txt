Excerpt from the XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025, covering marketing strategy and cybersecurity posture across short-, medium-, and long-term horizons. Marketing activity spans traditional media, outdoor/external carriers, digital channels, social media, and promotion of investment products via educational materials and targeted advertising. Short-term campaigns are pre-planned and managed in real time to maximize effectiveness and react flexibly to shifting client preferences, driving increased app traffic, user registrations, and early client-base building, with full results materializing only after several months. In 2025 the Company executed the largest advertising campaign in its history—the first of its scale—covering most of the foreign markets in which XTB operates. Medium-term marketing (3–5 years) becomes more strategic, centered on a coherent, credible message that promotes XTB's products and services while influencing investor choices, advancing financial education, and the idea of democratizing investing; activities include promoting investment tools available in mobile apps and communication that eases understanding of investment mechanisms and encourages more conscious financial management. This is positioned to build a strong brand, expand the mass-client base, raise trust in the organization as a provider of modern, secure investment solutions, and support long-term organizational growth and expansion of the financial-product portfolio aligned with changing market needs. Long-term (5+ years) emphasis is placed on the organization's chosen development direction and strategic goals.

On cybersecurity, the excerpt notes that following intrusions and disruptions in 2025, European enterprises rank just behind North American firms in attack frequency and are more than twice as frequently targeted as organizations in the East, Southeast, South Asian, and Oceania region. Per global IBM X-Force data, the most frequently attacked business sectors in Europe in 2025 were the financial and insurance sectors. In response, the XTB Group continuously invests in technology, security, and infrastructure—particularly in IT systems supporting client service and the development of digital services—and engages in investor education on online safety. In 2025 XTB participated in two social campaigns on this topic and launched a dedicated website (https://pl.xtb.com/bezpieczenstwo-w-sieci) to build awareness of safe internet use among investors, providing information on cybercriminal fraud methods and guidance to avoid them. Medium-term (3–5 years) cybersecurity development will concentrate on strengthening threat-monitoring systems, deploying advanced incident-detection mechanisms, automating responses to potential attacks, adapting security infrastructure to new regulations, and developing risk-management and incident-response procedures. Long-term (5+ years), significance will shift toward integrating artificial intelligence and big-data analytics for proactive threat detection; brokerage houses will also develop "zero trust" security architecture, enhance protection of users' digital identity, and invest in organizational and technological resilience against increasingly advanced cyberattacks.
