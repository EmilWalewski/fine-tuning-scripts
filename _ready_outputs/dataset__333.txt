XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025 (consolidated financial statements for year ended 31 December 2025) accounting-policy notes spanning lease accounting, provisions, equity, off-balance-sheet client instruments, financial-instrument results, commission income/cost, and employee benefits.

Lease identification (IFRS 16): at contract inception the Group assesses whether an arrangement is or contains a lease, i.e. conveys the right to control use of an identified asset for a period in exchange for consideration; where control exists only for part of the term, the lease applies to that part. Rights under lease, rental, tenancy and similar qualifying contracts are recognized as right-of-use assets within non-current assets, with corresponding lease liabilities. Initial recognition and measurement at lease commencement: right-of-use asset at cost; lease liability at present value of outstanding lease payments discounted at the lessee's incremental borrowing rates. Amortization of right-of-use assets is straight-line over the shorter of lease term or asset useful life, unless the Group is reasonably certain to obtain ownership, in which case depreciation runs to end of asset useful life. Short-term leases (term up to 12 months) and low-value asset leases are expensed straight-line as practical expedients. Low-value threshold: assets not exceeding PLN 43k, translated at the first-application rate of 1 January 2019 (equivalent to EUR 10k) or other-currency equivalent at NBP average closing rate.

Provisions (Note 4.8): recognized when a present legal or constructive obligation arises from past events, outflow of economic benefits is probable, and the amount can be reliably estimated despite uncertainty over amount or timing; measured at best estimate of settlement amount reflecting risk and uncertainty, at present value where cash-flow estimation is used; reimbursements from third parties recognized as separate assets when virtually certain.

Equity (Note 4.9): comprises capital and funds formed per statutes and the Articles of Association, including undistributed prior-year profits; share capital per parent's Articles; unregistered contributions at nominal value; supplementary capital from annual net-profit allocations (loss coverage or dividend); reserve capital (loss coverage, dividends, treasury-share purchases under incentive plan); foreign-translation FX differences; non-controlling interests; retained earnings.

Off-balance-sheet items (4.10): nominal values of OTC derivatives with clients/brokers and client financial instruments acquired on regulated exchanges held on client accounts. Result on financial instruments (4.11): all realized/unrealized income and costs from instrument trading including dividends, interest, FX; covers FVTPL assets (client/broker transactions), amortized-cost debt securities (effective-interest method), less client rebates and introducing-broker commissions. Commission income/costs (4.12): brokerage commissions and service fees recognized at transaction date; costs over service period. Employee benefits (4.13): short-term benefits and defined-contribution payments expensed when service received; profit-share/bonus when present obligation exists and is reliably measurable; accrual for unused holidays. Since 2012 the parent applies a variable-remuneration policy for material risk-takers; under the incentive program 100% of variable pay is granted in shares (Actual Bonus), vesting/paid over 3 years; equity-settled share-based payments measured at fair value of goods received, charged directly to equity.
