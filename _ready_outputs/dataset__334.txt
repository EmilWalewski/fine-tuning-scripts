XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025 Consolidated Annual Report excerpt addresses corporate governance, principally conflict-of-interest management via internal regulations and organizational measures, and disclosures against the "Best Practices of WSE-Listed Companies" (Dobre Praktyki GPW). Diversity policy (Principle 2.1): a diversity policy covers Management Board members of XTB S.A., adopted by the Supervisory Board; however it does not mandate a minority (gender) participation threshold of at least 30% in the body, departing from the recommended standard. The Company holds NO diversity policy for the Supervisory Board adopted by the General Meeting. A diversity policy is intended to define objectives and criteria across gender, education field, specialist knowledge, age, and professional experience, plus the timing and method of monitoring goal attainment; the 30% minority-participation benchmark is the recommended diversity condition. Company notes its governing-body members are specialists across varied knowledge areas with diversified sector experience matching their functions, and that hiring emphasizes breadth of qualifications and competencies (education, professional experience, managerial-cadre skills).

Principle 2.2: decision-makers selecting Management/Supervisory Board members should ensure body comprehensiveness and pursue the minimum minority participation target of not less than 30% per the adopted diversity policy. Company commentary: Supervisory Board composition is determined by the General Meeting, while Management Board composition falls under Supervisory Board competence. Selections are guided by current enterprise needs applying substantive (merit-based) criteria; potential conflicts of interest of Management and Supervisory Board members are analyzed within the suitability assessment prior to appointment, and an annual review of additional activities and control of the number of functions held by body members is conducted.

Remuneration (Chapter 6): rules for remunerating Management Board and Supervisory Board members are set out in the Policy on Granting Fixed Remuneration of Management Board and Supervisory Board Members of XTB S.A. dated 31.07.2023, the Variable Remuneration Components Policy dated 12.04.2024, and the Incentive Program Regulations dated 12.04.2024 (collectively the "Remuneration Policy"). These documents are available on the Company's investor relations site under Corporate Governance (https://ir.xtb.com/lad-korporacyjny/regulaminy-i-polityki/). The Management Board remuneration model comprises fixed remuneration, variable remuneration, and non-cash benefits; the rules and amounts of Management Board remuneration are set by the Supervisory Board. Management Board members are remunerated under indefinite-term employment contracts. Supervisory Board members are remunerated by virtue of their appointment relationship; the rules and amounts of Supervisory Board remuneration are determined by the General Meeting via resolution, and their remuneration comprises fixed components and non-cash benefits. Detailed remuneration rules appear in the Remuneration Policy section of the report.

Governance compliance: in 2025 XTB S.A. complied with the corporate governance principles in the "Best Practices of WSE-Listed Companies," except for three principles described in the report. The principles are published on the Polish Financial Supervision Authority (KNF) website (https://www.knf.gov.pl/dla_rynku/regulacje_i_praktyka/zasady_ladu_korporacyjnego). On 18 December 2014 the Management Board adopted a resolution on applying the KNF Corporate Governance Principles; application was confirmed by an Extraordinary General Meeting resolution of 28 January 2015. XTB S.A. applies the KNF Corporate Governance Principles to the extent they govern the functioning of brokerage houses and remain consistent with generally applicable law; per KNF expectation, the Principles were implemented by the Company as of 1 January 2015. During the reporting period the Company applied the KNF Corporate Governance Principles with the following reservations: (i) the principle in §8 sec. 4 (regarding the supervised institution's obligation to facilitate participation of all shareholders in meetings of the constituting body, including via electronic active participation) — per the Statute, participation in the General Meeting using electronic communication means will be ensured where the convening notice contains information on the possibility of shareholders participating via electronic communication means; (ii) the principle in §21 sec. 2 (concerning election of the chairperson of the supervisory body — text truncated).
