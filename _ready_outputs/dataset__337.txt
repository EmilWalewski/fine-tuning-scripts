Excerpt from XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025 detailing the GHG carbon-footprint calculation methodology and emission-factor sources. Methodology applies the GHG Protocol "A Corporate Accounting and Reporting Standard, revised edition" (March 2004; World Resources Institute and World Business Council for Sustainable Development, with amendments to Required gases and GWP values, February 2013) and the "Scope 2 Calculation Guidance, An amendment to the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard" (2015). Scope 2 reported under both Market-based approach (purchased-electricity emission factor per energy supplier) and Location-based approach (national grid emission factor). Emission factors selected per materiality principles: temporal (factors current for the 2025 reporting period; where unpublished for the reporting year, latest available factors applied), geographic (representative of the relevant area), and technological (reflecting applicable technology), sourced from recognized, peer-reviewed, officially published national/international research centres, industry associations, and regulatory/statistical bodies, consistent with GHG Protocol. Cited emission-factor sources and access dates include: GEA on R600A (isobutane) refrigerant composition; IEA 2024 Life Cycle Upstream Emission Factors (Pilot Edition), total upstream factors for Poland (2022 estimated); IPCC AR6 2023 (Earth's Energy Budget, Climate Feedbacks and Climate Sensitivity Supplementary Material) for GWP values; 2006 IPCC Guidelines for National Greenhouse Gas Inventories Chapter 2 Stationary Combustion, Table 2.4 default emission factors (commercial/institutional) for CH4 and N2O for natural gas; equipment EPD/product carbon-footprint cards for Apple, DELL, Google, Lenovo, and Samsung (cradle-to-gate and disposal-phase factors; accessed 23.02.2026). Electricity/energy sources: Naturgy Iberia S.A. (2025, 2024 supplier emissivity); Netzdienste Rhein-Main/Mainova AG (2025, 2024 supplier emissivity); Slovak Republic National Inventory Document 2025 (Odbor Emisie A Biopaliva, April 2025, Paris Agreement submission, stationary-combustion factors Slovakia); PGE Polska Grupa Energetyczna S.A. (2025, 2024 fuel mix); Smartest Energy (2025, 2024 supplier emissivity). KOBIZE (Krajowy Osrodek Bilansowania i Zarzadzania Emisjami) sources: 2025 calorific values (WO) and CO2 emission factors (WE) for 2023 for ETS 2026 reporting (Warsaw, December 2025), and 2025 CO2/SO2/NOx/CO/total-dust electricity emissivity indicators based on the national GHG/substances emissions database for 2024. Additional factors: M. Weiss, K. Cloos, E. Helmers (2020) energy-efficiency trade-offs in small-to-large electric vehicles (average power draw of electric car, e-bike, e-scooter, electric moped); Romania's Greenhouse Gas Inventory 1989-2023 (Ministry of Environment, Waters and Forests / National Environmental Protection Agency, December 2025) for Romanian stationary-combustion factors; Belize Ministry of Public Utilities, Energy, Logistics & Governance, Energy Report 2024 (transmission-loss percentage for Belize 2024); Poland's National Inventory Document 2025 (National Centre for Emissions Management, Institute of Environmental Protection National Research Institute, Ministry of Climate and Environment); Statcounter Global Stats (global market share of phone and tablet operating systems; accessed 23.02.2026); Sustainable Web Design 2024 Estimating Digital Emissions (electricity draw for data transmission; accessed 23.02.2026); Scottish Environment Protection Agency (SEPA) 2024 waste conversion factors for municipal-waste densities; Mainova strommix data; CNMC (Spain) electricity labelling; German stationary-combustion fuel factors. Other referenced factor categories: well-to-tank (WTT) factors for electricity, expenditure-based factors for purchased goods, services, capital goods and business travel, R410A refrigerant composition, district-heat direct/indirect emission factors, France district-heat transmission-loss percentage, business-travel accommodation factors, and average FX rates for 1.01.2025-30.06.2025. Most factor access dates recorded as 06.02.2026; one IEA reference dated 06.02.2025.
