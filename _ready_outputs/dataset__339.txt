Forecasts (7.52): the Board does not publish financial forecasts beyond those mandated under the 15 January 2015 Bond Act (art. 35), requiring website publication of financial-liability projections until full bond redemption. Forecasts published 23 December 2024 for 31.12.2025 versus actuals (tys. zł / % of balance-sheet total): liabilities from loans and borrowings forecast PLN 448.0M (6.96%) vs actual PLN 52.6M (52,572 tys. zł, 0.88%); debt securities liabilities forecast PLN 610.0M (9.47%) vs actual PLN 651.2M (10.89%); lease liabilities forecast PLN 122.9M (1.91%) vs actual PLN 148.8M (2.49%). Lower actual loan/borrowing/debt-securities debt reflects lower-than-assumed Group debt from higher net operating cash, slightly slower project launches due to weaker-than-planned demand, and lower/deferred (into 2026) land-acquisition spending. Higher lease debt mainly stems from extending the Warsaw head-office lease. The Board also increased bond-based debt in place of planned credit, issuing PLN 135M of bonds on 16 May 2025.
