At the end of 2025, Dom Development Group had 7,166 units under construction, down 6% from 7,657 at the end of 2024. Of these, 4,042 units had already been sold. The reported construction scale included 2,861 units in Warsaw and 1,079 in Kraków. Management stated that keeping more than 7,000 units under construction showed effective organizational and process management, especially given administrative permitting difficulties and limited investment land availability. All construction projects were said to be proceeding according to schedules and budget assumptions.

In 2025, the Group delivered 4,325 units in total, including 4,228 to retail customers and 97 to a PRS investor. Retail deliveries rose 8% year on year, from 3,916 in 2024. By market, retail deliveries were 1,915 in Warsaw, 1,075 in the Tricity, 874 in Wrocław, and 364 in Kraków. Important projects contributing to the result included Metro Zachód, Osiedle przy Ryżowej and Harmonia Mokotów in Warsaw, Osiedle Synteza in the Tricity, Osiedle Międzyleska in Wrocław, and Górka Narodowa in Kraków. The Group completed 37 projects with 4,761 units during the year.

The Group’s land bank, including purchased and controlled land together with units under construction, represented potential for future results. Management assessed it as adequate in both size and structure, with an optimal preparation-stage land bank expected to support at least four years of sales. At the end of 2025, the land bank covered 18,832 planned units, compared with 19,008 a year earlier. Warsaw accounted for 9,783 units, Tricity for 5,439, Wrocław for 1,711, and Kraków for 1,899. Management noted that Warsaw was the most difficult market for securing development plots, due to historical ownership issues, legal-status problems, competition, and administrative approvals. As a result, controlled land forms a significant part of the Warsaw land bank.

In 2025, Dom Development made several management changes. After Jarosław Szanajca resigned as President of the Management Board, Mikołaj Konopka took over at the beginning of the year. Justyna Wilk joined the Management Board as Member of the Board and General Director. On 1 March 2025, Monika Dobosz became Vice President of the Management Board and Chief Financial Officer, replacing Leszek Stankiewicz. At Euro Styl S.A., Filip Nawratil became President of the Management Board at the end of September 2025.
