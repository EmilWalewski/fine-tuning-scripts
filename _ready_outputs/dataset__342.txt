Excerpt from Dom Development S.A. Group's 2025 Management Report (Consolidated Sustainability Reporting section), covering the Internal Control System and ESG Risks, for the parent entity Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group (page references 180-181).

Internal Control System: Effective risk management is supported by a Group-wide internal control system structured around three lines of defense. The first line of defense comprises business process owners operationally managing risk at organizational-unit level, making ongoing business decisions within the risk management process (accounting for risk levels and mitigating actions) and supervising the control function in their areas to maintain effective internal control adequate to assigned tasks. The second line of defense covers risk management by a Risk Management Team aggregating risk at Group and individual-subsidiary level, supporting identification, measurement, monitoring and reporting; oversight rests with the person performing the compliance and Group risk management function, particularly in designing systemic solutions to improve internal control effectiveness. The third line of defense comprises internal audit conducting independent audits of internal processes (independent assessment of the risk management process). Together these three lines form a Group-level internal control system, proportionate and adequate to the scope, scale and organizational structure of activities, ensuring operational effectiveness, reliable financial and non-financial reporting, and compliance with law and internal regulations.

The internal control system is shaped at the parent-company (Spółka) level and operates across Group entities respecting formal separateness, proportionality and adequacy. It encompasses oversight, policies and procedures, organizational structures, reporting systems, IT-system solutions, the legal compliance function and other control mechanisms ensuring operational safety and stability. The system performs three key functions: a control function (all control mechanisms within Company processes); an independent compliance function plus assurance of systemic solutions improving internal control and risk management effectiveness; and an independent internal audit function.

Internal control over sustainability reporting is managed within existing mature corporate-governance methods and rests on: advanced accounting systems, business reporting systems and document-flow systems; a system for collecting utility/media consumption data; transparent internal procedures (including financial and compliance); a clear organizational structure; competencies, knowledge and experience of personnel involved; management oversight with regular Group performance assessment; verification of financial and non-financial statements by an independent statutory auditor; and internal audit activity.

Internal control effectiveness and risk management in the financial reporting process are ensured through an established division of duties and competencies in preparing financial information. Accounting books and financial reporting at both the Company and Group entities are maintained by highly qualified finance-accounting teams. The Group's consolidated financial statements are prepared by the Company's finance-accounting department staff under the supervision of the Vice-President of the Management Board of Dom Development S.A. Control effectiveness and ledger quality are assured via appropriate division of competencies, granting of corresponding authorizations in IT systems, and independent teams responsible for budgeting and management reporting. The Group's finance-accounting system is the data source for both standalone and consolidated financial statements, and for management reporting whose financial portion is designed to supply management with all required information. Both statutory and management statements follow the Group's adopted accounting policy compliant with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS).

Monthly financial-operational management reports supplement historical financial data from the ledgers with quantitative operational forecasts and financial forecasts; these are analyzed by middle and senior management of organizational units and by the Company's Management Board. For completed reporting periods, Group financial results are analyzed in detail against budget assumptions and forecasts. Given the developer-industry specifics, analysis covers not only aggregated cost and revenue groups but also financial and operational data for individual development projects. Identified deviations are verified and explained, and applicable errors corrected on an ongoing basis in Group entities' books. Both management and statutory reporting are conducted under supervision of the Financial Controller and the Vice-President of the Management Board / Company CFO. Group financial statements are verified by an independent statutory auditor; per Group standards they are always audited by an auditor of recognized, suitably high qualifications. The Company developed an "Audit Life Cycle" — a schedule of cooperation and mutual communication among the Management Board, the statutory auditor and the Audit Committee of the Supervisory Board — aimed at ensuring proper relations and communication between the Audit Committee and the auditor.

A key action reducing the Group's exposure to market risks is proper assessment of potential risks and control of current development investments, executed via the Company's investment models and decision-making procedures. The basis for calculating cost of products sold (per the applicable accounting policy) includes not only recorded costs but also detailed budgets of individual development projects, prepared and updated per the Group's best knowledge and experience. During project execution, all budgets are updated no less frequently than every 3 months, under a formalized process subject to special oversight by the management boards of the executing subsidiaries and by the Company's Management Board. In consolidation, elimination of intra-group sales is material, with particular emphasis on reliable assessment and subsequent elimination of unrealized margin on such sales.

ESG Risks: ESG risk management is an integral part of the entire risk management process; individual ESG-area risks are classified into the main business risks. The ESG risk management area is supported by the Non-Financial Reporting Team, whose actions aim, among others, to limit greenwashing risk through oversight of internal processes and ongoing cooperation with the reputational-risk area. ESG risk management principles are defined by the Non-Financial and Sustainability Reporting Procedure.
