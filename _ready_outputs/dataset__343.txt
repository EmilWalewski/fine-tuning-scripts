KRUK Group condensed interim consolidated financial statements (quarterly report) detail provisions, key management and supervisory board remuneration, and financial-instrument risk management. Total provisions reached 20.3M PLN at 31.12.2025 versus 19.9M PLN at 31.12.2024. The provisions roll-forward shows retirement and disability severance provisions rising from 15.9M PLN (1 Jan 2024) via 2.4M PLN accrual and 0.1M PLN utilization to 18.3M PLN (31.12.2024), then a 2.0M PLN accrual lifting the balance to 20.3M PLN (31.12.2025). The loyalty-program provision of 10.9M PLN (1 Jan 2024) was reduced by 0.3M PLN accrual, 2.2M PLN utilization, and an 8.95M PLN release on program closure, ending at nil. The tax-audit-outcome provision of 28.6M PLN (1 Jan 2024) was utilized by 26.9M PLN to 1.6M PLN (31.12.2024), then increased by 7.4M PLN and reduced by a 9.0M PLN release, ending at nil (31.12.2025). Other provisions of 7.5M PLN (1 Jan 2024) were fully released (7.5M PLN) for potential straight-line settlement differences.

Narrative: In December 2025 the tax proceeding in subsidiary KRUK Česká a Slovenská republika s.r.o. concluded, with the outcome consistent with the provision set in prior quarters. During 2025 the Group released an unused provision for the concluded tax audit at KRUK S.A. (1.6M PLN / 1,607k PLN). In 2024 the provision for potential straight-line settlement differences was released given a marginal level of complaints, with low assessed risk of future increase. At end-August 2024 the KRUK Group ended its "Marzenia do spełnienia" loyalty program, releasing the points provision of 9.0M PLN (8,952k PLN).

Key management (Management Board) remuneration of the Parent for FY2025 totaled 33.3M PLN (Q4 2025: 6.5M PLN) versus FY2024 25.95M PLN (Q4 2024: 5.9M PLN), comprising base salary/managerial contract (gross) 9.1M PLN FY2025 vs 9.0M PLN FY2024; additional benefits 0.17M PLN vs 0.18M PLN; and share-based payment remuneration 24.0M PLN FY2025 vs 16.8M PLN FY2024 — the share-based component being the principal driver of the year-over-year increase. Supervisory Board remuneration of the Parent totaled 1.7M PLN FY2025 (Q4 2025: 0.43M PLN) versus 1.6M PLN FY2024 (Q4 2024: 0.36M PLN), comprising base remuneration (gross) 1.65M PLN vs 1.55M PLN and additional benefits 0.04M PLN vs 0.037M PLN. Persons in the management body and parties closely related held 9.7% of votes at the Parent's general meeting at 31.12.2025 (31.12.2024: 9.8%).

Financial-instrument risk management covers credit risk, liquidity risk, and market risk. The Parent's Management Board is responsible for establishing and overseeing Group risk management; principles target identification and analysis of risks, setting of limits and controls, and monitoring, subject to regular review. Credit risk relates principally to loans granted, trade receivables, and acquired debt portfolios. Maximum credit-risk exposure (carrying amounts of financial assets) totaled 12,637.1M PLN at 31.12.2025 versus 11,385.7M PLN at 31.12.2024, comprising investments in debt portfolios 11,632.7M PLN (vs 10,500.3M PLN), loans granted 612.3M PLN (vs 502.9M PLN), hedging instruments 137.4M PLN (vs 114.3M PLN), trade and other receivables excluding tax receivables 42.1M PLN (vs 53.4M PLN), and cash and equivalents 212.6M PLN (vs 214.8M PLN). By geography (31.12.2025 vs 31.12.2024): Poland 5,193.5M PLN vs 4,669.9M PLN; Romania 2,090.9M PLN vs 1,697.6M PLN; Italy 3,287.9M PLN vs 2,849.1M PLN; Spain 1,821.3M PLN vs 1,909.2M PLN; other foreign markets 243.5M PLN vs 259.9M PLN. Investments in debt portfolios by type (31.12.2025 vs 31.12.2024): unsecured retail 10,416.3M PLN vs 9,375.2M PLN; secured retail 218.6M PLN vs 255.4M PLN; unsecured corporate 387.9M PLN vs 299.3M PLN; secured corporate 609.9M PLN vs 570.3M PLN; total 11,632.7M PLN vs 10,500.3M PLN.

Liquidity risk is managed to ensure timely settlement of obligations without losses or reputational damage, with objectives to safeguard against loss of repayment capacity, ensure financing for current operations and Group development, and effective management of available funding sources. Liquidity is assessed cyclically via sensitivity analysis of forecast portfolio collections. New Group debt may be incurred only if repayment is feasible from the Group's own assets, debt accounts for inter-company transfer capacity, timing and costs, and incurrence does not breach financial covenants set in credit agreements and bond issuance terms.
