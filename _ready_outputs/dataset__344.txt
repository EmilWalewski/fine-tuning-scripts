Excerpt from Dom Development S.A. consolidated sustainability report (Management Board report on activities of Dom Development S.A. Capital Group for 2025), section 3 Consolidated Sustainability Reporting, disclosure SBM-3: material impacts, risks and opportunities and their interrelation with strategy and the business model. Content is qualitative ESG disclosure, with no financial figures. As investor and general contractor, the Group identifies the construction industry as exposed to high accident rates (occupational safety) and positions itself as playing an important, positive role in sustainable urban development and the transformation of the cities and regions in which it operates. Group companies help shape these areas, strengthen their cohesion, increase their attractiveness, support their development, and contribute to revitalizing the local economic and social environment (sector indicator: support for development of sustainable cities; affected communities). On managing relations with the surrounding environment, Group companies exert a positive influence on relations with local communities through activities defined in their CSR strategies, while acknowledging that execution of investment projects and associated nuisances may negatively affect local communities. Consumer privacy: Group companies positively influence protection of consumer privacy, including through contractual arrangements/agreements, and are aware of risks linked to breaches of confidentiality and consequent threats to the rights and freedoms of natural persons in connection with processing of their personal data; the company maintains a range of policies and procedures in this area. Access to (high-quality) information for end consumers: Group companies affect the quality of information conveyed to clients, including clarity of provisions and elimination of abusive (unfair) clauses in contracts. Group companies have implemented communication principles minimizing the risk of greenwashing. Social inclusion of consumers and access to products and services: the Group's activity, tied to housing construction, helps increase housing supply on the market and improves availability of residential units in the country's main metropolitan areas (agglomerations). Responsible marketing practices: Group companies provide reliable, verified information to external stakeholders (including individual clients as well as investors and shareholders); information is verified and subject to internal control. Business conduct and corporate culture: the Dom Development Group builds its positive impact in the area of business practices by maintaining corporate culture and through systemic management of risk, compliance, and the internal control system.
