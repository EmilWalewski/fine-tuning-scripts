Financial risk management (Note 7.30): the Group is exposed to market risk (interest rate risk), credit risk, and liquidity risk. Market risk reflects the impact of changes in market prices — foreign exchange rates, interest rates, and equity instrument prices — on the Group's financial result or the value of financial instruments held; it principally comprises currency risk and interest rate risk. Currency risk: where significant currency positions exist, the Group uses forward and swap currency derivatives to hedge material future foreign-currency transactions; however, as at 31 December 2025 and 31 December 2024 the Group held no material assets, liabilities, or future payments in foreign currencies, so no hedging currency derivatives were required. Interest rate risk: bank loans taken at fixed interest rates expose the Group to fair-value risk, while loans and borrowings at variable rates create cash-flow risk. Under its current financing structure the Group holds no fixed-rate loans or bonds; it holds short-, medium-, and long-term variable-rate loans and bonds, creating cash-flow risk that is mitigated as described. As at the balance-sheet date the Group held no long-term financial instruments bearing fixed interest rates. Interest rate risk is largely naturally offset by holding both financial liabilities and financial assets bearing variable interest rates. Exposure on bond debt is further reduced through hedging instruments: CAP options, under which the bank guarantees to reimburse the Company for differences arising from market interest rates rising above a level fixed in the option (protecting against rate increases while retaining benefit from potential rate decreases); and Interest Rate Swaps (IRS), exchanging with the bank interest payments calculated under one interest rate for payments under another, with the Company swapping a variable rate for a fixed rate, both interest payments accruing on an agreed notional amount and settled on designated dates by comparing the applicable reference rate with the contracted rate.
