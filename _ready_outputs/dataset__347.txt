KRUK S.A. Group H1 2025 interim financial statement disclosures on guarantees, sureties and security pledges (Section 19, Management Board Statement). On 24 June 2025 KRUK S.A. and subsidiaries InvestCapital LTD, KRUK Romania S.R.L., Prokura NFW FIZ and KRUK España S.L.U. (borrowers) signed with a bank consortium (DNB Bank ASA, ING Bank Śląski S.A., Santander Bank Polska S.A., PKO BP S.A. and Bank Polska Kasa Opieki S.A.) an annex to the multicurrency revolving credit facility dated 3 July 2017 (as amended). The annex increased the maximum facility amount to EUR 740.0M. KRUK S.A., KRUK Romania S.R.L. and KRUK España S.L.U. provided sureties for all borrower obligations under the facility, and borrowers executed corresponding annexes to pledge agreements. Additionally, Prokura NFW FIZ (25 June 2025) and KRUK S.A. (26 June 2025) submitted declarations of voluntary submission to enforcement under Art. 777 §1 pt 5 of the Civil Procedure Code up to a maximum of EUR 1,110.0M, expiring no later than 31 December 2032.

To secure obligations under the 3 July 2017 revolving facility: on 22 January and 22 May 2025 a Spanish-law pledge agreement was signed between InvestCapital LTD and the lenders over portfolios acquired by InvestCapital LTD on the Spanish market; on 31 January 2025 a Polish-law registered pledge agreement was signed between Prokura NFW FIZ and DNB Bank ASA (as security agent) over portfolios acquired by Prokura NFW FIZ on the Polish market. As at 30 June 2025, the value of asset-based security established in favor of the lenders amounted to PLN 6,634.7M.

On 20 December 2024 a non-revolving working capital loan agreement was concluded between Prokura NFW FIZ, KRUK S.A. and PKO BP S.A. (Warsaw), granted up to PLN 40.0M and maturing 19 December 2029. To secure Prokura NFW FIZ obligations: on 8 January 2025 a surety agreement was concluded between KRUK S.A. and PKO BP S.A. under which KRUK S.A. guaranteed borrower obligations up to a maximum of PLN 60.0M; on 9 January 2025 annexes were signed to the financial pledge agreements on bank accounts dated 5 October 2021 (one between Prokura NFW FIZ and PKO BP S.A., one between KRUK S.A. and PKO BP S.A.); on 9 January 2025 Prokura NFW FIZ and KRUK S.A. submitted declarations of voluntary submission to enforcement under Art. 777 §1 pt 5 of the Civil Procedure Code up to a maximum of PLN 60.0M, expiring no later than 31 December 2032; on 31 January 2025 a registered pledge agreement over a set of rights (debt portfolios held by Prokura NFW FIZ) was concluded between Prokura NFW FIZ and PKO BP S.A., established up to a highest security sum of PLN 60.0M. As at 30 June 2025, the value of all portfolios pledged in favor of PKO BP S.A. amounted to PLN 235.1M.

In connection with the current account credit agreement dated 1 February 2022 (as amended) between Prokura NFW FIZ, KRUK S.A. and Bank Polska Kasa Opieki S.A. (Warsaw), to secure Prokura NFW FIZ obligations: on 12 June 2025 a registered pledge agreement over a set of rights (debt portfolios held by Prokura NFW FIZ) was concluded between Prokura NFW FIZ and Bank Polska Kasa Opieki S.A., established up to a highest security sum of PLN 120.0M. As at 30 June 2025, the value of all portfolios pledged in favor of Bank Polska Kasa Opieki S.A. amounted to PLN 144.9M.

On 20 December 2024 a revolving credit-account loan agreement was concluded between KRUK S.A. and Alior Bank S.A., granted up to PLN 200.0M and maturing 31 December 2029. To secure KRUK S.A. obligations: on 15 January 2025 a financial and registered pledge agreement over investment certificates was concluded between KRUK S.A. and Alior Bank S.A., the registered pledge established up to a highest security sum of PLN 300.0M.
