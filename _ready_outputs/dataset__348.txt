Excerpt from the XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025, presenting the double materiality assessment within the ESG/sustainability disclosure framework. The section is structured around standardized matrices mapping each identified topic to: description of impact/risk/opportunity, impact type (negative actual and potential; positive actual and potential), effect on people or environment, linkage to strategy and business model, current and anticipated effects, time horizon and actions undertaken, priority, current and projected financial effects, and the location of impact/risk/opportunity within the value chain. No quantitative financial figures, monetary amounts, or dated milestones are disclosed in this excerpt; content is exclusively qualitative.

A fragmentary negative-impact item references reputational damage and penalties, with mitigation via recruitment and training of qualified employees.

Cybersecurity-related management measures include additional authorization upon change of the payout account and cooperation with other market participants to combat cybercrime, including acquisition of information on discovered system vulnerabilities and cybercriminal methods of operation.

Under the "Business Conduct" theme, the report identifies several ESG opportunities, all classified as arising within XTB Group's own operations ("Operacje własne"):

(1) Opportunity to improve managerial process quality and increase the flexibility and resilience of XTB Group operations through effective ESG management, risk/opportunity management, and implementation of internal policies and procedures. This opportunity stems from the ESG Strategy. No effect on strategy, business model, or value chain; possible effect on the decision-making process. Management actions: (i) ESG strategy; (ii) creation of an ESG Team; (iii) anchoring ESG governance at the Management Board level; (iv) double materiality assessment; (v) preparation for reporting under the CSRD Directive. No current financial effects; the Group is unable to estimate the financial impact of the risk.

(2) Opportunity to enhance the sustainability reporting process by monitoring and adapting it to legal changes and implementing reporting best practices. This opportunity does not arise from XTB's strategy and business model. No effect on strategy, business model, value chain, or decision-making process. Management actions: (i) preparation for CSRD reporting; (ii) cooperation with ESG advisors; (iii) double materiality assessment. No current financial effects; financial impact not estimable.

(3) Opportunity for increased investor interest and access to preferential insurance terms as an effect of appropriate ESG management. This opportunity does not arise from XTB's strategy and business model. No effect on strategy, business model, or decision-making process; possible effect on the value chain. Management actions: (i) ESG strategy; (ii) planned implementation of an ESG service/portal; (iii) ESG-area activities and initiatives; (iv) sustainability reporting. No current financial effects; financial impact not estimable.

(4) Opportunity to increase efficiency and the quality of XTB's services and products by building a valuable, diverse organizational culture grounded in respect and ethical values. This opportunity arises from assumptions adopted under the ESG Strategy. No effect on strategy, business model, value chain, or decision-making process. Management actions: (i) creation of diverse project teams; (ii) creating space for sharing employee knowledge and passions; (iii) HR-area procedures and policies; (iv) initiatives undertaken; (v) implementation of the ESG strategy; (vi) signing of the Diversity Charter ("Karta Różnorodności"); (vii) DEI training; (viii) implementation of Anti-Mobbing, Anti-Discrimination, and DEI policies.

Recurring throughout: ESG governance is positioned at Management Board level via a dedicated ESG Team, with the double materiality study and CSRD readiness as central preparatory workstreams, alongside diversity and inclusion commitments (Diversity Charter, DEI training, anti-mobbing/anti-discrimination/DEI policies). For all identified opportunities the Group consistently states the absence of current financial effects and its inability to estimate the financial impact, with all impacts/opportunities located in the Group's own operations.
