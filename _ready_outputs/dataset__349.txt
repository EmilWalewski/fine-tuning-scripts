The review covers the interim condensed consolidated financial statements of the Capital Group whose parent company is LPP Spółka Akcyjna (registered office in Gdańsk, ul. Łąkowa 39/44), comprising the interim condensed consolidated statement of financial position as at 31 July 2025, the interim condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income, the statement of changes in equity, the statement of cash flows for the period 1 February 2025 to 31 July 2025, and selected explanatory notes. The parent company's Management Board is responsible for the preparation and presentation of these statements in accordance with International Accounting Standard 34 "Interim Financial Reporting" as adopted by European Commission regulations; the auditor is responsible for forming a conclusion based on the review. The review was conducted in accordance with National Standard on Review 2410, reflecting International Standard on Review Engagements 2410 "Review of Interim Financial Information Performed by the Independent Auditor of the Entity," consisting principally of inquiries of finance and accounting personnel and analytical and other review procedures; the engagement is substantially narrower in scope than an audit performed under National Standards on Auditing (reflecting International Standards on Auditing adopted by resolutions of the National Council of Statutory Auditors and the Polish Audit Oversight Agency) and consequently does not enable the auditor to obtain assurance that all significant matters identifiable in an audit have been disclosed; accordingly, no audit opinion is expressed. Conclusion: based on the review, nothing came to the auditor's attention causing it to believe that the attached interim condensed consolidated financial statements were not prepared, in all material respects, in accordance with IAS 34 "Interim Financial Reporting" as adopted by European Commission regulations (unmodified/clean review conclusion).
