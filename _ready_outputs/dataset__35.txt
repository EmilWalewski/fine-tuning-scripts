The interim condensed consolidated financial statements were prepared using amortized cost with the effective interest method and impairment allowances for credit-impaired financial assets, fair value for derivatives and certain loans whose cash flows are not solely payments of principal and interest, and historical cost for non-financial assets and liabilities.

The Group applied the same accounting policies as in its latest annual consolidated financial statements for the year ended December 31, 2024. It also applied the EU-endorsed amendments effective from January 1, 2025, namely amendments to IAS 21 on lack of exchangeability. These amendments did not have a significant impact on the Group’s interim condensed consolidated financial statements.

The financial statements reflect EU-endorsed IFRS, IAS and related interpretations issued and effective for annual periods beginning January 1, 2025. For standards and interpretations endorsed by the EU but effective from January 1, 2026, the Group is analyzing the possible impact of the Annual Improvements to IFRS, Volume 11, and the amendments to IFRS 9 and IFRS 7 on classification and measurement of financial instruments. The Group does not expect the amendments to IFRS 9 and IFRS 7 on nature-dependent electricity contracts to have a significant impact.

For standards and interpretations still awaiting EU endorsement and effective from January 1, 2027, the Group does not expect IFRS 19, Subsidiaries without Public Accountability: Disclosures, to have a significant impact. The Group is analyzing the scope of the impact of IFRS 18, Presentation and Disclosure in Financial Statements, on the presentation of its consolidated financial statements.
