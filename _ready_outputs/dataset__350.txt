Consolidated sustainability reporting of the Dom Development S.A. Capital Group, Management Board report on activities of Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group for 2025 (page 135), Section 3.3 covers EU Taxonomy disclosures under Article 8 of Regulation (EU) 2020/852. The European Green Deal, adopted by the European Commission in 2019, is the action plan for financing sustainable economic growth; Regulation (EU) 2020/852 of 18 June 2020, amending Regulation (EU) 2019/2088, establishes the framework facilitating sustainable investment and provides a uniform classification of sustainable activities (EU Environmental Taxonomy). The Delegated Act setting technical screening criteria is Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/2139 of 4 June 2021, updated by Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2485 of 27 June 2023. Also adopted in 2023: Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2486 of 27 June 2023, supplementing Regulation (EU) 2020/852 and amending Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/2178, together with Regulation 2026/73 of 4 July 2025. The Delegated Act covering nuclear energy and natural gas activities is Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/1214 of 9 March 2022. Taxonomy reporting assesses both eligibility and alignment. To qualify as environmentally sustainable, an investment must cumulatively meet four conditions: make a significant contribution to at least one of six environmental objectives; do no significant harm (DNSH) to any objective; be conducted in line with Minimum Safeguards; and meet the technical screening criteria (TSC). Significant-contribution and DNSH criteria are defined via technical screening criteria set in Implementing Regulation 2026/73 and the Taxonomy delegated acts (Delegated Regulation 2021/2139, 2022/1214, 2023/2486, 2023/2485, and 2020/852). Mandatory disclosures of non-financial undertakings cover key performance indicators expressing the percentage share of taxonomy-aligned, taxonomy-eligible-but-non-aligned, and taxonomy-non-eligible activities, plus accompanying information, presented across three KPIs: turnover, capital expenditure (CapEx), and operating expenditure (OpEx). The six environmental objectives are: climate change mitigation (CCM); climate change adaptation (CCA); sustainable use and protection of water and marine resources (WTR); transition to a circular economy (CE); pollution prevention and control (PPC); and protection and restoration of biodiversity and ecosystems (BIO).

Accounting policy: non-financial undertakings must explain, within the accounting-policy disclosure, how turnover, CapEx, and OpEx were determined and assigned to the numerator. KPI calculation was based on definitions in Annex I to Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/2178; consolidation principles analogous to those used in preparing the financial statements were applied for the Group. Data were drawn from the financial-accounting system of the Dom Development S.A. Group and from the financial-accounting systems of individual subsidiaries within the Group. Mechanisms to avoid double counting were applied via appropriate consolidation eliminations consistent with applicable accounting rules. For OpEx, all accounts in the Group's financial-accounting system were reviewed and identified items meeting the OPEX definition were each assigned to the relevant taxonomy-eligible activity type.

Turnover KPI: the denominator comprised total revenues from sales of finished goods, services, and merchandise, constituting the Group's total consolidated revenues for 2025, disclosed in note 7.34 of the consolidated financial statements. The numerator captured revenues from taxonomy-eligible activities: 4.15 Distribution of district heating/cooling; 7.1 Construction of new buildings; 7.7 Acquisition and ownership of buildings; 2.1 Accommodation activities; and 5.1 Construction, extension and operation of water collection, treatment and supply systems. For the turnover KPI, all assigned eligible turnover related to revenues from sales of finished goods and services (IFRS 15). Given the nature of the Group's activities, no instances of own consumption were recorded in 2025.

CapEx: for the Dom Development Group the denominator includes the sum of values shown in note 7.6 intangible assets (other increases) and note 7.7 property, plant and equipment (other increases). The numerator comprises capital expenditure relating to eligible activities, broken down into: 7.1 Construction of new buildings; 6.5 Transport by motorbikes, passenger cars and light commercial vehicles; and 7.7 Acquisition and ownership of buildings. CapEx incurred by the Group in 2025 on taxonomy-eligible activities concerned land and buildings, means of transport, and increases from right-of-use assets.

Operating expenditure (OpEx): the denominator basis comprised costs related to maintaining assets in good condition and short-term rental, including car servicing and repair costs, building maintenance costs, and operating lease/rental costs. The numerator comprises OpEx relating to eligible activities, i.e., non-capitalised costs of rental (excluding services) of construction equipment and office equipment used during project execution, and rental of construction containers. These values are presented by the general-contractor companies carrying out new-building construction activity: Dom Construction and Euro Styl Construction, as no qualifying numerator expenditure was identified in the remaining companies.
