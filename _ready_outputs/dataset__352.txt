Excerpt from the XTB S.A. Consolidated Annual Report 2025 (Skonsolidowany Raport Roczny Grupy Kapitałowej XTB S.A.) addressing Group-wide human capital management strategy. XTB is restructuring HR processes to centralize and unify personnel procedures across the Group, instill an efficiency-driven culture, attract and retain talent, increase the agility and effectiveness of strategic and operational workforce processes both within and across teams, and build a resilient organizational culture. Management acknowledges that governing a large, geographically dispersed workforce operating across multiple time zones and distinct legal jurisdictions presents challenges requiring a deliberate operational and personnel strategy. Amid rapid headcount growth, the Group prioritizes preserving operational efficiency, recognizing that expanding team sizes can cause process fragmentation, bottlenecks, and reduced cost efficiency; mismanagement could force the organization to balance growth and flexibility against the need to maintain quality standards, process execution speed, and operational cost optimization. XTB responds by strengthening internal communication through implementation of consistent procedures, reporting standards, and digital tools supporting information flow, thereby ensuring compliance with legal regulations, timely delivery of tasks and projects, and minimized error risk arising from inter-unit communication noise. The Group also focuses on recruitment process efficiency and talent management, deploying uniform standards for career development, training, incentive systems, and employee evaluations based on consistent, clear, and fair assessment criteria applied to XTB employees worldwide while accounting for local market and cultural conditions. These measures support operational stability, cost optimization, and process efficiency while advancing employee competency development and strategic business objectives.
