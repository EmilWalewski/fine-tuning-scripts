LPP SA Group interim condensed consolidated financial statements for the 9-month period 1 February–31 October 2025. Dividend: per WZA (AGM) resolution, on 30 October 2025 LPP paid dividend for fiscal 2024 totaling PLN 660 per share, reduced by the PLN 330 interim advance already paid in April 2025.

Opportunities and risk factors potentially affecting Group results over at least the next quarter: development of Sinsay omnichannel concept built on traditional stores (mainly in smaller towns) supported by online channel; geopolitical instability and possible supply-chain disruption; continued expansion of Sinsay online offer into new product categories; expansion into new markets (Southern Europe and Central Asia); rollout of all brands' mobile apps to further markets; slowing regional economic growth and its effect on customer behavior; attractiveness of collections; favorable USD/PLN rate positively affecting gross margin on sales; operating cost (SG&A) control; rising minimum wage in Poland negatively affecting Group operating costs; Polish GDP growth forecasts and minimum-wage increases potentially raising purchasing propensity; implementation of new technological and AI solutions in logistics, e-commerce and product preparation; rising competition especially in budget product segment; growing scale of operations including stronger negotiating power with suppliers and carriers; prospective European Commission regulation restricting sales by non-EU online platforms within the EU.

Targets: full fiscal 2025 sales plan of ~PLN 23–23.5bn, with year-over-year growth in traditional sales (driven by space growth and positive LFL) and online growth; planned 2025 retail space increase of 25–30% YoY, focused mainly on Sinsay stores (~900); expected gross margin on sales of 54.5–55.0%; operating-cost-to-sales ratio maintained at ~40.5–41.0%; capital expenditure of PLN 2.9bn, of which PLN 1.6bn on stores; targeted safe debt level (net debt/EBITDA ~1.1). Q4 contribution to 2025 plan: in 1 November–9 December the Group achieved revenues 14% higher YoY in constant currencies; plans to open 350–400 salons in the final quarter. For fiscal 2026, on ~20% YoY space growth, positive LFL and double-digit YoY online growth, the Group expects revenue of ~PLN 28–29bn at gross margin of 54.0–54.5%; cost efficiency maintained (operating cost/sales 40.0–41.0%); capex of PLN 2.6bn, of which PLN 1.7bn on stores; net debt/EBITDA maintained at ~1.1.
