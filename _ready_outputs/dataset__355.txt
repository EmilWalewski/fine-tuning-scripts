XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025 Consolidated Annual Report excerpt, comprising the table of contents and introductory letter to the Sustainability Statement, signed by Paweł Szejko, Management Board Member for Financial Affairs (CFO) of XTB S.A. The document structures its sustainability disclosure into four principal sections: (1) General Information, covering basis of preparation of the sustainability statement, governance and oversight within the Group, sustainability management, strategy/business model/value chain, key stakeholders, material impacts/opportunities/risks (including double materiality assessment, material impacts, material opportunities and risks), and ESRS compliance tables; (2) Environment, addressing climate change (double materiality assessment, material impacts, material opportunities and risks), the XTB Group Climate Policy, fuel and energy consumption, greenhouse gas emissions, alignment of XTB Group activities with the EU Taxonomy (financial taxonomy and minimum safeguards); (3) Society, encompassing Our Employees (employment structure, diversity and equality, working conditions, employee training and development, policies/regulations, workplace safety and comfort, human rights incidents and complaints) and Consumers and End-Users (consumer/end-user policies, reporting channels and remedial actions); and (4) Corporate Governance, covering XTB Group characteristics, administrative/management/supervisory bodies (Management Board of XTB S.A. and company bodies, Supervisory Board of XTB S.A.), corporate culture and ethics, risk management and internal control, supplier relationship management, and due diligence statement.

Operational and growth highlights stated in the introductory letter: the XTB investment application is used by over 2.16 million people worldwide. In 2025 a record number exceeding 864 thousand new clients joined the Group, representing growth of +73.4% year-over-year. XTB describes itself as an organization operating at the intersection of technology and finance, emphasizing long-term value creation where innovation pairs with care for people, environment and transparent governance principles. Consistent expansion of the product offering, particularly in the long-term wealth-building segment, strengthened XTB's leading position in key European markets, with priorities cited as security, broad product offering, and financial education supporting responsible investing.

ESG strategy and reporting: XTB developed and updated its ESG Strategy through 2027, established its first strategic targets, and published its first report compliant with CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive). A new ESG online service enables real-time tracking of initiatives. In 2025 particular attention was devoted to the quality and verification of reported data, translating into greater operational efficiency and more effective risk management, while acknowledging that building the sustainability area is a long-term process requiring time and consistency.

Organizational culture and HR initiatives: In 2025 XTB initiated a process of strengthening its organizational culture, with key elements being responsibility, development through technology, and efficiency. The Group unified its quarterly performance appraisal system, based on transparent, consistent principles and shared goals. Continued employee engagement initiatives across all offices included the "2 Hours for Family" campaign, annual preventive health screenings under the "Pink October" and "Movember" initiatives, and growing interest in sporting activities, with nearly 100 employees participating in the Prague 5 (Praska 5) and the Warsaw Night Half Marathon. Foreign subsidiaries and branches showed rising engagement, exemplified by a team's participation in a beach-cleaning and tree-planting action in Cyprus aimed at restoring a local ecosystem affected by fires.

Foundation and community activity: The XTB Foundation developed long-term partnerships supporting financial education for youth, including a further edition of the "Academy of Tomorrow" ("Akademia jutra"), and engaged in actions assisting Polish entrepreneurs from flood-affected areas under the "Postcards from the Flood" ("Pocztówki z powodzi") campaign.

Market recognition: Four XTB good practices were included in the latest publication of the Responsible Business Forum (Forum Odpowiedzialnego Biznesu). XTB received the title of ESG Company of 2024, awarded at the Invest Cuffs conference, cited as confirmation that consistency, engagement and transparency yield measurable results and build credibility with stakeholders.
