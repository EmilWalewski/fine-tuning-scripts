Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group — 2025 operational results (Section 1.6, Management Report). The 2025 Polish residential market in major agglomerations saw demand growth versus 2024, driven by interest-rate cuts lowering mortgage rates and raising borrowing capacity, plus a clear central-government declaration of no borrower-support program. A record housing supply persisted, slowing price growth, though supply was strongly regionally differentiated: clear oversupply on some markets, while major agglomerations such as Warsaw and Tri-City (Trójmiasto) remained relatively balanced and matched to sales pace. The fast-changing regulatory environment challenged developers, with many smaller players gradually withdrawing while larger entities increased activity, producing progressive market consolidation and rising share of large organizations on key residential markets.

Group net unit sales reached 4,448 units in 2025 — the highest in the Group's full 29-year history — up 4% from 4,269 in 2024 (versus 3,906 in 2023 and 3,093 in 2022). Quarterly sales were at least 1,000 units in every quarter of 2025. Mortgage-financed transactions rose to 58% in Q4 2025 and 56% for full-year 2025; cash transactions (significant investment-driven demand) represented 44% of total Group 2025 sales.

Market positions: Dom Development S.A. remained the undisputed Warsaw leader with a 13.2% market share. The Group held its leading Tri-City position with 12.5% share (Euro Styl S.A., 8 years in the Group, materially scaled up). Dom Development Wrocław Sp. z o.o. finished 2025 among top local developers with 12.4% share, holding first place in the ranking (up from second in 2023). On the Kraków market, Dom Development Kraków Sp. z o.o. successfully launched new projects; the Group contracted a record 535 apartments in Kraków, representing 7.8% of local 2025 sales.

Average transaction value (apartment price including optional turnkey finishing, storage cell, parking space) was PLN 816k at Group level in 2025, broadly stable year-over-year. By market, Kraków again had the highest average transaction value at PLN 925k and Tri-City the lowest at PLN 711k; Warsaw reached PLN 859k and Wrocław PLN 775k.

Net sales volume by entity (units, 2025 vs 2024): Warsaw — Dom Development S.A. 2,015 units vs 1,791 (+13%); Tri-City — Euro Styl S.A. 1,082 vs 1,038 (+4%); Wrocław — Dom Development Wrocław Sp. z o.o. 816 vs 920 (-11%); Kraków — Dom Development Kraków Sp. z o.o. 535 vs 520 (+3%); Group total 4,448 vs 4,269 (+4%).

Net sales value by entity (PLN m, 2025 vs 2024): Warsaw — Dom Development S.A. 1,639 vs 1,501 (+9%); Tri-City — Euro Styl S.A. 731 vs 728 (0%); Wrocław — Dom Development Wrocław Sp. z o.o. 594 vs 643 (-8%); Kraków — Dom Development Kraków Sp. z o.o. 494 vs 464 (+6%); Group total 3,458 vs 3,336 (+4%).

Entity highlights: Dom Development S.A. exceeded 2,000 apartments sold in Warsaw, generating PLN 1,639m net sales value, driven mainly by established multi-stage estates Metro Zachód, Wilno and Urbino. Euro Styl S.A. sold 1,082 units in Tri-City for PLN 731m net sales value, led by high-quality urban-fabric projects Osiedle Synteza, Osiedle przy Błoniach and Konstelacja. Dom Development Wrocław recorded 816 units sold for PLN 594m net sales value, led by Osiedle Rapsodia and Hubska 100. Dom Development Kraków achieved record sales of 535 units for PLN 494m net sales value, led by Osiedle Górka Narodowa and Apartamenty Park Matecznego.

Management emphasizes profitability maximization, with Group companies focused on efficiently replenishing the offer while balancing sales pace and achieved prices. The Group holds an established position in the premium-standard segment, with high quality, on-time delivery and comprehensive service reflected in above-average value of apartments sold.

Group offer (Section "Oferta Grupy"): at year-end 2025 the Group's offer reached 3,502 units, consistent with the Management Board's assumptions. During 2025 the Group launched nearly 4,200 units across 36 developments into sales, simultaneously achieving record sales in Group history while rapidly replenishing and expanding the project portfolio.
