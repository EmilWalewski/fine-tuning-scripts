At 31 March 2025, Asseco Group’s trade, tax and other liabilities totalled 18.6 M PLN long-term and 2,904.1 M PLN short-term, compared with 18.1 M PLN and 3,128.0 M PLN at 31 December 2024. Short-term trade payables and contractual penalties were 1,721.9 M PLN, down from 1,868.0 M PLN, including invoiced liabilities of 1,091.5 M PLN, uninvoiced liabilities of 620.0 M PLN and contractual penalties of 10.4 M PLN. Current corporate income tax liabilities were 142.1 M PLN, budget liabilities were 277.4 M PLN, and other short-term liabilities were 762.7 M PLN, mainly employee-related liabilities of 624.2 M PLN. Trade payables were non-interest-bearing.

Liabilities from contracts with customers at 31 March 2025 totalled 168.2 M PLN long-term and 1,377.3 M PLN short-term, versus 124.3 M PLN and 1,335.8 M PLN at 31 December 2024. The short-term balance included 93.2 M PLN from IT contract valuation and 1,284.1 M PLN from customer contracts. Within customer contract liabilities, service and licence fees were the largest item, at 164.3 M PLN long-term and 1,105.0 M PLN short-term. Implementation liabilities were 0.6 M PLN long-term and 99.0 M PLN short-term, equipment delivery liabilities were 34.7 M PLN short-term, and other prepaid services were 3.3 M PLN long-term and 45.4 M PLN short-term.

The provisions table shows provisions of 123.5 M PLN at the beginning of the period and 114.0 M PLN at the end of the reporting period in the clearly presented current column. The ending balance comprised 45.5 M PLN short-term and 68.5 M PLN long-term provisions. During the period, additions included 2.5 M PLN from obtaining control over subsidiaries and 9.0 M PLN of newly created provisions, while decreases included 8.4 M PLN used, 7.6 M PLN released and 5.0 M PLN of negative foreign-exchange translation differences.

For accruals and deferred income, the clearly presented totals show 50.3 M PLN long-term and 547.1 M PLN short-term at 31 March 2025. Accrued costs consisted mainly of provisions for unused holidays of 312.9 M PLN short-term and bonuses for employees and management of 1.5 M PLN long-term and 227.3 M PLN short-term. Deferred income included 47.3 M PLN long-term and 5.3 M PLN short-term from grants for asset construction, plus other deferred income of 1.5 M PLN long-term and 1.6 M PLN short-term. The deferred income subtotal was 48.8 M PLN long-term and 6.9 M PLN short-term. The 31 December 2024 accruals table is partly malformed, so only the explicit total of 52.2 M PLN long-term and 629.0 M PLN short-term is included.
