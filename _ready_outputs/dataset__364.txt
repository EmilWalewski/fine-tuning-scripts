Asseco Group interim condensed consolidated financial statements, supplementary notes covering fair value of financial liabilities and Note 6.10 Other financial liabilities, for the three-month period ended 31 March 2025 (figures in mln PLN). Comparative at 31 December 2024: total carrying value 2,935.8 (Level 1: 1,127.3; Level 2: 337.3; Level 3: 1,471.2); bank loans 1,466.8 (current account 90.3, non-revolving 1,376.5); debt securities 1,464.6 (Level 1: 1,127.3; Level 2: 337.3); borrowings 4.4. Fair value Level 1 = quoted prices for identical assets in active markets; Level 2 = models with directly/indirectly observable inputs; Level 3 = models with unobservable inputs.
