Dom Development S.A. consolidated sustainability reporting excerpt covering diversity metrics and ESG governance. The Company has implemented a Diversity Policy since 2022, respecting a diverse, multicultural society and emphasizing equal treatment regardless of gender, age, disability, health, race, nationality, ethnicity, religion, political conviction, union membership, sexual orientation, gender identity, family/social status, lifestyle, or position. Core objectives: full utilization of employee potential, skills, experience and talent in an atmosphere of respect; supporting strategic goals by defining diversity principles for Supervisory Board members, Management Board members and employees; recruiting employees with diversified skills, education and professional experience; and guaranteeing a non-discriminatory approach in recruitment and HR management. Diversity actions for Management and Supervisory Board members are published at https://inwestor.domd.pl. Senior management is defined as the Management Board of Dom Development S.A., a definition differing from ESRS.

ESG governance structure: Given the high materiality of ESG issues and their broad impact on business and Group development, the Company established within its organizational structure a Non-Financial Reporting Team — an organizational unit responsible for environmental, social and governance matters, current objectives, and operational coordination of the Group's sustainability management system. Responsibility of each body or person for impacts, risks and opportunities is reflected in the scope of tasks and powers held by individual Group units and incorporated into internal Group regulations.

Supervisory Board: exercises ongoing supervision over all areas of the Company's activity, including sustainable development. No separate ESG-management assessment is performed; the assessment covers the entire activity, of which sustainability is one pillar. In overseeing Group business strategy, the Board evaluates long- and short-term factors affecting operations, including market, regulatory and technological changes. It applies an internal procedure for periodic review of material transactions under the Act on public offering, conditions for introducing financial instruments to organized trading, and public companies; it consents to material transactions where statutory exemptions cannot apply and to agreements/benefits by the Company and related entities per applicable regulations and the Company's Statute. The Board considers impacts, risks and opportunities of major transactions, assessing effectiveness of policies and control mechanisms, analyzing key-risk identification/monitoring reports and crisis-management plans, and weighing trade-offs (e.g., risk-mitigation costs vs. potential impact of inaction). The Audit Committee is informed of material impacts/risks/opportunities, due-diligence implementation and regulatory compliance, and results of actions under adopted strategies and policies; information is provided by Management Board Members and dedicated persons for risk management, regulatory compliance, internal audit and ESG. Reporting is generally periodic (at least semi-annually), with ad hoc reporting for material changes or extraordinary situations, delivered via financial and non-financial reports, risk analyses and internal audit results; Audit Committee meetings include presentations on strategy/target effectiveness. In 2025 the Supervisory Board positively opined the double-materiality analysis results for sustainability reporting, reviewed the ESG action schedule ensuring compliance with sustainability reporting criteria, and supervised execution of actions/targets under the ESG DOM 2030 Strategy.

Management Board: from 2025, the President of the Management Board is responsible for ESG matters, as ESG affects all areas of Group operations. Material impacts, risks, opportunities and due-diligence processes are reported and consulted by the ESG Coordinator with the responsible Management Board Member and the President during systematic direct meetings and ongoing reviews. The Board monitors progress on targets related to material impacts/risks/opportunities by reviewing ESG Strategy actions and operating results, analyzing financial and non-financial data via cyclical reviews involving the ESG Coordinator, and cooperating closely with regulatory compliance, internal audit and risk management functions. Reporting is generally quarterly, with immediate reporting in material or extraordinary situations; the internal control system supports effectiveness assessment and identification of improvement areas. The Board weighs trade-offs (e.g., balancing economic benefits against potential negative social/environmental effects to optimize stakeholder value), monitors sustainability risks, and sets targets via strategic priorities, policies and oversight of their implementation. In 2025 the Board addressed impacts, risks and opportunities identified as material during the double-materiality analysis.
