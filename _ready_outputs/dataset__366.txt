XTB S.A. (XTB Spółka Akcyjna), a financial institution of Polish origin with global reach, listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange (Giełda Papierów Wartościowych w Warszawie S.A.), heads the international XTB Capital Group; excerpt drawn from the Q3 2025 Report ("Raport za III kwartał 2025") of Grupa Kapitałowa XTB S.A. Corporate identification: registered office and headquarters at ul. Prosta 67, 00-838 Warszawa; website www.xtb.com; KRS registration date 22 September 2004; KRS number 0000217580; NIP 5272443955; REGON 015803782. The Company is supervised by the Polish Financial Supervision Authority (Komisja Nadzoru Finansowego) and conducts regulated activity under an authorization dated 8 November 2005, no. DDM-M-4021-57-1/2005.

The Group provides investors continuous, instant online access to financial markets via its proprietary investment platform and mobile application, combining traditional brokerage services with current investment and finance technology to deliver easier, competitive access to a broad range of investment instruments. XTB developed and continues to develop its proprietary, universal online investment platform xStation and the XTB mobile app; both are All-in-One tools enabling investors to actively manage funds for investment purposes, supplemented by charts, analyses, research, and online training, with financial education positioned as a key element of XTB's activity.

Product offering spanning multiple investor groups: equities (shares); ETP funds (Exchange-Traded Products), comprising (i) exchange-listed ETFs (Exchange-Traded Funds) based on equities, stock indices, bonds, as well as commodities, precious metals, or cryptocurrencies, and (ii) ETCs (Exchange-Traded Commodity) reflecting prices of specified commodities such as gold or commodity groups; CFD contracts (Contract For Difference), a type of over-the-counter derivative whose underlying assets may be currency pairs (FOREX), shares, major stock indices, commodities and goods, increasingly popular cryptocurrencies, and others; XTB Investment Plans (Plany Inwestycyjne XTB) dedicated to passive investing, enabling low-cost building of an investment portfolio based on ETP funds; interest on free funds, paid to clients on uninvested balances remaining on XTB accounts; products dedicated to long-term and retirement saving, currently the Individual Retirement Account (IKE) and Individual Retirement Security Account (IKZE) available to Polish citizens, the Individual Savings Account (ISA) available to UK citizens, and the Plan d'Épargne en Actions (PEA) available to French citizens; eWallet, a virtual wallet service with a multi-currency card supporting cashless in-store payments, mobile transactions, and contactless ATM cash withdrawals worldwide; and Fractional Rights (Prawa Ułamkowe), conferring a fiduciary right to fractional parts of shares or units in ETFs, ETNs (Exchange-Traded Notes), or ETCs, enabling investing without share-price or fund-price constraints to ease portfolio management.

XTB serves both an expanding range of products for retail (individual) clients and the institutional client segment. Institutional services are conducted under the X Open Hub (XOH) brand, providing modern transactional technology and liquidity to financial institutions, with solutions meeting specific institutional requirements including integration with client systems and advanced analytical tools supporting investment decision-making.

Business model: The XTB Group business model centers on the human as the recipient of services and closely corresponds to the directions and goals of the Group's adopted sustainable development strategy. Its core premise is delivering clients modern solutions enabling realization of their investment goals through instant access to financial markets worldwide; the key technology products for reaching mass-market clients are the proprietary XTB investment platform and mobile application. Operating revenues of the XTB Group arise from: spreads (the difference between the offer/sell price and the bid/buy price); accrued swap points (the cost of holding positions over time); fees charged to clients; and net result (profits less losses) from the Group's market-making activity. Additionally, within operating activity, XTB earns revenue from interest on client cash.

Revenue diversification by client segment: The Group diversifies revenue by serving individual and institutional clients. INDIVIDUAL clients comprise natural persons and legal persons, served with financial-instrument trading services and access to a broad range of investment products under the XTB brand. INSTITUTIONAL clients are legal entities; services provide liquidity for trading in financial instruments and make transactional infrastructure available to institutions that provide financial-instrument trading services under their own brand to their own clients, delivered under the X Open Hub brand.

Revenue diversification by markets of operation: The XTB Group also diversifies revenue geographically, consistently executing a strategy to build a global brand. The principal market, where XTB consistently generates above 20% of revenues, is Poland. The Group additionally serves clients across Europe, the Middle East, Latin America, and Southeast Asia. XTB's Management Board (Zarząd) places primary emphasis on organic growth, increasing penetration of European markets while successively building presence in Latin America and Asia; in line with these actions the Capital Group's composition may expand to include new subsidiaries, with geographic expansion conducted on a continuous basis and its effects spread over time. Growth is also pursued through mergers and acquisitions, particularly with entities that would enable the Group to achieve geographic synergy (mutually complementary markets); the Management Board intends to execute such transactions only where they entail measurable benefits for the Company and its shareholders.
