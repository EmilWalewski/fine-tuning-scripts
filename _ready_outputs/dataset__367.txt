Geographic revenue diversification underpins XTB's global-brand strategy. (Belize).

Strategic developments: In 2025 Management focused primarily on obtaining licenses and permits to commence operations in Indonesia and Brazil, successfully completing both formal-legal processes and securing the Indonesian license first. The Indonesian subsidiary has already acquired its first clients, offering equities and ETFs, with CFD instruments planned for the turn of 2025/2026. In Brazil the licensing process was completed during the year, with the Group obtaining its operating permit and initiating registration on the list of supervised institutions; given current conditions in the Brazilian brokerage sector, particularly local protectionism, XTB is considering all business options, including ceasing further activity in that market. Europe remains a principal market; Management regularly monitors branches and subsidiaries to increase XTB's geographic share. Growth is also pursued via mergers and acquisitions, especially with entities enabling geographic synergy (mutually complementary markets), executed only where they bring measurable benefits to the Company and its shareholders.

Client-segment diversification: beyond the retail segment, the Group develops institutional activity under the X Open Hub (XOH) brand, providing liquidity and technology to other financial institutions. Revenues from this segment, like the retail segment, can fluctuate materially period to period, a phenomenon typical of the Group's adopted business model.
