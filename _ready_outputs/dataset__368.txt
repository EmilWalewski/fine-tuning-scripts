Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group report on apartment handovers and dividend policy. Revenue and associated cost recognition follow International Accounting Standards (specifically IFRS 15), under which the moment of unit handover to buyers—occurring after full payment and in the first weeks following project occupancy permits—constitutes the basis for recognizing sales in the income statement. Consequently, the financial results of the Company and Group are directly tied to the number and structure of units transferred in a given period and do not fully reflect the entity's current situation; quarterly fluctuations relate strictly to the construction project cycle and concentration of handovers rather than to seasonality, as the business is not seasonally dependent. To compensate, the Company additionally discloses current sales data, including preliminary sale agreements and paid reservation agreements measured net of cancellations.

On dividends: since its debut on the Warsaw Stock Exchange (GPW S.A.), Dom Development S.A. has shared its profits with shareholders, distributing over PLN 2 billion in dividends across the 19 years since listing while maintaining a very strong balance sheet and high cash position. Distributions totaling PLN 6.00 per share represented 59% of the Group's 2024 profit. In 2025, for the third time in its history, the Company paid an advance on the 2025 dividend of PLN 7.00 per share, totaling PLN 181 million; this PLN 7.00 per share / PLN 181 million payment represented 32% of the Group's consolidated 2024 profit and supplemented the prior-year dividend advance of PLN 155 million paid in the preceding year.

Customer/supplier concentration: neither the Company nor the Group depends on a single supplier or customer. The sole material counterparty is Dom Construction Sp. z o.o., the general contractor for the Group's projects. Its turnover exceeded 49.5% of the Company's 2025 sales revenue, amounting to PLN 796 million, and represented 43.7% of the Group's 2025 sales revenue, amounting to PLN 1,424 million.
