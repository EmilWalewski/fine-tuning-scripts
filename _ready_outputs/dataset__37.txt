XTB S.A.’s consolidated annual report states that, under resolution no. 6 of the Ordinary General Meeting of 14 May 2025, the company’s 2024 net profit of PLN 855.2 million was partly allocated to a dividend of PLN 640.8 million, while PLN 214.4 million was transferred to reserve capital. The dividend for 2024 was PLN 5.45 per share and was paid on 25 June 2025.

The historical dividend table shows dividends from profit for 2016 through 2024, with no dividend for 2017. The dividend from 2024 profit amounted to PLN 640.752 million, with the dividend day on 16 June 2025, payment on 25 June 2025, PLN 5.45 per share, and a dividend yield of 6.9%. For 2023, the dividend was PLN 590.198 million, PLN 5.02 per share, paid on 20 June 2024, with a 7.1% yield. For 2022, it was PLN 570.484 million, PLN 4.86 per share, paid on 21 July 2023, with an 11.8% yield. Earlier disclosed dividends included PLN 176.075 million for 2021, PLN 210.117 million for 2020, PLN 28.172 million for 2019, and PLN 37.563 million for 2016. For 2018, the report lists both an advance dividend of PLN 41.084 million paid on 18 December 2018 and a remaining dividend amount of PLN 19.955 million paid on 10 May 2019. The dividend yield is defined as the dividend per share divided by the market price of the share on the last day on which shares could be bought with dividend rights.

After the reporting period, on 17 February 2026 XTB S.A. signed an agreement to sell 100% of shares in its subsidiary XTB AFRICA (PTY) LTD in South Africa. Completion depends on approval of changes in the subsidiary’s shareholding structure by the South African Financial Sector Conduct Authority. The agreement value was not material under the company’s asset-value criteria, and the transaction was described as having no material impact on XTB’s financial position. The sale resulted from the subsidiary not beginning operating activity.

The report describes XTB Group’s risk management system as a set of policies, procedures, mechanisms and tools supporting management of individual risk types according to their materiality and characteristics. Its main objectives are to identify and assess the significance of risk types, measure or estimate risk levels including hard-to-measure risks, control risk levels through limit monitoring and responses to breaches or warning levels, and support business objectives while keeping risk within the accepted risk appetite. The system is based on internal regulations titled “Risk Management System at XTB S.A.” and “Risk Management Strategy at XTB S.A.”

The Group applies a Three Lines model. The first line includes Trading, Marketing and Sales, Customer Service, IT, and operational risk owners. The second line includes the Risk Control Department and the Legal and Compliance Department. The third line is the Internal Audit Department, reporting to the Management Board and Supervisory Board. At the strategic level, the Management Board is responsible for establishing and monitoring risk management policy. In the parent company, the Risk Management Committee includes members of the Supervisory Board and prepares the risk appetite proposal, gives opinions on the risk management strategy, supports the Supervisory Board in supervising strategy implementation, and reviews remuneration policy in relation to the risk profile, capital and liquidity.
