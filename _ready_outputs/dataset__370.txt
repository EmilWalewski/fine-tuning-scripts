Asseco Group interim condensed consolidated financial statements (additional explanatory notes), reporting period 1 January–31 March 2025 with comparative period 1 January–31 March 2024 (income statement, cash flow, statement of changes in consolidated equity) and balance sheet data at 31 March 2025 versus comparatives at 31 December 2024; presented in PLN, all values in millions of PLN unless stated otherwise, with possible PLN 0.1M rounding differences.

Geopolitical impact (note 2.2): Management, based on risk analysis including current geopolitics, concluded going concern is not threatened for at least 12 months from 31 March 2025. Following Russia's February 2022 invasion of Ukraine, the geopolitical situation of the region hosting the Parent and other Group companies changed materially; during the reporting period Asseco Group conducted no material business activity in Russia or Belarus and held no funds in Russian bank accounts. Certain Group companies, mainly the Formula Systems segment, employ staff and use outsourced developers from Ukraine and Russia. ASEE Group holds one subsidiary based in Ukraine (Lviv), acquired in 2024, whose impact on ASEE Group and Asseco Group financials is immaterial. Following the October 2023 Hamas attack on Israel and Israel's subsequent military operation in Palestine, the geopolitical situation in Israel—where many Formula Systems segment companies (identical to Formula Group) operate—changed; Formula Group companies maintain established business continuity programs and, at publication, all Formula Systems segment companies operate normally and meet contractual obligations on time. The Group recorded no material impact from the economic-political situation in Ukraine, sanctions on Russia, or the Israel situation on these statements or its operations.

Compliance statement (note 2.3): Prepared per IAS 34 "Interim Financial Reporting" as endorsed by the EU. Scope of the interim condensed consolidated statements, an element of the quarterly report, complies with the Polish Minister of Finance Regulation of 29 March 2018 on current and periodic information by securities issuers (consolidated text: Journal of Laws 2018 item 757), covering reporting period 1 January–31 March 2025 and comparative period 1 January–31 March 2024.

Functional/presentation currency and hyperinflation (note 2.4): Statements presented in Polish zloty (PLN); the Parent's functional and presentation currency is PLN. Subsidiaries' functional currencies are those of their primary economic environments. For consolidation, foreign entities' statements are translated to PLN using NBP exchange rates at period-end (balance sheet items) and the arithmetic average of NBP rates effective on the last day of each month of the period (income statement, other comprehensive income, cash flow items); translation effects are recognized in equity under "Exchange differences on translation of subsidiaries, associates and joint ventures." For subsidiaries in hyperinflationary economies, income statement and other comprehensive income items are translated to PLN using NBP period-end rates.

Professional judgment and estimates (note 2.5): Preparation under IFRS requires estimates and assumptions; actual results may differ.
(i) Consolidation of entities where the Group holds under 50% of votes: Management's control judgment is unchanged from that detailed in point 2.5 of the Basis of Preparation in the consolidated financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2024, published 27 March 2025. The Parent controlled Formula Systems (1985) Ltd ("Formula"/"Formula Systems"), in which the Group holds under 50% of share capital; the same applies to entities in which Formula Systems holds direct, non-absolute-majority interests: Sapiens International Corporation NV, Magic Software Enterprises Ltd, and Matrix IT Ltd. Control conclusion was also retained for Asseco Business Solutions S.A. (a direct subsidiary of Asseco Enterprise Solutions, a.s.), in which the Group holds 47.05% of AGM voting rights (excluding treasury shares), and for NXTBK Inc. (subsidiary of Nextbank Software Sp. z o.o.), in which the Group holds 39.9% of share capital and AGM votes. All these entities were consolidated using the full method in Asseco Group's interim condensed consolidated statements for the three months ended 31 March 2025.
(ii) Estimates: No material changes in estimation methodology occurred in the three months ended 31 March 2025. The Group discloses any changes to prior-period estimates that significantly affect the current interim period; significant accounting policies for items carrying material risk of significant carrying-value adjustment are described in point 2.5 of the consolidated statements for the year ended 31 December 2024.
(iii) Hyperinflation: An additional area of judgment is foreign companies operating in hyperinflationary economies. Management analyzed qualitative and quantitative factors (including whether cumulative three-year inflation approaches or exceeds 100%) and concluded Turkey is a hyperinflationary economy. Consequently, financial data of subsidiaries operating in Turkey within ASEE Group (Asseco International segment) were restated for purchasing-power changes using a general price index to express amounts in measurement units current at period-end. The hyperinflation impact is described in note 2.11.
