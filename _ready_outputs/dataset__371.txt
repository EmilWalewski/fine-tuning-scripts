XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025 Consolidated Annual Report, ESG strategy and management section. As a developing fintech, XTB integrates sustainable development criteria (Environmental, Social, Governance) into its operating activity and business strategy. In 2024 XTB adopted an ESG Strategy for 2024-2027. Stated business objectives: strengthening position in Western and Central-Eastern European markets; further geographic expansion into new markets including Latin America and Asia; further development and strengthening of the institutional client segment via X Open Hub; broadening the Group's product and service offering and technological innovation; growth through acquisitions, mergers and joint ventures; employee development and competence building; diversity and flexibility. Sustainability pillars: responsible managerial practices, regulated activity, risk management, responsible payment practices, technology and product portfolio development, responsible marketing, transparent communication, financial education, business ethics, fair competition, mature organizational culture, human rights and competitive employment conditions.

Charitable and educational activities. Career Project at Avalon Foundation: at the foundation's request (caring for people with disabilities), in April 2025 two two-hour workshops on financial management and investing basics were held for its beneficiaries. "Pocztowki z powodzi i Biznes Restart," initiated by agency AWX, aimed at helping Lower Silesian tourism-sector entrepreneurs affected by last year's flood; program partners offered legal, logistical, marketing, IT, training and financial assistance. XTB Foundation granted PLN 40,000 in financial grants for sustainable-development innovations. XTB Foundation financially supported Fundacja Komandosi Zycia (organizer of "Pod skrzydlami mistrzow" sports camps for children from care facilities, associated with former XTB ambassador Joanna Jedrzejczyk). The foundation supported employee initiatives: sports activities, participation in open events such as city marathons, and preventive USG screenings. Volunteering: in May 2025 XTB employees joined a nationwide Polish river-bank cleanup; over 30 volunteers cleaned the Narew riverbank near Minsk Mazowiecki, collecting over one tonne of waste. XTB again participated in the Szlachetna Paczka campaign.

Educational programs. "Finanse z klasa" (Akademia Jutra): first edition started October 2024, concluded February 2025; covered 2,588 secondary-school students from Mazowieckie voivodeship; six Q&A sessions with XTB experts held; participants defined financial goals and proposed a 10-year financial management plan; final held 21 February on the GPW (Warsaw Stock Exchange), with winners announced and prizes awarded. Second edition began September 2025, covering 5,000 Polish secondary-school students, with school enrollment closing within two weeks. Stock Exchange Academy (organized by the Investor Club at SGH): XTB Foundation was strategic partner; an XTB expert delivered a technical-analysis lecture and board member Filip Kaczmarzyk participated in a thematic debate. Young Stock Exchange Investor competition (Koszalin University of Technology): XTB Foundation was strategic partner and main sponsor; over 600 students from across Poland participated; final stage required executing 10 investment transactions on the xStation5 demo platform for the best result. Cooperation with the Polish Economic Society (PTE): XTB Foundation is strategic partner and sponsor of the National Economic Knowledge Olympiad; the final stage in early April 2025 selected 5 winners who represented Poland at the International Economic Knowledge Olympiad in Greece in September, winning 4 medals (3 silver, 1 bronze); second consecutive year as strategic partner. Second PTE project, Patronage Classes, organized workshops for Business and Management (BiZ) teachers in economic education (only 30% of BiZ teachers completed economics-related studies).

Foundation and Group funding. In 2025 XTB Foundation allocated PLN 372.1 thousand to statutory financial-education purposes and approximately PLN 257 thousand to charitable/sponsorship purposes (versus approximately PLN 141.7 thousand and approximately PLN 10.7 thousand respectively in 2024). In 2025 the XTB Group made donations of over PLN 4.7 thousand for charity, PLN 19 thousand for education, and PLN 850 thousand to XTB Foundation statutory activity (versus PLN 185 thousand to the foundation's statutory activity in 2024).

Strategic outlook. Plans include stakeholder communication enhancement via website modernization and improved information access, and systematic monitoring of market/technology trends (AI, financial-process automation, changing investment habits). In 2025 and early 2026 the Group introduced long-term saving and investing accounts in Poland, France and the UK, and launched an AI module tracking media sentiment around stocks, ETFs and CFD instruments; full 2026 product scope detailed in the report's 2026 Product Plan. Long-term (5+ years): expected further market digitalization, growing investment-process automation, AI and investment algorithms, growth in passive investing, thematic investments, and mobile-app-oriented services for younger investors, with security maintenance as a key factor. Mid-term (3-5 years): adapting product/service offering to changing client needs, developing new investment products and intuitive mobile applications. Per Puls Biznesu data, at end of Q3 2025 27.8 million clients actively used mobile banking apps (up approximately 8.1% year-over-year and approximately 1.1% versus Q2). A 360 Research Reports report on the global micro-investing app market estimated market value at USD 1.12 billion in 2024; mobile apps represented 85% of all global users and Android systems accounted for 62% of all downloads; Generation Z and Millennials constituted over 91% of fintech investors.
