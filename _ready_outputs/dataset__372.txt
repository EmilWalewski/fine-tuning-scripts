Macroeconomic impact on ECL estimates, Wonga loan portfolio: ECL on amortised-cost loans determined using parameters PD (probability of default), PPS (probability of prepayment), LGD (loss given default) and EAD (exposure at default). LGD depends on post-default recoveries, realised via receivables sale or amicable then judicial debt collection; collection recoveries for LGD are based on historical payment curves for similar receivables, periodically verified. In H1 2025 Wonga updated its payment curve due to higher recoveries observed over a longer time horizon. Analysed macro variables: consumer bankruptcy, unemployment levels (total registered, newly registered, repeat registered), registered unemployment rate, new job offers in period; significant variables added to the loss-estimation model where they improve forecast accuracy alongside behavioural scoring.
