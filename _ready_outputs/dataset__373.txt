Registered office relocated effective 31 March 2025 from ul. Wołowska 8, 51-116 Wrocław to ul. Bolkowska 3, 53-612 Wrocław, Poland. Entity is registered in the National Court Register (Krajowy Rejestr Sądowy) maintained by the District Court for Wrocław-Fabryczna in Wrocław, VI Commercial Division of the National Court Register, ul. Poznańska 16-17, 53-230 Wrocław; registration date 7 September 2005; register number KRS 0000240829.

Scope of business: the Parent Entity and most of its subsidiaries engage in the restructuring and recovery of receivables purchased by Group entities and of receivables entrusted by financial institutions and other parties; certain subsidiaries additionally conduct consumer lending activity. The condensed interim consolidated financial statements cover the reporting period 1 January to 30 June 2025 and comprise the financial statements of the Parent Entity and its subsidiaries (collectively the "Group" or "Capital Group"). This is a half-year report comprising condensed interim consolidated financial statements.
