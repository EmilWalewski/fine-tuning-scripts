Dom Development S.A. Management Board Report on Operations for H1 2025, covering sections on development strategy of the Company and its Capital Group and on sustainable development. Reference made to the residential district Mokotów Sportowy, Warszawa. The Management Board's strategic objective for Dom Development S.A. is maximization of the Company's and the Group's value over the long term by maintaining its position as leader of the residential housing market operating in the country's principal metropolitan areas; strategy execution rests on defined pillars. The Dom Development S.A. Capital Group, a leading entity on the Polish residential market, plays a significant role in shaping urban space and improving quality of life in the cities where it carries out projects. The Management Board actively promotes sustainable urban development, implementing proprietary standards in investment planning; each element of a developed estate — apartments, common spaces, architectural solutions, and the layout of green areas — is designed to create a comfortable, aesthetic, and functional environment fostering high-quality urban space. Sustainable development activities of the Dom Development Group companies are defined by the ESG DOM 2030 Strategy adopted in 2022, with the full text of the DOM 2030 Strategy published on the Company's website at https://inwestor.domd.pl/pl/esg.
