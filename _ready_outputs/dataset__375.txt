KRUK Group does not recognize CIT based on a forecast average annual effective rate, as this would not eliminate in-year tax irregularities. All deferred tax figures are stated in thousands of PLN.

Narrative on deferred tax provision drivers: the level of the deferred tax provision is affected by changes in assumed future cash flows from investment companies to KRUK S.A. in the foreseeable future. These flows depend on, among others: KRUK S.A. liquidity needs and obtained/forecast available new debt financing for KRUK S.A.; obtained/forecast available new debt financing in investment companies; planned outlays on debt portfolios (which influence the forecast liquidity position of investment companies); and planned collections from purchased debt portfolios in investment companies. Consequently, the deferred tax provision item on "assumed future cash flows from subsidiaries" may be subject to material changes across reporting periods.

IAS 12 exemption: the Group applies the IAS 12 exemption and does not recognize a deferred tax provision on retained earnings of related entities where it controls the timing of reversal of temporary differences and it is probable those differences will not reverse in the foreseeable future. The aggregate amount of temporary differences underlying the unrecognized deferred tax provision on retained earnings as at 31 December 2025 is 6,351,904 thousand PLN (6,351.9M PLN), versus 5,081,798 thousand PLN (5,081.8M PLN) as at 31 December 2024.

OECD Pillar Two / top-up tax: effective 1 January 2025, under the Act of 6 November 2024 on top-up taxation of constituent entities of international and domestic groups ("the Act"), the OECD Pillar Two rules took effect in Poland. These regulations require taxpayers to pay a top-up tax equal to the difference between the jurisdiction's effective tax rate (computed per the Act) and a 15% minimum rate. The threshold for group coverage is consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million in two of the four years immediately preceding the tax year. As at 31 December 2025 the Group had not exceeded this limit: FY2025 revenue, converted to EUR at the ECB average rate from December of the year preceding the examined year (4.2704), equals EUR 747 million. Per Group estimates, KRUK Group will meet the threshold at the earliest in 2028, i.e. consolidated revenue may reach the EUR 750 million threshold at the earliest in 2026 and 2027.

The Group is assessing its Pillar Two exposure. Given that its subsidiaries operate in jurisdictions (Polish, Maltese and Luxembourg) for which the estimated effective tax rate, based on gross profit for the reporting period ended 31 December 2025, is below 15%, it is analyzing the potential impact of these regulations on the Group's taxation level. That rate will in practice differ from the rate determined under IAS 12. In particular, the Group is assessing the applicability of permitted exclusions and so-called safe harbours under the Act and their impact on the effective tax rate for top-up tax calculation. Based on current assessment, application of the Act may increase the Group's annual effective tax rate not earlier than 2028; accordingly, no current tax charge arises as at the reporting date. The Group applies the IAS 12 exception permitting non-recognition and non-disclosure of deferred tax assets and liabilities related to Pillar Two income tax.
