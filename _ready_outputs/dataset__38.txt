At 31 March 2025, Asseco Group reported total receivables of PLN 4,507.8 million, consisting of PLN 107.3 million long-term and PLN 4,400.5 million short-term. At 31 December 2024, total receivables were PLN 4,658.5 million, including PLN 94.5 million long-term and PLN 4,564.0 million short-term.

Trade receivables totaled PLN 4,132.2 million at 31 March 2025, comprising PLN 37.4 million long-term and PLN 4,094.8 million short-term. At 31 December 2024, they totaled PLN 4,286.0 million, including PLN 29.9 million long-term and PLN 4,256.1 million short-term. Invoiced receivables were PLN 5.6 million long-term and PLN 3,264.8 million short-term at 31 March 2025, compared with PLN 7.2 million and PLN 3,485.8 million at 31 December 2024. Unbilled receivables were PLN 31.5 million long-term and PLN 972.7 million short-term at 31 March 2025, compared with PLN 21.8 million and PLN 912.8 million at year-end 2024. The expected credit loss allowance for trade receivables was PLN 0.4 million long-term and PLN 152.8 million short-term at 31 March 2025, versus nil long-term and PLN 154.9 million short-term at 31 December 2024.

Income tax receivables were PLN 94.6 million short-term at 31 March 2025 and PLN 135.2 million at 31 December 2024. Budget receivables were PLN 69.9 million at 31 March 2025 and PLN 46.5 million at 31 December 2024. Other receivables were PLN 69.9 million long-term and PLN 141.2 million short-term at 31 March 2025, compared with PLN 64.6 million and PLN 126.2 million at 31 December 2024.

Contract assets arise when progress on implementation contracts exceeds issued invoices. They were entirely short-term: PLN 557.5 million at 31 March 2025 and PLN 554.6 million at 31 December 2024. Of the 31 March 2025 balance, PLN 29.3 million related to related parties and PLN 528.2 million to other entities.

The carrying amount of receivables and contract assets did not differ from fair value. The Group stated that it applies a policy of selling only to verified customers and, due to the macroeconomic and geopolitical environment, has intensified monitoring and collection procedures. As of the report publication date, it saw no grounds to increase expected credit loss allowances or change its policy.

The allowance for trade receivables moved from PLN 154.9 million at 1 January 2025 to PLN 153.2 million at 31 March 2025. During the period, PLN 20.3 million was created, PLN 2.1 million was used, PLN 14.3 million was released, PLN 1.0 million arose from obtaining control over subsidiaries, and foreign exchange differences added PLN 6.6 million. In the comparative three-month period to 31 March 2024, the allowance decreased from PLN 131.3 million to PLN 129.5 million.
