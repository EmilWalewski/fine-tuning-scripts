Asseco Group quarterly report for the three-month period ended 31 March 2025 (figures in millions of PLN), Section B commentary, sub-section 3.2 covering the Asseco International segment. Selected financial data for the segment for Q1 2025 versus the restated comparable period Q1 2024. Sales revenue rose 10.8% to PLN 1,058.3M from PLN 955.1M. EBIT declined slightly by 0.8% to PLN 101.3M from PLN 102.1M, with EBIT margin contracting 1.1 percentage points to 9.6% from 10.7%. Non-IFRS EBIT grew 10.0% to PLN 119.2M from PLN 108.4M, while non-IFRS EBIT margin held flat at 11.3% in both periods. EBITDA edged up 0.5% to PLN 152.3M from PLN 151.6M, though EBITDA margin fell 1.5 percentage points to 14.4% from 15.9%. Operating cash flow before tax (CFO BT) increased 48.5% to PLN 159.8M from PLN 107.6M. CAPEX rose 30.2% to PLN 36.6M from PLN 28.1M, and lease expenditures increased 10.4% to PLN 14.9M from PLN 13.5M. Free cash flow (FCF) jumped 64.1% to PLN 108.3M from PLN 66.0M. The cash conversion ratio improved by 30.0 percentage points to 91% from 61%. Cash and cash equivalents (comparable period 31 December 2024) decreased 5.0% to PLN 850.2M from PLN 895.0M. Interest-bearing debt (comparable period 31 December 2024) increased 7.0% to PLN 518.0M from PLN 484.2M, comprising loans, borrowings and issued bonds up 10.1% to PLN 266.5M from PLN 242.0M, and lease liabilities up 3.8% to PLN 251.5M from PLN 242.2M.
