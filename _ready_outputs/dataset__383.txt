Dom Development S.A. Capital Group 2025 financial results discussion (Management Report on Operations), covering asset structure, liabilities/equity, and liquidity ratios as at 31 December 2025 versus 31 December 2024. Asset structure remained dominated by current assets, totaling PLN 5,729M, while non-current assets represented only 4% of the balance sheet. Cash—defined as cash and cash equivalents augmented by funds held on open escrow accounts—stayed very high at PLN 522M, securing flexibility to respond to market changes. Funds on Group companies' residential escrow accounts reached PLN 253M (vs PLN 217M a year earlier), driven mainly by increased sales volume; high levels reflect strict release requirements, with the modest ~12% ratio to invoiced sales recognized under deferred income liabilities (both 2025 and 2024 year-end) evidencing operational efficiency and timely construction completion (escrow funds released proportionally to construction progress). Inventories remained the key asset, comprising 85% of the balance sheet and rising 18% over 2025 to PLN 5,087M, principally projects under construction and investment land for future projects totaling PLN 4,421M.

Consolidated balance sheet (PLN '000, with balance-sheet share and YoY change): Total non-current assets PLN 252.7M (4%) vs PLN 207.5M (+22%); Inventories PLN 5,087.1M (85%) vs PLN 4,301.2M (+18%); Trade and other receivables PLN 101.3M (2%) vs PLN 109.7M (−8%); Other current assets and loans granted PLN 8.1M (<1%) vs PLN 29.0M (−72%); Income tax receivables PLN 3.8M (<1%) vs PLN 50.1M (−92%); Cash and cash equivalents plus short-term financial assets PLN 528.2M (9%) vs PLN 591.0M (−11%); Total current assets PLN 5,728.6M (96%) vs PLN 5,081.0M (+13%); TOTAL ASSETS PLN 5,981.3M (100%) vs PLN 5,288.5M (+13%). Equity components: Share capital PLN 25.8M (<1%, 0% change); Share premium PLN 276.5M (5%, 0%); Reserve, supplementary capital and undistributed result PLN 1,686.0M (28%) vs PLN 1,399.4M (+20%); Equity attributable to parent PLN 1,988.3M (33%) vs PLN 1,701.7M (+17%); Minority interests PLN 0.13M (<1%) vs PLN 0.17M (−27%); TOTAL EQUITY PLN 1,988.4M (33%) vs PLN 1,701.8M (+17%). Equity closed 2025 at PLN 1,988M, 17% above 31 December 2024, driven predominantly by net profit of PLN 654M and payment in 2025 of the remaining portion of the dividend from 2024 profit (amount truncated in text). Total Group liabilities reached PLN 3,993M, of which PLN 2,047M was deferred income classified as current liabilities—the dominant liability item. Despite record sales revenue recognized in the 2025 consolidated income statement, deferred income rose PLN 173M YoY, confirming effective use of market conditions and the margin/profit maximization policy. Debt structure was comfortable: short-term interest-bearing liabilities comprised bonds of PLN 110M and loans of PLN 10M; remaining interest-bearing debt of PLN 578M was long-term, consisting of issued bonds of PLN 535M and loans of PLN 43M with staggered maturities reaching 2030. Total Group interest-bearing debt at year-end was PLN 698M. Available financial resources stood at a record over PLN 1.2bn—PLN 522M cash plus PLN 717M unused credit lines.

Liquidity: Management secured a very comfortable liquidity position via medium/long-term rather than short-term financing structure, investment timing/assortment decisions, and property acquisition strategy. Current ratio (current assets/current liabilities, excluding deferred income) was 5.25 at year-end 2025 vs 5.37 in 2024. Quick ratio ((current assets − inventories)/current liabilities) fell to 0.59 from 0.82; cash ratio (cash/current liabilities) fell to 0.25 from 0.38—declines attributed to materially lower CIT receivables alongside higher CIT liabilities and somewhat lower cash holdings, deemed positive outcomes of rational resource management. Combined favorable liquidity and record available external financing (PLN 1.2bn) constitute a key competitive advantage amid an unstable market environment.
