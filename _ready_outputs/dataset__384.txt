XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025, GHG emissions/ESG disclosure detailing Scope 3 carbon-footprint composition. In 2025, as in 2024, Scope 3 (indirect value-chain emissions) dominated the Group's footprint at 98.03% of total emissions (Scope 1 + Scope 2 market-based + Scope 3). Scope 3 Category 1 (Purchased goods and services) remained the leading category at 35,719 tCO2e, equal to 85.94% of the organization's total carbon footprint; its high value reflects the Group's business model generating substantial spend on services, primarily marketing. Category 1 emissions rose 74% year-over-year, consistent with a 78% increase in spend in that category. The emission profile and main sources remained stable year-over-year despite changes in total emissions. Remaining Scope 3 categories carried materially lower values, ranging from 19.52 tCO2e (Category 4 — Upstream transport and distribution) to 2,016 tCO2e (Category 11 — Use of sold products); collectively these smaller categories/scopes accounted for 12.09% of organizational emissions, with relative shares unchanged year-over-year.

Scope 3 Category 2 (Capital goods): emission growth driven mainly by the opening of a new office in the United Arab Emirates for subsidiary XTB Financial Services LLC; expenditures on this project represented 8% of total Category 2 spend yet accounted for 45% of Category 2 emissions. A second material driver was the purchase of an IT infrastructure device, whose emissions were estimated from EPD reference data for comparable products due to absence of an available EPD card; this expenditure represented 12% of Category 2 spend and 25% of Category 2 emissions.

Scope 3 Category 3 (Fuel- and energy-related emissions not included in Scope 1 and 2): change driven primarily by shifts in electricity consumption at the Group's key operational locations; year-over-year emission growth tied directly to an approximately 27% increase in electricity consumption across three locations versus 2024. Emissions in this category remain strongly correlated with energy consumption volume and with factors affecting Scope 1 and Scope 2 reported emissions, producing year-over-year variability.

Scope 3 Category 4 (Upstream transport and distribution): the largest share of emissions came from courier-service expenditures incurred at XTB S.A., which accounted for approximately 81% of total Category 4 GHG emissions. Emissions rose +2% year-over-year, driven by an approximately 6% increase in courier-service spend at that company. Category 4 emissions were estimated 100% from financial data, leaving the result sensitive to exchange-rate differences on expenditures borne by XTB S.A. branches and subsidiaries. Category 4 represented 1.91% of all Group GHG emissions for 2025 (Scope 1 + Scope 2 market-based + Scope 3).

Scope 3 Category 7 (Employee commuting): emissions rose 92% year-over-year; commuting-related emissions rose 98% and remote-work emissions rose 52% year-over-year, both increases driven primarily by headcount growth versus the prior year. The share of public transit in commuting fell from 72.29% (2024) to 60.73% of total kilometers traveled, while internal-combustion car travel rose from 24.08% to 32.20%; this modal shift contributed to higher category emissions. An additional driver was introduction of a new survey and updated methodological assumptions, notably a question on home-to-workplace distance enabling more accurate identification of transport modes, including combined and alternative commuting forms; absence of this metric in the prior survey edition means prior-year emissions may have been underestimated.

Scope 3 Category 11 (Use of sold products): emissions rose 37% year-over-year, driven mainly by a 63% increase in client count over the comparable period.

A further reported change: emissions in the relevant category rose 63% year-over-year, driven primarily by increased municipal-waste emissions, which grew from 32 to 55 tCO2e, a change directly linked to the opening of the new United Arab Emirates office.
