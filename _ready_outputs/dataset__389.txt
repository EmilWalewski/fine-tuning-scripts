XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025 Consolidated Annual Report excerpt detailing the double-materiality assessment of ESG impacts across own operations, upstream, and downstream value chain, organized by ESRS topics with impact descriptions (actual/potential, positive/negative), strategic linkages, time horizons (short-, medium-, long-term), and priority ratings.

Equal treatment and equal opportunities (high priority, own operations): drivers include work-life balance via flexible employment terms, adjustable work start hours and remote/hybrid/on-site modes. Actions: pay-gap calculation; remuneration, training and diversity policies; consistent regular employee appraisals; co-financing of training, courses and postgraduate studies; internal training via dedicated training department; available misconduct-reporting channel; diversity training; invitation of women to corporate bodies (XTB S.A. Supervisory Board). Positive impacts may strengthen organizational culture, raise employee competencies and comfort, reduce turnover and retain talent. Negative impacts: potential widening of the gender pay gap raising perceived inequality, negative annual employee satisfaction survey scores and higher turnover. Reflected in ESG Strategy goals on anti-mobbing policy, DEI policy and pay-gap identification; HR department expansion noted.

ESRS S4 Consumers and End Users (high priority; own operations and downstream): actions include communication channels (e.g., helpline), qualified Customer Service department, analysis of client opinions, irregularities/complaints reporting channel, transparent product/service presentation, regulated activity compliant with law, complete client documentation, free educational materials and expert economic commentary, and evidence-based social-media communication. Positive impacts: stronger XTB brand trust, new client acquisition, fewer complaints via the universal investment app and diverse XTB investment product offering. Negative potential impacts: insufficient attention to client opinions causing offer-market misalignment and client loss; insufficient investor knowledge and misunderstanding of risk-bearing investment products; communication bearing greenwashing characteristics.

Corruption and bribery (standard priority; upstream, own operations, downstream): XTB operates in a highly regulated financial-services sector where transparency and integrity are paramount. Actual positive impact via internal regulations, misconduct-reporting process, an established audit and compliance unit, and the implemented Anti-Corruption Policy in XTB S.A. This ESG Strategy 2024-2027 goal of creating an Anti-Corruption Policy was completed in the last quarter of 2024. Potential negative impact: occurrence of corruption undermining trust and XTB brand reputation; non-compliance could result in high fines, sanctions and even revocation of operating licenses. Actions: Anti-Corruption Policy; training for Management Board, Supervisory Board members and employees in particularly exposed functions; misconduct-reporting procedure and incident register; internal audit, compliance and legal units overseeing processes and procedure currency.

Supplier relationship management including payment practices (moderate priority; upstream, own operations, downstream): XTB influences suppliers by limiting payment delays. Actual positive impact through cooperation with responsible suppliers and business partners and timely payments (no payment delays); no negative impacts identified. Correlated with business and ESG strategies through building valuable value-chain partner relations supporting high product/service quality and corporate-governance development; dedicated communication/PR department within company structure.

Data protection / data security (moderate priority): actions include modern, updated network and system security safeguards plus employee training and awareness campaigns. Potential negative impact: data breach causing reputational damage and erosion of client trust; data processed by XTB is appropriately secured against unforeseen circumstances, with processing inherent to the investment-product business model.

Sustainability reporting (ESRS topic): XTB S.A. has fulfilled the sustainability reporting obligation since 2020. Actions: tracking legal changes and trends, adapting and improving the reporting process, cooperation with qualified advisors, and independent auditor review. Linked to the ESG Strategy underpinning the Group's sustainability activities; failure to meet the statutory reporting obligation carries legal consequences.
