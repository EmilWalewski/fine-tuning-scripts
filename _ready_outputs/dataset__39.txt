In XTB S.A. Group’s 2025 consolidated annual report, stakeholders identified key sustainability topics across environmental, social and governance areas. In the environment and climate area, important issues included responsible management of electronic waste, minimizing negative climate impact, reducing greenhouse gas emissions and sustainable ICT, with relevance for stakeholders, employees and suppliers. In the social area, stakeholders emphasized security and confidentiality of personal data, effective communication with employees, and customer service quality and satisfaction. In governance, stakeholders pointed to business ethics, legal and regulatory compliance, anti-corruption practices, and prevention of corruption, bribery and money laundering. Customers additionally highlighted fair competition, anti-corruption measures and business ethics.

Stakeholders also indicated that the Group should engage in educational activities, including raising employees’ environmental awareness and supporting financial management education for children and young people. Suggestions from surveyed entities often included greater involvement in charitable and social initiatives, such as forest planting and public waste collection.

The results of the double materiality assessment were validated by the Management Board of XTB S.A., which approved the list of material topics forming the basis for identifying ESRS disclosures. The identified impacts, opportunities and risks informed the updated ESG Strategy for 2024-2027. Risks were aligned with and incorporated into the internal Risk Management System, while the Sustainability Team conducts an annual review of the materiality assessment results.

In 2025, XTB reviewed and updated the double materiality assessment by inviting selected employee representatives who had participated in the original study to provide opinions. Since no comments were received and there were no changes in the Group’s business model, activities or environment, only minor updates were made. In cooperation with the Risk Control Department, an additional ESG risk related to cybersecurity was introduced. Following work with the auditor, the Group removed a positive actual governance impact related to a transparent reporting process compliant with law, because it was considered to result solely from regulation rather than from the company’s own initiative.

As there were no significant changes, the list of material topics exceeding the materiality threshold from the impact and financial materiality perspectives remained unchanged from 2024 for 2025 reporting. The topics were ESRS E1 Climate Change, ESRS S1 Own Workforce, ESRS S4 Consumers and End Users, and ESRS G1 Business Conduct.

Impact materiality was assessed for positive and negative impacts using scale, scope and probability, with irreversibility of effects also considered for negative impacts. Financial materiality of opportunities and risks was assessed using the scale of possible financial effects and likelihood of occurrence across time horizons. In 2024, based on the double materiality assessment results, XTB carried out a climate scenario analysis for identified climate risk using TCFD guidelines. The analyzed physical risk concerned external events and business continuity risks linked to possible sudden, extreme weather events in locations of certain Group companies and branches. Two scenarios were developed, “2°C or lower” and “4°C,” selected because of the best data availability.
