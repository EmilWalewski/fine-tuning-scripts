Excerpt from Section 7, Additional Information and Notes to the Standalone Financial Statements of Dom Development S.A., for the year ended 31 December 2025. The note addresses key accounting judgments, estimates, and revenue recognition policies. Perpetual usufruct rights: all future payments arising from perpetual usufruct rights, over the contractual grant period for individual properties (which may extend up to 99 years), are discounted; this period is independent of the Company's expected ownership horizon or the planned use of such properties for development investments.

Subsection 7.5, Key Values Based on Professional Judgment and Sources of Estimation: management's professional judgment and adopted assumptions, alongside accounting estimates, are most significant when applying accounting policies to the matters below. Development project budgets: property (land) acquisition decisions rest on analyses whose key element is so-called purchase budgets, prepared to assess projects' future profitability. From the point of property acquisition, project budgets are updated per management's best knowledge and experience. All construction project budgets are reviewed and, where needed, updated at least every 3 months. Updated project budgets serve as the basis for: verifying project profitability and any impairment write-down of inventory value; and preparing financial forecasts, annual budgets, and medium-term plans.

Revenue recognition for product sales: revenue from property sales (residential units, commercial space, etc.) is recognized upon transfer to the buyer of control over the acquired property together with significant risks and rewards of ownership. Per management's assessment, this occurs upon handover of the property to the buyer based on a handover protocol signed by the parties, conditional on the buyer having made 100% of payments toward the property purchase price. Seasonality: the Company's operating activity is not subject to material seasonality effects.
