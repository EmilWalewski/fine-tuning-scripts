XTB S.A. Capital Group Q3 2025 report (nine months ended 30 September 2025) addresses factors affecting Group results, prior-forecast realization, related-party transactions, and material legal proceedings. Management identifies trends expected to persist through end-2025 and beyond: the XTB Group business model; continued growth of the XTB client base and reach to the mass-market client, deemed key to dynamic development and global-brand building, tied to expansion of the product portfolio including an All-in-One investment application offering easy, intelligent, efficient trading, investing and saving with instant access to funds; and institutional-segment services (X Open Hub), which carry different risks and cause large period-to-period revenue swings. The institutional segment's share of total operating revenue was 2.2% for 9M ended 30.09.2025, versus full-year figures of 4.4% (FY2024), 6.8% (FY2023), 1.3% (FY2022), 0.3% (FY2021), 13.2% (FY2020), and 8.7% (FY2019). Financial- and commodity-market volatility in 2025, regulatory changes, and other factors may affect institutional partners' condition, lot turnover volume, and XTB revenue from these clients. Given dynamic growth, Management estimates total operating costs may run up to roughly 40% higher than 2024; marketing outlays may rise about 80% versus 2024, assuming average client-acquisition cost comparable to 2023-2024. Final operating-cost level depends on headcount growth pace, variable remuneration components, marketing spend, geographic-expansion pace into new markets, and the impact of potential new regulations and external factors on revenue. XTB is building presence in non-European markets (Latin America and Asia) pursuing a global-brand strategy emphasizing organic growth and deeper European penetration; the Group composition may broaden with new subsidiaries. Growth is also possible via mergers and acquisitions, especially for geographic synergy (complementary markets), to be pursued only where tangible benefits accrue to the Company and shareholders. Management expectations carry heightened uncertainty regarding future economic conditions.

Regarding prior forecasts: XTB S.A. Management Board published no financial-result forecasts for 2025.
