Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group financial results review for H1 2025 (Management Board Report on Activities), covering liabilities/equity, company cash flows, and profitability ratios. The Management Board maintains emphasis on financial security via a conservative funding structure. As at 30 June 2025, the Company held shareholders' equity of PLN 1,647.0M, representing 40% of total liabilities and equity. The next major liabilities item was deferred income/advances ("zobowiązania z tytułu przychodów przyszłych okresów") of PLN 1,140.4M (27% of total), comprising client deposits toward purchase of dwellings and parking spaces, recognized as sales revenue only upon handover to buyers; this balance depends on the relationship between sales pace (increasing it) and handover pace (reducing it). Interest-bearing debt stood at PLN 744.3M, of which only 28% was short-term. Dividend from 2024 profit was set at PLN 335.4M, comprising an advance of PLN 154.8M paid in 2024 plus the remaining PLN 180.6M, paid on 3 July 2025. As at end of H1 2025 the balance sheet showed a new "dividend payable" item of PLN 180.6M, resulting from the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders' resolution on 12 June 2025 distributing the Company's 2024 net profit and allocating part to dividends for Dom Development S.A. shareholders. The balance-sheet structure chart (30 June 2025) lists asset/liability categories (cash, inventories, fixed assets; short-term liabilities, deferred income, bonds/credits/leasing, trade and other payables, long-term bonds/credits/other, equity, other) without clear numeric mapping.

Selected cash flow data (H1 2025 vs H1 2024, with change): cash and equivalents at beginning of period PLN 129.9M vs PLN 34.1M (+281%); net operating cash flow PLN (32.4M) outflow vs PLN 105.1M inflow (-131%); net investing cash flow PLN 249.2M vs PLN 188.5M (+32%); net financing cash flow PLN 134.3M vs PLN 5.0M (n/a); cash and equivalents at end of period PLN 481.0M vs PLN 332.7M (+45%). In H1 2025 the Company reported a net operating cash outflow of PLN 32.4M versus a PLN 105.1M inflow a year earlier. Negative investing flows were driven mainly by large payments of trade payables (reflecting the balance-sheet decrease in trade, service, tax and other payables) and higher-than-prior-year spending on acquisition of real estate for future development projects. The difference in net investing cash flow (PLN 249.2M inflow in H1 2025 vs PLN 188.5M a year earlier) stemmed chiefly from PLN 299.9M in dividend inflows in H1 2025, PLN 144.1M more than the comparative period; concurrently, net flows on loans granted were negative at PLN (54.2M) versus a net inflow of PLN 16.0M in the comparative period. Net financing inflow of PLN 134.3M (vs PLN 5.0M outflow a year earlier) was driven mainly by inflows of PLN 135.0M from a new debt securities issue. During H1 2025 the Company's cash position rose net by PLN 351.1M, reaching PLN 481.0M as at 30 June 2025.

Company profitability ratios (formula; H1 2025 vs H1 2024): gross sales margin (gross profit on sales / net sales revenue) 32.9% vs 28.6%; operating (EBIT) margin (operating profit / net sales revenue) 14.9% vs 18.1%; net margin (net profit / net sales revenue) 76.3% vs 31.8%; ROE (trailing-12-month net profit / equity at start of period) 32.6% vs 32.8%; ROA (trailing-12-month net profit / total assets at start of period) 13.6% vs 13.2%. The Management Board assesses the 32.9% gross sales margin as very good, its year-over-year increase reflecting strong competence in reading market trends, executing sales policy, cost control, and operational process optimization. The decline in operating margin to 14.9% resulted from lower sales revenue (per section 3.3.1) with selling and general administrative costs held stable, as those costs do not depend directly on the number of dwellings handed over. The significant rise in net margin to 76.3% from 31.8% was largely due to the high value of dividends received from subsidiaries during the period (per section 3.3.3). ROE remained high at 32.6%, calculated as trailing-12-month net profit relative to equity at the start of that period (i.e., as at 30 June 2024); ROA, calculated analogously, was 13.6%. The Board notes that interim ROE and ROA based on trailing-12-month net profit may be distorted and should be assessed by dynamics and over full financial years (1 January–31 December) per section 3.3.1.
