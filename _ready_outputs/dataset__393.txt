XTB S.A. Group 2025 Consolidated Annual Report excerpt covering sustainability (ESG) governance, oversight, and non-financial reporting process. Non-financial reporting reports to the Management Board Member for Finance, the highest decision-making authority and Board representative in the process. In 2024, during the Group's double materiality assessment, XTB identified an opportunity from improved reporting quality (strengthening valuable stakeholder relations) and a legal/reporting risk that could materialize from failure to adapt to a highly volatile legislative environment and improper fulfillment of reporting obligations; risk is mitigated via early/comprehensive preparation, legislative-change monitoring, cooperation with qualified staff and external advisors, and training. Data verification operates in stages: first link, named employees of subsidiaries, branches, and XTB S.A. departments collecting/verifying completeness and correctness and supplying timely, quality data and audit evidence; second link, the Sustainability Team (a unit of XTB S.A.'s Finance, Accounting and Administration Department) consolidating and verifying data; followed by sustainability assurance (attestation). For carbon footprint calculation, external advisor Fundacja Climate&Strategy provides an additional reliability/accuracy assurance layer.

Comparability (BP-2): In 2025 no change occurred in data collection, aggregation, or presentation methods versus base year 2024, except recalculation of 2024 greenhouse gas emissions (Section 2.1.3). Where data for GHG emissions calculation could not be obtained, an estimation method was applied (described in 2.1.3). The Group did not depart from the medium- and long-term time horizons defined in ESRS 1 section 6.4. Metrics include value-chain data, partly estimated using indirect sources. Despite estimated data in carbon footprint calculations, the Group does not identify metrics or monetary amounts based on a high degree of uncertainty. Proposed methodologies and time horizons enable consistent treatment of quantitative and qualitative metrics in future reporting periods.

Sustainability reporting management (GOV-5): The Sustainability Team, within the Finance, Accounting and Administration Department of XTB S.A., is responsible for preparing the Group's annual sustainability statement; its location within the finance function deliberately supports coherence and integration with the Group's financial reporting. The non-financial reporting project is managed by the Sustainability Manager, who engages appropriate organizational resources, is responsible for collecting and analyzing data from all Group organizational units, and works closely with the advisor and auditor. Progress, opportunities, and challenges are reported on an ongoing basis to the Management Board Member for Finance, the highest decision-making link. Board members can broaden sustainability knowledge through training, the Sustainability Team's expertise, and external experts in GHG management and calculation. Competence development in 2025: the Management Board Member for Finance completed two courses on sustainability reporting standards, requirements and application examples, and on sustainability reporting and its analysis, delivered by the Education Center of the Polish Chamber of Statutory Auditors (Polska Izba Biegłych Rewidentów); one member of the XTB S.A. Supervisory Board completed ACCA training "ESG in Finance. Due diligence in reporting environmental topics in ESRS."

Governance and oversight (GOV-1, Section 1.2): This part presents the composition of governing/supervisory bodies of XTB S.A., the parent entity for branches and subsidiaries, influencing their strategy and operations. The Management Board (Zarząd) holds executive function: representing and conducting XTB S.A.'s affairs, making decisions in the Company's best interest, drafting development strategy, and defining main business objectives. In 2025 a change occurred in the Management Board composition: Mr. Andrzej Przybylski, previously responsible for the risk area at XTB, resigned as Board member; effective 01.12.2025, with Polish Financial Supervision Authority (KNF) consent, Mr. Bartosz Osiński assumed the role of Management Board Member for Risk. Full board CVs, competence descriptions, and Board operating rules appear in the Management Board's report on Group activities (chapter "Statutory Bodies of XTB"; see also Section 4.2.1). The Management Board is the highest body managing sustainability. Among Board members, Paweł Szejko, Management Board Member for Finance, was delegated to direct management and oversight (reflecting the Internal Regulations of the Finance, Accounting and Administration Department), ensuring coherence and integrity of non-financial and financial reporting through single-person management of both areas.

In 2025, two teams were established within the Division: the Sustainability Team (responsible for ESG development at XTB, ESG reporting, and ESG Strategy execution) and the Social Projects Team (pro-employee and pro-environmental actions and financial education aligned with XTB's business model and the XTB Foundation). Environmental, governance, and social criteria are considered by the Board during ongoing management activity; sustainability and impact-management topics are addressed at Board meetings as needed. Within the reporting period, in early 2026 a Board meeting was held summarizing ESG actions delivered in 2025. The sustainability area is subject to reviews: during cyclical meetings of the Sustainability Manager with the Management Board Member for Finance; within XTB S.A. Board meetings as needed; and during quarterly meetings of the XTB S.A. Audit Committee, which oversees the sustainability area.

Sustainability management (GOV-2, Section 1.3): The highest body managing sustainability is the XTB S.A. Management Board, represented in this area by Paweł Szejko, Management Board Member for Finance, delegated to manage the Sustainability Division and Team, regularly monitoring operational work, consulting, and presenting its activities to other Board members.
