XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025 strategic outlook section, structured by time horizons (short-term 1-2 years, medium-term 3-5 years, long-term 5+ years) across thematic megatrend areas. On digitalization and product development, the Company expects long-term focus on further development of mobile applications and digital platforms enabling convenient personal finance management, investing, and portfolio analysis, anticipating new business models and innovative investment products tailored to younger investor generations seeking simplicity, transparency, and rapid information access. Cites Fintech Market Statistics and Forecast 2026-2034: global fintech market valued at USD 253.4bn in 2025, projected to reach USD 938.7bn by 2034, at a CAGR of approximately 15%, underscoring growing importance of digital financial platforms, mobile apps, and automated investment solutions.

On cybersecurity (short-term 1-2 years), the Company stresses criticality of cybersecurity, modern technology, and top-tier IT systems/infrastructure protection. Per KPMG experts, 83% of companies in Poland experienced at least one attempted cyberattack in 2024, indicating high prevalence and significance of the threat. Per CrowdStrike European Threat Landscape Report 2025, European firms were targets of nearly 22% of all ransomware attacks (ransomware defined as malware that blocks access to data/devices after infecting a system, then demands ransom payment to restore access).

On legal and regulatory environment (short-term 1-2 years), changing national and European regulations require rapid response and implementations in compliance procedures, corporate governance, and data security. Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA), adopted by the EU to unify crypto-asset market rules across member states, introduces new licensing requirements for crypto-asset service providers, increases market transparency, and strengthens investor protection. Polish legal provisions remain not yet adapted to MiCA, preventing introduction of crypto-assets into the offering dedicated to the domestic market, which generates over 50% of Group revenues. Additionally, financial market supervisory authorities issue binding recommendations on conducting business; example cited is Spain, where authorities specify which financial product advertising activities are permitted. Medium-term (3-5 years): legal environment changes remain a key strategic factor; national/EU legislation may significantly determine pace and manner of implementing new technologies and scope of digital solutions, particularly in fintech, artificial intelligence (AI), and investment platforms; organizations must factor regulatory development into planning. Growing importance of regulations governing AI use in the financial sector (rules on decision-making algorithms, automated data analysis, investment-supporting tools), alongside expected changes to consumer/investor protection (increased information requirements, product transparency, greater emphasis on data security and user protection); systematic monitoring of regulatory changes integrated into strategic planning will be key. Long-term (5+ years): rising importance of regulations related to reporting, transparency, and compliance standards; maintaining highest adaptation to changing regulations, driving continuous development of internal processes, control systems, and cooperation with supervisory bodies.

On organizational structure changes (short-term 1-2 years), the XTB Group employs and collaborates with 1,516 people worldwide under employment contracts, B2B contracts, and other agreements, recognizing the importance of an appropriate workforce; at this development stage XTB decided to reorganize an operational area. Medium-term (3-5 years): focus on developing organizational culture based on efficiency and employee competencies, particularly in IT, data analysis, cybersecurity, and operation of modern investment tools; strengthening cooperation among business, operational, and technology teams; developing training programs and knowledge management systems. Long-term (5+ years): cyclical review and revision of employee/collaborator management approach to adapt organizational structure and work models to changing market and technological conditions; systematic monitoring of team competency development and organizational progress; building an attractive, stable employer brand to attract qualified specialists and support long-term development of employees and the institution.

On sustainability/ESG (short-term 1-2 years), sustainability remains a foundational element; the Company reports in line with EU ESRS reporting standards, to which XTB has adapted and will continue adapting after final standard changes are implemented into Polish law. Group activities develop the environmental (E), social (S), and corporate governance (G) areas; the Group executes its ESG Strategy goals over the 2024-2027 horizon, builds employee awareness through internal communication, and strengthens stakeholder dialogue including via the ESG website https://esg.xtb.com/. Over the coming two years the Group will summarize Strategy execution and plans to set further milestones and goals, and to verify them.
