Creotech Instruments Spółka Akcyjna (the "Company," "Issuer," "Entity") interim condensed financial report for the six-month period ended 30 June 2025, with all amounts in thousands PLN unless stated otherwise. Company details: joint-stock company (spółka akcyjna); registered office at ul. Jana Pawła II 66, 05-500 Piaseczno; registry maintained by the District Court for the Capital City of Warsaw, XIV Commercial Division; REGON 141246690. Creotech Instruments S.A. was formed via transformation of Creotech Spółka z ograniczoną odpowiedzialnością on 2 January 2012, pursuant to the resolution of the Extraordinary Meeting of Shareholders dated 22 November 2011, notarial deed Repertorium A No. 12116/2011, notary Krzysztof Borawski, Warsaw. The Company is not a subsidiary of any entity holding a full or partial interest in it; duration is indefinite. Core activities: design, manufacture, and sale of subsystems and systems used in the space industry, including satellites; design, manufacture, and sale of subsystems/systems for drone operations management and for generating, processing, or sharing satellite and drone data; subsystems/systems used in quantum computers, quantum cryptography, time-synchronization systems, and other systems used in scientific activity; and contract manufacturing of industrial electronics to customer order.

Basis of preparation: interim condensed financial statements prepared on the historical-cost principle, in accordance with EU-endorsed IFRS, primarily IAS 34 "Interim Financial Reporting"; no difference between effective IFRS and EU-endorsed IFRS for the Company's accounting policies. The interim statement of comprehensive income covers the six months ended 30 June 2025 with comparatives for the six months ended 30 June 2024; the statement of financial position covers 30 June 2025 with comparatives at 31 December 2024; cash-flow and changes-in-equity statements cover the six months ended 30 June 2025 with prior-year comparatives. Statements prepared on a going-concern basis for at least 12 months from period-end; the Management Board reviewed prior risks and identified no circumstances threatening going concern as at the approval date.

Group structure: in Q2 2025 the Company established and took control of two subsidiaries, Creotech Quantum S.A. and Creotech Geo P.S.A., forming a capital group. Both subsidiaries currently conduct minimal operations and, given absence of operating revenues, minor costs, and no employment, were deemed immaterial under IAS 1 para. 29/31 and IAS 8 para. 5 and para. 8; accordingly, as at 30 June 2025 the parent prepared only separate (standalone) interim financial statements and did not prepare consolidated group statements, considering disclosures on subsidiaries within the separate statements to satisfy the qualitative characteristics of usefulness, relevance, and faithful presentation.

Functional and presentation currency: Polish złoty (PLN); rounding differences of up to 1 thousand PLN in note summations result from rounding conventions (amounts above PLN 500 rounded up, below PLN 500 rounded down).

IFRS standards effective from 1 January 2024 and EU-endorsed: amendments to IFRS 16 Leases — lease liabilities in sale-and-leaseback transactions (issued 21 November 2023); amendments to IAS 1 Presentation of Financial Statements — classification of liabilities as current/non-current (issued 20 December 2023); amendments to IAS 7 and IFRS 7 — supplier finance arrangements disclosures; Annual Improvements to IFRS — Volume 11 (amending IFRS 1, IFRS 7, IFRS 9, IFRS 10, IAS 7); amendments to IFRS 9 and IFRS 7 — nature-dependent electricity contracts.

Published standards not yet EU-endorsed: amendments to IFRS 9 and IFRS 7 — classification and measurement of financial instruments (effective annual periods from 1 January 2026); IFRS 18 Presentation and Disclosure in Financial Statements (from 1 January 2027); IFRS 19 Subsidiaries without Public Accountability: Disclosures (from 1 January 2027); amendments to IAS 21 — lack of exchangeability (from 1 January 2025). Management assessed these changes as having no material impact on the Company's financial statements.

Accounting policy changes versus 31 December 2024. Property, plant and equipment: minimum economic useful life for "other fixed assets" raised from 1 year to 13 months. PP&E useful-life ranges: Buildings and structures 7–67 years; Technical equipment and machinery 3–10 years; Transport means 5–15 years; Other fixed assets 13 months–10 years. Inventories: prior policy used FIFO for inventory cost-of-issue except for non-interchangeable items and goods/services produced for specific projects (specific-identification method); revised policy applies FIFO or specific identification, measuring issues at the actual price of each consumed asset where project or contractual requirements dictate, with specific identification for cost of non-interchangeable items and project-dedicated goods/services. These changes have no material impact on the Company's current or prior financial statements.
