Section 7 (Additional Information and Explanatory Notes) of the consolidated financial statements of the Grupa Kapitałowa Dom Development S.A. capital group for the year ended December 31, 2025, sets out key accounting policies. Revenue recognition policy for property sales (residential units, commercial space, etc.): revenue is recognized when control over the acquired property and the significant risks and rewards incident to ownership pass to the buyer; per management's assessment, this occurs upon handover of the property to the buyer based on a signed acceptance protocol (protokół odbioru), conditional on the buyer having made 100% of payments toward the property's purchase price. Seasonality: the Group's operating activity is not subject to material seasonal effects. Determination of joint control in a joint venture: the Group exercises joint control over an entity where, together with all co-investors, it must act jointly to direct the entity's relevant activities, meaning no single co-investor individually controls the entity and none can direct it without cooperation of the others. The Group determines, based on its own judgment, the type of joint arrangement to which it is a party by considering its rights and obligations, the structure and legal form, and the contractually agreed terms.
