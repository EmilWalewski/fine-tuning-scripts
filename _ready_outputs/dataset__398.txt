XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025 Consolidated Annual Report excerpt addressing information security, digital accessibility, and consumer complaint management. The XTB mobile investment application supports password authorization and access protection via PIN code or biometrics; from 2025 clients log in using two-factor authentication (2FA). A dedicated team oversees process compliance with applicable standards, with particular emphasis on ISO 27001 (information security management system); it monitors implementation and maintenance of procedures including business continuity, conducts regular internal reviews, identifies areas for improvement, and coordinates actions required to maintain conformity, enabling effective risk management and protection of information assets. Periodic and as-needed reviews of documentation, processes, procedures, and systems are performed by external auditors against standards including ISO 9001, ISO 27001, and ISO 22301, as well as the binding DORA regulation; these tests/audits confirm compliance with international standards, effectiveness of implemented policies, procedures and control mechanisms, management system maturity, emergency and risk readiness, and care for quality, information security, and environment.

Accessibility: In line with the European Accessibility Act (EAA), an EU legal act implemented mid-2025 extending accessibility obligations to many private enterprises offering digital services, XTB undertook adaptation measures. Beyond external training for designers, developers, and testers, internal training was conducted for testers and administrative and sales structures covering accessibility implementation and testing, plus assistance and service for persons with special needs. In 2026, additional XTB employee groups will be covered by such training.

Consumer/client documentation: As a financial market entity, XTB is obligated to transparently provide clients with necessary information on cooperation terms. Its website hosts regulations and documents required when signing an investment account agreement, including: Regulations on provision of order-execution services for acquisition/disposal of property rights and securities, maintenance of securities, property rights, and cash accounts by XTB S.A. (General Conditions); Order Execution Policy (key assumptions aligned with EU directives on markets in financial instruments, organizational requirements, and operating conditions for investment firms); Regulations on preparation of general recommendations by XTB S.A. (Recommendations Regulations); Service Agreement with XTB S.A. and annexes; IKE (individual retirement account) agreement; Risk Awareness Declaration; MiFID questionnaire; and basic information on XTB S.A. Clients also have access to documents including the XTB S.A. Complaint Management Strategy, the Instruction on Filing Complaints, and other documents covering applications and powers of attorney.

Complaint channels and corrective actions (ESRS S4-1 / S4-3): The XTB Group implemented a comprehensive complaint management process to facilitate complaint submission, rapid analysis, and effective remediation of negative impacts on consumers and end users. Core principles: Transparency (clients receive precise information on complaint channels, process stage, and reasoned decisions); Speed (complaints accepted and handled promptly per established time standards to compensate losses or inconveniences quickly); Effectiveness (process addresses both effects and root causes of problems, preventing recurrence). The complaint process is a valuable information source; resolution is not the process end. Systematic reports and analyses of client submissions are prepared, enabling identification of causes and implementation of adequate corrective actions. After remediation, effectiveness is assessed and the process monitored to confirm full resolution and prevent recurrence. XTB ensures no unequal treatment of clients who filed complaints, grievances, or appeals.

Complaint volumes: In 2025 the XTB Group received a total of 9,565 complaints from consumers and end users, versus 6,816 in 2024 (+40% year-over-year). The increase is linked to client base growth (+59% YoY). Complaints received in 2025 represented 0.4% of the total 2,164,867 clients as of 31.12.2025 (-0.1 percentage points YoY). As in the prior year, the most frequently reported complaints concerned technical problems with application use and orders placed by clients.

Governance/policy context: XTB has not implemented a single unified policy addressing all material consumer-area sustainability matters; instead each material topic is covered by an independent procedure spanning complaint handling and irregularity reporting, internal control and audit, ethics, conflicts of interest, anti-corruption and anti-bribery, and anti-money laundering. Human rights matters material to clients form the basis of the ESG Strategy for 2024-2027 and the XTB S.A. Code of Ethics. During the reporting period, no cases of non-compliance with the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights, the ILO Declaration on Fundamental Principles and Rights at Work, or the OECD Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises pertaining to consumers or end users were recorded.
