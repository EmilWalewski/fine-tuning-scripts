XTB S.A. Capital Group Q3 2025 report, Section 32 (Capital Management). Group capital management governed by the parent entity's "Capital Management Policy in XTB S.A.", approved by the parent's Supervisory Board. Policy defines long-term capital objectives, current and preferred capital structure, capital contingency plans, and capital planning principles; aim is sustainable long-term shareholder value growth and prudent yet effective capital adequacy aligned with the Group's risk profile, prudential regulations, and risk appetite. Capital objectives, set on a horizon similar to the business strategy, are approved by the Management Board. Capital planning assesses current and projected regulatory and internal capital requirements versus available capital; capital contingency plans for adequacy crises are detailed in the "Risk Management System in XTB S.A." procedure. Under the ICARAP process the parent estimates internal capital to cover all material risks identified per Group procedures and stress-test results, with qualitative assessment.

The parent must maintain own funds exceeding the higher of: capital requirements per Regulation (EU) 2019/2033 (IFR, prudential requirements for investment firms, amending Regulations (EU) 1093/2010, 575/2013, 600/2014, 806/2014); and internal capital estimated per the Minister of Development and Finance Regulation of 8 December 2021 on internal capital/liquid assets estimation, risk management, supervisory review, and remuneration policy in brokerage houses. The IFR requirement equals the highest of: fixed-overhead requirement; permanent minimum initial-capital requirement; and K-factor requirement. As at the reporting date, the K-factor requirement is the highest for the parent. Own funds are computed per Part Two of IFR; rules per CRR/IFR and the parent's capital adequacy ratio procedure, not governed by IFRS. The Group currently holds exclusively highest-quality Tier I own funds.

Prudential consolidation under IFR covers subsidiaries that are investment firms, financial institutions, ancillary services undertakings, or agents. Included subsidiaries with inclusion dates: XTB Limited (UK) from 31 Oct 2015; XTB International from 30 Apr 2017; XTB Limited (CY) from 31 Jul 2018; XTB MENA Limited from 31 Jul 2022; XTB Africa (PTY) Ltd from 31 Aug 2022; XTB S.C. Limited from 31 Dec 2023; PT XTB Indonesia Berjangka from 17 Jan 2024; XTB Financial Consultation L.L.C from 30 Sep 2024; XTB Agente de Valores SpA from 11 Feb 2025. The Group is not required to maintain capital buffers under the macro-prudential supervision act.
