Non-pay benefits for board members and key managers include health, holiday, recreational and sports benefits, and holiday vouchers. These non-cash benefits are also available to other employees. Management Board members were remunerated under employment contracts in both 2025 and 2024. Because Management Board members have non-compete agreements, Paweł Szejko is entitled after termination of employment to compensation equal to 50% of gross remuneration received before termination, for the non-compete period, paid in 12 monthly installments.

The internal control and risk management system for preparing standalone and consolidated financial statements reports directly to the Management Board of the parent company. Financial statements are prepared by the Accounting Team within the Finance, Accounting and Administration Department under the supervision of the Chief Accountant, while the Finance Director supervises annual report preparation. The parent company controls and analyzes costs against financial objectives. To reduce financial reporting risks, the Group annually audits financial statements, including subsidiaries’ statements, through an auditor and continuously monitors results of individual areas against financial objectives. Annual standalone and consolidated financial statements are audited by an independent statutory auditor, and half-year standalone and consolidated statements are reviewed by an auditor. Quarterly and half-year condensed consolidated statements, annual standalone statements and annual consolidated statements are approved by the parent company’s Management Board before publication.

Documents supporting responsible corporate culture and ethics include the Articles of Association, Code of Ethics, Diversity Policy, Anti-Mobbing and Anti-Discrimination Policy, Training Policy, Anti-Corruption Policy, Work Regulations and HR Policy. The current Articles of Association were adopted by the Extraordinary General Meeting on 31 July 2023. Amendments to the Articles of Association are decided by the General Meeting and require a three-fourths majority. Amendments increasing shareholder obligations or reducing personally granted rights require the consent of all affected shareholders.

XTB’s internal regulations include standards on anti-discrimination, anti-mobbing and equal treatment, protecting against violence, harassment and unjustified dismissal. Employees and collaborators must react if they witness exclusion or stigmatization, in line with the Anonymous Reporting Procedure. XTB applies a diversity policy for Management Board members and considers professional qualifications, reputation, experience, suitability, gender, age, origin and education when recruiting company authorities. Diversity and equal treatment cover employees, company bodies and key managers, including gender, age, education, qualifications, professional experience, nationality, ethnic origin, religion, beliefs, health, psychosexual orientation, family status, lifestyle, place of residence, and the form, scope and basis of employment. The company also employs students and graduates for internships and supports employee-led charitable initiatives.
