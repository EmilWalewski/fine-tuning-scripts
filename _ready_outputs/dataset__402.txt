XTB S.A. Capital Group Q3 2025 Report excerpt detailing material litigation, administrative/control proceedings, and regulatory environment. Litigation: (1) Client claim from August 2019 (served December 2019), disputed value 7M PLN, alleging unlawful Company conduct; Management deems it entirely groundless, attributing client losses solely to client's erroneous investment decisions, as confirmed during a 2016 Polish Financial Supervision Authority (KNF) inspection, subsequent supervisory correspondence, and an independent Roland Berger advisory expert analysis of the client's transaction history, which confirmed transactions were not delayed and execution times were faster than the client average. (2) Claim from July 2020 (served November 2020), disputed value approx. 2.8M PLN, alleging XTB's failure to apply financial security measures, allegedly enabling misappropriation of funds by the plaintiff's employee who was also an XTB client; Management deems it entirely groundless. June 2023 court of first instance dismissed the claim, finding no material XTB breaches. Plaintiff appealed 22 August 2023; files transferred to Warsaw Court of Appeal September 2023, appeal served on XTB counsel March 2024, response filed 9 April 2024. Appeal hearing scheduled 10 May 2024, rescheduled to 21 June, 9 July, then 9 August 2024 when the court closed the hearing. Judgment publication deferred to 9 September 2024, 8 November 2024, finally 31 January 2025, when the judge reopened the closed hearing for the third time and adjourned indefinitely; Company awaits further resolution.

Administrative/control proceedings: Czech National Bank (CNB) inspection of Czech branch began 7 September 2023, now concluded; Company received non-final decision imposing a 2.0M CZK fine (approx. 343 thousand PLN at NBP average rate 30.06.2025) and filed an appeal. KNF inspection began 17 October 2023, verifying legal/regulatory compliance, concluded 10 October 2024 with post-inspection recommendations mostly implemented; on 16 May 2025 Issuer received notice of KNF-initiated administrative proceedings to determine grounds for administrative sanctions under art. 167 of the 29 July 2005 Act on Trading in Financial Instruments, linked to suspected breaches of EU Delegated Regulation 2017/565 and Ministry of Finance regulation provisions. KNF extended proceedings to 14 August 2025, then 14 October 2025, then 31 December 2025; conducted ex officio; Issuer cooperating and asserting no grounds for any sanction. IFSC inspection of subsidiary XTB International Limited (Belize) began 12 February 2024; recommendations implemented. CNMV inspection of Spanish branch (AML compliance) began 14 February 2024; recommendations being implemented. KAS (Head of National Revenue Administration) tax inspection of XTB S.A. began 1 October 2024, covering application of the APA-approved transfer pricing method between XTB S.A. and related foreign entities for period 1 January 2019 - 31 December 2023; ongoing, completion extended to 23 December 2025. CNMV inspection of Portuguese branch (AML compliance) began 28 February 2025, concluded with recommendations protocol to which Company submitted comments; awaiting authority response. Customs-fiscal inspection by Head of Mazovian Customs-Fiscal Office (Warsaw) initiated 28 May 2025, covering XTB S.A.'s withholding-tax payer obligations under art. 21 of the CIT Act and art. 29 of the PIT Act for period 1 January-31 December 2021; ongoing, completion extended to 28 November 2025. Dubai Financial Services Authority inspection of subsidiary XTB Mena Limited (Dubai, UAE) for AML risk assessment began September 2025; ongoing. State Labour Inspectorate (Warsaw) inspection initiated 24 September 2025, concluded 29 October 2025, covering labour law and OHS compliance for period 1 January 2024 - 24 September 2025; Company received an instruction (admonition).

Regulatory environment: Group operates in a strictly regulated environment subject to numerous international and local regulations, including: sales practices (client acquisition, marketing); maintaining capital at required levels; anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorism-financing practices and KYC client identification procedures; reporting obligations to regulators and trade repositories; personal data protection and professional secrecy; investor protection and risk disclosure for brokerage services; supervision of Group activities; inside information handling, prevention of unlawful disclosure and market manipulation; and public disclosure obligations as an issuer.
