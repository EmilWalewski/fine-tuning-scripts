KRUK S.A. Group interim condensed consolidated quarterly financial statement excerpt covering foreign-exchange risk hedging and basic earnings per share. Currency-risk exposure arises primarily from investments in subsidiaries and financial liabilities measured in foreign currency (per note 12). The Group's FX risk-management policy addresses: (a) FX risk-management objectives; (b) key Group principles for managing FX risk; (c) acceptable impact of FX risk on Group results and equity (FX risk appetite); (d) methods of measuring and monitoring FX risk and exposure; (e) procedures upon breach of acceptable FX risk appetite and defined FX risk limits; (f) FX-hedging principles; and (g) roles and responsibilities in the FX risk-management process. During 2019-2025 the Group undertook hedging of FX risk arising from translation of net assets in a foreign entity by entering into hedging transactions through Group companies, with the objective of limiting the impact of exchange-rate differences in the consolidated statement from consolidation of foreign subsidiaries. These transactions were concluded by KRUK S.A. and settled via exchange-rate differences without currency delivery; details of concluded and executed transactions were presented in the most recent annual consolidated financial statements prepared as at 31 December 2024 and for the financial year then ended. FX risk is also hedged via cross-currency interest rate swaps (CIRS), described in the preceding interest-rate-risk hedging section.
