Statement-of-financial-position items translated at the NBP mid-rate published at the end of the reporting period, equal to PLN/EUR 4.2267 at current period-end versus 4.2730 at comparative period-end. Across both rate sets, PLN strengthened against EUR period-over-period (current rates lower than comparative). The standalone statement of financial position is stated to require analysis jointly with the explanatory notes to the separate financial statements, which form an integral part thereof.
