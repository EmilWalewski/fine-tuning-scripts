Consolidated Sustainability Reporting section of the 2025 Management Board Report on the activities of Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group, covering Group Strategy and Value Chain. Group business strategic objectives (development and consolidation of operations, strong balance sheet, ROE maximization) are detailed in chapter 1.3; sustainable development is the first strategic pillar. As leader of the Polish residential market, the Dom Development S.A. Capital Group exerts material influence on spatial order and quality of life in the agglomerations where it operates, with the Management Board supporting sustainable urbanization through internal investment-planning standards spanning apartments, common estate areas, and architectural solutions including green spaces, aimed at creating livable places and high-quality urban fabric. Group sustainability actions and targets are defined by the ESG DOM 2030 Strategy. The CEO (President of the Management Board) coordinates ESG Strategy implementation and other compliance policies; Management Board Members and presidents of subsidiaries are responsible for meeting specific targets and actions. All targets relate to the core business — development and sale of residential real estate — and the strategy is executed by all Group companies. Social targets address two key stakeholder groups: customers and local communities. The strategy addresses current and future sustainability challenges; existing ESG Strategy assumptions have been met; targets respond to both organizational and industry ESG needs but do not reference international standards or third-party initiatives. Certain material impacts identified in the double-materiality analysis are indirectly reflected in the ESG DOM 2030 Strategy and ongoing operations. Emission reduction and circular-economy (GOZ) implementation minimize material risks and negative climate/environmental impacts; material social and governance impacts, risks, and opportunities are managed operationally through intra-group policies. The strategy is published on the Company's website.

Business Model: the Group's business model incorporates a management approach aligned with sustainable development principles, designed so capital resources are used responsibly and maximally efficiently while limiting adverse environmental and climate impact and accounting for stakeholder expectations. A material value-growth driver is developing and sustaining long-term relationships with Contractors per sustainability principles; the model also increases total Group value by integrating activities across companies and the value chain, raising operational efficiency via synergies and economies of scale. The Group holds resources needed for complex, capital-intensive undertakings, including: external inputs (materials, raw materials, and utilities used in construction); appropriately designed, implemented, and monitored operational and support processes; and intangible resources — highly qualified and experienced staff, unique expertise in project management, construction processes, real estate sales, and stakeholder relationship management, business and partner relationships, and IT systems and technologies. The model is strictly dependent on owned resources, the foundation of operations and key value source; project delivery requires broad access to material, human, and intangible resources enabling effective investment management and long-term growth. The Group manages resource accumulation, development, and securing comprehensively: ensuring stable, high-quality supplies of materials and utilities via reliable suppliers to minimize operational disruption risk; acquiring and developing qualified staff; investing in technologies optimizing construction and operational processes; and securing inputs through strict risk management, quality control, and supply-chain optimization to ensure operational stability. The business model and value chain deliver measurable benefits to customers (high quality and durability, timely delivery, buyer satisfaction), investors (financial stability, predictable operating results, process transparency, ESG compliance raising company value and attractiveness to long-term investors), and other stakeholders (business partners, subcontractors, suppliers offered stable, long-term, transparent cooperation), supporting development and innovation across the construction sector.

Value Chain: defined as the full range of activities, resources, and relationships tied to the entity's business model and external operating environment. Mapping of material activities, resources, and relationships was conducted in a workshop involving Group coordinators of the ESG area and the risk-management and compliance area, analyzing the business model, strategy, business relationships, and operational activities across all Group companies, mapping primary and supporting activities in delivery sequence and identifying linkages. The resulting Dom Development Group value-chain model, based on material-flow and business-relationship analysis, spans key stages — from producers and suppliers of primary raw materials for building-material production, through Contractors providing supporting services, to Group operations. Although the Group does not manage the entire value chain, it effectively shapes its key elements. Upstream relations mainly affect the Group's general-contracting (construction) activity; downstream relations affect companies handling real estate sales and servicing. Warranty service belongs to the Group's own operations but, due to subcontractor cooperation for customers, affects both upstream and downstream. The value chain's central point is the Group and its operational, management, and support processes, founded on its own workforce resources; own operations encompass management processes including organizational units coordinating individual areas, plus support processes.
