Management Board report on operations of the Dom Development S.A. Capital Group for FY2025. Consolidated net profit of the Group reached 654M PLN (2024: 569M PLN). Gross margin on sales stood at 34% (2024: 32%). The Group reports high earnings potential, supported by deferred income (przychody przyszłych okresów) of 2.0 billion PLN as at 31.12.2025 (vs 1.9 billion PLN at 31.12.2024) and net cash of 577M PLN at 31.12.2024; a separate headline figure of 522M PLN is presented. Following the strengthening of its position on the Krakow market in 2025, the Group operates across the 4 most attractive agglomerations in Poland. Demand for apartments remained at a high level, with a high share of purchases financed by mortgage loans. Construction projects are delivered on schedule with the highest quality, and the Group confirms very high quality of investments, customer service and organization, reinforced by its strengthened Krakow market position.

Market commentary: the past year was marked by falling interest rates, with the Monetary Policy Council (Rada Polityki Pieniężnej) cutting rates six times by a combined 1.75 percentage points. Combined with continued wage growth, this materially improved customers' creditworthiness and drove a revival in housing demand, particularly in the second half of the year. An important factor that had previously deferred purchase decisions also disappeared: uncertainty over announced borrower-support programs, as it became clear during the year that no new programs would be introduced. Concurrently, the primary market in 2025 was characterized by a record-high supply, giving customers greater choice and negotiating power and stabilizing prices. The supply structure was strongly differentiated by region: oversupply was visible in parts of the market, while in the largest agglomerations such as Warsaw and the Tri-City (Trojmiasto) supply remained relatively balanced and aligned with the pace of sales.

Operating conditions for development activity are becoming increasingly demanding year over year, primarily due to rising regulatory complexity. The price-transparency act (ustawa o jawnosci cen) introduced in the past year, new technical conditions, and the so-called shelter act (ustawa schronowa) currently being processed significantly complicate the investment process. Legislative changes are also accelerating market consolidation. For smaller entities, rising operational barriers constitute a significant challenge, causing some of them to withdraw from the market, while larger organizations -- equipped with adequate capital, competence and marketing resources -- adapt better to these new realities. This process leads to an increasing share of large firms in key residential markets. Market consolidation is progressing, and the Group is likewise analyzing the market with regard to directions and paths for the expansion of its activity. Headline themes accompanying the report include demand revival amid record-high supply, high results potential, and growth in retail units sold and units under construction as at 31.12.2025 (specific volume figures for retail units sold and units under construction not stated in numeric form in the text).
