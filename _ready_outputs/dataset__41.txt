XTB reports that its business processes are carried out with attention to tax compliance. The company’s core tax function is handled by the Tax Team within the Finance and Accounting Department, managed by the Tax Director and supervised by the Management Board member responsible for finance, who also serves as CFO. XTB has implemented tax governance procedures, including instructions defining responsibility for tax settlement tasks, internal organizational rules for the Finance, Accounting and Administration Department dated December 9, 2025, and procedures addressing tax scheme reporting obligations. Tax risks are identified and assessed continuously through the daily work of the Tax Team and through regular meetings with the Tax Director and the finance board member. On December 31, 2025, XTB completed a tax audit that had been ongoing since October 2024 concerning corporate income tax and the application of a transaction pricing method recognized by the Head of the National Revenue Administration.

In the area of fair competition, XTB states that no violations of fair competition rules were identified in 2025 and no proceedings were conducted in this respect.

In 2025, XTB continued strengthening a high-performance culture by reorganizing its Talent function, formerly HR, to better support a dynamically growing organization. The new structure separates responsibilities across People Operations & Enablement, Performance & Strategy, Talent Acquisition, and Culture & Engagement. This is intended to make HR processes more specialized, predictable, and scalable. A key focus is building a more mature performance management system through regular quarterly performance reviews and preparing a new bonus policy for 2026 that will be more strongly linked to actual employee achievements and effectiveness.

XTB’s employee development work focuses on strengthening managerial skills and ensuring regulatory compliance through mandatory training, while supporting leaders in building high-performing teams. The company is also developing mechanisms for early detection of performance, behavioral, and employee turnover risks in order to protect operational stability and human capital. Recruitment remains focused on high-quality hiring and equal access to processes, while Culture & Engagement initiatives support wellbeing, transparent communication, and employee engagement.

XTB is also strengthening employee communication, especially at its headquarters, which is the Group’s largest center, in response to rapid employment growth. Employees’ interests in relation to the Management Board are represented by employee representatives, direct supervisors, and the Chief People Officer, supporting ongoing dialogue.

Employees are described as one of XTB’s key stakeholder groups. The company defines its employee area as all Group employees employed under employment contracts, while its own workforce also includes people working under B2B and civil law contracts. With more than 1,500 employees and collaborators worldwide, XTB emphasizes responsibility for work-life balance through flexible and competitive employment conditions and benefits adjusted to employee needs. According to a survey from the end of 2024, 84.6% of employees would recommend XTB as a good place to work. The company also supports workplace safety through occupational health and safety training and regular first aid courses.

XTB’s double materiality analysis did not identify significant human rights impacts in the employee area, but the company states that maintaining high standards remains a priority. Employees are to be treated with respect and equality, with employment and promotion decisions based on competence. Human rights commitments are further described in the section on Minimum Guarantees and in XTB’s policy on diversity management, equal treatment, and inclusion culture. Structural changes in the Talent/HR area improved the quality of data used to calculate the gender pay gap indicator. In 2025, XTB reviewed identified material impacts, opportunities, and risks in the employee area, with no significant changes except minor editorial updates. The results were shared with all employee representatives for consultation to confirm that ESRS topics covered all material employee issues, and no comments were raised.

In 2025, XTB carried out a wide range of employee initiatives, including some in cooperation with the XTB Foundation. These included Inner Power workshops for employees to share passions and interests, sports activities such as running events, chess, badminton, office yoga, football, and basketball, as well as an XTB picnic, Children’s Day at headquarters, and a holiday meeting. Health and wellbeing initiatives included cancer prevention screenings under Pink October and Movember, first aid training, and flu vaccination at headquarters. The whole XTB Group participated in the “Two hours for family” initiative and received the title of international ambassador of the campaign promoting work-life balance. Employee volunteering activities included the Clean River campaign, beach cleaning and tree planting in Cyprus, and participation in the Szlachetna Paczka charity initiative.
