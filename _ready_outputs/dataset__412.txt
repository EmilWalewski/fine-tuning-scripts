Asseco Group Q1 2025 quarterly report (period ended 31 March 2025; figures in PLN millions), Section B commentary covering Asseco Poland's ZUS contracts and the healthcare, security, telecom/energy verticals. Healthcare e-services integrate medical facilities at regional and sector levels, enabling patient self-service via web portals and mobile apps. In 2021 Asseco established Krajowy Operator Chmury Medycznej (Chmura dla zdrowia / National Medical Cloud Operator), enabling medical units—in cooperation with Asseco Cloud—to deliver SaaS services; it specializes in cloud EDM (electronic medical records) deployments and Voicebot-based solutions such as the Virtual Receptionist. In Q1 the company launched a new product, Centralny Portal Personelu (CPP / Central Personnel Portal), successfully deployed at its first client; solutions enhance data security and enable electronic settlement of the Profilaktyka 40 PLUS program. Krajowy Operator Chmury Medycznej currently serves 300+ clients, with nearly 10 software-producing firms already using its EDM repository as an alternative to building proprietary EDM. In Q1 2025 Asseco Poland finalized acquisition of a 51% stake in Infocomp (Toruń), specializing in advanced IT and process outsourcing of hospital systems; Infocomp's 80-specialist team provides continuous comprehensive outsourcing to 13 hospitals running Asseco AMMS+ and Infomedica+ systems, and serves several dozen healthcare-sector clients in total. In the security segment, Asseco Poland is a recognized provider of tailored solutions to domestic and international institutions, having completed 70+ technologically advanced projects for EU agencies and NATO; clients include NATO, Frontex (European Border Agency), ECHA (European Chemicals Agency), EDA (European Defence Agency), ESA (European Space Agency), the Polish Ministry of National Defence, and the National Cyberspace Security Centre. In Q1 2025 it continued developing a solution enabling transfer/use of data from military CSD (Coalition Shared Data) servers directly within analytical software; the CSD_eP solution, based on the ArcGIS Pro platform, supports collection, storage, and sharing of reconnaissance data (satellite imagery, reports, maps, drone video) within the NATO coalition. Asseco strengthens its cybersecurity advisory position via ComCERT, Poland's first CERT-type advisory firm independent of any telecom operator; it analyzes client system vulnerabilities, provides incident support and post-event analysis, develops client Security Operations Center (SOC) teams, and operates outsourced SOC units for several of Poland's largest entities. ComCERT offers a proprietary software-code protection product whose technology was verified as unique by Israeli experts from TSG IT Advanced Systems. It holds ISO/IEC 27001:2022, ISO 22301:2019, and national/international Industrial Security Clearance certificates. During the period ComCERT began cooperation with Biuro Informacji Kredytowej (BIK / Credit Information Bureau), enriching BIK Alerts with darknet monitoring to detect leaked data (email, password, PESEL, ID-card number). In Q1 2025 it continued a 2-year contract concluded in 2024 with Bank Gospodarstwa Krajowego (BGK) for first-line SOC and CSIRT server/network infrastructure monitoring. ComCERT collaborates with other Asseco Group companies: with Asseco Poland on the CYBER4DE project ("Cyber Rapid Response Toolbox for Defence Use"), and as subcontractor to Asseco Data Systems maintaining CERT and SOC solutions deployed in the Republic of Togo; it is also preparing client support for the amended National Cybersecurity System Act and the EU NIS2 directive (published in the EU Official Journal 27 December 2022). In the telecom and energy sectors, Asseco is the leading Polish IT-solutions producer for the domestic energy sector via Asseco Utility Management Solutions (AUMS); over 65% of Polish energy bills are generated using AUMS Billing & CIS. Billing-system clients include energy concerns Enea, Tauron, and PGE; cooperation continues with PGNiG Obrót Detaliczny (central billing system deployed 2023). Asseco Poland continued its PSE contract for data migration to the Central Energy Market Information System (CSIRE), building the Initial Data Migration Tool using Amazon Web Services (AWS); CSIRE requires migrating data from ~300 electricity-market participants and will ultimately aggregate data from 19M+ electricity metering points. AUMS billing-system adaptation projects for energy concerns proceed on schedule. Asseco develops AUMS Market Connector 1.0 (on-premise and SaaS) integrating domain systems with external partners (CSIRE, PSG/Edi, KSeF), and the AUMS Elmo investment project—a public PWA mobile app for EV drivers presenting charging stations, managing charging sessions, and settling payments via registered cards, plus a supplier panel for tariff management (cloud and on-premise). In Q1 2025 Asseco Poland continued cooperation with Polkomtel Sp. z o.o. on a three-stage project transforming the B2C sales, service, and settlement automation systems of Grupa Polsat Plus, under a 2021 framework agreement.
