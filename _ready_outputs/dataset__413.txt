Dom Development S.A. (Warsaw Stock Exchange ticker: DOM, listed since 2006; constituent of the mWIG40 index), operating since 1996, is the leading Polish residential developer, offering multi-family apartments across four major metropolitan areas: Warsaw, the Tri-City (Gdansk/Gdynia/Sopot) region, Wroclaw, and Krakow. The Company is a joint venture of Polish managers and UK investors. Since its market debut it has combined low leverage with consistent dividend distributions, including a record PLN 335M for FY2024 (comprising a PLN 155M interim dividend paid in 2024 and PLN 181M paid in 2025), plus a PLN 181M interim dividend for FY2025.

Corporate governance: As of 31 December 2025, the Management Board comprised Mikolaj Konopka (President/CEO), Monika Dobosz (Vice President and CFO), and Board Members Justyna Wilk, Grzegorz Smolinski, and Terry Roydon. Founders actively support the Board: former CEO Jaroslaw Szanajca, holding 5.64% of shares, has served as Chairman of the Supervisory Board since 1 January 2025; Grzegorz Kielpsz, former General Director, is a Supervisory Board Member and remains a key shareholder. As of 31 December 2025 the Company was controlled by dominant shareholder Groupe Belleforet S.a r.l. (Luxembourg), holding 54.81% of shares, with representatives on both the Management and Supervisory Boards since inception.

Group expansion history: The Group has operated in Wroclaw since 2008 (projects now via Dom Development Wroclaw Sp. z o.o.). In 2017 it entered the Tri-City market through acquisition of the Euro Styl S.A. Capital Group, making Euro Styl a leader in that market. On 1 July 2021 the Company acquired a 77% majority stake in Sento S.A. (Krakow developer); in Q1 2022 its Krakow presence was substantially enlarged via acquisition of companies from the BUMA Group and the remaining Sento minority stake (becoming sole shareholder). On 1 February 2022, Dom Development Krakow Sp. z o.o. was established, consolidating operations and local talent of the acquired entities and launching new Krakow projects. Since 2010 the Group has supported buyers in obtaining mortgages via Dom Development Kredyty Sp. z o.o. The Group maintains in-house general contracting: Dom Construction Sp. z o.o. (established 2018) for Warsaw, Wroclaw, and Krakow; Euro Styl Construction Sp. z o.o. for Tri-City.

In 2024 Euro Styl S.A. entered two investment agreements to pursue development projects through dedicated SPV joint ventures, building on land acquired directly by those ventures (detailed in notes 7.1 and 7.39 of the 2025 Consolidated Financial Statements).

Strategy (1.2): The Management Board's strategic objective is long-term enhancement of Company and Group value by maintaining market leadership in residential real estate across Poland's largest metropolitan areas.

Sustainability/ESG: On 28 June 2022 the Board adopted the ESG DOM 2030 Strategy, defining environmental, social, and governance targets through 2030. Commitments include a 30% reduction in CO2 emissions per unit under construction by 2030 versus 2021 levels (Scopes 1 and 2); ensuring 90% of Group projects meet "15-minute city" criteria (walkable access to key infrastructure) by 2030; and increasing female representation on the Management and Supervisory Boards to at least 30%. The full DOM 2030 Strategy is published at inwestor.domd.pl/pl/esg. The Group emphasizes long-term relationships with employees, clients, and subcontractors, evidenced by low employee turnover and high client satisfaction (Net Promoter Score, NPS); given a development production cycle of at least three years, workforce experience and subcontractor collaboration form a competitive advantage and support nationwide brand strength.
