Interim condensed standalone financial statements of DOM DEVELOPMENT S.A. for the six-month period ended 30 June 2025, prepared under IFRS as adopted by the EU and IAS 34 governing interim financial reporting; management confirms no differences between the Company's applied accounting policies under IFRS and IFRS as endorsed by the EU. Accounting policies are consistent with those applied in the standalone financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2024, except for adoption of new/amended standards effective for annual periods beginning 1 January 2025: amendments to IAS 21 "The Effects of Changes in Foreign Exchange Rates" (issued August 2023), easing assessment of currency exchangeability, estimation of spot rates where currency is not exchangeable, and requiring additional disclosures on alternative exchange-rate determination; Management analyzed these and found no material impact on financial position, results, or disclosures. The Company did not early-adopt any published-but-not-yet-effective standard. Standards issued but not yet effective or not yet EU-endorsed: amendments to IFRS 10 and IAS 28 on sale/contribution of assets between an investor and its associates or joint ventures, resolving the inconsistency between IFRS 10 and IAS 28 (full gain/loss recognized where non-monetary assets constitute a "business," partial recognition otherwise) — EU endorsement deferred; IFRS 18 "Presentation and Disclosure in Financial Statements" (issued April 2024), replacing IAS 1, effective 1 January 2027, addressing the statement of profit or loss, disclosures on performance measures, and aggregation/disaggregation — not yet EU-endorsed; amendments to IFRS 9 and IFRS 7 on classification and measurement of financial instruments (issued May 2024 by IASB) covering recognition/derecognition dates with relief for liabilities settled via electronic cash transfer systems, SPPI assessment guidance, new disclosures for instruments with contingent cash flows, and updated FVOCI equity-instrument disclosures, effective 1 January 2026; and Annual IFRS Improvements amending IFRS 1, IFRS 7, IFRS 9, IFRS 10, and IAS 7, effective 1 January 2026. Management is assessing the impact and does not expect material effect on the interim statements when first applied. Key judgment areas, beyond accounting estimates, rely on management's professional judgment and business assumptions. Development project budgets: land/property purchase decisions rest on analyses including acquisition budgets assessing future project profitability; project budgets are updated per management's best knowledge and reviewed/updated at least every 3 months, forming the basis for inventory profitability analysis, potential impairment write-downs, and financial forecasts, annual budgets, and medium-term plans. Revenue from property sales (residential units, commercial space) is recognized when control and significant risks/rewards of ownership pass to the buyer — upon handover per signed acceptance protocol, conditional on the buyer's 100% payment of the purchase price; revenue from incomplete construction services is recognized by the stage-of-completion method, measured by costs incurred from contract date to the balance-sheet date. Operations are not subject to material seasonality. As at 30 June 2025, no circumstances required impairment write-downs on property, plant and equipment, and no collateral was established on fixed assets.
