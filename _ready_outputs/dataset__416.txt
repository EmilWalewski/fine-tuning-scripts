The Group's core activities span three segments: production, trade and services in aluminium and aluminium-alloy processing — Extruded Products Segment (SWW); production, trade and services in aluminium facade and window-and-door systems, special systems (fire doors and partitions, smoke-tight barriers), roller blind systems and roll-up gates for construction, and (following the acquisition of SELT Sp. z o.o.) sun-shading systems — Aluminium Systems Segment (SSA); and production and sale of flexible-packaging materials and plastic packaging — Flexible Packaging Segment (SOG). The KĘTY Group is an international concern employing over 6 thousand people at the end of 2024, comprising 9 domestic and 14 foreign companies, selling products to more than 4.5 thousand customers across more than 60 markets; all Group companies are covered by the consolidated statement.
