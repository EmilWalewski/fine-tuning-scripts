Currency risk hedging: Group FX exposure arises mainly from investments in subsidiaries and foreign-currency financial liabilities (note 12). FX risk management policy covers objectives, key management principles, acceptable impact on Group results and equity (FX risk appetite), measurement and monitoring methods, procedures for breaches of appetite/limits, hedging principles, and roles/responsibilities. In 2019-2025 the Group undertook actions to hedge FX risk from translation of net assets of a foreign operation via hedging transactions concluded by Group companies, aiming to limit FX-difference impact in the consolidated statement from consolidation of foreign subsidiaries; transactions were concluded by KRUK S.A. and settled via FX differences without currency delivery (details disclosed in last annual consolidated financial statements as at 31 December 2024). Hedging also includes cross-currency interest rate swaps (CIRS), described under interest-rate risk hedging. Hedging instruments classified as hedges of future cash flows and hedges of net investment in foreign operation; instrument types IRS and CIRS.
