XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025; workforce/ESG (ESRS S1) disclosures. Group executed 2024-2027 ESG Strategy (updated 2024 via stakeholder opinion survey within double materiality assessment); strategic targets set with HR-area staff who report progress to Sustainable Development Team. Internal communication strengthened via bilingual messaging, intranet, employee newsletter "xPress," "Town Hall" meetings, and internal mailing; CEO communicates financial results and strategic plans during recurring online meetings; organizational culture reinforced by integration events. Double materiality assessment (ESRS 2 SBM-3, chapter 1.6 Statements) identified material impacts, risks, opportunities for own workforce: positive impacts (employment flexibility, cooperation with employee representatives, implemented HR policies/regulations); negative actual/potential impacts (pay gap, insufficiently developed communication lagging Group growth). The pay gap, first calculated for XTB Group in 2024, drove enhanced internal communication and reinforcement of the remuneration area, including establishment of a dedicated remuneration organizational unit.

Headcount: as of 31.12.2025 Group employment was male-dominated: 791 men vs 385 women plus 1 person not identifying as either gender. 1,177 employees held permanent (indefinite-term) employment contracts, representing 90% of Group employment, up 29% y/y (growth dynamic close to total headcount growth). Fixed-term employees rose +35% y/y, 5 p.p. above overall employment growth dynamic. Reported figures include +30%, 100%, and totals 668, 877; subcategory changes +23%, +49%, bz. (no change), +26%.
