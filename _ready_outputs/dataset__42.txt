Grupa Kapitałowa Grupa Kęty S.A. reported Q3 2025 revenue from contracts with customers of PLN 1,447 million, up from PLN 1,356 million in Q3 2024. For the first nine months of 2025, revenue was PLN 4,243 million versus PLN 3,896 million in the comparable 2024 period. Total operating costs were PLN 1,215 million in Q3 2025 and PLN 3,611 million for nine months 2025, compared with PLN 1,158 million and PLN 3,326 million respectively in 2024.

Profit from sales reached PLN 232 million in Q3 2025 and PLN 632 million for nine months 2025, compared with PLN 198 million and PLN 570 million in the prior-year periods. Operating profit was PLN 234 million in Q3 2025 and PLN 639 million for nine months 2025, versus PLN 200 million and PLN 579 million in 2024. Profit before tax was PLN 215 million for Q3 2025 and PLN 581 million for nine months 2025. Net profit from continuing operations was PLN 176 million in Q3 2025 and PLN 467 million for nine months 2025, compared with PLN 148 million and PLN 465 million in 2024. Net profit attributable to shareholders of the parent was PLN 176 million in Q3 2025 and PLN 466 million for nine months 2025. Basic earnings per share were PLN 17.96 in Q3 2025 and PLN 47.51 for nine months 2025.

Total comprehensive income was PLN 178 million in Q3 2025 and PLN 466 million for nine months 2025, compared with PLN 144 million and PLN 457 million in the respective 2024 periods. The group reported no discontinued operations in the periods presented.

At 30 September 2025, total assets were PLN 4,378 million, compared with PLN 4,221 million at 31 December 2024. Non-current assets decreased to PLN 2,540 million from PLN 2,573 million, while current assets increased to PLN 1,838 million from PLN 1,648 million. Within current assets, inventories were PLN 890 million, trade and other receivables were PLN 875 million, and cash and cash equivalents were PLN 70 million.

Total equity at 30 September 2025 was PLN 1,896 million, down from PLN 1,937 million at year-end 2024. Long-term liabilities decreased to PLN 1,000 million from PLN 1,227 million, mainly with long-term credit liabilities declining to PLN 832 million from PLN 1,059 million. Short-term liabilities rose to PLN 1,482 million from PLN 1,057 million, including dividend liabilities of PLN 381 million, trade and other liabilities of PLN 602 million, and short-term credit liabilities of PLN 345 million. Total liabilities and equity matched total assets at PLN 4,378 million.
