XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025 Consolidated Annual Report (section). As of year-end 2025 the Group employed 1,516 staff and associates, of whom 616 belonged to the Product and Technology Department, responsible for developing and maintaining products, proprietary technology, transactional systems, and security. The Group emphasizes social and relational capital: internal collaboration, organizational culture, and engagement-building mechanisms supporting knowledge exchange, project execution, and adaptation to market change; relational capital covers ties with clients, investors, and supervisory authorities, with client trust built on service quality, transaction security, and financial education, while investor and regulator relations underpin financial stability, regulatory compliance, and institutional reputation.

In 2025 the XTB Group acquired 864,286 new clients versus 498,438 a year earlier, a 73.4% increase. Active clients rose from 701,089 to 1,189,422, up 69.7% year-over-year.

Productive capital comprises all material and technological resources required to deliver financial and investment services, chiefly IT infrastructure, data centers, analytical systems, risk-management tools, and operational hardware, ensuring smooth operational processes, rapid order processing, high service availability, and deployment of new products and fintech solutions.

Strategic investments: the Group identified three priority areas requiring systematic investment as the foundation of competitive advantage — technology development, infrastructure maintenance/expansion, and cybersecurity (ensuring operational stability, transaction security, and flexibility in responding to changing market conditions); product offering directed at retail and institutional clients, whose development, market attractiveness, and innovativeness affect client acquisition, user satisfaction, and ultimately Group results; and marketing expenditure, covering brand development, product promotion, traditional and digital media communication, and competitive positioning, supporting a strong client base, retention of existing users, and institutional reputation.

R&D achievements — building competitive advantages in the fintech segment via technology development, infrastructure maintenance, and cybersecurity. As a technology entity operating in the financial sector, XTB conducts continuous work developing highly innovative, comprehensive solutions in online transactions and investment in financial instruments, making it a FinTech-type organization. The aim is to develop innovative technologies and solutions enabling further development of the product offering; XTB owns a range of proprietary technological solutions.

Value chain — downstream: defined as all activities, processes, and products reaching the end recipient (the final stage where service/product reaches the client or user). In the XTB Group value chain the key end recipient is the client. For retail clients, persons actively using the mobile application and transactional platform are significant, enabling order execution, access to market analyses, and real-time investment monitoring. Institutional clients use XTB's technological solutions, systems, and liquidity support. After-sales client service — technical support, problem resolution, advisory, and maintenance of satisfaction and loyalty — is therefore important. The product offering enables clients to invest in various financial instruments such as equities, ETFs, and long-term savings products. In the sustainability context, downstream also covers responsible management of waste and e-waste generated in operations; given the nature of its business, the XTB Group does not generate significant waste, and resulting e-waste is disposed of in accordance with applicable law. Supplier relationship management is detailed in the XTB Capital Group Sustainability Statement 2025 (Supplier Relationship Management section).
