Consolidated Sustainability Report section SBM-2 "Stakeholder Interests and Views" from the Management Board Report on the activities of Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group in 2025 (Grupa Kapitałowa Dom Development S.A., page 67). Stakeholder engagement is structured around systematic identification of groups influencing overall operations and individual projects, analysis of their expectations and interest levels, selection of appropriate communication strategies and cooperation forms, ongoing relationship monitoring, response to needs, and effectiveness assessment; objectives are increased acceptance of Group actions, risk reduction, and improved decision quality. Engagement character, themes, form, and frequency differ by group, aiming at effective business management, long-term relationships, strengthened market position, and sustainable goal delivery.

Stakeholder map with engagement methods, topics, and objectives: Employees — engaged via Employee Opinion Barometer (annual satisfaction survey), direct conversations with supervisors, workshops/conferences/training, integration trips, internal intranet, newsletters, and informational mailing; topics include working conditions, professional and personal education/development, OHS (BHP) matters, current company information, new policies/procedures/guidelines; objectives: understanding employee opinions/needs, increasing engagement and satisfaction, building sense of belonging, consulting employee initiatives, informing on key issues. Local communities — engaged through cooperation with local associations on CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility) activities, organization/support of local events, and non-financial reports; topics include manner and duration of works in a given investment, CSR activities, delivery of the ESG Strategy DOM 2030, and the Green Investment Card (Zielona Karta Inwestycji, an internal Group standard published at domd.pl); objectives: understanding local community opinions/needs, conveying current investment information and clarifying doubts. Clients — engaged via direct communication with Sales Department, Customer Service Office (Biuro Obsługi Klienta), and Warranty Service (Serwis Gwarancyjny) through in-person, email, telephone, and website contact, NPS (Net Promoter Score) surveys, and social media; topics include investment and surroundings information, apartment finishing standards and materials used, delivery timelines, mortgage acquisition, purchase satisfaction, dwelling usage feedback, and tenant-requested changes (zmiany lokatorskie); objectives: high-quality service, clarifying client doubts and answering questions, offered high product quality, support in obtaining a mortgage, understanding resident opinions/satisfaction, and execution of tenant changes. Competition — engaged through participation in conferences and meetings of the Polish Association of Developers (Polski Związek Firm Deweloperskich, PZFD); topics include new industry regulations and jointly delivered urban-space initiatives; objectives: jointly developing positions on legal acts in technical and financial scope, planning cooperation, and co-building urban spaces with accompanying infrastructure. Media — engaged via press releases and conferences, analysis/assessment of current company and industry situation, and interviews given by company representatives; topics include Group activities, market situation, legal environment changes, Group financial results, corporate events, and CSR activity; objectives: conveying complete and reliable information on Group activities and answering questions, informing on investments and CSR. Public authorities — engaged through cooperation on obtaining permits and administrative decisions, fulfillment of reporting obligations, direct meetings with administration representatives, and participation in consultations; topics include industry regulations, reporting obligations, permits and requirement compliance, and ESG Strategy DOM 2030; objectives: ensuring legal compliance of operations, quality and completeness of reported documents, and conveying complete/reliable Group information. Business partners (suppliers and subcontractors) — engaged via direct cooperation and contacts on business arrangements regarding price negotiations, contracts/procedures/cooperation-establishment terms, and cooperation summaries; topics include product/service parameters, quality, valuation, cooperation terms, schedules, delivery timelines, and product certifications; objectives: establishing cooperation, setting commercial terms, work schedules and payment deadlines, and ensuring transparency and fairness of business relations. Property managers — engaged through cooperation in investment handover and direct contacts; topics include quality of conveyed investment information, building usage across its life cycle, the Green Investment Card, and recommendations for the future; objectives: efficient investment handover to property managers and clarifying doubts. Capital investors — engaged via Investor Relations Department (analysis/assessment of current and periodic reports), General Shareholders' Meetings (Walne Zgromadzenia Akcjonariuszy), Investor Days, and meetings with stock-market analysts and bank representatives; topics include financial results, business strategy delivery, ESG Strategy DOM 2030 execution and CSR activities; objectives: ensuring full information on financial results, strategy delivery, actions and plans, and clarifying doubts. Credit institutions — engaged via contacts with Dom Development Kredyty; topics include regulatory compliance and compliance regarding applicable regulations and ESG Strategy delivery; objectives: ensuring full information and clarifying doubts. Insurance companies — engaged through contacts with the Legal Department and Finance Department; topics include policies and insurance programs concluded by Group companies; objectives: concluding policy/insurance-program purchase agreements and discussing cooperation terms. Non-governmental organizations — engaged via Marketing Department contacts focused on CSR activities; topics include cooperation possibilities and CSR actions; objectives: initiating cooperation and planning/delivering CSR projects. Industry organizations — engaged through cooperation on developing industry positions, contacts with PZFD, training/presentations, and direct discussions/meetings; topics include market situation, financial results, legal/technical/financial analyses, training, and legal regulations; objectives: initiating cooperation, education and knowledge exchange, and opining on draft acts and regulations within public consultations.
