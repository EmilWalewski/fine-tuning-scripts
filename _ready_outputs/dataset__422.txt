Financial assets measured at fair value through profit or loss arise from financial instrument transactions concluded with Group clients and related hedging transactions. Counterparty credit risk relates to client/counterparty default; on OTC transactions the Group applies a "stop out" mechanism (collateral being client cash deposited with the brokerage; if current account balance is at or below 50% of paid collateral blocked in the trading system, the largest loss-generating position is automatically closed at current market price). Initial margin is set as a percentage of transaction nominal value depending on instrument type, client account, account currency and balance. Additional clauses (e.g., minimum cash balance requirements) apply for selected clients. Credit quality of these assets is high with no material differentiation.
