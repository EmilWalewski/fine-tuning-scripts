Disclosure of loan guarantees, sureties, and securing pledges. On 30 October 2025 KRUK S.A. and subsidiaries InvestCapital LTD, KRUK Romania S.r.L., Prokura NFW FIZ, and KRUK España S.L.U. (borrowers) signed with a bank consortium comprising DNB Bank ASA, ING Bank Śląski S.A., Santander Bank Polska S.A., PKO BP S.A., and Bank Polska Kasa Opieki S.A. (lenders) an amendment to the multi-currency revolving credit facility agreement dated 3 July 2017 (as amended). The amendment increased the maximum facility amount to EUR 755.0M and extended availability by a further 12 months, to 3 July 2030. Under the amended agreement, KRUK S.A., KRUK Romania S.r.L., and KRUK España S.L.U. granted sureties for all borrower obligations under the revolving facility, and borrowers executed corresponding amendments to pledge agreements. On 3 November 2025 Prokura NFW FIZ and KRUK S.A. submitted declarations of voluntary submission to enforcement under art. 777 §1 pt 5 of the Polish Code of Civil Procedure up to EUR 1,132.5M, expiring no later than 31 December 2033.

To secure obligations under the 3 July 2017 revolving facility: on 22 January, 22 May, 17 September 2025, and post-balance-sheet on 28 January 2026, InvestCapital LTD and the lenders signed a Spanish-law pledge agreement over portfolios acquired by InvestCapital LTD on the Spanish market; on 31 January 2025 Prokura NFW FIZ and DNB Bank ASA (as security agent) signed a Polish-law registered pledge agreement over portfolios acquired by Prokura NFW FIZ on the Polish market; on 3 December 2025 InvestCapital LTD and DNB Bank ASA (as security agent) signed a Romanian-law pledge agreement over portfolios acquired by InvestCapital LTD on the Romanian market. As at 31 December 2025, the value of asset security established in favor of the lenders was PLN 7,366.9M.

On 20 December 2024 a non-revolving working-capital credit agreement was concluded between Prokura NFW FIZ, KRUK S.A., and PKO BP S.A. (Warsaw). The credit was granted up to PLN 40.0M, maturing 19 December 2029. To secure Prokura NFW FIZ obligations: on 8 January 2025 a surety agreement was concluded between KRUK S.A. and PKO BP S.A. under which KRUK S.A. granted a surety up to PLN 60.0M; on 9 January 2025 amendments were concluded to the financial pledge agreements over bank accounts dated 5 October 2021 between Prokura NFW FIZ and PKO BP S.A., and between KRUK S.A. and PKO BP S.A.; on 9 January 2025 Prokura NFW FIZ and KRUK S.A. submitted declarations of submission to enforcement under art. 777 §1 pt 5 up to PLN 60.0M, expiring no later than 31 December 2032; on 31 January 2025 a registered pledge agreement over a set of rights (debt portfolios held by Prokura NFW FIZ) was concluded between Prokura NFW FIZ and PKO BP S.A. The registered pledge was established up to a maximum security sum of PLN 60.0M. As at 31 December 2025, the value of all portfolios pledged to PKO BP S.A. was PLN 227.5M.

In connection with a current-account credit agreement dated 1 February 2022 (as amended) between Prokura NFW FIZ, KRUK S.A., and Bank Polska Kasa Opieki S.A. (Warsaw), to secure Prokura NFW FIZ obligations, on 12 June 2025 a registered pledge agreement over a set of rights (debt portfolios held by Prokura NFW FIZ) was concluded between Prokura NFW FIZ and Bank Polska Kasa Opieki S.A. The registered pledge was established up to a maximum security sum of PLN 120.0M. As at 31 December 2025, the value of all portfolios pledged to Bank Polska Kasa Opieki S.A. was PLN 137.1M.

On 15 September 2025 a guarantee-limit agreement was concluded between KRUK S.A. and Santander Bank Polska S.A. The limit was granted up to EUR 0.55M, maturing 30 April 2029, securing all obligations under office-space lease agreements. To secure these obligations, KRUK S.A. on 15 September 2025 granted a power of attorney over bank accounts held at Santander Bank Polska S.A., and on 18 September 2025 submitted a declaration of submission to enforcement under art. 777 §1 pt 5 up to EUR 0.825M, expiring no later than 31 December 2032. Guarantees obtained by KRUK S.A. under the bank-guarantee mandate agreement with Santander Bank Polska S.A. dated 17 December 2014 were charged against the above guarantee-limit agreement; consequently the guarantee mandate agreement was terminated.
