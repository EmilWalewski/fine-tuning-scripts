Asseco Group quarterly report for the three-month period ended 31 March 2025 (figures in millions of PLN), section B (commentary and supplementary information), covering operational activity of Asseco subsidiaries. Asseco Data Systems' (ADS) LEO leasing-lifecycle platform provides end-to-end management of leasing contracts plus add-on products (insurance, CFM fleet-management services covering fuel cards, tyres, inspections); during the period ADS deployed the new LEO 4 version at clients, modified LEO for National e-Invoice System (KSeF) integration and client-specific requirements, and ran multiple development projects. In Q1 2025 ADS subsidiary Pirios, leader of the Polish Contact Center solutions market, supported enterprises and institutions in retail-customer communication via virtual agents executing tens of thousands of business processes for major Polish firms across energy, banking, insurance, telecommunications, and public administration; Pirios also holds Smart City project experience and offers dedicated metropolitan solutions.

In Q1 2025 Asseco Cloud continued designing and deploying proprietary and leading cloud-provider solutions, delivering full support from design through implementation and maintenance, plus IT outsourcing expertise in a managed-services model, expanding cloud-computing cooperation across multiple industries and strengthening its position as a Polish-market integrator. In retail, cooperation continued with Dino Polska, comprising a contract for maintenance of key production systems, an agreement supporting implementation of a new ERP system, and strategic consulting on Dino Polska's digital transformation. Asseco Cloud continued cooperation with Carrefour Polska, providing advanced IT competencies and resources to support and develop the company's key business processes. Under cooperation with ADS, services are delivered to automotive-sector firms specializing in financial products and services, including BMW Financial Services (Leasing), providing computing power and IT competencies in a service model. Asseco Cloud is a valued public-sector supplier: in consortium with ADS it provides maintenance and development services for the New IT Insurance System (nSIU) for KRUS, and continued realization of a 2023 consortium agreement (with ADS) for maintenance and development of the Farmer retirement-and-pension benefits system. Additionally, in Q1 2025 Asseco Cloud performed agreements for maintenance and development of IT systems for the internal needs of Asseco Poland and Asseco Data Systems.

Subsidiary DahliaMatic experienced an intensive recent period, offering a broad range of Enterprise Resource Planning systems; drawing on over twenty years' experience, it strengthens its expert position in implementing, maintaining, and developing world-leading ERP systems for large and mid-sized enterprises (Oracle E-Business Suite, SAP S/4HANA, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Softlab ERP). Beyond standard ERP deployments, its product portfolio includes proprietary solutions functioning as system complements and standalone tools: Archillea, an electronic document-flow system supporting full digitization, automation, and optimization of business processes across finance and accounting, administration, production, procurement, logistics, and HR; the Carthena procurement platform with predefined purchasing-process scenarios for mid-sized and large organizations; and a proprietary sanctions-list verification system operating both as a Carthena extension and as an independent verification tool. DahliaMatic also delivers dedicated services in Web Design & Development, Big Data & Data Analysis, and DevOps, held by its Octark team, which is actively expanding into AI-based solutions, including a chat built using AWS solutions.

DahliaMatic successfully began expansion into a new, promising market — education and higher education — establishing cooperation with the Krakow University of Technology (Politechnika Krakowska) covering implementation and care of a Microsoft Dynamics 365 system, and initiating a business relationship with Kozminski University (Akademia Leona Koźmińskiego). It continued significant projects for existing clients, including maintenance and development of the Oracle E-Business Suite (EBS) for the Agency for Restructuring and Modernisation of Agriculture (ARiMR) under a 29-month contract signed in September 2023; this system holds strategic importance for ARiMR, used both for accounting-financial management and for processes related to distribution and settlement of EU aid for over 2 million farmers. In May 2024, under a framework agreement, DahliaMatic commenced a comprehensive upgrade of the Oracle E-Business Suite and Oracle databases at ARiMR.
