Supplementary notes to Asseco Group's interim condensed consolidated financial statements for the three-month period ended 31 March 2025 (figures in millions PLN), detailing structural changes across three operating segments: Asseco Poland, Asseco International, and Formula Systems; transaction acquisition details cross-referenced to Note 6.2. Within the Asseco Poland segment, the sole reported change was the acquisition of new-company shares: Asseco Poland acquired a 50.83% voting-rights stake in Infocomp Sp. z o.o. on 14 January 2025 (no impact on equity attributable to shareholders of the Parent disclosed). Within the Asseco International segment, acquisitions of new companies comprised Payten Holding S.A.'s purchase of a 51% voting-rights stake in Fawaterk for E-payments LLC on 15 January 2025 (no equity impact disclosed) and Asseco Business Solutions S.A.'s acquisition of a 60% voting-rights stake in Tax Order Sp. z o.o. on 27 March 2025 (no equity impact disclosed). A change in holdings occurred via a dilution of the interest in Asseco Business Solutions S.A. to 47.05% voting rights resulting from the allotment of shares to participants of an incentive/motivational program on 27 March 2025, with a positive impact of 3.9M PLN on equity attributable to shareholders of the Parent. A disposal and loss of control transaction within the segment: Payten Teknoloji A.S. sold 100% of its shares in Mobven Teknoloji A.S. on 11 February 2025, with a negative impact of (3.3)M PLN on equity attributable to shareholders of the Parent. Within the Formula Systems segment, acquisitions of new companies comprised: Sapiens North America Inc.'s acquisition of a 100% stake in 2397506 Ontario Inc on 20 January 2025; Matrix IT Systems Ltd's acquisition of a 70% stake in Gav Expert Ltd and Gav Systems Ltd together with its subsidiary on 4 February 2025; Sapiens Iberia S.A.'s acquisition of a 60% stake in Financieros Experimentales Serfiex, S.A. (Servicios) on 20 February 2025; and K.M.T (M.H.) Technologies Computers Ltd.'s acquisition of a 100% stake in Expim Ltd (Communication) on 18 March 2025 (all four with no equity impact disclosed). Changes in holdings within Formula Systems: Michpal Micro Computers (1983) Ltd increased its interest to 100% in Smart Form System Ltd (Formally) through the exercise of a put option on 31 March 2025, with a (0.1)M PLN equity impact; Formula Systems (1985) Ltd reduced its interest in Matrix IT Ltd to 48.14% during Q1 2025, with a (0.1)M PLN equity impact; and Formula Systems (1985) Ltd reduced its interest in Sapiens International Corp. NV to 43.50% during Q1 2025, with a (0.4)M PLN equity impact. A business combination/merger was executed: the merger of Sapiens Software Solutions Decisions Ltd., Tiful Gemel Ltd., and Sapiens Cognitive Ltd into Sapiens Technologies (1982) Ltd as the acquiring entity on 10 February 2025 (voting-rights share and equity impact reported as not applicable). A change of parent entity occurred over CloudZone Portugal Lda, transferred from Matrix I.T. Cloudzone Ltd. to CloudZone EMEA B.V. in March 2025 (100% voting rights, no equity impact disclosed).
