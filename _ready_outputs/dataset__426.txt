Asseco Group quarterly report, Section B (commentary and additional information), subsection 3.3 covering the Formula Systems segment. Grupa Michpal, supplier of HR/payroll software and related services, recorded higher year-over-year sales. ZAP Group, operator of leading consumer websites in Israel, executed digital marketing and advertising projects during the period; it built Israel's leading marketplace, where over 400 stores currently offer more than 70 thousand products. The launch of this e-commerce platform represents a key step in transforming ZAP Group's business model, enabling significantly closer cooperation with small and medium-sized enterprises and translating into higher sales volume. Additional contributors to the Formula Systems segment results: Insync Staffing, a US provider of human-resources consulting and outsourcing solutions; Shamrad Electronics (1977), an Israeli supplier of advanced security and control systems; and Ofek Aerial Photography (1987), a company focused on advanced aerial and satellite mapping services plus aggregation and processing of geographic data. During the reported period, Ofek acquired YONA Engineering Consulting & Management, a firm specializing in road, aviation, and railway engineering. Hshahar Telecom and Electricity, operating in the telecommunications sector, delivered infrastructure projects for all leading Israeli telecom companies.
