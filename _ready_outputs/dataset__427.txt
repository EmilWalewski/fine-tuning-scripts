Dom Development S.A. Capital Group reports full capacity to finance current development projects, funded via equity, debt securities issued by Dom Development S.A., bank loans, and customer advance payments on purchased units. Management aligns maturity structure of issued debt securities and bank loans primarily to development project cycle length, with emphasis on systematically replenishing the land bank for future Group projects. The Group's investment-execution capacity was confirmed in 2025: management reconciled payment of a PLN 181M interim dividend with maintaining high cash balances and low net interest-bearing debt. As of 31 December 2025, Dom Development S.A. and its Group hold substantial financial resources and strong standing among banks and bondholders, translating into high availability of attractive external financing via long-term bonds and loans. Management delivered on prior declarations to increase market diversification: further expansion into the Kraków market, maintained scale in Warsaw, and dynamic growth of subsidiaries operating in Tri-City and Wrocław; growing subsidiary share of Group sales is expected to continue. Land bank reached nearly 19,000 units in 2025, enabling approximately 4 years of sales. In 2025 the Company completed the final stage of a residential estate (397 units) for a professional institutional PRS-segment (Private Rental Sector) investor, built on land sold to that investor in 2022; 300 units were delivered in 2024 and the remaining 97 units in early 2025. Scale of in-house contracting subsidiaries Dom Construction Sp. z o.o. and Euro Styl Construction Sp. z o.o. grew alongside operations; own general contracting is expected to remain the dominant execution mode, without excluding competitive external local contractors.
