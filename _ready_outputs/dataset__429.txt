Material litigation: Flexible Packaging companies Alupol Packaging S.A. and Alupol Packaging KĘTY Sp. z o.o. imported aluminum foil from Thailand in 2021-2022, treated as Thai-origin per certificates issued by Thai state authorities. In 2023 these transactions were subject to customs-fiscal audits by the Silesian Customs-Fiscal Office in Katowice and the Pomeranian Customs-Fiscal Office in Gdynia, which—contrary to the companies' position and evidence—found the foil's EU export resulted from the producer relocating activity from China to Thailand solely to avoid anti-dumping and countervailing measures on Chinese foil. Resulting decisions imposed customs liabilities totaling 37.2M PLN plus interest of 11.5M PLN, settled within statutory deadline in 2024. The companies appealed to second-instance bodies citing selective, arbitrary evidence assessment; during 2025 second-instance decisions upheld the first-instance rulings. The companies then appealed in full to the Provincial Administrative Courts in Gdańsk and Gliwice, contesting groundless rejection of goods' origin and absence of evidentiary proceedings; appeals cover over a dozen customs decisions. The Provincial Administrative Court in Gliwice set 28 October 2025 as the hearing date for the first of the Group's several appeals.
