XTB S.A. Capital Group 2025 Consolidated Annual Report; ESG section covering consumers and end-users (relations, cybersecurity, and consumer-related policies). Content is qualitative/narrative with no financial figures disclosed in this excerpt. The Group's strategy and business model, as a provider of investment and financial services, are built on client needs and regional community expectations; client impact runs through communication quality, product offering and documentation, service level and availability, contact channels, educational materials, opinion-driven decisions, and information transparency. A double materiality assessment identified material positive and negative impacts, opportunities, and risks. Identified potential negative impacts: insufficient listening to client opinions/needs, inadequate investment knowledge and misunderstanding of loss-bearing investment products, and inappropriate communication actions. Mitigation includes continuous product-offering expansion to reach new investor groups, transparent contractual provisions, investor education (notably on leveraged financial instruments), and avoidance of greenwashing in product, app, press, and marketing communications. Product and reputational risk tied to client complaints (e.g., service outages, contract/document misunderstanding, premature risk-taking) is minimized via transparent contracts and educational campaigns. Effectiveness monitored through online client opinions, interest in XTB services, and Customer Service contact. Remediation processes available via breach-reporting procedure, contact form, and direct customer-service contact. The Group increased headcount and trains experts to sustain service quality and operational/management processes, allocating appropriate human and material resources for continuation in future reporting periods; however, no formal, measurable targets have yet been adopted in this area. An ESG strategy for 2024-2027 was adopted, defining mission, vision, and strategic development directions, though without specific numerical targets for managing impacts, risks, and opportunities; the strategy was consistently executed in 2025. Client contact spans the full lifecycle (initial prospect contact, contract conclusion, ongoing service, education, and relationship end) via website form, hotline, and customer-service department. In 2023, cooperation was established with the Association of Individual Investors (Stowarzyszenie Inwestorów Indywidualnych, SII) to strengthen investor dialogue and interest representation. Highest governance structure for client relations is the Management Board of XTB S.A. plus directors heading customer-service teams. Cybersecurity is a priority; in 2025 a managerial-area risk was identified covering criminal attempts to access client accounts/assets, unauthorized transactions and fund transfers, theft of confidential data, and DDoS attacks. The Group maintains organizational structures and cybersecurity experts producing regular statistics and reports, including for the Management Board. In 2025 it launched its first dedicated cybersecurity landing page, ran internal and external information campaigns, and designated October 2025 as cybersecurity month; employees undergo annual training with an exam covering information security, data privacy, and password security. Physical infrastructure runs through Data Centers across multiple geographic regions with industry-standard physical protection (secure access, burglar alarms, CCTV, motion sensors); centralized IT security monitoring targets malicious activity, vulnerabilities, and incidents. Personal-data change requests are always confirmed via multi-factor authentication across email, SMS, and telephone channels. Client data is encrypted per accepted industry standards; an internal GDPR (RODO) control process is conducted at least annually, with regular independent security audits aligned to GDPR and ISO 27001, plus internal/external penetration tests. Access management controls include Legal and Compliance approval, enforcing Need-to-Know and Least Privilege. In 2025, client documentation for persons with special needs was implemented (available in the Accessibility tab at xtb.com) with a dedicated mailbox dostepnosc@xtb.com; accessibility status is systematically reported via declarations to the Financial Ombudsman (Rzecznik Finansowy). No serious human-rights problems or incidents related to the value chain were recorded in the reporting year. Consumer/end-user policies (S4-1) derive from the overarching business and ESG strategies, aligned with the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights and OECD Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises, viewed from internal and external perspectives; the Management Board of XTB S.A. is the highest authority responsible for implementation. Policies and regulations cover service improvement and product management; personal-data, information, and cyber-security; and risk management and business continuity.
