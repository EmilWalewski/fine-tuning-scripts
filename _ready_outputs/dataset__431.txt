XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025 excerpt detailing shareholder rights and General Meeting (Walne Zgromadzenie) governance procedures. On pre-emptive rights: the Management Board presents the General Meeting a written opinion justifying disenfranchisement of pre-emptive rights and the proposed issue price of new shares or its determination method; a resolution depriving shareholders of pre-emptive rights requires a majority of at least four-fifths of votes. These requirements do not apply where the capital-increase resolution provides that new shares are taken up entirely by a financial institution (sub-underwriter/subemitent) obliged to subsequently offer them to shareholders to enable exercise of pre-emptive rights, or where new shares are taken up by a sub-underwriter to the extent shareholders do not subscribe offered shares. Liquidation rights: residual assets after satisfying or securing creditors are distributed among shareholders proportionally to their contributions to share capital. Voting rights: each share carries one vote; the Statute provides no voting privileges; shareholders may vote differently with each share held; a proxy may represent more than one shareholder and vote differently per shareholder's shares. A shareholder may not vote, personally or by proxy, on resolutions concerning their own liability to the Company (including discharge/absolutorium, release from obligation, or disputes with the Company), though this restriction does not apply when voting as proxy for another shareholder. Participation in the General Meeting is limited to persons who are shareholders sixteen days before the meeting date (record date / dzień rejestracji uczestnictwa). The participation list is determined from the securities-depository registry per the Act on Trading in Financial Instruments and the share register, displayed at the Company's seat for three days preceding the meeting; shareholders may request the list free of charge by email. Holders of collective accounts (rachunek zbiorczy) receive participation certificates; where the account is maintained by KDPW (or its appointed entity), holder information must be transmitted to KDPW before first issuance, and lists are delivered via KDPW participants or appointed banks. Shares may be transferred between the record date and meeting close. Holders of dematerialized bearer shares must request a registered participation certificate from their securities-account operator, submitted no earlier than after the meeting convocation announcement and no later than the first business day after the record date. Holders of registered shares, interim certificates, pledgees and usufructuaries with voting rights participate if entered in the share register on the record date. Proxies must disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest, may not grant further proxy in such cases, and vote per shareholder instructions. Convocation procedures: the General Meeting is convened by announcement on the Company website and per current-information transmission rules under the Act on Public Offering, made at least twenty-six days before the meeting. The announcement must contain the date, time, place, detailed agenda, precise participation and voting procedures, the record date, notice that only record-date shareholders may participate, and indication of where full documentation, draft resolutions, or Management/Supervisory Board remarks and the website address for meeting information may be obtained. Under the Reports Regulation, the Company must publish via current report the meeting date, time, place and detailed agenda; in case of intended Statute amendments, existing provisions, proposed changes, and where applicable a new consolidated Statute text with enumeration of new provisions; and draft resolutions with attachments material to resolutions. Shareholder initiative rights: shareholders representing at least one-twentieth of share capital may submit draft resolutions in writing or electronically before the meeting, and may demand inclusion of specified matters in the agenda of the nearest meeting, with the demand submitted to the Management Board no later than twenty-one days before the meeting date and permissible in electronic form; the Management Board must announce agenda changes promptly and no later than eighteen days before the meeting, in the manner proper for convocation. Meeting venue is the Company's seat. In 2025, General Meeting proceedings were broadcast online via a dedicated website whose address was indicated via the Company website no later than 7 days before the meeting date; during 2025 no convocation announcement included the possibility of shareholder participation via electronic communication means. The Management Board refuses information disclosure where it could harm the Company, an affiliated company, or a subsidiary company/cooperative, in particular through disclosure of technical, commercial or organizational trade secrets; a Management Board member may refuse where disclosure could ground their criminal, civil or administrative liability.
