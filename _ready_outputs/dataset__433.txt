Consolidated sustainability reporting excerpt from the Management Board Report on the activities of Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group (Grupa Kapitałowa Dom Development S.A.) for fiscal year 2025, mapping the Group's value chain across upstream, internal, and downstream segments. Upstream covers all activities performed by third parties prior to own operations: sourcing of primary raw materials, delivery of finished products, and all services required for Group functioning. The Group classifies its suppliers and producers into three tiers. Tier 1: subcontractors. Tier 2: suppliers of construction materials and finished products—including concrete, construction reinforcement, silicate blocks, insulation materials, and windows—plus enterprises providing construction-process services and equipment such as construction machinery, scaffolding, and site-facility equipment. Tier 3: producers of primary raw materials such as steel, aluminium, glass, wood, and construction ceramics used to manufacture building materials, as well as energy raw materials including gas, electricity, and vehicle fuels; water is treated as a separate raw material used in production, operational, and sanitary-installation processes. The supply chain additionally incorporates subcontractors of construction services (excavations, demolitions, casting of structural elements, masonry and finishing works) and design enterprises handling architecture, structural design, and installations design. Subcontracting extends beyond construction to support functions, including external law firms and external experts. Principal upstream stakeholders are suppliers (Tier 2 and Tier 3) and subcontractors, identified as the most frequent interaction groups and as critical enablers of the Group's operational activity and business objectives. Internal processes ("Procesy wewnętrzne") within the Group divide into operational processes and support processes. Operational processes comprise four segments: investment execution, real estate sales, credit intermediation services, and serviced-apartment operations. Investment execution encompasses land purchase, design, obtaining permits, sales, construction, arrangement and fit-out of apartments, and apartment handover. Credit intermediation and serviced-apartment operations are characterized as marginal branches of activity. Support processes include marketing, HR, administration, finance, accounting, audit, risk and compliance management, IT & Data Governance, occupational health and safety (BHP), investor relations, and legal services. Principal stakeholders at the internal-process stage are employees, equity investors, credit institutions, public authorities, and insurance companies. Downstream focuses on delivery of products and services to end recipients; investments executed by the Group are sold mainly to individual clients. Operational activity provided to apartment buyers consists of apartment handover and warranty service, performed either by subcontractors or by Dom Development's own resources. The final value-chain segment comprises waste (both municipal waste and construction/demolition waste) and wastewater. Principal downstream stakeholders are individual and transactional clients, end users (residents of housing estates created by Group companies), local communities, and property managers. The excerpt is descriptive of the value-chain structure and stakeholder mapping; it contains no financial figures, dates beyond the 2025 reporting year, or quantitative metrics.
