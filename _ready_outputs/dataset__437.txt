XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025. XTB S.A. serves as parent entity, conducting operations through 7 foreign branches across Europe plus subsidiaries located in strategic financial centers of Europe, Latin America and the Middle East. Under the MiFID II single European passport regime, XTB operates branches based on KNF authorization in the following EU member states: Czechia, Spain, Slovakia, Romania, Germany, France and Portugal. Activity is regulated and supervised by competent authorities in markets where the Group operates; foreign jurisdiction subsidiaries hold separate brokerage licenses.
