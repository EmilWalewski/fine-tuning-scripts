XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025 (ESG/ESRS Social section). XTB advanced its 2024-2027 ESG Strategy, updated in 2024 following a double-materiality stakeholder opinion survey involving employee representatives in workshops and in-depth interviews; identified actual and potential, positive and negative impacts on own workforce (disclosed under ESRS 2 SBM-3, chapter 1.6 Statements). Positive impacts cover employment flexibility, cooperation with employee representatives, and workforce-ordering policies/regulations; negative impacts cover wage differentials (pay gap) and internal communication lagging behind the Group's dynamic growth. Strengthened internal communication via bilingual messaging, intranet, employee newsletter xPress, regular "Town Hall" meetings, internal mailing; cyclical online sessions where the CEO communicates financial results and strategic plans. Pay gap first calculated for Group XTB in 2024 drove communication actions and reinforcement of the remuneration area through a dedicated organizational unit.

Working conditions (S1-10/S1-16): market-based pay aligned to competencies, engagement and results, compliant with local regulations across XTB's operating countries; regular gender pay-level analysis by department with corrective action where gaps detected. In 2025, within the restructured Talent (HR) division, a dedicated remuneration experts team was created to monitor and manage the Group pay gap, plus introduction of cyclical, Group-consistent employee evaluation. Equal-pay indicators reported for the second time (2025 Statement); methodology unchanged versus 2024; indicators not assessed by an independent expert; data quality/completeness improved without affecting year-over-year comparability.

Pay gap indicator (employees on employment contracts across XTB branches/subsidiaries as of 31.12.2025; hourly rate from contractual base plus variable components paid 01.01-31.12.2025): total remuneration (base plus variable) mean gap 29.86% in 2025 vs 31.56% in 2024, a -1.7 p.p. decline (Table 26). Distribution: mean 29.86%, lower quartile 19.96%, median 25.18%, upper quartile 29.90%; median below mean, indicating wider dispersion and larger gap toward the upper part of the pay distribution. Pay-gap reduction is a 2024-2027 strategic objective.

Total remuneration ratio (highest earner to median of remaining employees): fell 21.2% year-over-year from 15.53 (2024) to 12.23 (2025), reducing disparity (Table 27). Non-wage benefits in 2025: private medical care, MyBenefit platform co-financing, and Multisport package.
