Excerpt from XTB S.A. Capital Group Consolidated Annual Report 2025 detailing the Group's ESG stakeholder-engagement framework and double materiality assessment methodology. The report maps four principal stakeholder categories with corresponding dialogue channels, engagement examples, and objectives. Regulatory environment: supervisory bodies including KNF (Polish Financial Supervision Authority), Financial Ombudsman (Rzecznik Finansowy), UOKiK (consumer/competition authority), and local market supervisors; engagement via cyclical reports submitted to KNF across multiple XTB S.A. areas and correspondence exchange, aimed at transparent, responsive communication and fulfillment of legal/regulatory obligations. Social environment: parties interested in financial education, prospective clients, social partners, institutions supporting cultural and sporting events, universities, schools, and the XTB Foundation; engagement through the product website, investor-relations and ESG site, advertising/promotion, social media, educational activities, pro-social campaigns, sponsorship, partnerships, and HR fairs. Cited examples: stakeholder survey within the materiality study, XTB Investing Masterclass conference, XTB IT Academy, webinars/training, Confitura conference, IT job fairs, participation in the "Szlachetna Paczka" charity campaign, and cooperation with KSW MMA; objectives include understanding prospective-client preferences, tailoring the XTB offer, forming strategic partnerships, and talent acquisition. Market environment: industry organizations, suppliers, and local-market business partners; engagement via trade fairs, direct contact, advertising, industry meetings/events/webinars, and email. Cited examples: Google Finance Summit (GtFT), Wrocław letSwift x Toast meetup powered by XTB, JUG meetings in Bydgoszcz and Łódź, ngPoznan meetup powered by XTB, Devoxx Poland conference, and CocoaHeads Tricity 2025; objectives: building durable business relationships, knowledge exchange, and brand-trust promotion. Capital-market representatives: shareholders, individual and institutional investors of XTB S.A., the Warsaw Stock Exchange (GPW), analysts, and rating agencies; engagement via current and periodic reporting, transparent communication, e-AGM broadcasts (e-walne), investor conferences, investor materials, interviews and meetings with Management Board members, the IR website, partnerships, investor presentations, the Investor Calendar, and cooperation with the Association of Individual Investors (Stowarzyszenie Inwestorów Indywidualnych); objectives: incorporating shareholder guidance into XTB policy creation, adapting the reporting system to shareholder needs, and building investor trust through reporting diligence and regulatory compliance.

Section 1.6.1 covers the double materiality assessment (DMA). The 2025 sustainability statement was prepared on the basis of a double materiality analysis conducted in 2024 in accordance with ESRS standards, subjected to a currency/relevance review in 2025. The 2024 DMA was carried out in five stages. Input parameters of the original DMA process were identification of key stakeholder groups, processes, and the value chain. The study focused on Group XTB activity based on its value-chain elements; at each stage, dialogue with stakeholder representatives was conducted to identify analysis topics (stakeholder opinion survey) and to assess impacts, opportunities, and risks. Results of the stakeholder opinion survey directly shaped the current ESG Strategy, helped set development directions dedicated to environmental, social, and corporate-governance areas, and informed goals responding to identified stakeholder needs; they also drove the decision to intensify XTB Foundation activity, particularly in financial education. The preparatory study stage included review of basic enterprise information, analysis of Group XTB's business environment, analysis of current sustainability practices, mapping of the value chain (upstream / own operations / downstream), and identification and description of key stakeholders. Step 2 (cross-sectional stakeholder opinion survey) used an online survey covering Group XTB employees, XTB S.A. Supervisory Board members, XTB clients, and representatives of other stakeholders (suppliers/business partners, local communities/social partners, media representatives, shareholders and investors). Question selection and the invited stakeholder group were preceded by preparatory workshops dedicated to mapping stakeholders and the value chain regarding mutual influences with the Group. To capture the full stakeholder perspective and supplement survey research, two rounds of in-depth interviews were conducted: the first round covered selected online-survey addressees; the second round followed the E, S, G area workshops and covered key representatives of XTB S.A. branches and subsidiaries. Step 3 (preliminary double materiality analysis: assessment of impacts, risks, and opportunities) comprised a series of workshops on actual and potential impacts on people and the environment and on Group risks/opportunities tied to social, environmental, and governance issues indicated in the ESRS standard and based on Group XTB value-chain elements. Five E, S, G workshops engaged managers and specialists from key XTB areas plus advisory-firm experts, identifying ESG impacts, risks, and opportunities material from the enterprise perspective. Step 4 (methodology for selecting material topics): impact materiality assessed by considering the probability of a given impact's occurrence split by time horizon (for potential impacts) and the severity of the impact (accounting for scale, scope, and character/reversibility of negative effects). Financial materiality assessed based on the probability of a given risk/opportunity occurring split by time horizon and the scale of financial consequences to the firm upon materialization. Appropriate materiality thresholds were adopted to determine topic materiality.
