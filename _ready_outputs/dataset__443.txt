Dom Development S.A. Capital Group Management Board report for H1 2025 outlines strategic pillars of geographic diversification, ROE maximization, and balance-sheet strength. The Group is a stable, highly organized operating structure targeting growth in Poland's largest urban agglomerations, prioritizing the Kraków, Wrocław, and Tri-City markets. Dom Development S.A. remains the undisputed leader of the Warsaw residential market, holding over 10% market share for many years; the Board expects continued scale growth supported by a strong recognizable brand, attractive land bank, and long operational experience. Subsidiaries Euro Styl S.A. and Dom Development Wrocław Sp. z o.o. have materially increased operating capacity and now rank among leading local developers, with further regional scale growth anticipated. The Kraków residential market is among Poland's most significant by transaction volume and price levels but is markedly more fragmented than other major agglomerations; accordingly, the Board established Dom Development Kraków Sp. z o.o. in 2022, built on two recognized local developers, SENTO and BUMA, acquired via acquisitions completed in 2021 and 2022 respectively. The Board views the Kraków market as offering significant growth and primary-segment consolidation potential.

Balance-sheet strength: the Board prioritizes stable financial standing and liquidity across the Company and Group. Low debt combined with substantial own financial resources enables measured decision-making despite cyclical real-estate and construction conditions. Large cash reserves and access to secured credit lines provide advantage in land acquisition, which often requires rapid deployment of significant amounts. Amid rising external financing costs, solid balance-sheet condition and high liquidity are deemed a key competitive advantage.

ROE maximization: the Board pursues long-term shareholder value, principally via maximizing return on equity, through project profitability focus, maintaining Group operating scale aligned to organizational resources, and continuing a policy of regular dividend payments from generated profit.

Shareholder structure (1.2): as of the report date, the parent entity is Groupe Belleforêt S.à r.l., holding 54.81% of Dom Development S.A. shares. The structure is relatively stable; Groupe Belleforêt representatives, co-founders of Dom Development S.A., sit on both the Management Board and Supervisory Board, providing strategic and substantive support. Company shares are also held by numerous Open Pension Funds, including PTE Allianz Polska S.A. (managing Allianz Polska Open Pension Fund and Allianz Polska Voluntary Pension Fund) and Aviva Powszechne Towarzystwo Emerytalne Aviva Santander S.A. (managing the Second Allianz Polska Open Pension Fund); the combined fund stake rose to 9.70% of share capital.
