Fair value hierarchy of financial liabilities measured at fair value at 30 June 2025: Loans (Kredyty) classified within Level 2 at PLN 0.001M (1), with no amounts in Level 1 or Level 3; total Level 2 = 1. At 31 December 2024, the corresponding hierarchy table shows nil values across levels for Loans.

The Company assigns its financial assets and liabilities to a three-tier fair value hierarchy. Level 1 fair value of instruments traded on active markets derives from quotations at the end of the reporting period, with the current bid price used as the market quotation for valuing financial assets. Level 2 fair value of instruments not traded on an active market is determined using appropriate valuation techniques that maximize the use of observable market data and minimize reliance on entity-specific estimates. Level 3 applies when at least one significant input is not based on observable market data, typically unquoted equity securities. During the reporting period and the comparative period, no transfers occurred between Level 1 and Level 2 of the fair value hierarchy.

Changes in classification of financial instruments resulting from a change in the purpose or use of those assets: none occurred during the period covered by these interim condensed financial statements.

Contingent assets and liabilities (12.1): The Company discloses contingent assets at period-end where an inflow of resources embodying economic benefits is probable, and where practicable estimates their financial effects by measuring them in accordance with the principles applied to provisions. The Company discloses contingent liabilities at period-end where it has a possible obligation arising from past events whose existence will be confirmed only by the occurrence or non-occurrence of one or more uncertain future events not wholly within the Company's control, or a present obligation arising from past events for which an outflow of resources embodying economic benefits is not probable or the obligation cannot be measured with sufficient reliability. No contingent liability is disclosed where the probability of an outflow of economic benefits is remote. The Company estimates the financial effects of disclosed contingent assets based on the value of previously recognized costs it expects to recover (e.g., under signed insurance contracts) or the value of the subject matter of proceedings in which the Company acts as claimant. It estimates possible future obligations constituting contingent liabilities based on the value of claims in pending proceedings in which the Company is the defendant.

Contingent assets (12.1.1): As at the date of preparation of these interim condensed financial statements, the Company holds no contingent assets.
