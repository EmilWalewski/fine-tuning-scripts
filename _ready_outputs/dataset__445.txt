Section 11.2 addresses fair value determination and hierarchy. Disclosed financial liabilities comprise lease liabilities of 21.5M PLN (21,454k) at 30.09.2025 versus 22.5M PLN (22,522k) at 31.12.2024, with carrying amount equal to fair value at both dates; reported credits/loans are nil at both dates. For all other classes of financial assets and liabilities, fair value also equals carrying amount. The Level 1/Level 2/Level 3 fair value hierarchy tables for financial liabilities measured at fair value show no measured amounts (credits nil) at both 30.09.2025 and 31.12.2024.

The Company classifies its financial assets and liabilities into three fair value hierarchy levels. Level 1: fair value of instruments traded on active markets derives from end-of-period quotations, using the current bid (buy) price as the market quotation for valuing financial assets. Level 2: fair value of instruments not traded on an active market is determined using appropriate valuation techniques that maximize use of observable market data and minimize reliance on Company-specific estimates. Level 3: applies when at least one significant input is not based on observable market data, typically unlisted equity securities. During both the reporting period and the comparative period, no transfers occurred between Level 1 and Level 2 of the fair value hierarchy.

Regarding changes in classification of financial instruments resulting from a change in their purpose or use: no such reclassifications occurred during the period covered by this interim condensed financial statement.

Section 12.1 covers contingent assets and liabilities. The Company discloses contingent assets at period-end where an inflow of economic benefits is probable, estimating financial effects where practicable by applying the measurement principles used for provisions; such estimates are based on previously recognized costs the Company expects to recover (e.g., under signed insurance contracts) or the value of the subject matter of proceedings in which the Company acts as plaintiff. The Company discloses contingent liabilities where it has a possible obligation arising from past events whose existence will be confirmed only by uncertain future events not wholly within the Company's control, or a present obligation from past events for which an outflow of economic benefits is not probable or the amount cannot be measured with sufficient reliability; no contingent liability is disclosed where the probability of an outflow of economic benefits is remote. Possible future obligations constituting contingent liabilities are estimated based on the value of claims in pending proceedings in which the Company is the defendant.

Section 12.1.1 Contingent assets: as at the date of preparation of this interim condensed financial statement, the Company holds no contingent assets.
