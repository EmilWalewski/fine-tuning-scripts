Excerpt from the Management Report on the activities of Dom Development S.A. and its Capital Group for 2025 (Grupa Kapitałowa Dom Development S.A.), within the Consolidated Sustainability Statement, section SBM-2 "Interests and Views of Stakeholders." The section maps key stakeholder groups, their expectations, and the Group's corresponding actions; content is qualitative with no financial figures.

Property managers/operators: responsible for investing in the managed property and maintaining its sound financial and technical condition; expectations focus on execution quality, cost efficiency of implemented solutions, and care for building users. Group responses: attention to quality of materials used during investment delivery, implementation of solutions reducing building operating costs, incorporation of user needs reported by managers, ensuring safety and regulatory compliance, responding to ESG inquiries, and adapting external sustainability communication.

Equity investors: their decisions often determine project/investment financing; they seek to better understand risks and opportunities, expecting stable financial results, high profitability, and management transparency, plus the company's capacity to deliver large, capital-intensive projects. Group responses: concentration on the premium ("podwyższony") segment offering high margins at large scale, expansion of the business model into aparthotel operations to improve efficiency and optimize margins, and transparent reporting of financial results and strategy.

Credit institutions (external) – banks and investment funds: expect financial credibility, responsible project management, and minimization of investment risk, with the core aim that financing be repaid per contract and investments deliver expected returns. Group responses: ensuring financial stability regularly indicated in financial reports, risk minimization and insurance contracts for Group projects, building a strong investment portfolio with timely project delivery, and ensuring transparency and communication. ESG-focused surveys are addressed to these institutions regarding their own activities, including long-term perspective and impact on people and environment.

Insurance companies (external): cooperating with Group companies, focus on minimizing risk associated with project delivery; expect responsibility and implementation of appropriate safety and risk-management procedures to limit potential financial losses. Group responses: conclusion of comprehensive insurance policies and maintenance of relations with insurers.

Industry/trade organizations (external): support development of the construction sector and promote good practices; expect active participation in industry life, adherence to ethical and technical standards, and cooperation for sector development; they provide legal aid, training and advisory services, foster entrepreneurial attitudes, develop human capital through training, co-shape the industry image, and serve as a platform for information exchange with public authorities and competitors. Group responses: promoting sustainability actions, engagement in educational activities, supporting legislative actions, and care for industry ethics and image.

Clients (external): persons who have concluded property purchase agreements; make purchasing decisions considering business transparency and ESG/CSR actions; demand highest-quality products and appropriate complaint handling; their awareness significantly shapes ESG/CSR actions. They seek apartments in convenient locations with access to urban infrastructure, meeting technical standards and incorporating pro-environmental solutions, and expect transparent communication of purchase processes, defect reporting/repair, and warranty service. Group responses: creating an offer better reflecting client expectations, improving processes, avoiding mistaken design decisions, offering apartments across segments (popular, premium), delivering multi-stage projects integrated with urban infrastructure, and designing investments reflecting real needs. Engagement methods: surveys, in-depth interviews, ESG-focused surveys.

End users (external): reside on Group estates, use buildings designed and built by the Group, and benefit from on-site amenities; require high quality of customer service and an efficiently functioning warranty service; expect functional, aesthetic, durable, safe facilities adapted to their needs. Group responses: designing and introducing solutions reflecting residents' actual needs and preferences, raising residents' ecological awareness, introducing solutions lowering usage costs of common parts, and ensuring high build quality.

Stakeholder management process and methods of execution: the Group implements mechanisms to effectively collect, analyze, and convey information on key stakeholders' views and interests to managing bodies. Consultation and dialogue with stakeholders: meetings, surveys, workshops, and public consultations gathering opinions on the activity's environmental, social, and governance impacts, covering clients, local communities, business partners, investors, and public institutions. Analysis of collected data: stakeholder comments are organized, assessed for materiality, and combined with assessment of risks, opportunities, and impacts. Regular reporting to the Management Board and supervisory bodies: meetings with company Management Boards held at least quarterly; data on environmental, local community, and governance impacts is included in reports and presented to supervisory bodies; the Management Board receives information on potential risks linked to stakeholder actions (social protests, regulatory changes). Incorporation of stakeholder views in decision-making: information is used in setting actions and assessing impacts, risks, and opportunities when making key investment decisions. Mechanisms for submitting opinions and remarks: dedicated e-mail addresses and helplines allow stakeholders to submit opinions, complaints, and proposals; submissions are analyzed by relevant teams, with key information passed to managing bodies as concise reports. Stakeholder satisfaction and expectations research: regular client satisfaction surveys (NPS) and employee surveys (Employee Opinion Barometer) are conducted, with results discussed at Management Board level; external media monitoring (including social media) enables rapid response to potential problems. Risk management and regulatory compliance: managing bodies are informed of changes in regulations affecting development activity, such as environmental or building regulations impacting stakeholder interests; internal and external audit results on compliance are presented to supervisory bodies. Transparency in external communication: annually published financial and non-financial reports and other documents inform both stakeholders and supervisory bodies about actions related to the entity's impact on its environment.
