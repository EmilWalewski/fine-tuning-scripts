Excerpt from the Consolidated Sustainability Reporting section ("Climate risk analysis") of the Management Board Report on the activities of DOM DEVELOPMENT S.A. and its Capital Group for 2025 (GRUPA KAPITAŁOWA DOM DEVELOPMENT S.A.). Defines the materiality assessment methodology applied to climate-related impacts. Four scoring scales are specified, each rated 0-5: scale (magnitude of the impact and consequences of a given issue), scope (size of the area affected by the issue), irreversibility (whether and to what degree negative impacts can be remediated), and likelihood (probability that the issue will occur). Scale ratings: 0 None, 1 Minimal, 2 Low, 3 Medium, 4 High, 5 Absolute. Scope ratings: 0 none, 1 limited, 2 local, 3 medium, 4 widespread, 5 global. Irreversibility ratings: 0 very easy to remediate, 1 relatively easy to remediate in the short term, 2 remediable in the short term, 3 difficult to remediate or medium-term, 4 very difficult to remediate or long-term, 5 irreparable/irreversible. Likelihood ratings: 0 none, 1 very low / practically impossible, 2 low, 3 medium, 4 large / high, 5 very large / practically certain. Materiality thresholds for potential positive impact (composite of scale, scope, likelihood): Critical 13-15; Significant 11-12; Important 8-10; Informational 5-7. Materiality thresholds for actual negative impact (composite of scale, scope, irreversibility), graded by severity: Critical 13-15; Significant 11-12; Important 8-10; Informational 5-7. Scores in the 0-4 range fall below the materiality thresholds and are classified as "None" (Brak). Page reference 105.
