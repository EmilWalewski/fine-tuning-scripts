Section 12.3 (Subsequent events) of Creotech Instruments S.A.'s report for the six months ended 30 June 2025 (all amounts in thousands PLN unless stated otherwise) details material post-reporting-period events.

On 17 July 2025 the Management Board announced execution of Annex No. 1 to the contract with the State Treasury – Armament Agency (Agencja Uzbrojenia) for development and delivery of the "Mikroglob" Satellite Earth Observation System (SSOZ). The Annex changes the mechanism for advance payments made to the Company, routing them via an escrow account, enabling use of advances to finance part of previously unincurred expenses for purchase of selected components, subassemblies and services up to approx. PLN 101.54M net. Advances will be settled through deductions from delivery invoices during project execution. Combined with proceeds from the early-2025 Series K share issue, these advances should, per the Board, secure full project financing over the contract term, eliminating the need for additional financing instruments, notably any future share issue for "Mikroglob." The advance-payment change carries a cost reflected by reducing total contract value from PLN 452,586,125.44 net to PLN 451,041,409.98 net, a decrease of approx. 0.34%.

On 18 July 2025 the European Space Agency (ESA) accepted the first part of documentation under the Requirements Key Point review of the "COUNTRY AWARENESS MISSION IN LAND ANALYSIS CAMILA" ("CAMILA") project. For this acceptance the Company is entitled to remuneration from ESA of EUR 3,178,284, of which approx. 30% accrues directly to the Company and the remainder covers subcontractor compensation.

On 8 August 2025 the Board signed a contract with Italian firm Leonardo S.p.A. for assembly of electronic units within the "LISA COLD GAS MPA MPE MODULES MANUFACTURING" project (LISA MPE – Laser Interferometer Space Antenna Micro Propulsion Electronics), executed by prime contractor Leonardo S.p.A. on ESA's behalf. The value of the Company's tasks is approx. EUR 3,656,611.00, subject to minor recalculation during the project; total project duration is 67 months. The Company's scope is assembly and integration of module electronics for the micro-propulsion control subsystem responsible for precise satellite positioning within the LISA constellation. The Company's contract-manufacturing strategy concentrates on space-sector orders.

On 13 August 2025 the Board signed a contract with Teplice Observatory and Planetarium (Czech entity) for sale of a specialized sCMOS camera developed by Creotech Instruments S.A., used to search for near-Earth space objects (including space debris). This is the first commercial sale of the Company's astronomical sCMOS camera with accessories, opening a new business line that, following the ongoing demerger, will ultimately be served by the quantum company. Contract value is EUR 196,000 net, with delivery planned within 6 months of signing.

On 29 August 2025 the Boards of Creotech Instruments S.A. (Divided Company) and Creotech Quantum S.A. (Acquiring Company, Warsaw) agreed and adopted the demerger plan under art. 533 §1 in conjunction with art. 529 §1 pt 4 of the Commercial Companies Code (KSH), via transfer to the Acquiring Company of part of the Divided Company's assets as an organized part of enterprise comprising the quantum-systems segment: (i) control components and software for quantum computers; (ii) components/systems/software for quantum key distribution (QKD); (iii) time distribution and synchronization systems including White Rabbit technology; (iv) advanced digital cameras for ground applications; (v) ultrasensitive single-photon detectors on superconducting technology; and (vi) cooperation with large scientific organizations including CERN (Demerged Business). The demerger proceeds without reduction of the Divided Company's share capital. The Acquiring Company's share capital will increase from PLN 150,000.00 to PLN 435,434.70 (by PLN 285,434.70) via issue of 2,854,347 new ordinary bearer Series B shares, nominal value PLN 0.10 each (Demerger Shares), allotted to Divided Company shareholders at a 1:1 exchange ratio. Valuations as at 1 July 2025: Divided Company PLN 1,255,090,232; Demerged Business PLN 208,338,788; Acquiring Company PLN 149,820. Demerger Shares are covered by the Demerged Business (PLN 208,338,788). The Demerged Business transfers on registration of the capital increase. Demerger Shares will seek admission to GPW (Warsaw Stock Exchange) regulated market, with a prospectus filed with KNF. The Demerger Plan was published 29 August 2025 on both companies' websites; the court-appointed expert's opinion under art. 537 §1 KSH will follow.
