In 2025, XTB Group generated a net result on financial instruments of PLN 2,044.6m, up from PLN 1,800.6m in 2024. The gross result on financial instruments was PLN 2,113.3m, driven mainly by CFDs on commodities at PLN 923.7m, CFDs on indices at PLN 760.7m and CFDs on currencies at PLN 290.4m. Shares and ETFs contributed PLN 78.3m, while CFDs on shares and ETFs added PLN 60.0m. Bonuses and rebates paid to clients reduced the result by PLN 17.3m, and commissions paid to cooperating brokers reduced it by PLN 51.4m.

The gross result composition changed materially versus 2024. Spreads represented 48% of the gross result compared with 53% a year earlier, swaps fell to 25% from 41%, while market making rose sharply to 25% from 5%. Commissions and fees remained a small component at 2%, compared with 1% in 2024.

Commission and fee income increased to PLN 20.3m from PLN 12.3m. This included PLN 15.0m from retail partners and PLN 5.3m from institutional partners.

Operating income by geography totaled PLN 2,146.1m in 2025, compared with PLN 1,873.4m in 2024. Central and Eastern Europe was the largest region at PLN 1,447.9m, including Poland at PLN 1,168.4m. Western Europe generated PLN 387.4m, the Middle East PLN 182.5m and Latin America PLN 128.3m. Poland was the only country exceeding 20% of Group revenue, with a 54.4% share in 2025 versus 52.7% in 2024.

Personnel costs rose to PLN 413.0m from PLN 311.6m. Salaries accounted for PLN 355.8m, social insurance and other benefits for PLN 43.4m, and employee benefits for PLN 13.8m.

Marketing expenses increased significantly to PLN 584.9m from PLN 344.8m. Online marketing was the main component at PLN 405.0m, while offline marketing amounted to PLN 179.8m. Client contests were immaterial at PLN 0.1m.
