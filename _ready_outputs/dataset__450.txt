Capital Group interim Q3 2025 consolidated report excerpt covering related-party transactions, post-balance-sheet events, and management commentary on operational achievements and key risk factors (raw material prices, exchange rates, product demand, debt level). Totals (Razem): sales 530,108; purchases 65,541; receivables 178,182; liabilities 20,894; dividends 581,371; loan interest 1,036 (all in millions PLN per source annotation).

Post-balance-sheet events (Note 34): no other material events occurred after the balance sheet date affecting this interim condensed consolidated financial statement.

Operational achievements: high capacity utilization (80–90%) sustained across all Group segments despite weak market conditions on many markets and the summer holiday period, supported by sufficient order intake. In Q3 the Group completed contracting of main equipment and the production hall for a new investment in the Flexible Packaging Segment — a BOPP film production line at the Oświęcim plant. Hall construction and equipment manufacturing by contractors will run over the next two years; line start-up planned for first half of 2028.

Product demand (Q3 2025): all segments achieved rising order levels despite continued weak conditions. Volume sales rose in Extruded Products Segment +2% y/y; Aluminum Systems Segment +8% y/y for architectural products and +2% y/y for roller-shutter products; Flexible Packaging Segment +2% y/y. By value: Extruded Products Segment revenue -2% y/y, Flexible Packaging Segment revenue -5% y/y, while Aluminum Systems Segment achieved +20% y/y revenue growth, half attributable to longer consolidation period of Selt (integrated into the Segment in September 2024).

Exchange rates: per estimates, over half of sales denominated in foreign currencies, mainly EUR; roughly half of costs also in foreign currencies, primarily EUR and USD. Q3 2025 average EUR/PLN 4.2594, ~1% lower y/y; average USD/PLN 3.6471, -6% y/y. Given the Group's currency position, domestic currency weakening versus EUR positively affects export profitability and domestic competitive position. Group holds foreign-currency trade receivables and payables; EUR/PLN fluctuations are key.

Raw material prices: aluminum (including aluminum scrap, sheet, and foil) is the primary raw material, ~half of raw material/material costs, exposing the Group to LME (London Metal Exchange) price-change risk, which forms the contract basis. Q3 2025 average 3M aluminum price ~2,617 USD/t, +~8% in USD, +1% in PLN terms y/y. Short-term price changes can affect profitability, especially in Extruded Products and Aluminum Systems Segments, as price-change pass-through to customers is delayed (1–1.5 months in SWW, 3–6 months in SSA). Results stabilized by aluminum purchase-price hedging transactions and partial natural hedge via product quoting based on current raw material prices.

Debt level (period-end): debt of 832M PLN from long-term loans and 346M PLN from short-term loans. About 21% of loan value held in foreign currencies (mainly EUR), reflected in profit and loss via monthly revaluation of FX impact on loan value.

Forward-looking guidance — product demand: the construction industry situation (domestic and European markets receiving over 50% of consolidated Group revenue) and FMCG sales (flexible packaging ~20% of consolidated revenue) most materially affect achieving planned sales. Management assumes construction demand will hold near Q3 levels, except sun-protection products (pergolas, awnings), where Q4 demand falls cyclically by ~30–40% vs Q3. For packaging, the pre-holiday period favors increased FMCG consumption, potentially raising packaging demand by several percentage points vs Q3, subject to consumer sentiment. Seasonally in December, demand for Group products will materially decline due to planned production shutdowns at many customers and within the Group, plus the holiday period.

Forward-looking — exchange rates: given the Group's currency position, potential zloty strengthening versus EUR would negatively affect sales and margins; Group intends to maintain part of its debt in foreign currencies and continue currency-risk hedging via forward transactions.

Forward-looking — raw material prices: based on market signals of expected economic improvement, a slight rise in raw material prices is anticipated in the coming months of 2025.

Forward-looking — debt level: net debt estimated to increase by ~0.2 bln PLN in Q4 2025, driven mainly by Q4 operating results, payment of the second dividend tranche, and investment-process payments. Debt servicing cost will rise next quarter due to higher debt level. Interest rates assumed to remain unchanged or fall at end of Q4 by no more than 0.25 basis points. About 8% of company debt is based on a fixed interest rate or secured by an IRS transaction.
