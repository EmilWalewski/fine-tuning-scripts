At 30 September 2025, financial assets with cash flows exposed to interest rate risk were 7,163.7 M PLN, up from 5,800.5 M PLN at 31 December 2024, mainly current bank cash of 7,138.1 M PLN. Related financial liabilities were 3,961.4 M PLN, up from 2,710.1 M PLN, driven mostly by customer liabilities of 3,933.3 M PLN.

A 50 bp interest rate increase would raise pre-tax profit by 2.8 M PLN for Q3 2025 and 10.8 M PLN for the nine months to 30 September 2025; an equivalent decrease would reduce it by the same amounts. For 2024 comparatives, the effects were 2.0 M PLN for Q3 and 7.4 M PLN for the nine-month period.

The Group also held bonds exposed to fair-value interest rate risk. For the nine-month period, a 50 bp yield-curve increase would reduce pre-tax profit by 0.03 M PLN in 2025 and 4.6 M PLN in 2024, while a 50 bp decrease would increase it by 0.03 M PLN in 2025 and 5.3 M PLN in 2024.

Other price risk covered commodities, equity instruments and debt instruments not driven by interest rates. Financial assets at fair value through profit or loss exposed to this risk totaled 768.2 M PLN at 30 September 2025, up from 536.5 M PLN at 31 December 2024. Related liabilities totaled 154.5 M PLN, up from 74.2 M PLN. The largest 2025 asset exposures were equity instruments at 527.9 M PLN and commodities at 240.2 M PLN.

For a +/-5% price change over nine months, total sensitivity was +14.9 M PLN or -14.9 M PLN in 2025, compared with +46.9 M PLN or -46.9 M PLN in 2024. In 2025, commodities had a -76.3 M PLN/+76.3 M PLN effect, equity instruments had a +91.3 M PLN/-91.3 M PLN effect, and debt instruments had a -0.1 M PLN/+0.1 M PLN effect.
