Dom Development Group achieved new records in 2025, selling 4,448 net units, the highest volume in its history and 4% above the previous record set in 2024. Sales exceeded 1,000 units in every quarter, with Q4 reaching a record 1,232 units. Total sales value amounted to PLN 3.46bn, up 4% year on year, while mortgage-financed transactions represented 56% of Group sales. The average gross value of an apartment sold, including potential finishing, storage room and parking space, was PLN 816k, slightly below PLN 833k in 2024, mainly due to project and unit mix.

The Group delivered a record 4,228 units to retail clients, up 8% year on year, and another 97 units to a PRS investor, bringing total deliveries to 4,325 units. Revenue rose 3% year on year to PLN 3.26bn, and consolidated net profit exceeded PLN 654m, increasing by nearly 15%. Management attributed the result to higher handovers and stable construction costs. Customer satisfaction also improved, with NPS reaching 69 points.

Dom Development entered 2026 with a strong operating base and aims to keep sales significantly above 4,000 units while preparing longer-term capacity for more than 5,000 apartments annually. At the end of 2025, its offer included around 3,500 units available for sale, with sell-out time depending on the city ranging from 2.5 to 3.2 quarters, materially below the market average. Ready units remained a low single-digit share of the offer. The Group began 2026 with nearly 7,200 units under construction, 56% already sold, and a cumulative net presale backlog of 5,200 units worth over PLN 4bn, compared with under 5,000 units worth PLN 3.7bn at the end of 2024.

In 2025 the Group spent over PLN 840m on land acquisitions in Warsaw, Tricity, Wrocław and Kraków, compared with over PLN 560m in 2024. Its land bank at year-end supported construction of over 18,800 units, enough to maintain current sales levels for more than four years. Management also noted that rising regulation, including the price transparency law, new technical conditions and the shelter law, is increasing investment-process complexity and accelerating market consolidation. Larger developers with capital, competence and marketing resources are better positioned, while Dom Development is analyzing potential acquisitions.

Selected 2025 consolidated results included sales revenue of PLN 3,255.6m, up 3% from PLN 3,168.2m in 2024; finished-goods sales revenue of PLN 3,133.6m, up 7%; gross profit of PLN 1,121.1m, up 10%; gross margin of 34.4%, up 2.2 p.p.; EBIT of PLN 801.0m, up 15%; EBIT margin of 24.6%, up 2.6 p.p.; profit before tax of PLN 810.1m, up 15%; net profit of PLN 654.1m, up 15%; net margin of 20.1%, up 2.1 p.p.; and EPS of PLN 25.36, up 15%. Dom Development is Poland’s largest residential developer, operating in Warsaw, Wrocław, Kraków and Tricity through Euro Styl, with in-house general contractors Dom Construction and Euro Styl Construction, around 58,000 homes delivered over 30 years, and a Warsaw Stock Exchange listing since 2006.
