In 2025 the Company reported several significant operational developments. On 9 January 2025 it signed an agreement with the European Space Agency for the “Space Based Observation Mission (SBOM) Feasibility Study,” where it acts as project leader. The project covers phase 0-A preparation of a satellite and mission for detecting space debris from low Earth orbit, based on the Company’s proprietary HyperSat microsatellite platform. The phase 0-A contract value is about 125 k EUR, of which more than 100 k EUR is attributable to the Company. The 12-month project is proceeding according to plan, with later talks with ESA planned regarding a satellite construction contract.

The Company is also participating in the “Small Spacecraft Platforms Evolution for Zero Debris Implementation” study, aimed at defining requirements and specifications for a small satellite platform compliant with space debris reduction requirements through 2030. The work is based on HyperSat, and the Company’s remuneration is 60.5 k EUR over 12 months.

On 22 January 2025 the Company signed an ESA agreement for the “Starterkit for a base station of a private 5G mmWave network with ultra-precise time synchronization” project, again as leader. The project value is about 600 k EUR, of which ESA finances about 480 k EUR, with the Company receiving roughly 50% of the project value and ESA funding. The project targets TRL 7 and concerns ultra-precise time and frequency synchronization for 5G mmWave and non-terrestrial network infrastructure, with Microamp Solutions Sp. z o.o. as subcontractor and testing at the ESA 5G/6G Hub in Harwell.

The Company issued 475,000 series K shares at PLN 160 per share through private subscription agreements concluded between 14 and 19 February 2025, based on an extraordinary general meeting resolution of 30 January 2025. The rights to shares were admitted and introduced to trading on 12 March 2025, and the series K shares were admitted and introduced to trading on the GPW main market on 30 April 2025.

On 11 March 2025 the Company signed an ESA agreement for the “DTM Tools” project as sole contractor. The project will create DTM Tools and a web portal for drone and infrastructure users, supporting drone operation preparation, risk analysis, planning and real-time mission management using satellite communication. The project value is 1.849642 M EUR, of which ESA finances 1.479713 M EUR, and the implementation period is 36 months.

On 30 April 2025 the Company signed a subcontract with OHB Digital Connect GmbH for the ESA BLAM project, “Background Light and Atmosphere Metrology for QKD at Urban Locations.” The Company’s task value is 175.514 k EUR within a 60-month project. It is responsible for designing and producing a subsystem for measuring atmospheric light background for satellite-based quantum key distribution, including monitoring atmospheric turbulence and urban light pollution effects.

No unusual or material factors or events affecting the condensed financial statements occurred. The Company did not publish forecasts. It also did not grant sureties or guarantees during the reporting period.
