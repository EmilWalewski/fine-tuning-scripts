The Company is carrying out key R&D projects according to planned schedules, with any changes or delays reflected in project schedules agreed with co-financing institutions. Its major contract is Mikroglob, signed on 20 December 2024 with the State Treasury - Armament Agency for production and delivery of the Satellite Earth Observation System SSOZ “Mikroglob”, covering phases B, C, D and E1 under ECSS methodology. The gross contract value is PLN 556,680,934.29 and completion is due by 31 March 2027. Under this contract, task B1 was completed after approval of output documentation on 4 February 2025, giving total net remuneration of PLN 20,997,105.05. Task B2 was completed after approval on 4 April 2025, with total net remuneration of PLN 20,534,712.98. Phase C was completed on 2 June 2025, with total net remuneration of PLN 20,034,627.65, and approval of C documentation started phase D, which is now being executed.

In Q3 2025 the Company continued CAMILA, led by the Company and supervised by ESA on behalf of Poland’s Ministry of Development and Technology. The project is intended to build a constellation of at least three observation satellites based on the Company’s HyperSat platform, together with ground segment, launch and mission management. In July 2025 ESA accepted the first project documentation under the Requirements Key Point review, triggering remuneration of EUR 3,178,284, of which about 30% belongs directly to the Company and the remainder covers subcontractors. In October 2025 ESA activated two additional options for a fourth observation satellite and commercial ground-station access, extending completion to November 2029 and increasing the contract value by EUR 7,095,783 to EUR 59,021,294.

The Company also continues PIAST, focused on designing, constructing and deploying observation nanosatellites for optoelectronic imaging reconnaissance. The project is based on HyperSat, integrated in Poland by Creotech, co-financed by NCBR under SZAFIR, and scheduled from June 2021 to February 2026. The Company continues eCAUSIS with Austrian Institute of Technology and Fraunhofer Gesellschaft, developing a quantum key distribution module and cryptographic key management software. In Q3 2025 work continued on a smaller second prototype with more integrated electronics, while preparations advanced for module-pair production.

Factors expected to affect at least the next quarter include possible shifts in sales recognition because production orders and project milestones often occur near quarter boundaries. Mikroglob revenue is especially important, as only about 14% of contract value had been invoiced by the end of Q3 2025. Component availability and price changes remain relevant for advanced electronics, though the risk is mitigated through cooperation with many producers and brokers and partly transferred to clients or passed on in proprietary products. Commissioning of the production-facility investment is planned for the end of 2025 after obtaining an occupancy permit. ESA budget arrangements for 2026-2028, including Poland’s contribution expected to be announced on 27 November 2025, may directly affect future revenues of Polish space-sector companies, including Creotech.
