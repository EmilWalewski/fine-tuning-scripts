Management Board Member since 10.07.2018. The Management Board operates under Board Rules; meetings are held at least monthly at the company’s seat or, if all members consent, elsewhere in Poland. A meeting may be held without formal convocation if all members are present and no one objects to the meeting or proposed agenda items. Resolutions require an absolute majority of votes cast, with the President of the Management Board holding the casting vote in a tie. Board members may vote in writing through another board member, except on matters added to the agenda during the meeting. Resolutions may be adopted in writing or using direct remote communication means and are valid if all board members were notified of the draft resolution. Under the Articles of Association, the President directs board work, defines internal allocation of tasks and powers, may assign departments to specific board members, convenes and chairs meetings, and may authorize other board members to convene and chair meetings. If the President is absent or the position is vacant, meetings are convened by the oldest board member. Additional special powers of the President may be set by the Board Rules. Statements on behalf of the company require joint action by two Management Board members. Remuneration policy states that pay levels should not encourage Management Board members to take excessive risk to obtain variable remuneration. Variable remuneration is an annual bonus dependent on management-objective achievement and on sustainable, risk-adjusted results of individual members, the company and the group; members are informed of goals and assessment rules determining award and amount. Variable components depend, among other factors, on individual work effects with company business risk considered. The ratio of variable to fixed remuneration may not exceed 200% for each Management Board member, and for the member responsible for Risk may not exceed 100%. Payment of variable remuneration is split into tranches with long-term company interests considered. All variable remuneration was awarded in XTB treasury shares. Disposal of at least 40% of variable remuneration paid in financial instruments is deferred for three to five years, with the period set considering the business cycle, nature and risk of the business and the person’s duties. If variable remuneration does not exceed the PLN equivalent of EUR 50,000 and one-quarter of a board member’s total annual remuneration, it is not deferred; if the person’s total remuneration in the previous financial year exceeds the PLN equivalent of EUR 1,000,000, deferral applies to 60% of variable remuneration.
