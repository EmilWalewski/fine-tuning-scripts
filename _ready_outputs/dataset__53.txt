XTB has had internal procedures for selecting an audit firm since 13 October 2017, approved by the Supervisory Board and prepared with the Audit Committee. These procedures were updated on 14 April 2025 and 25 November 2025 to reflect sustainability reporting requirements and are periodically reviewed and updated. They cover the selection of an audit firm for financial statement audits and sustainability reporting attestation, including requests for proposals, evaluation and selection of offers, agreement conclusion, re-selection and agreement extension. The related policy sets rules and criteria for inviting audit firms, evaluating offers, assessing independence through the Audit Committee and choosing the final offer.

In 2024 the Supervisory Board selected PricewaterhouseCoopers Polska spółka z ograniczoną odpowiedzialnością Audyt sp.k. as the auditor for the Company’s 2024-2025 financial statements. The mandate covered reviews of standalone and consolidated interim financial statements for the six months ended 30 June 2024 and 30 June 2025, and audits of annual standalone and consolidated financial statements for the years ended 31 December 2024 and 31 December 2025. The selection followed XTB’s audit firm selection policy and procedure. The Audit Committee reviewed offers under statutory auditor regulations and internal criteria, recommended two audit firms to the Supervisory Board and indicated PwC as the preferred offer. The Supervisory Board then selected PwC after considering the recommendation.

PwC also performed permitted non-audit services based on Supervisory Board consent dated 21 February 2024, following the Audit Committee’s recommendation of 26 January 2024. Before recommending the engagement, the Audit Committee approved the permitted services and positively verified the auditor’s independence. The services included audit work on 2024 standalone and consolidated financial statements, review of standalone and consolidated interim financial statements for the period from 1 January to 30 June 2025, review of the client asset custody process as of 31 December 2024, attestation of the Supervisory Board’s remuneration report for the year ended 31 December 2024, attestation of the ESRS non-financial report for the year ended 31 December 2024, and verification of XBRL tagging of the annual consolidated financial statements under ESEF.

The Supervisory Board may appoint additional committees and determine their composition. The Remuneration Committee began 2025 with Jan Byrski as Chair and Grzegorz Grabowicz, Katarzyna Dąbrowska, Ewa Stefaniak and Bartosz Zabłocki as members. By 31 December 2025, Aleksander Chłopecki served as Chair, with Grzegorz Grabowicz, Katarzyna Dąbrowska, Ewa Stefaniak, Bartosz Zabłocki and Jakub Zabłocki as members. During 2025 the Remuneration Committee held four meetings, adopted four resolutions and recorded 100% average attendance. Aleksander Chłopecki, Bartosz Zabłocki, Grzegorz Grabowicz, Katarzyna Dąbrowska, Ewa Stefaniak and Jakub Zabłocki each had 100% attendance, while Jan Byrski was marked not applicable because no meetings took place during his term.
