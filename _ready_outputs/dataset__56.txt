Dom Development S.A.’s long-term provisions decreased to 13.225 M PLN at 31 December 2025 from 14.956 M PLN a year earlier, after 0.010 M PLN of new provisions and 1.741 M PLN of utilization or release. Other long-term liabilities fell to 24.476 M PLN from 30.432 M PLN, including long-term guarantee deposits of 6.245 M PLN and other liabilities of 18.231 M PLN.

Lease liabilities under IFRS 16 totaled 84.545 M PLN, up from 50.363 M PLN. Current lease liabilities were 46.935 M PLN, including 40.835 M PLN related to perpetual usufruct of land and 0.138 M PLN of other items. Long-term lease liabilities were 37.610 M PLN, including 0.626 M PLN of other items. Management estimated that of the 40.835 M PLN current land lease liability, 3.824 M PLN is payable within 12 months, 3.094 M PLN after 12 months, and 33.917 M PLN will be transferred to buyers of premises.

Trade, service, tax and other liabilities totaled 424.885 M PLN versus 464.969 M PLN. Tax liabilities were 1.894 M PLN, cost accruals were 231.761 M PLN, the social benefits fund liability was 0.835 M PLN, and other liabilities were 0.454 M PLN. Cost accruals consisted mainly of estate construction costs of 197.093 M PLN, employee costs of 23.142 M PLN, office rent costs of 0.306 M PLN, and other accruals of 11.220 M PLN. Trade payables are non-interest-bearing and generally mature in 14 to 30 days, excluding guarantee deposits. Retained guarantee deposits totaled 20.213 M PLN, including 13.968 M PLN short-term and 6.245 M PLN long-term, down from 28.539 M PLN.

Short-term provisions decreased to 14.965 M PLN from 19.005 M PLN. They included 4.287 M PLN for repair costs, 0.085 M PLN for retirement-related provisions, and 10.593 M PLN for litigation and other matters. During 2025, 6.504 M PLN of provisions were created and 10.544 M PLN were utilized or released. Deferred income, mainly customer payments for products not yet recognized in profit or loss, increased to 905.442 M PLN from 860.963 M PLN. The company reported no special post-employment benefit programs.

Financial assets classified as loans and receivables totaled 618.404 M PLN, including 6.542 M PLN of long-term receivables, 580.296 M PLN of loans granted, 29.288 M PLN of trade and other receivables, and 2.278 M PLN due from related parties. Cash was 6.197 M PLN, short-term deposits 62.320 M PLN, and short-term financial assets 148.153 M PLN. Maximum credit risk exposure was 835.074 M PLN, compared with 849.653 M PLN in 2024. Financial liabilities measured at amortized cost totaled 1,192.392 M PLN, including 10.000 M PLN of bank loans, 651.214 M PLN of issued bonds, 446.633 M PLN of trade, accrual and other liabilities, and 84.545 M PLN of lease liabilities. The fair value of financial assets and liabilities did not differ materially from carrying amounts.

The company is exposed to market risk, credit risk and liquidity risk. Market risk mainly covers interest-rate and foreign-exchange risk. At both 31 December 2025 and 31 December 2024, the company had no significant foreign-currency assets, liabilities or future payments, so no currency hedging derivatives were needed. Interest-rate risk arises from floating-rate loans and bonds; the company had no fixed-rate loans or bonds and no long-term fixed-rate financial instruments at the reporting date. This risk is partly naturally offset by both floating-rate financial liabilities and floating-rate financial assets, and is further mitigated for bond debt through instruments such as CAP options and interest rate swaps converting variable interest payments into fixed-rate exposure.
