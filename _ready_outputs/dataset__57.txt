In Q3 2025, XTB’s operating expenses rose to PLN 322.7M from PLN 208.5M a year earlier, an increase of PLN 114.1M. The main driver was marketing spend, up PLN 69.9M year on year to PLN 141.5M, reflecting higher online and offline campaign outlays. Employee remuneration and benefits rose by PLN 26.1M to PLN 105.2M, mainly due to higher headcount, while other external services increased by PLN 11.4M to PLN 32.0M, including higher IT systems and licence costs, market data costs, and legal and advisory services. For the first nine months of 2025, total operating expenses were PLN 931.4M, compared with PLN 618.9M a year earlier, including PLN 405.9M of marketing costs.

Quarter on quarter, operating expenses increased by PLN 29.7M, mainly due to an PLN 18.2M rise in marketing spend and a PLN 7.5M increase in employee remuneration and benefits. Management expects 2025 operating expenses could be up by around 40% versus 2024, with marketing spend potentially rising by around 80%, while maintaining an average client acquisition cost broadly comparable with 2023-2024. The final cost level will depend on hiring pace, variable compensation, marketing outlays, geographic expansion, regulation, external factors affecting revenues, and customer responsiveness to marketing activity.

XTB continued to expand its client base strongly. In Q3 2025, the Group acquired a record 221,762 new clients, up 105.1% from 108,104 in Q3 2024. Total clients reached 1,904,475 at 30 September 2025, while active clients in the quarter increased to 919,976 from 522,899 a year earlier, up 56.9%. Active clients for the first nine months of 2025 totaled 1,005,589. In the first nine months of 2025, XTB acquired 583,405 new clients, already exceeding the 498,438 acquired in full-year 2024. The average client acquisition cost in Q3 2025 was PLN 600, versus PLN 700 a year earlier, and PLN 700 for the first nine months of 2025.

Management’s priority remains further client-base growth and strengthening XTB’s global market position by reaching mass-market clients. The company aims in 2025 to acquire an average of at least 150,000-210,000 new clients per quarter. In October 2025, the Group acquired 109,401 new clients.

Marketing remains a key growth driver alongside technology development and product expansion. In Q3 2025, XTB prepared and launched in September the largest marketing campaign in its history, covering 13 markets: Poland, Czechia, Slovakia, Hungary, Romania, France, Germany, Portugal, the United Kingdom, Italy, Chile, the Middle East, and Indonesia. The campaign features brand ambassador Zlatan Ibrahimović and is being run across digital channels, social media, television, VOD platforms, and, in selected markets, radio, cinema, and traditional and digital outdoor media. Activities are being phased in and are planned to continue through the end of the year.
