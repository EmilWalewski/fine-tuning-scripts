Dom Development S.A.'s 2025 consolidated sustainability reporting describes remuneration governance and pay. From 1 January to 31 December 2025 the Remuneration Committee, a permanent Supervisory Board committee, comprised Dorota Podedworna-Tarnowska as Chair, Mark Spiteri as member, and Anna Maria Panasiuk as member. The committee has at least three Supervisory Board members appointed by the Supervisory Board, at least two independent under Statute clause 7.7; the chair is selected from independent Supervisory Board members and members may be dismissed anytime. Its public rules, approved by the Supervisory Board, cover periodic assessment of Management Board remuneration principles and recommendations, proposals for remuneration and additional benefits, including management option programmes convertible into Company shares, and draft remuneration policy submission. The CEO and HR Director attend by invitation; the committee held 3 meetings in 2025. The remuneration policy for management and supervisory bodies and key managers supports corporate strategy and short- and long-term objectives; in fiscal 2025 there were no material changes to the policy adopted by the Annual General Meeting on 31 August 2020. The policy follows Polish and European rules for public-company remuneration, aims at transparent Management Board and Supervisory Board pay aligned with business strategy, current business environment and market practice, supports long-term company welfare and interests of shareholders, investors and stakeholders, development stability, one remuneration title for all Management Board duties for the Company while allowing subsidiary roles, Supervisory Board pay adequate to company-wide and market practice, effective policy management, and conflict-of-interest prevention. Under the Statute, the Supervisory Board determines contract terms and remuneration for the President and other Management Board members after Remuneration Committee recommendation. Management Board and key managers' remuneration comprises fixed base pay, variable performance-linked bonus and non-cash benefits; in 2025 the system operated as in prior years, based on an incentive mechanism linked to Company financial results and business and financial goals, with bonuses conditional on fulfilment of individual annual goals connected with Company business objectives. 2025 remuneration due or potentially due for service in Dom Development S.A. governing bodies, in M PLN, was: Mikołaj Konopka 1.200 fixed, 2.864 variable, 0.032 non-cash benefits; Leszek Stankiewicz 1.000 fixed, including 0.750 severance connected with resignation from Vice-President under resolution no. 9/11/24 of 29.11.2024 paid by 15.03.2025, 0 variable, 0.015 benefits; Monika Dobosz 1.167 fixed, 2.845 variable, 0.013 benefits; Justyna Wilk 1.020 fixed, 1.740 variable, 0.020 benefits; Grzegorz Smoliński 1.020 fixed, 1.660 variable, 0.022 benefits; Terry Roydon 0.072 fixed, 2.509 variable and no benefits. Variable pay was based on estimated 2025 bonus calculations before approval of the financial statements used for calculation. The Company had no pension or similar-benefit liabilities for former managing or supervising persons.
