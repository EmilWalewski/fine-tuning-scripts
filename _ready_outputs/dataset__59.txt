XTB S.A. consolidated annual report 2025 excerpt describes XTB S.A. operating through 7 foreign branches in Europe and pursuing international investment-services leadership through mass-client acquisition. Final operating expense levels depend mainly on employment growth, variable employee remuneration, marketing expenditure, geographic expansion into new markets, and potential effects of new regulations and other external factors on Group revenue. Management justifies higher costs by the need to compete for a leading position in the international investment services market; competition is viewed not only locally but against global entities. Product-offer expansion and access to new client groups broadened the competitive set beyond entities mainly offering CFDs, while XTB Group increased its share of the overall financial-instruments market. Brand reach is supported by wide online and offline marketing to current and potential clients. XTB ambassadors listed include Mads Mikkelsen, ambassador in 2016-2018 and multiple Best Actor award winner at European Film Festival and Cannes Film Festival competitions; Jose Mourinho, ambassador in 2019-2020, Portuguese former footballer and coach of Chelsea, Inter Milan, Real Madrid, Manchester United and AS Roma; Jiri Prochazka, ambassador in 2022, Czech MMA fighter and UFC light-heavyweight champion; Joanna Jedrzejczyk, ambassador in 2022-2023, one of the most successful MMA fighters and multiple UFC champion; Conor McGregor, ambassador in 2022-2024, Irish MMA fighter and two-time UFC champion; Iker Casillas, ambassador in 2022-2024, Spanish former footballer, three-time FIFA Golden Glove winner, 2010 FIFA World Cup champion with Spain and UEFA Champions League Golden Cup winner with Real Madrid in 2000 and 2002; and Zlatan Ibrahimovic, ambassador in 2024-2026, Swedish former footballer, one of modern footballs leading strikers and 12-time Swedish Footballer of the Year. In 2025 XTB Group spent nearly 585M PLN on marketing, including targeted advertising campaigns in Poland and abroad, up 69.6% year on year. Management estimates 2026 marketing expenditure may rise by about 50% versus the prior year, assuming average customer acquisition cost remains comparable with 2023-2025. In the medium term, defined as the three-year 2027-2029 horizon, management estimates marketing outlays may grow by about 30-40% year on year, with average customer acquisition cost similar to 2023-2026. XTB Foundation also supports brand-building through financial education from basics and investment knowledge dissemination, particularly among young people, strengthening XTBs association as a credible and trusted investment partner. In 2025 brand-building effectiveness was confirmed by advertising-industry awards: Effie Awards 2025 Poland gold statuette and Effie Awards 2025 Europe bronze statuette for an XTB campaign run at the turn of 2024 and 2025. The campaign contributed to an approximately 27% increase in the growth dynamics of new accounts; top-of-mind brand awareness rose from 8.4% to 33%, and spontaneous awareness rose to 41%. Nominated and awarded Effie Awards activities, campaigns and solutions worldwide enter the Effie Index, a global marketing-effectiveness indicator covering agencies, marketers, brands, networks and holdings; Poland is one of more than 55 programs worldwide. Customer acquisition cost in XTB Group was 0.7 thousand PLN per client in 2025, unchanged from the prior year, and recent-quarter stability indicated continued marketing-strategy effectiveness. KDPW monthly data on brokerage account openings showed XTB as the leader generating the highest annual account increase. At end-2025, banks and brokerage houses participating in KDPW maintained 2,534,606 securities accounts, of which XTB clients represented 32.4%, corresponding to 821,748 XTB-run accounts. At end-2024 Poland had 1,969,689 brokerage accounts, with 19.3% of their owners being XTB clients, corresponding to 380,222 XTB-run accounts.
