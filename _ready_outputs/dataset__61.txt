Dom Development S.A. Group, in its 2025 Management Board report, identifies credit intermediation risks, especially potential termination of cooperation agreements by banks, improper execution of duties arising from regulations and supervisory authority guidelines, unacceptable legal status of an investment preventing financing, and unethical or non-compliant performance of obligations against regulations and contractual provisions. Mitigation measures comprise monitoring regulatory changes, monitoring real-estate legal status, and supervising process execution in line with internally defined standards and policies. ESG risk management is integrated into the overall risk management process; individual ESG risks are classified among the main business risks. The ESG risk management area is supported by the Non-financial Reporting Team, while mitigating actions are implemented under the non-financial and sustainable development reporting procedure.
