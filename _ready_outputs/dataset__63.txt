XTB S.A. Group consolidated annual report 2025 section 3.1.4, S1-13, describes employee training and development as a foundation for professional performance. XTB Group operates a training policy based on individual needs, aligning development programs with current employee requirements and business objectives; the training system is governed by the Procedure for conducting educational training and confirming employees' knowledge and competencies. The company supports education through co-financing for training, courses, conferences, university studies, postgraduate studies and specialist examinations, including broker licence and CIIA examinations, with funding rules regulated by XTB training policy principles. Onboarding support for new employees is an additional training-policy element: new hires receive access to an internal knowledge base containing presentations and recordings on the industry and company products, enabling fast and effective work commencement. Training funds are included in individual XTB department budgets; in foreign branches, the training budget is set by the branch director. Employees are delegated to training by their direct supervisor or through individual applications accepted by the director, according to needs. Main external training categories include technical IT training, specialist training to expand professional knowledge, including postgraduate studies and broker-licence preparation courses, soft-skills training and language training through learning co-financing, course-cost reimbursement and access to the eTutor online foreign-language e-learning platform. The benefits and support catalogue also includes the Helping Hand well-being platform providing psychological support, psychoeducation and prevention through online therapist sessions, webinars, workshops and educational articles; reimbursement of corrective glasses costs; holiday vouchers; vacation subsidies for employees with at least two years of service; an allowance for using entitled leave in the given calendar year for employees with at least two years of service; option to join group life insurance on favourable terms; an additional paid birthday leave day; an additional paid care day for young parents; an additional paid care day for a child with a disability certificate; two days off for volunteering; meal co-financing; and a referral program. The scope and form of non-wage benefits in subsidiaries are adapted to local conditions and employee remuneration standards, including additional medical benefits, group insurance and holiday gifts. Employees also receive annual bonuses as financial gratification under adopted rules.
