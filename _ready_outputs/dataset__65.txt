XTB S.A. Capital Group consolidated annual report section for 2025 describes XTB S.A. operating activity also through 7 foreign branches located in Europe. XTB mission: provide clients worldwide with access to an advanced investment platform, professional service and comprehensive financial education; the Group pursues sustainable development and service excellence, builds trust, professionalism and long-term client relationships, and supports client success on financial markets. XTB vision: become a leading provider of investment products, recognized for innovation, transparency and excellent customer service; be perceived as a trusted partner enabling clients to achieve financial goals by delivering an All-in-One platform, knowledge and investment support, with respect for sustainable development. Daily values comprise Technology, Trust and Support. Technology is treated as a key element of investing through XTB; the Group continuously develops competencies, expands knowledge, improves its investment offering, invests in employees professional and personal development, provides opportunities for improvement and new skills, relies on diverse and professional teams, and aims to create a modern, innovative investment environment where technology underpins highest-quality service. Trust is built with stakeholders through responsible actions, transparent communication and openness to dialogue, plus an organizational culture based on respect, cooperation and partnership; operations are supervised by major global regulatory institutions, guaranteeing high standards of transparency and safety, while competencies and service quality are continuously improved to ensure stakeholder stability. Support covers clients through the advanced investment platform, investment knowledge and professional expert assistance; employees through work comfort, a positive atmosphere, flexible employment forms, training, events and a broad benefits package; and shareholders through responsible management and a considered dividend policy creating long-term value based on stable development.
