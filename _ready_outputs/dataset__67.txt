Group's 2025 consolidated sustainability reporting section IRO-2 states that disclosure requirements for material sustainability matters, impacts, risks and opportunities were determined using qualitative usefulness criteria, especially the significance of the sustainability report. The Group presents ESRS disclosures covered by the consolidated sustainability statement with ESRS clause references and report pages. The materiality review confirmed the 2024 assessment that ESRS E2 matters had no material impact. Group companies do not manufacture, use or acquire substances causing pollution of living organisms and food resources through emissions, products, products or services; substances of very high concern through emissions, products, products or services; or microplastics through emissions, products, products or services. These topics were excluded after preliminary ESG analysis of the business model, business strategy and value chain by ESG-responsible personnel. Air, water and soil pollution topics occur during construction of new projects and/or demolition works, but are local and time-limited. Construction-process nuisances are addressed in a specific ESRS S3 disclosure.
