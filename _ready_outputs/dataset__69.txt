Creotech Instruments S.A. reported that key R&D projects follow schedules, with changes or delays handled under contract clauses. On 20 December 2024 it signed with the State Treasury - Armament Agency a PLN 556,680,934.29 gross contract to manufacture and deliver the SSOZ 'Mikroglob' Satellite Earth Observation System, covering phases B, C, D and E1 under ECSS methodology, due by 31 March 2027; detailed schedule and terms are undisclosed for state-security reasons, while terms are standard for such contracts. In Mikroglob, on 4 February 2025 the Agency approved B1 output documentation, partial acceptance of phase B; task B1 was completed for PLN 20,997,105.05 net, including PLN 16,976,962.55 for documentation and PLN 4,020,142.50 for concluding and performing a third-party satellite-launch agreement. On 4 April 2025 B2 documentation was approved; task B2 generated PLN 20,534,712.98 net for delivered documentation. On 2 June phase C was completed for PLN 20,034,627.65 net, including PLN 16,014,485.15 for documentation and PLN 4,020,142.50 for the third-party launch-agreement milestone; C approval started phase D, now in execution. In the first half of 2025 the Company negotiated, signed and began CAMILA, 'COUNTRY AWARENESS MISSION IN LAND ANALYSIS', delivering a national observation-satellite constellation. Creotech is leader and supplier of complete satellite platforms with selected partners' optical and radar payloads, under European Space Agency supervision and commissioned by Poland's Ministry of Development and Technology. CAMILA covers at least three HyperSat-based observation satellites, ground segment, launch and mission management, with optional extensions by ordering-party decision. The April 2025 contract value is EUR 51,925,511, including EUR 25,594,951 directly for the Company and the remainder for subcontractors delivering selected subsystems. In July 2025 the first documentation package was accepted by the European Space Agency in the RKP Requirements Key Point review, triggering EUR 3,178,284 remuneration, about 30% directly for the Company and the balance for CAMILA subcontractors. Creotech continued Polish ImAging SaTellites, PIAST, to design, build and orbit observation nanosatellites for optoelectronic image reconnaissance, based on HyperSat; satellites will be integrated in Poland by Creotech, made in Polish technologies and controlled from Poland. The consortium comprises Wojskowa Akademia Techniczna im. Jarosława Dąbrowskiego, the Company, Centrum Badań Kosmicznych PAN, Sieć Badawcza Łukasiewicz - Instytut Lotnictwa, Scanway Sp. z o.o. and PCO S.A.; the National Centre for Research and Development co-finances PIAST under SZAFIR, with implementation from June 2021 to February 2026. The Company continued eCAUSIS, co-financed by the European Commission with Austrian Institute Of Technology and Fraunhofer Gesellschaft Zur Forderung Der Angewandten Forschung EV, to develop an advanced quantum key distribution module and cryptographic key-management software. In Q2 2025 work continued on a smaller, more integrated second prototype with more Creotech design and know-how; preparation began for receiver/transmitter module-pair production, and in May the EuroQCI meeting in Athens presented Creotech's solution to strong specialist interest. Next-quarter results may be affected by sales shifts, as many production orders and project or contract milestones settle around quarter-ends and slippage can materially affect recorded sales and financial results; availability and price of specialist components, mitigated by many manufacturers or brokers, with customer-borne price risk in electronics production and Company-borne risk for own products, mainly scientific-measurement apparatus and quantum-computer subsystems, partly offsettable through final-product price increases; and production-base investment, with construction/refurbishment planned to end by October 2025 and full launch in the new location planned for Q4 2025 after an occupancy permit.
