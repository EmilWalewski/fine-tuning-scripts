In H1 2025 KRUK Group incurred 805.5M PLN of expenditure on purchases of debt portfolios, 10% lower year on year versus 899.7M PLN in H1 2024. Collections from portfolios acquired by KRUK Group reached 1,910.3M PLN in H1 2025, 10% higher than in the comparable 2024 period; more than half of H1 2025 inflows came from foreign markets. The 172.8M PLN year-on-year increase in collections was generated mainly by 72.2M PLN higher inflows in Italy (+19% YoY) and 42.5M PLN higher inflows in Poland (+6% YoY). The situation in Ukraine did not affect items presented in the financial statements as at the reporting date, nor recognition or measurement of items after the reporting date. From 1 January to 30 June 2025 the Group redeemed bonds under issue terms: AL2 on 2 February 2025 with nominal value of 52.5M PLN, representing the second redemption tranche; AE4 on 27 March 2025 with nominal value of 115.0M PLN; and AH1 on 28 June 2025 with nominal value of 50.0M PLN. In the same period the Group issued new unsecured bonds: AP3 on 5 February 2025 with nominal value of 100.0M PLN, floating interest based on WIBOR3M plus 2.80 p.p. margin, redemption date 5 February 2031; and AL5 on 2 June 2025 with nominal value of 400.0M PLN, floating interest based on WIBOR3M plus 3.00 p.p. margin, redemption date 2 June 2032. On 12 June 2025 the Management Board of KRUK S.A. adopted a resolution establishing the XII Bond Issue Programme with total nominal value not exceeding 900.0M PLN, under which the Parent may issue public bonds. After the reporting period, under the XI Bond Issue Programme, on 7 July 2025 KRUK issued unsecured AP4 bonds with nominal value of 100.0M PLN, floating interest based on WIBOR3M plus 2.70 p.p. margin, redemption date 7 July 2031. On 23 June 2025 the Ordinary General Meeting of KRUK S.A., in line with the Management Board recommendation, resolved to pay a dividend to shareholders of the Parent of 18.00 PLN per share. The 349,252,434.00 PLN dividend will be paid from the Parent net profit earned in 2024; the record date for shareholders entitled to the dividend for the financial year ended 31 December 2024 was set at 10 July 2025; 19,402,913 KRUK S.A. shares are covered; payment date was set at 25 September 2025. The Group profit earned in 2024 will be distributed under the KRUK S.A. Dividend Policy adopted by the Parent Management Board on 2 December 2021. On 16 January 2025 the Parent Management Board adopted the KRUK S.A. Dividend Policy for financial years from the year starting 1 January 2025 through the year ending 31 December 2029. Under that policy KRUK S.A. aims at sustainable development and Group value growth resulting in profit growth and dividend payment while maintaining appropriate liquidity and respecting ESG principles; the Parent Management Board assumes it will recommend to the Ordinary General Meeting a dividend of at least 30% of KRUK Group consolidated net profit attributable to parent shareholders for the preceding financial year, provided debt including the recommended dividend does not exceed 3.0 net debt/cash EBITDA.
