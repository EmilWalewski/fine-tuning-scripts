In Q1 2025, within the Asseco International segment of the Asseco Group quarterly report for the three months ended 31 March 2025, Asseco Lietuva signed contracts with the Ministry of the Interior for implementation and development of the Lithuanian Migration Information System, MIGRIS, and with the National Museum of Art for implementation and development of the museum information system, LIMIS, under the eKultūra platform development project. During the reporting period Asseco Lietuva provided the Customs Department under the Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Lithuania with maintenance, development and modification services for customs systems: MLS, the customs permit system; VLS, the Single Window system; MDAS, the customs declaration processing system; and PPMKS, the system for presenting goods for customs inspection. Asseco Poland acted as strategic subcontractor to Asseco Lietuva in the VLS, MDAS and PPMKS projects. Asseco Lietuva also developed functionality of its proprietary products and added a new version of the AVILYS document management system to its offering.
