KRUK Group’s 2025 outlays on new debt portfolios totaled 2,222.9M PLN, 21% lower year on year versus 2,827.9M PLN invested in the corresponding prior-year period. Recoveries from purchased portfolios reached 3,919.7M PLN in 2025, up 11% year on year; more than half of 2025 collections came from foreign markets. The 383.4M PLN year-on-year increase in collections was driven mainly by Italy, where payments rose by 199.7M PLN, or 26% year on year, and Poland, where payments rose by 118.6M PLN, or 8% year on year. From 1 January to 31 December 2025, the Group redeemed bonds in line with issue terms: AL2 on 2 February 2025 with nominal value of 52.5M PLN as the second redemption installment, AE4 on 27 March 2025 with nominal value of 115.0M PLN, AH1 on 28 June 2025 with nominal value of 50.0M PLN, and AK1 on 25 September 2025 with nominal value of 25.0M PLN. In the same period, the Group issued new unsecured bonds: AP3 on 5 February 2025 with nominal value of 100.0M PLN, floating interest based on WIBOR3M plus a 2.80 p.p. margin, and maturity on 5 February 2031; AL5 on 2 June 2025 with nominal value of 400.0M PLN, floating interest based on WIBOR3M plus a 3.00 p.p. margin, and maturity on 2 June 2032; and AP4 on 7 July 2025 with nominal value of 100.0M PLN, floating interest based on WIBOR3M plus a 2.70 p.p. margin, and maturity on 7 July 2031. On 12 June 2025, the Management Board of KRUK S.A. adopted a resolution establishing the 12th Bond Issue Programme with total nominal value not exceeding 900.0M PLN, under which the Parent Entity may issue public bonds. On 23 June 2025, the Annual General Meeting of KRUK S.A., in line with the Management Board recommendation, resolved to pay a dividend to shareholders of the Parent Entity of 18.00 PLN per share. The dividend payment of 349,252,434.00 PLN was made from the Parent Entity’s 2024 net profit; the dividend record date for the financial year ended 31 December 2024 was set at 10 July 2025, covered 19,402,913 KRUK S.A. shares, and was paid on 25 September 2025. The Group’s 2024 profit was distributed under the KRUK S.A. Dividend Policy adopted by the Parent Entity’s Management Board on 2 December 2021. On 16 January 2025, the Parent Entity’s Management Board adopted a new KRUK S.A. Dividend Policy for financial years from the year beginning 1 January 2025 through the year ending 31 December 2029. Under the policy, KRUK S.A. aims at sustainable Group development and value growth leading to profit growth and dividend payment while maintaining an appropriate liquidity level and respecting ESG principles; the Management Board assumes it will recommend to the Annual General Meeting a dividend of at least 30% of consolidated net profit attributable to Parent Entity shareholders for the previous financial year, provided debt including the recommended dividend does not exceed 3.0 net debt/cash EBITDA. Since the last financial year-end, contingent liabilities or contingent assets had not changed up to the report publication date, except for expiry of guarantees according to indicated dates. Security established on Group assets was presented as at 31 December 2025 in a guarantees issued/bills of exchange table, and as at 31 December 2025 InvestCapital Ltd had no liabilities covered by surety.
