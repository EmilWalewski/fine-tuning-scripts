Dom Development S.A.'s General Meeting operates as an Ordinary or Extraordinary General Meeting under the Commercial Companies Code of 15 September 2000, the Company's Articles of Association, and the public General Meeting Rules of 5 September 2006, amended by Resolution No. 27 of 15 May 2008 and Resolution No. 31 of 21 May 2009 of the Ordinary General Meeting. The Ordinary General Meeting, convened by the Management Board, is held in Warsaw within 6 months after each financial year-end. Participation rights belong to persons holding shareholder status 16 days before the meeting date, the record date for participation. Management Board and Supervisory Board members attend without invitations; other persons, especially auditors and experts, may be invited by the Management Board when their opinions are needed for matters under consideration. The statutory auditor should attend any General Meeting covering the Company's financial matters. The General Meeting is valid and may adopt resolutions only if shareholders representing at least 50.1% of all votes are present or represented. Resolutions are adopted by an absolute majority of valid votes unless the Commercial Companies Code or the Articles of Association provide otherwise. Voting may use an electronic vote casting and counting system. Pledgees and usufructuaries of shares may not exercise voting rights. A resolution to remove from the agenda, or discontinue consideration of, matters placed on the agenda at shareholders' request requires a 3/4 majority of votes cast and prior consent of all present shareholders who submitted the request. After the attendance list is signed and checked, the Chair submits the agenda to vote; the General Meeting may accept it unchanged, change item order, remove items subject to the Articles of Association, or add new matters for discussion without adopting resolutions on them. The Chair may not remove or change agenda items without General Meeting consent. Each participant may speak on currently considered agenda items and submit formal motions; formal motions on meeting and voting procedure are heard out of order. Resolutions are adopted on agenda matters after voting; voting is open subject to the Articles of Association and the Commercial Companies Code. In 2025, Dom Development S.A.'s Ordinary General Meeting was held on 12 June 2025, convened at the Management Board's request; shareholders did not request the convening of a General Meeting. The meeting complied with the Commercial Companies Code, the Articles of Association, the public General Meeting Rules, and Best Practices for Companies Listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. Shareholders could review draft resolutions on the agenda no later than 26 days before the planned meeting date. During verification of shareholder credentials, the Company did not challenge documents submitted by shareholders or proxies confirming representation rights. The Chair ensured efficient proceedings; the meeting was neither cancelled nor interrupted. Present Management Board and Supervisory Board members were ready to provide explanations within their competences and applicable law. Resolutions were adopted under conditions protecting minority shareholders' rights, including the right to submit reservations or objections; no adopted resolution was challenged in court. The Ordinary General Meeting took place within the deadline under Article 395 of the Commercial Companies Code, and financial statement documentation for 2024 was published on the Company's website almost 3 months before the meeting. All resolutions adopted by the General Meeting in 2025 served the Company's interest and accounted for other stakeholders' rights; adopted resolutions are available at https://inwestor.domd.pl/pl/wza. Amendments to Dom Development S.A.'s Articles of Association, under Article 430 § 1 and Article 415 § 1 of the Commercial Companies Code, require a General Meeting resolution adopted by a 3/4 majority and registration in the register. If an amendment increases shareholder obligations or reduces rights personally granted to individual shareholders, Article 354 of the Commercial Companies Code requires consent of all shareholders affected by the resolution. The Management Board submits amendments to the registry court. The General Meeting may authorize the Supervisory Board to establish the consolidated text of the amended Articles of Association or introduce other editorial changes specified in the General Meeting resolution.
