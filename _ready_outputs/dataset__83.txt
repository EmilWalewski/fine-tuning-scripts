XTB S.A. Supervisory Board exercises permanent oversight over the Company in all areas of activity. Under §15 of the Articles of Association, the Supervisory Board has five to nine members appointed for a three-year common term; detailed operating rules and organisation are set by the Supervisory Board Regulations. Jakub Zablocki has a personal right to appoint and dismiss one Supervisory Board member serving as Chair through a written appointment or dismissal statement delivered to the Company, as an alternative appointment method under Art. 385 §2 of the Commercial Companies Code; this right lasts while he, through controlled entities under the Accounting Act, jointly with such entities or personally, holds Company shares representing at least 33% of total votes at the General Meeting. Other Supervisory Board members are appointed and dismissed by the General Meeting. The General Meeting determines the number of Supervisory Board members for a given term; absent a different resolution, the Board has five members, and if elected by separate group voting under Art. 385 of the Commercial Companies Code, it also has five members. Board members may elect from among themselves a Vice-Chair and Secretary. The Chair directs Supervisory Board work and represents it before other Company bodies; in the Chair's absence or vacancy, those powers are exercised by a member authorised by the Chair, and absent such authorisation by the oldest Board member.
