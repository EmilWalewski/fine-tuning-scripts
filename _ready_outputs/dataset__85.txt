Creotech Instruments Spolka Akcyjna reported revenue of 95.1M PLN for the 9 months ended 30 September 2025, up 72.1M PLN year on year, driven by progress on the “Mikroglob” project. Total operating costs rose by 59.7M PLN to 99.8M PLN over the comparable 9-month period. The largest cost drivers were external services, up 43.9M PLN mainly due to subcontractor services in “Mikroglob”, and salaries, social security and other employee benefits, up 9.8M PLN mainly due to the increased scale of operations. Significant profit and loss items, in the report’s clear 9-month comparison, show product sales revenue of 84.6M PLN for 01.01.2025-30.09.2025 versus 15.5M PLN for 01.01.2024-30.09.2024, grant income of 10.5M PLN versus 7.5M PLN, materials and energy consumption of 6.7M PLN versus 6.2M PLN, external services of 50.4M PLN versus 6.5M PLN, salaries, social security and other benefits of 31.4M PLN versus 21.6M PLN, depreciation and amortisation of 10.6M PLN versus 6.0M PLN, and net loss from continuing operations of 3.0M PLN versus 14.5M PLN. Total assets at 30 September 2025 were 317.0M PLN, up 154.6M PLN from 31 December 2024. Non-current assets were 120.2M PLN, up 12.7M PLN from year-end 2024, primarily due to a 6.5M PLN increase in intangible assets connected mainly with higher capitalised development expenditure. Current assets increased by 141.9M PLN versus year-end 2024, mainly from a 47.5M PLN increase in contract assets and a 75.7M PLN increase in cash generated by proceeds from the series K share issue and advances received in the “Camila” and “Mikroglob” projects. Equity at 30 September 2025 was 140.7M PLN, up 69.8M PLN from year-end 2024 due to proceeds from the series K issue. Trade payables and other liabilities increased by 71.9M PLN versus year-end 2024 in connection with the start of key purchases for the “Mikroglob” and “Camila” projects and advances received in those projects. During the interim condensed financial reporting period ended 30 September 2025, no changes occurred in business conditions or the economic environment that affected the fair value of the Company’s financial assets or financial liabilities. The Company primarily develops space technologies, scientific and control-measurement apparatus, and quantum technologies; production for customers is an additional revenue source. The Management Board determined operating segments using reports applied for strategic decisions, with segment reporting consistent with internal reporting presented to managers and operational decision-makers. The basic segmentation follows core sales assortment groups from which the Company earns or plans to earn revenue. Four non-aggregated segments were identified: Space, covering design, production and sale of subsystems and systems used in the space industry, including satellites; Geospatial, covering design, production and sale of subsystems and systems for drone operation management and for generating, processing or providing satellite and drone data; Quantum Systems, covering design, production and sale of subsystems and systems used in quantum computers, quantum cryptography, time synchronisation systems and other subsystems or systems used in scientific activity; and Manufacturing, covering contract production of industrial electronics for customers. The Company does not present assets and liabilities, other operating income and expenses, or finance income and costs by reportable segment because those amounts are not regularly presented to the chief operating decision-maker. For the 9 months ended 30 September 2025 and 30 September 2024, sales revenue consisted of product and service sales revenue of 83.9M PLN, 99.19% of total sales, versus 15.5M PLN, 99.98%; the same amounts were revenue from contracts with customers, with no revenue excluded from IFRS 15. Sales of goods and materials were 0.7M PLN, 0.81% of total sales, versus 0.004M PLN, 0.02%; the same amounts were revenue from contracts with customers, with no revenue excluded from IFRS 15. Total sales revenue was 84.6M PLN in 2025 versus 15.5M PLN in 2024, entirely revenue from contracts with customers in both periods.
