From 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025, the Audit Committee of Dom Development S.A., a permanent Supervisory Board committee, comprised Dorota Podedworna-Tarnowska as Chair, Mark Spiteri, and Edyta Wojtkiewicz. It must have at least three Supervisory Board members; at least two, including the Chair, must be independent under Article 129(3) of the Act of 11 May 2017 on Statutory Auditors, Audit Firms and Public Oversight and section 7.7 of the Company Statute; at least one must have accounting or financial-statement audit expertise, and at least one must know the Company sector. Dorota Podedworna-Tarnowska and Edyta Wojtkiewicz met independence criteria; all members had accounting or audit knowledge. Dorota Podedworna-Tarnowska graduated in finance and banking, holds a PhD in economics, and has expertise in economics, finance, accounting, financial revision, and financial statement audit. Edyta Wojtkiewicz graduated from the Finance and Banking Faculty of the Warsaw School of Economics and gained experience as financial controller, chief accountant, and finance director. Mark Spiteri is a chartered accountant with development and real-estate sector expertise from project management and cooperation with developers and consulting firms. The Committee powers are defined in Audit Committee Regulations approved by the Supervisory Board and Article 130 of the Act of 11 May 2017. Duties include supervising Management Board compliance with law and regulations, especially the Accounting Act of 29 September 1994 and the Act of 11 May 2017; overseeing financial information preparation, accounting policy selection, new-regulation impacts, estimates, forecasts, and auditor recommendations; recommending statutory auditor appointment and dismissal; controlling auditor independence, objectivity, rotation, and remuneration; reviewing auditor work; controlling risk management, internal control, and internal audit effectiveness; monitoring compliance, whistleblowing or other irregularity-reporting systems, and capital group sustainability reporting. The Committee cooperates with the Supervisory Board, Management Board, middle management, internal audit, and external audit. Meeting invitees include the Vice-President of the Management Board for Finance, Deputy Finance Director and Financial Controller, and external and internal audit representatives. In 2025 the Committee held five meetings; at three, members received information on sustainability reporting and assurance. The audit firm auditing the Company financial statements provided permitted non-audit services in 2025: assurance on the assessment of the Supervisory Board Report on Remuneration of Management Board and Supervisory Board members of Dom Development S.A. for 2024, and assurance of 2024 sustainability reporting of the Dom Development S.A. Capital Group. The Committee assessed the audit firm independence and approved those services. Under the policy for selecting a statutory financial statement audit and sustainability reporting assurance firm, the Supervisory Board selects the firm on an Audit Committee recommendation. Criteria include mandatory independence and impartiality; written independence confirmation from Dom Development S.A. and its group; discussion of independence threats and safeguards with the Committee; audit or assurance remuneration not contingent on events, results, or additional non-audit services for the audited or assured entity or related entities by the audit firm, affiliate, or network member; service quality and reliability; experience and capacity in auditing public-interest entities and similar-profile entities or assuring sustainability reporting, especially in development and real estate; nationwide audit or assurance capability in the required scope; ongoing legal-change monitoring; and verification of qualifications and experience of directly engaged persons. Under the permitted non-audit services policy, the statutory audit firm, affiliates, and network members may not directly or indirectly provide Dom Development S.A. or related entities with prohibited non-audit services other than financial statement audit or financial revision from the first day of the audited period until the audit report. For internal control procedures, risk management, and IT systems, the restriction applies in the financial year immediately preceding the audited period. Services under Article 136(2) of the Act of 11 May 2017 are not prohibited and may be provided only if unrelated to Dom Development S.A. tax policy, after Committee assessment of independence threats and safeguards and approval. If a statutory auditor or audit firm provides Dom Development S.A. or Dom Development S.A. Capital Group companies with permitted non-audit services for at least three consecutive financial years, total remuneration for those services is capped at 70% of average remuneration paid in the last three consecutive financial years for statutory audit of Dom Development S.A. financial statements and consolidated financial statements of the Dom Development S.A. Capital Group.
