Dom Development Group sustainability compliance system supports implementation of the ESG DOM 2030 Strategy and effective corporate governance by preventing legal risk from non-compliance with laws, internal Group regulations and adopted standards of conduct in relation to material impacts, risks and opportunities. The Chief Risk and Compliance Officer provides substantive support for sustainability, advises on management of the area, assists in assessment and measurement of non-compliance risk and in remedial actions mitigating risks generated at individual company or Group level, and supports the ESG Coordinator in preparing management reports and information for Management Boards of Group companies and the Company Supervisory Board, developing good practices, and creating and improving uniform standards under GOV-2. The internal audit function operates independently from operational functions and objectively adds value by improving Dom Development Group operations through systematic assessment of risk management, control and organizational governance processes; it provides assurance on sustainability processes and sustainability advisory. Internal audit is supervised by the Assurance Lead, functionally reporting to the Audit Committee and organizationally to the President of the Management Board of Dom Development S.A., supporting independence and objectivity. Assurance and advisory tasks are aligned with ESG DOM 2030 and the Internal Audit Strategy for 2024-2027, covering ESG risk monitoring, assessment of compliance with sustainability reporting regulations including CSRD and ESRS, and verification of reliability and quality of ESG data reported by the Group. The Assurance Lead reports to the Management Board and Audit Committee on audit plan execution, audit results, and implementation status of recommendations arising from identified observations, applying both business and sustainability perspectives; these matters are discussed at relevant meetings. Under the Internal Audit Plan aligned with the Internal Audit Strategy, the ESG area will be subject to internal audit in 2027; in 2026 the Group will verify implementation of recommendations from the 2025 DDSA Group construction site safety audit report; in 2025 internal audit covered Governance and participated in anti-corruption threat analysis. Dom Development S.A. has been listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange since 2006, and its shares are included in the mWIG40 index. The Group primarily builds and sells residential real estate to individual customers, offering apartments in multifamily buildings in major Polish agglomerations: Warsaw, Wroclaw, Krakow and the Tricity, where it operates under the Euro Styl brand. Total revenue breakdown and employee numbers by location under IFRS 8 are presented in note 7.33 to the consolidated financial statements and section 1.5 of the report. The only significant financial reporting segment is real estate, M.68.11 Buying and selling of own real estate. Dom Development S.A. is the largest developer in Poland by asset size, revenue and profits; by profitability it ranks first among Polish real estate companies listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange based on return on equity. The Group also has a social role: by fulfilling its business mission, its companies directly contribute to the Sustainable Development Goals, use customer satisfaction as a key activity measure, listen to customer opinions and needs, build responsibly for future social and climate challenges, reduce the housing shortage and improve quality of life in major Polish cities. The Group Management Board is responsible for a sustainability approach based on three pillars: minimizing environmental impact and supporting urban biodiversity; supporting local communities by creating high-quality urban fabric conducive to integration; and ensuring the highest corporate governance standards and transparent communication with all stakeholders. The Group set schedules for achieving objectives in each of the three pillars. Group companies do not conduct activities related to fossil fuels, including coal, oil and gas.
