XTB Group is supervised by regulatory and public authorities; in Poland brokerage activity requires KNF authorization, and the Company is a KNF-licensed brokerage house. The Act amending certain acts to ensure digital operational resilience of the financial sector, dated 25 June 2025, followed a Government Legislation Centre draft of 18 April 2024 and implements DORA in Poland. It confirms KNF as competent authority, requires annual KNF reporting by 31 January on ICT contracts, changes banking outsourcing through broader outsourcing bases and added ICT duties for banks and entrepreneurs, authorizes KNF review of operational-resilience test results, and sets sanctions: financial entities up to approximately PLN 21M or 10% of revenue, responsible persons up to approximately PLN 3M, and management-board, supervisory-board or other management-function bans up to 1 year. The act was passed on 25 June 2025, signed by the President of Poland on 31 July 2025 and entered into force on 7 August 2025. DORA, amending Regulations (EC) No. 1060/2009, (EU) No. 648/2012, (EU) No. 600/2014 and (EU) No. 909/2014, was published in the EU Official Journal on 27 December 2022. It targets resilience to digital and ICT threats through ICT risk management, external technology-provider rules, periodic digital-resilience testing, detailed incident classification and reporting, and information-exchange systems among financial entities on effective ICT threat-defense methods. DORA entered into force on 16 January 2023, compliance was required by 17 January 2025, and the Company exercised due care to prepare and adapt. Directive 2022/2464 aims to make more undertakings report material, comparable and reliable sustainability information useful to investors. Related statutory changes cover accounting simplifications for small and micro entities, sustainability-reporting duties for large entities, listed entities and certain financial institutions, assurance of such reporting, and simplified capital-group reporting. The Act of 6 December 2024, signed by the President of Poland on 12 December 2024, entered into force 14 days after publication; revenue-threshold and enterprise-size criteria changes entered into force on 1 January 2025, while sustainability-reporting provisions are phased in during 2024-2027. The Act of 26 April 2024 on accessibility requirements for certain products and services by business entities, adopted by the Sejm on 26 April 2024, implements Directive (EU) 2019/882 of 17 April 2019 and aims to make websites, mobile applications and other digital services accessible to persons with disabilities by adapting digital content to accessibility standards.
