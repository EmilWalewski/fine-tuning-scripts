XTB S.A. Group report excerpt describes ongoing litigation, administrative and supervisory proceedings. In one appeal case, the court set the hearing for 10 May 2024, then adjourned it to 21 June 2024, 9 July 2024 and 9 August 2024; on 9 August 2024 the court closed the hearing and heard the parties. Judgment publication was postponed to 9 September 2024, 8 November 2024 and finally 31 January 2025. At the 31 January 2025 hearing the judge reopened the closed hearing for the third time and adjourned the proceedings without setting a date. An expert prepared an opinion, objections were filed, the court has taken no further actions, and the Company awaits a new hearing date and further decisions. As at 31 December 2025 and the report publication date, the parent and subsidiaries were not parties to material proceedings before a court, arbitration authority or public administration body, although several important proceedings were ongoing. Court proceedings involving the Company and Group companies as defendants mainly concern employee claims and customer claims. At the report date, total claims pursued against the Company and Group companies were approximately PLN 17.4M, including three employee-claim proceedings worth approximately PLN 0.84M, ten customer-claim proceedings with total claims of approximately PLN 13.8M, and one proceeding concerning alleged failure by the Company to apply financial security measures with a dispute value of PLN 2.8M. Key proceedings include an August 2019 customer lawsuit alleging illegal Company actions, delivered to the Company in December 2019, with a dispute value of PLN 7.0M. The Management Board considers the customer claim entirely groundless and states the customer's losses resulted solely from erroneous investment decisions, demonstrated during the 2016 Polish Financial Supervision Authority (KNF) inspection, later correspondence with the supervisor and an independent consulting firm's expert opinion. Administrative and control proceedings include several reviews connected with Group operations. On 7 September 2023 the Czech National Bank began an inspection of the Company's Czech branch, covering operational procedures and compliance with local financial regulations. The Company cooperated by providing required documents and explanations. At the report date the inspection had ended, the Company had received a non-final decision imposing a CZK 2.0M fine, approximately PLN 0.343M at the average NBP rate of 30 June 2025, disagreed with the decision and filed an appeal; part of the recommendations overlapped with the Company's own findings already being implemented before the inspection. On 17 October 2023 the Polish Financial Supervision Authority Office began an inspection of whether the Company's activity complied with law, regulations, permit conditions, fair trading principles and clients' interests; the inspection ended on 10 October 2024, post-inspection recommendations were issued and fully implemented. On 12 February 2024 IFSC, the Belize capital-market supervisory commission, began an inspection of subsidiary XTB International Limited in Belize; at the report date the Company had implemented post-inspection recommendations. On 14 February 2024 CNMV began an AML compliance inspection of the Company's Spanish branch, assessing anti-money-laundering policies and their practical effectiveness. The Company actively cooperated with inspectors and provided necessary information. At the report date it was implementing post-inspection recommendations, some of which it had already identified and started implementing before the inspection. On 1 October 2024 the Head of the National Revenue Administration opened a tax inspection of XTB S.A. covering the application of the transaction-pricing method recognized by the Head of KAS between XTB S.A. and related foreign entities under decision DKP9.8041.24.2020 of 5 January 2023. The inspection covered 1 January 2019 to 31 December 2023 and ended on 31 December 2025 when XTB S.A. received the inspection protocol. On 14 January 2026 XTB S.A. filed objections to KAS findings in the 17 December 2025 tax inspection protocol; by letter dated 28 January 2026 the Head of KAS responded to those objections. On 28 February 2025 CNMV began an AML compliance inspection of the Company's Portuguese branch, including detailed analysis of customer identification procedures and transaction monitoring. The Company provided all required documents and explanations; the inspection ended, the Company received a protocol with recommendations and submitted comments. At the report date the Company had not yet received the authority's response, while some recommendations were consistent with its own findings implemented before the inspection. On 16 May 2025 the issuer received notice that KNF had opened administrative proceedings to determine whether grounds existed to impose the administrative sanction under Article 167(2)(1) in conjunction with Article 167(1)(1) and Article 167(2a) of the 29 July 2005 Act on Trading in Financial Instruments.
