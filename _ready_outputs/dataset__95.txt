Dom Development S.A. separate financial statements for 2025 describe core accounting policies. Investments in subsidiaries, associates and joint ventures are carried at historical cost less impairment. Property, plant and equipment are measured at acquisition cost less accumulated depreciation and impairment, with straight-line depreciation over useful lives: buildings and structures 2.5%-4.5%, vehicles 20%, and equipment and other fixed assets 10%-30%. Office-space right-of-use assets are accounted for under IFRS 16 and amortised over the lease term.

Inventories comprise finished goods, mainly residential units and parking spaces, and work in progress, measured at the lower of production or acquisition cost and net realisable selling price. Net selling price is estimated by the Management Board using market prices. For development projects, inventory impairment testing compares production cost with net realisable selling price. If a project is expected to generate a loss, a write-down of work in progress is recognised immediately in profit or loss. Allowances may also cover real estate where the development process carries material timing risk. Budgets are prepared for every development project and updated at least quarterly. For impairment testing they include past and forecast net revenues less direct land, design, construction and project-preparation costs, including show units and construction-site sales offices, plus related external financing costs and expected customer claims where applicable. A positive project margin means no inventory allowance is needed; a negative margin, after detailed cash-flow verification, results in an allowance equal to the estimated negative margin, recognised in cost of sales. Reversal is possible when the expected margin turns positive. Multi-stage projects are tested as one project, except stages where sales and construction have started, which are tested separately.

Borrowing costs are expensed when incurred unless directly attributable to qualifying assets, in the Companys case work in progress, where they form part of production cost. Capitalisation applies only while a development project is active, including design or construction work or procedures to obtain key administrative decisions. It ceases when substantially all activities needed to prepare apartments for handover are completed and is suspended if project activity stops.

Trade and other receivables are measured under IFRS 9 using expected credit loss estimates. Bank deposits with original maturity above three months are current financial assets. Cash and short-term deposits with original maturity up to three months are stated at nominal value and include bank cash, cash on hand and short-term deposits; cash and cash equivalents in the cash-flow statement equal those balances less overdrafts. Interest-bearing bank loans, borrowings and debt securities are initially recognised at fair value less transaction costs and later measured at amortised cost using the effective interest method. Drawdowns and repayments are shown gross in financing cash flows, except overdrafts, where movements are net because of short maturities and rapid turnover.

Current trade, tax and other liabilities are carried at the amount payable. When the time value of money is material, especially for retained guarantee deposits, liabilities are discounted to present value and discount unwinding is recognised as finance cost. Provisions are recognised when a present legal or constructive obligation from past events exists, an outflow is probable and the amount can be reliably estimated; related costs are presented net of reimbursements. Revenue is recognised when economic benefits are probable and revenue can be measured reliably. Revenue from sale of products, including residential units and commercial space, is recognised when control, significant ownership risks and rewards transfer to the buyer, which the Company assesses as the handover of the property under a signed acceptance protocol after the buyer has paid 100% of the purchase price.
