XTB describes margin trading as leveraged trading enabling investors to acquire instruments worth more than their own funds using broker-borrowed funds. Following the success of Investment Plans for long-term ETF portfolio construction, XTB plans Investment Plans 2.0 with equity investing and extended trading hours, enabling share purchases and sales outside the main exchange session. Environmental and social factors are not dominant product-design drivers, but XTB addresses conscious clients through financial instruments, including ESG-related ETFs. Since 2023 the platform has presented ESG-rating information for most offered company securities and ETFs. In 2025 XTB offered over 200 ETFs giving diversified exposure to shares or bonds of companies with ESG ratings of 3 or above against specific benchmarks; ESG ETF benchmarks exclude companies failing methodology-based ESG criteria.

The Group strategy is to build a universal investment application for short- and long-term investors. For 2026 XTB plans product expansion including spot cryptoasset trading, after Cyprus Securities and Exchange Commission authorisation at the end of 2025. Cyprus clients are expected to access crypto trading in the XTB application in the first half of 2026 under MiCA requirements. XTB remains interested in Polish authorisation when possible and intends to expand to other markets. Planned exchange-traded options would give buyers the right to buy or sell a specified number of shares at a predetermined price within a specified period; calls benefit from share-price increases and puts from decreases. Long-term investment products include Osobiste Konto Inwestycyjne, proposed by the Ministry of Finance to stimulate capital-market participation and long-term saving, allowing investment up to PLN 100k without Belka tax, including a savings part up to PLN 25k, while excess funds would be subject to a 0.8%-0.9% asset tax. The bill remains at the Ministry of Finance. XTB also plans to extend retirement-account offers in other European countries and strengthen the Foundation's activity.

Stakeholder research shaped double materiality workshops covering positive, negative, actual and potential impacts, opportunities and risks across XTB Group activity areas. A qualified consulting-firm expert moderated the work, and the final results were validated by the Management Board of XTB S.A. and presented to supervisory bodies. In 2025 the double materiality results were reassessed for currency and sent to internal stakeholders from the original research and to selected employee representatives for opinions and confirmation that material topics covered all Group activity areas. Discussion with Risk Control identified an additional cybersecurity risk. Updated 2025 results were presented for approval to the Management Board and supervisory bodies. The 2025 statement is based on the 2024 double materiality assessment.

The assessment gathered views from employees, employee representatives, retail and institutional clients, business partners, media, suppliers and investors, and was supplemented by interviews with Supervisory Board representatives and representatives of XTB S.A. branches and companies. Stakeholders identified key ESG issues as responsible e-waste management, minimising climate-change impact and reducing greenhouse gas emissions. Other material topics were personal-data security and confidentiality, effective employee communication, customer-service quality and satisfaction. Governance priorities included business ethics, legal and regulatory compliance, anti-corruption practices and prevention of corruption, bribery and money laundering. Educational activity was also important, including employee environmental awareness and financial-management education for children and youth, while respondents often suggested stronger engagement in charity and social actions.

Stakeholder dialogue covers retail and institutional clients through the Customer Service Center, hotline, chatbot, email, investment application, webinars, educational materials, advertising, website, social media and surveys. These activities support product use, client activation and acquisition, brand trust and investment education. Employees and co-workers are engaged through employee representatives, internal mailings and intranet, meetings, evaluation talks, direct Management Board contact, procedures, office communications and surveys. Examples include Inner Power meetings, volunteering, integration events, product testing, weekly xPress newsletter, annual satisfaction survey, EduXchange meetings, Earth Day and Health Day, #2h4family and product-test invitations, aimed at incorporating feedback into strategy and decisions, matching training to needs, improving satisfaction and work comfort, and building awareness and positive habits. Media representatives are engaged through press releases, direct PR contact, responses to inquiries and articles written by XTB employees to build brand awareness and provide credible information.
