Dom Development S.A.'s unaudited interim condensed separate financial statements for the six months ended 30 June 2025 recognize IFRS 16 right-of-use assets for office space leases, perpetual usufruct land rights and other assets, mainly cars. Lease liabilities totaled PLN 43.0M at 30 June 2025 versus PLN 50.4M at 31 December 2024: current PLN 38.4M versus PLN 43.1M, non-current PLN 4.7M versus PLN 7.3M. Current liabilities included perpetual usufruct land rights PLN 31.7M versus PLN 37.1M, leased office and other space PLN 6.0M versus PLN 5.8M, other PLN 0.7M versus PLN 0.1M; non-current liabilities included leased office and other space PLN 4.6M versus PLN 7.2M and other PLN 0.03M versus PLN 0.07M. Perpetual usufruct fees are discounted over the granted term for each property, potentially up to 99 years, independently of expected ownership period or planned development use. At 30 June 2025 perpetual usufruct land rights carried a PLN 31.7M lease liability classified as current; management estimates PLN 2.1M payable within 12 months, PLN 1.5M after 12 months and PLN 28.2M transferable to buyers of premises. Deferred income represents customer payments for premises and parking spaces until handover and revenue recognition; it depends on sales pace versus handover pace. Customer advances not yet recognized in profit or loss rose to PLN 1,140.4M at 30 June 2025 from PLN 861.0M at 31 December 2024, with no other items. Operations are essentially homogeneous, mainly construction and sale of residential and service/commercial premises with related support activities. The Company operates only in Warsaw, while the Dom Development S.A. Group, its parent group, also operates in Tricity, Wroclaw and Krakow through subsidiaries. Group reporting segments are geographical: Warsaw, Tricity, Wroclaw, Krakow; segment results are assessed mainly by sales revenue, gross profit on sales and gross margin. Because the Company's activity is within one Warsaw segment, these separate interim statements do not include segment information; segment data are in the interim consolidated statements.
