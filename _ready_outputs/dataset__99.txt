Dom Development S.A. standalone financial statement excerpt for 2025 reports Warsaw project deliveries, subsequent financing, debt forecasts, auditor fees and selected data translated into euro. Completed construction in 2025 totaled 2,416 apartments and service units: 236 in Q1, 405 in Q2, 720 in Q3 and 1,055 in Q4. Major Q4 completions included Dzielnica Mieszkaniowa Metro Zachod Stage 8 Phase 4 with 249 units, Stage 8 Phase 3 with 208, Osiedle Przy Alejach Stage 1 Phase 2 with 150, Apartamenty Rudnickiego Stage 1 Phase 1 with 143, Osiedle Przy Forcie Stage 1 Phase 1 with 124, Osiedle Harmonia Mokotow Stage 3 Phase 1 with 105, Dzielnica Mieszkaniowa Metro Zachod Stage 6 Phase 1 with 57, and Osiedle Przy Alejach Stage 1 Phase 4 with 19. Subsidiaries also conducted projects in Wroclaw, Tricity and Krakow. Retail premises handed over to clients totaled 1,915 units in 2025 versus 1,969 in 2024, with quarterly handovers of 341, 156, 494 and 924 units. Notes specify that Q4 2024 total Company handovers were 918 units, including 618 retail units and 300 units delivered to a PRS investor, while Q2 2025 total handovers were 253 units, including 156 retail units and 97 PRS units.

Subsequent events included a 5 February 2026 current-account credit facility with VeloBank S.A. up to PLN 100.0M, valid until 31 December 2027. Under the bond issue programme established on 14 April 2025, on 4 March 2026 the Company issued 250,000 unsecured bearer bonds series DOMDEM2040331 with PLN 1,000 nominal value each, aggregate nominal value PLN 250.0M and redemption date 4 March 2031. Issue value equalled nominal value, interest is WIBOR 6M plus a 1.2% margin, interest is paid semi-annually, and the bonds will be introduced to Catalyst alternative trading.

The Management Board does not publish parent or Group financial forecasts except financial-liability forecasts required under the Bond Act until full bond redemption. Forecasts from 23 December 2024 versus actual standalone values at 31 December 2025 show credit and loan liabilities forecast at PLN 378.0M, or 8.48% of total assets, versus actual PLN 10.0M, or 0.26%; debt securities forecast at PLN 610.0M, or 13.68%, versus actual PLN 651.214M, or 16.87%; and lease liabilities forecast at PLN 50.270M, or 1.13%, versus actual PLN 34.545M, or 2.19%. Lower actual total financial liabilities reflected lower debt due to stronger than planned operating cash generation, slower launches of new projects and related expenditure after offer adjustment to weaker than planned market demand, lower purchases of new land for future projects, and deferral of some expenditures to 2026. Higher than planned lease debt mainly reflected extension of the Warsaw headquarters office lease. The Company also shifted financing toward bonds, issuing PLN 135.0M of bonds on 16 May 2025 instead of previously planned bank debt.

The 2025 and 2024 standalone and consolidated financial statements were audited by PricewaterhouseCoopers Polska sp. z o.o. Audyt sp.k. and related PwC entities. Fees for services paid or payable were PLN 1.256M in 2025 versus PLN 1.484M in 2024. Parent-company fees were PLN 0.706M versus PLN 0.661M, including audit PLN 0.534M, half-year review PLN 0.124M and remuneration-report assessment PLN 0.048M. Subsidiary fees were PLN 0.550M versus PLN 0.823M, all for financial statement audit in 2025. Other services in 2024 included assurance of sustainability reporting. The Company audit fee also includes audit of condensed standalone financial statements for six-month periods ended 30 June 2025 and 30 June 2024. PwC also audits the consolidation package for SCOP Luxembourg 2007 S.a r.l.; that fee is borne by SCOP and excluded from the table.
